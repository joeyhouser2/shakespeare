Hope by disdayne growes chereles
feare doth love, love doth feare,
beautie peareles. Farewell.

If no delayes can move thee,
life shall dye, death shall live
stil to love thee. Farewell.

Yet be thou mindfull ever,
heate from fire, fire from heat
none can sever, Farewell.

True love cannot be chainged,
though delight from desert
be estranged. Farewell.
