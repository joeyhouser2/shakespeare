Once liu'd, once I knew delight,
No griefe did shadowe then my pleasure:
Grac'd with love, cheer'd with beauties sight,
I joyed alone true heau•nly treasure,
O what a heau•n is love firmely embraced,
Such power alone can fixe delight
In Fortunes bolome ever placed.

Cold as Ice frozen is that hart,
Where thought of love could no time enter:
Such of life reape the poorest part
Whose weight cleaves to this earthly center,
Mutuallioies in hearts truly united
Do earth to heavenly state conuert
Like heau'n still in it selfe delighted.
