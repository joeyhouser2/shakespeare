Oft have I dream'd of joy,
yet I never felt the sweete,
But tired with annoy,
my griefs each other greete.
Oft have I left my hope,
as a wretch by fate forlome.

But Love aimes at one scope,
and lost will stil returne:
He that once loves with a true desire
never can depart,
For Cupid is the king of every hart.
Come, come, etc.
