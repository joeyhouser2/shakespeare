False world farewell the enemy to rest, now do thy worst, I do not weigh thy spight:
Free from thy cares I live for ever blest,
Enioying peace and heavenly true delight.

Delight, whom woes nor sorrowes shall amate, nor feares or teares disturbe her happy state.
And thus I leave thy hopes, thy joyes untrue, and thus, and thus vaine world againe adue.
