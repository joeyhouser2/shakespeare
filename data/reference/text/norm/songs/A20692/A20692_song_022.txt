1 O, I am as heavy as earth,
Say then who is Humor now.
1 Why then tis I am drownde in Woe,
But never Humor, etc.
1 Mirth then is drownde in sorrowes brim,
Oh, in sorrow all things sleepe.
1 In her presence all things smile,
Humor frolike then a while.
But never Humor, etc.

2 I am now inclind to mirth, humor I as well as thou.
2 No no wit is cherisht so, but never humor: etc.
2 No no foole the light's things swim▪ heavie things sinck to the deepe: but never humor: etc.
