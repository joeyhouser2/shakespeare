1 Fortune sweares, weakest harts
The booke of Cupids arts
Turne with her wheele,
Sences themselves shall prove
Venture her place in love
Aske them that feele.

2 This discord it beget
Atheist that honour not
Nature thought good,
Fortune should ever dwell
In court where wits excell
Love keepe the wood.

3 So to the wood went I
With love to live and die
Fortune forlorne,
Experience of my youth
Made me thinke humble truth
In desert borne.

4 My saint is deere to me,
And Jone her selfe is she
Jone faier and true,
Jone that doth ever move,
Passions of love with love
Fortune adiew.
