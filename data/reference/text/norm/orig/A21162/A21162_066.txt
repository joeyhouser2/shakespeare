I Have no joye, but dreame of joye, and joye to thinke on joye,
A joye I withstoode, for to enioye, to finishe mine annoye,
I hate not without cause alas, yet Love I knowe not why,
I thought to hate, I can not hate, although that I should dye.
A foe moste sweete, a frende moste sower, I joye for to embrace.
I hate the wrong, and not the wight, that workt my wofull case:
What thyng it is I knowe not I, but yet a thyng there is,
That in my fancie still perswads, there is no other blisse.
The joyes of life, the pangs of death, it make me feele each daie,
But life nor death, this humour can, devise to weare awaie.
Faine would I dye, but yet in death no hope I see remaines,
And shall I live? since life I see, a course of sorie paines:
What is it then that I do seeke, what joye would I aspire,
A thyng that is devine belike, to high for mans desire.
