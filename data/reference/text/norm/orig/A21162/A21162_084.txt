IN wealth we se some wealthie men, abound in wealth moste welthely
In wealth we se those men again, in wealth do live moste wretchedly.
And yet of wealthe havyng more store,
Then erst of wealth they had before.
These welthy men do seme to want, they seme to want the most they have
The more postes, the more they crave, the more they crave the greater store
That moste they have, they thinke but skant.
Yet not content, wo be therefore,
The simple men that lesse wealth have with lesser wealth we se content,
Content are they twixt wealth and scath, a life to lead indifferent.
And thus of wealth these men have more,
Then those of which we spoke before.
