If thou: delight, in quietnes of life,
Desire: to shoun, from bralls, debate and strife,
To live: in love with god, with frend and foe,
In rest: shalt sleepe: when others cannot so.

Give eare: to all, yet do not all beleeve,
And see: the end, and then do sentence ge eve:
But say: for truth of happy lives assind,
The best: hath he that quiet is in mind.
