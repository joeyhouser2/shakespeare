Not stayed state, but feeble stay, not costly robes, but bare aray:
Not passed welth, but present want, not heped store but sclender skant
Not plenties purse, but poore estate, not happy hap, but troward fate:
Not wish at will, but want of joy, not harts good helth but harts annoy:
No fredomes use, but prisoners thrall, not costly seate, but lowest fall:
Not weale I meane, but wretched wo, doth truely try, the feend fron foe:
And nowght but frowarde Fortune prooves, who fauning faines, or simply loves.
