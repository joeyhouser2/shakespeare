Even as the waxe doeth melte, or dewe consume awaie
Before the Sunne, so I behold through carefull thoughts decaie:
For my beste lucke leads me, to such sinister state,
That I do waste with others Love, that hath my self in hate.
And he that beats the bushe, the wished birde not getts,
But such I see as sitteth still, and holds the foulyng netts.

The Drone more Honie sucks, that laboureth not at all,
Then doeth the Bee, to whose moste paine, least pleasure doeth befall:
The Gardner sowes the seeds, whereof the flowers do growe,
And others yet do gather them, that tooke lesse paine I knowe.
So I the pleasaunt Grape have pulled from the Vine,
And yet I languishe in greate thirste, while others drinke the wine.

Thus like a wofull wight, I wove the webb of woe,
The more I would weede out my cares, the more they seme to growe:
The which betokeneth, forsaken is of me,
That with the carefull Culuer climes, the worne and withered tree.
To entertaine my thoughts, and there my happ to mone,
That never am lesse idle loe, then when I am alone.
