Shall I no waie winne you, to graunt my desire?
What woman will graunt you, the thyng you require:
You only to Love me, is all that I crave,
You only to leave me, is all I would have.
My deare alas, now saie not so,
To Love you beste I must saie no:
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
The swifter I followe, then you flie awaie,
Swift hauks in their fliyng, oft tymes misse their praie,
Yet some killeth deadly, that flie to the marke:
You shall touche no feather, thereof take no carke.
Yet hope shall further my desire,
You blowe the coales, and raise no fire,
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt,
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
To Love is no daunger, where true Love is ment,
I will Love no ranger, least that I repent:
My Love is no raunger, I make God avow,
To trust your smothe saiyngs, I sure knowe not how.
Moste truthe I meane, as tyme shall well trie,
No truthe in men, I ofte espie,
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
Some women maie saie naie, and meane Love moste true,
Some women can make fooles, of as wise men as you:
In tyme I shall catche you, I knowe when and where:
I will sone dispatche you, you shall not come there.
Some speds at length, that ofte have miste,
I am well armde, come when you liste.
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
Yet worke your kinde kindly, graunt me Love for Love,
I will use you frendly, as I shall you prove:
Moste true you shall finde me, I this do protest,
Then sure you shall binde me, to graunte your request.
O happie threede, now have I spunne,
You syng before the conquest wonne.
Why then, will you swarue,
even as you desarue:
Love still,
I will,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
come to me than.
