With painted speache I list not prove, my cunnyng for to trie,
Nor yet will use to fill my penne, with gilefull flatterie:
With pen in hand, and hart in brest, shall faithful promise make
To love you beste, and serve you moste, by your greate vertuts sake.

And sure dame Nature hath you deckt, with gifts above the reste,
Let not Disdaine a harbour finde, within your noble breste:
For Love hath lead his lawe alike, to men of each degrre,
So that the Begger with the Prince shall Love as well as he.

I am no Prince I muste confesse, nor yet of Princes line,
Nor yet a brutishe Begger borne, that feeds emong the Swine:
The fruite shall trie the Tree at laste, the blossomes good or no,
Then do not judge of me the worse, till you have tried me so.

As I deserve, so then reward, I make you judge of all,
If I be false in woorde or deede, let Lightnyng Thunder fall:
And Furies fell with franticke fi•ts, bereve and stay my breathe,
For an example to the reste, if I shall breake my faithe.
