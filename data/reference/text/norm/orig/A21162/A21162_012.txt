All mortal men this day reioice, in Christe that you redemed hath,
By death with death sing we with voice, to him that hath appesd gods wrath
Due unto man for sinful path, wherein before he went astray,
Give thankes to him with perfect faith that for man kinde hath made this glorious day.
This day he rose fron tombe again, wherin his precious corse was laid,
Whom cruelly the Jewes had slaine, with blooddy wounds ful il araid:
O man be now no more dismaid, if thou henceforth from sin do stay,
Of death thou needest not be afraide, Christe conquered death for this his glorious day.
His death prevailed had not whitt, As Paule the apostle wel doth write:
Except he had uprisen yet, from death to life by Godlike might:
With most triumphant glittering light,
This day his glorye shined I say, and made us brighte as sunne this glorious day
O man arise with Christ therfore, since he from sin hath made thee fre,
Beware thou fall in sinne no more, but rise as Christ did rise for thee:
So maist thou him in glory see, when he at day of doome shall say,
Come thou my child and dwell with me, God graunt us all to see that glorious day.
