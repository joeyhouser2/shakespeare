A Lacke when I looke backe, upon my youth thatz paste,
And deepely pouder youthes offence, and youths reward at laste.
With sighes and teares I saie, O God I not denie,
My youth with follie hath deserved, with follie for to die.
But yet if ever synfull man, might mercie move to ruthe,
Good Lorde with mercie do forgive, the follies of my youthe.

In youth I rangde the feelds, where vices all did growe,
In youth alas I wanted grace, such vise to overthrowe,
In youth what I thought sweete, moste bitter now do finde,
Thus hath the follies of my youth, with folly kept me blind
Yet as the Egle casts her bill, whereby her age renueth,
So Lorde with mercie do forgive, the follies of my youth.
