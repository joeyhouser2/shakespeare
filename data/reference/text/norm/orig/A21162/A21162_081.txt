I Am not as seme to be, nor when I smile, I am not glad:
A thrall although you count me free, I moste in mirth, most pensive sad:
I smile to shade my bitter spight, as Haniball that sawe in sight:
His countrie soile with Carthage toune: by Romain force defaced doun.

And Cesar that presented was, with noble Pompeis princely heade,
As twere some judge to rule the cace a flould of teares he semde to shed
Although in deede it sprong of joye, yet other thought it was annoye,
Thus contraries be used I finde, of wise to cloke the covert mynde.

I Haniball that smiles for grief, and let you Cesars grief suffice,
The one that laughs at his mischief, the other all for joye that cries:
I smile to see me scorned so, you weepe for joye to see me woe,
And I a harte by Love slaine deade, presents in place of Pompeis head.

O cruell happ, and harde estate, that forceth me to love my fo,
Accursed be so foule a fate, my choyse for to prefixe it so:
So long to fight with secret sore, and finde no secret salve therefore,
Some urge their paine by plaint I finde, but I in vain do breath my winde.
