JN youth when I at large did lead, my life in lusty libertie,
When hevy thoughts no one did spread, to let my pleasant fantasy:
No fortune seemd, so hard could fall,
This freedome then, that might make thrall.
And twentie yeeres I skarse had spent, when to make ful my happy fate
Both treasures great were on me cast, with lands and titles of estate:
So as more blest then I, stoode than,
Eke as me thought was never man.
For of Dame Fortune who is he, could more desire by just request,
Then health, with wealth, and liberty, all which at once I this possest:
But masking in this joly joy,
A soden syght, prooud all a toy.
For passing on these mery dayes, with new device of pleasures great,
And now and then to view the rayes of beauties works with cunning feat:
In heavenly hewes, all which as one,
I oft behelde, but bound to none.
And one day rowling thus my eyes, upon these blessed wights at ease,
Amongst the rest one did I se, who straight my wandring lokes did sease:
And stayed them firme, but such a sight,
Of beauty yet saw never wight.
What shall I seke to praise it more, where tongs cannot wel praise the sane
But to be short to lovers lore, I straight my sences all did frame:
And were it wit, or were it chaunce,
I woonne the Garland in this daunce.
And thus where I before had thought, no hap my fortune might encrese
A double blis this chance forth brought, so did my ladies love me please
Her faith so firme, and constant such,
As never hart, can praise too much,
But now with torments strange I tast, the fickle stay of fortunes whele,
And where she raised from high to cast, with greater force of grefe to feele:
For from this hap of soden frowne,
Of Princes face she threw me downe,
And thus exchange now hath it made, by liberty a thing most deare,
In hateful prison for to fade, where sundred from my loving feare,
My wealth and health, stands at like stay,
Obscurely to consume away.
And last when humain force was none, could part our love wherin we lived,
My ladies life alas is gon, most cruel death hath it bereved:
Whose vertues, her, to God, hath wonne,
And left me here, a man undone.
