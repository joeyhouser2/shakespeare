IN hope the Shipman hoiseth saile, in hope of passage good,
In hope of healthe the sicke man, doeth suffer losse of blood:
In hope the prisoner linckt in chaines, hopes libertie to finde,
Thus hope breds health, and health breds ease, to every troubled mind.

In hope desire getts victorie, in hope greate comfort spryngs,
In hope the Lover lives in joyes, he feares no dreadfull styngs:
In hope we live and maie abide, such stormes as are assignde,
Thus hope breds health, and health breds ease, to every troubled mynde.

In hope we easely suffer harme, in hope of future tyme,
In hope of fruite, the paine semes sweete, that to the tree doeth clime:
In hope of Love such glorie growes, as now by propfe I finde,
That hope breds health, and health breds ease to every troubled mynde.
