Where griping grief the hart would wounde, and dolfull dumpes the mind oppresse,
There Musick with her silver sound, is wont with spede to give redresse:
Of troubled mynds for every sore, sweete Musick hath a salve in store.
In joy it maks our mirth abound, in grief it chers our heavie sprits,
The carefull head relief hath found, by Musicks pleasant swete delits:
Our senses, what should I saie more, are subiecte unto Musicks lore.

The Gods by Musick hath their praie, the foule therein doeth joye,
For as the Romain Poets saie, in seas whom Pirats would destroye,
A Dolphin saude from death moste sharpe, Arion plaiyng on his harpe
Oh heavenly gift, that turnes the minde, like as the sterne doeth rule the ship,
Of musick whon the gods assignde to confort man, whom cares would nip
Sith thou both man and beast doest move, what wiseman then will thee reprove.
