Powre downe poore eies the teares of true distresse▪
Heare but (oh heavens) the horror of my crie,
Judge of the care that can have no redrresse,
Let me not live to see my lover di•… ▪
In sorrowes rules, like sorrow never read,
Phillip sweet-knight, sweete Phillip Sidney dead.

Paine more then art, or Nature can expresse,
Hell to the world to loose a heavenly friend,
Joy is become but sorrow and distresse;
Life with my Love let death and dolor end.
In bitter teares hath hart of honor blead,
Past hope of helpe to see perfection dead.
