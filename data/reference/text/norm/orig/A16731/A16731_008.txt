Pure of the faire that never fadeth hue,
Exceeding sweet that every sweet exceedeth:
Neere to the heavens where highest glaces growe'
Excellent fruit that such a fancie feedeth,
Love in the eyes, and honour in the heart,
O Princes, Angelles Goddesse, heavenly feature,
Perfection farre above all natures arte,
Exception none, was ever such a creature.
Rich, vertuous, wise, faire, courteous, comely, kinde,
Joy to the hearts of all that do beholde her,
Courtly of grace, and of a princely minde,
High in the heavens, the Angels have enrolde her.
