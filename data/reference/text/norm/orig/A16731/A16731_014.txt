Those eies that hold the hand of every heare,
That hand that holds the heart of every eye,
That wit that goes beyond all Natures art.
The sence too deepe for Wisedome to discrie.
That eye, that hand, that wit, that heavenly sence,
Doth shew my only mistresse excellence.

Oh eyes that pearce into the purest heart,
Oh hands that hold the highest thoughts in thrall,
Oh wit that weyes the depth of all desart,
Oh sence that shewe the secret sweete of all.
The heaven of heavens with heavenly powers preserve thee.
Love but thy selfe, and give me leave to serve thee.

To serve, to live, to looke upon those eyes,
To looke, to live, to kisse that heavenly hand,
To sound that wit that doth amaze the minde,
To know that sence, no sence can understand.
To understand that all the world may know,
Such wit, such sence, eyes, hands, there are no moe.
