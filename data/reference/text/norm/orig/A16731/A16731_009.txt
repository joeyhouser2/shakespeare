Mine eyes have seene the Idoll of my heart,
Mine eyes have heard the wonder of the wise,
Mine heart hath toucht the comfort of mine eyes.

Nowe handes be true unto your happie heart,
Tongue say thy heart shall all in silence serve,
Heart to thy head do not thy thoughts impart,
Eyes see the sight that doth your sight preserve.

And nowe thou eye, thou tongue, thou hand and heart,
But looke, or speake, or touch, or turne awrie,
The heavens pronounce the due of your desart.
Be true and live, but if a Traitor, die.
