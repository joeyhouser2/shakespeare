GO Muse unto the Bower, whereas my mistres dwels,
And tell her of her seruants love, but tel her nothing els.
And speake but in her eare, that none may heare but she,
That if she not the sooner helpe, there is no helpe for me.
Not that I •…eare to speake, but it is strange to heare,
That she will never looke on him, that holds her love so deare.
Perhaps she knowes it not, or if she do she will not,
Yet let her kindnes have a care, that though she hurt she kil not.
And though it be to strange, yet let her this beleve me,
That dead men live, yet I am dead, yet live if she releve me,
For yet are not so colde the coales of kinde desire,
But in the ashes lives a sparke, to kindle love a fire.
Which fier his fuell hath, but from those fairest eies,
Where faith doth burne and fancie flame, and favor never dies
