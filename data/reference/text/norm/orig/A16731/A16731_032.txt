Love and my Mistres were at strife who had the greater power on me,
Betwixt them both oh what a life, nay what a death is this to be.
She said she did it with her eie•…he said he did it with his dart,
Betwixt them both (a sillie wretch) t'is I that have the wounded hart.
She said she only spake the word, that did enchant my pearing sence,
He said, he only gave the sound, that entred hart without defence,
She said they were her only heares, on which the daintie Muses waite:
He said he was the only meane, that entred Muses in conceite.
She said her Bewtie was the marke, that did amaze the highest mind:
He said he only made the mist, whereby the sences grew so blind.
She said, that only for her sake, the best would venture life and lim:
He said she was too much deceiu'd, they honoured her because of him.
Long while (alas) she would not yeeld, but it was she that rul'de the rost,
Until by proofe she did confesse, if he were gone her joy was lost▪
And then she cried, oh daintie Love, I now do find it is for thee,
That I am lou'd and honored both, and thou hast power to conquer me▪
But when I heard her yeeld to Love, oh how my hart did leape for joy,
That now I had some little hope, to have an end of mine annoy.
For though that Fancie Bewtie found, a power all to pitilesse,
Yet Love would never have the hart, to leave his seruant comfortles:
But as too soone before the field, the •…pet sounds the overthrow,
So all too soone I joyed too much, for I awaked and nothing so.
