Perfection •…eereles, Uertue without pride,
Honor and learning linckt with highest Love,
Joy of the thought in true discretion tride,
Love of the life that highest honors prove.
In Angels armes with heavenly hands embraced▪
Paradice pleased, and all the world disgraced.

Seeke all the world, oh seeke and never finde,
In earthly mould the mount of such a minde:
Divinest gifts that God on man bestoweth,
No glory such as of such glory groweth.
End of the joyes that hath all griefe begun,
Yet let me weepe when all the world is done.
