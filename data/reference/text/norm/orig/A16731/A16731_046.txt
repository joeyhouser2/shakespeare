Sweet birds that sit and sing amid the shadie vallies,
And see how sweetly Phillis walks amid her garden allies▪
Go round about her bower and sing, as ye are bidden,
To her is only knowne his faith, that fron the world is hidden.
And she among you all that hath the sweetest voice,
Go chirpe of him that never told, yet never changd his choise.
And not forget his faith, that liu'd for ever lou'd,
Yet never made his fancie knowne, nor ever favour mou'•….
And ever let your ground of all your grace be this,
To you, to you, to you the due of love and honour is.
On you, on you, on you▪ our musicke all attendeth,
For as on you our Muse begun, in you all musicke endeth.
