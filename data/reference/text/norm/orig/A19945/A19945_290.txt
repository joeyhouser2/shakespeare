Pale Death himselfe did love my Philomell,
When he her vertues and rare beauty saw,
Therefore he sicknesse sent: which should expell,
His rivals life, and my deare to him draw.
But her bright beauty dazled so his Eies,
That his da•…t life did misse, though her it hit:
Yet not there with content, new meanes he tries,
To bring her unto Death, and make life flit.
But Nature soone perceiving, that he meant
To spoile her only Phoenix, her chiefe pride,
Assembled all her force, and did prevent
The greatest mischiefe that could her betide.
So both our lives and loves Nature defended,
For had she di'de, my love and life had ended.
