EV'n as my hand my Pen and Paper laies,
My trembling hand, my Pen from Paper staies,
Lest that thine eies which shining made me love you,
Should frowning on my sute bid cease to move you,
So that I feare like one at his wits end,
Hoping to gaine, and fearing to offend,
What pleaseth hope, the same despaire mislikes,
What hope sets downe, those lines despaire out strikes.
So that my nursing murthering Pen affords,
A grave and cradle to my new-borne words.
But whil'st like clouds tost up and downe the aire,
I racked hang t'wixt hope and sad despaire,
Despaire is beaten vanquisht from the field,
And unto conq'ring hope my heart doth yeeld.
For if that Nature love to beauty offers,
And Beauty shunne the love that nature profsers:
Then, either uniust beauty is too blame,
With scorne to quench a lawfull kindled flame,
Or else unlawfully if love we must,
And be unlou'd, then nature is uniust,
Uniustly then nature hath hearts created,
There to love most, where most their love is hated,
And flattering them with a faire•… seeming ill,
To poyson them with beauties sugred Pill.
