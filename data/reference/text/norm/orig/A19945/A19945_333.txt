Dearling of these, of future times the glory,
Branch royall sprung from many a regall stemme,
On whose faire structure, written is the story
Of natures chefest skill, worlds choisest Jemme,
Wits richest Cabinet, vertues best aray,
Centre where lines of all hearts loves do meete▪
Sweete ground whereon the Muses love to play,
Ripe in wit, though greene in yeares, of forme most sweet
Scotlands faire fruit, Englands great hope, Frances love▪
Irelands awe, Cambriaes joy, great Brittains •…ame,
Abridgement of all worth, the mighty Jove,
Long lengthen your good daies, and still your name,
And when you shall have honoured long this land
Grant you a glorious Saint in heaven to stand.
