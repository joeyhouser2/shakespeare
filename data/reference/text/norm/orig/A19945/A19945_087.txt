YE wofull Sires, whose causelesse hate hath bred
Griefe to your selves, death to my love and me,
Let us not be dis-joynd when we are dead,
Though we alive conioind could never be.
Though cruell starres denide us two one bed.
Yet in one tombe us two entombed see.
Like as the dart was one and one the knife
That did begin our love and end our life.
