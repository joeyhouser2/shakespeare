The lowest trees have tops, the Ant her gall,
The flie her splene, the little sparkes their heate:
The slender haires cast shadowes, though but small,
And Bees have stings, although they be not great.
Seas have their sourse, and so have shallow springs,
And love is love, in Beggars, as in Kings.

Where rivers smoothest run, deepe are the fords,
The Diall stirres, yet none perceives it move,
The firmest faith is in the fewest words,
The Turtles cannot sing, and yet they love.
True harts have eies, and eares no tongues to speake,
They heare, and see, and sigh, and then they breake.
