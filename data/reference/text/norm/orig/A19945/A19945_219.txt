What mou'de me then? say Love, for thou canst tell:
Of thee•… learn'd this skill, if skill I have.
Thou knowest the Muse, whose helpe I alwaies crave,
Is none of those that on Parnassus dwell.
My muse is such as doth them all excell,
They all to her alone their cunning gave,
To sing, to dance, to play, to make so brave:
Thrice threefolde graces her alone befell.
From her do flow the streames that water me,
Here is the praise, if I a Poet be▪
Her only looke both will and skill doth give.
What maruell then if I those lawes refuse,
Which other Poets in their making use,
Since by her looks I write, by which I live?
