IF your fond love want worth and great desart,
Then blame your selfe that you me cruell find:
If worth alone move everie noble mind,
Why to no worth should I my love impart?

And if the lesse to grieve your wounded heart,
I seeke your dazled eies with words to blind,
To just disfavour I great favour bind,
With deeds and not with words your love to twhart▪

the freeing of your mind from selfe accusing,
By granting your desarts should ease your paine.
And since love is your fault t'were some abusing,
With bitter words t'enuenome just disdaine.
Then if't be true, all glosing I refraine,
If false, why should no worth worths due obtaine?
