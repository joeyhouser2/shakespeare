IF my harsh humble stile, and rimes ill dressed,
Arrive not to your worth and beauty glorious,
My muses shoulders are with weight oppressed,
And heavenly beames are o're my sight victorious.

If these dimme colours have your worth expressed,
Laid by lovers hand, and not by Art laborious,
Your Sun-like raies have my wits haruest blessed,
Enabling me to make your praise notorious.

But if alas! (alas▪ the heavens defend it,)
My lines your eyes, my love your heart displeasing,
Breed hate in you, and kill my hope of easing:
Say with your selfe, how can the wretch amend it?
I wondrous faire, he wondrous dearely loving,
How can his thoughts but make his pen be moving?
