Bvt if my Lines may not be held excused,
Nor yet my Love find favour in your Eies,
But that your Eies as Judges shall be used,
Even of the fault which from them-selves doth rise,

Yet this my humble fuite do not despise,
Let me be judged as I stand accused,
If but my fault my doome do equalize,
What er'e it be, it shall not be refused.

And since my love alreadie is expressed,
And that I cannot stand upon deniall,
I freely put my selfe upon my triall,
Let Justice judge me as I have confessed:
For if my doome in justice scales be wayed
With equall love, my love must be repayed.
