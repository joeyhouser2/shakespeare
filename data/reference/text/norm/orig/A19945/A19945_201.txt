Mvst my devoted heart desist to love her?
No: love I may, but I may not confesse it,
What harder thing than love▪ and yet depresse it?
Love most conceal'd, doth most it selfe discover.
Had I no Pen to shew that I approve her,
Were I tong-tide that I might not addresse it,
In plaints and Prair's unfained to expresse it,
Yet could I not my deepe affection cover.
Had I no Pen, my very teares would shew it,
Which write my true affection in my face.
Were I tong-tide, my sighs would make her know it,
Which witnes that I grieve at my disgrace.
Since then, though silent, I my love discover,
O let my pen have leave to say, I love her!
