When as the Sun eclipsed is, some say,
It thunder, lightning, raine, and wind portendeth:
And not unlike but such things happen may,
Sith like effects my Su•… eclipsed sendeth

Witnesse my throat made hoarse with thundring cries,
And heart with loves hot flashing lightnings fired:
Witnesse the showers which still fall from mine eies,
And brest with sighes like stormy winds neare rived.

O shine then once againe sweete Sun on me,
And with thy beames dissolve clouds of despaire
Whereof these raging Meteors framed be,
In my poore heart by absence of my faire.
So shalt thou prove thy beames thy heate, thy light,
To match the Sun in glory, grace and might.
