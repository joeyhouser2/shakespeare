IF stepdame nature have been scant,
In dealing beauties gifts to me:
My wit shall helpe supply that want,
And skill in steed of shape shall be.
My stature I confesse is small,
And therefore nill I boast of warre.

My name shall fill the heavens and all,
This skin shall serve to hide that skarre.
My head to beare the helme unfit,
My hands unapt to murther men:
But little heads oft hold much wit,
And feeble hands can guide a Pen.
