Desire and Hope have mou'd my minde,
To seeke for that I cannot finde,
Assured faith in woman-kinde,
And love with love rewarded:
Selfe-love, all but himselfe disdaines,
Suspect as chiefest vertue raignes,
Desire of change unchang'd remaines.
So light is love regarded.

True friendship is a naked name,
That idle braines in pastime frame,
Extremes are alwaies worthy blame,
Enough is common kindnesse.

What flouds of teares do lovers spend?
What sighes from out their hearts •…hey send?
How many may, and will not mend?
Love is a wilfull blindnesse.
What is the love they so desire?
Like love for love, and equall fire:

Good loving wormes, which love require,
And know not when they have it.
Is love in words? faire words may faine.
Is love in lookes? sweet lookes are vaine.
Both these in common kindnesse raigne,
Yet few or none so crave it.

Thou wouldst be lou'd, and that of one,
For vice? thou maist seeke love of none,
For vertue? why of her alone?
I say no more, speake you that know the truth,
If so great love be aught but feare of youth,
