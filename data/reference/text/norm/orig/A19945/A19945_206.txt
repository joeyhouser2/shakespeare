Love is a sowre delight, a sugred griefe,
A living death, an ever-dying life.
A breath of reasons law, a secret thiefe
A sea of teares, an everlasting strife.
A baite for fooles, a scourge of noble wits,
A deadly wound, a shot that ever hits.
Love is a blinded god, away-ward boy,
A laborinth of doubts, an idle lust,
A slave of beauties will, a witlesse toy,
A ravenous bird, a tyrant must uniust.
A burning heate in frost, a flattering foe,
A private hell, a very world of woe.

Yet mightie Love regard not what I say,
Who in a trance do lie, reft of my wits
But blame the light that leads me thus astray,
And makes my tongue thus rave by franticke fits,
Yet hurt me not, lest I sustaine the smart,
Which am content to lodge her in my heart.
