While Love in you did live, I only liu'd in you,
While you for me did burne, for you alone I burned,
While you did sigh for me, for you I sight and mourned,
Till you prou'de false to me, to you I was most true.

But since Love died in you, in you I live no more,
Your heart a Seruant new, mine a new Saint enioyeth:
My sight offends your eies, mine eies your sight annoieth
Since you held me in scorne, by you I set no store.

Yet if dead Love, if your late flames returne,
If you lament your change, and count me your sole treasure,
My love more fresh shall spring, my flame more bright shall bu•…ne.
Ile love none else but you, and love you without measure,
If not (untrue) farewell: in sand Ile sow no graine,
Nor plant my love but where love yeelds me love againe.
