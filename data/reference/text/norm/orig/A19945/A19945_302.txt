O Faithlesse world, and thy most faithlesse part▪
A womans heart:
The true Shop of variety, where sits,
Nothing but fits,
And feavers of desire, and pangs of love,
Which toyes remove.
Why was she brone to please, or I to trust
Words writ in dust?
Suffring her eyes to governe my despaire,
My paine for ayre,
And fruit of time rewarded with untruth,
The foode of youth▪
Untrue she was, yet I beleeu'd her eyes,
Instructed spies,
Til I was taught, that love was but a schoole
To breede a foole.
Or sought she more then triumphs of denial,
To see a triall,
How far her smiles commanded my weaknes?
eelde and confesse.
Excuse not now thy folly, nor her nature:
Blush and endure
Aswell thy shame, as passions that were vaine▪
And thinke thy gaine,
To know that love, lodg'd in a womans brest
Is but a guest.
