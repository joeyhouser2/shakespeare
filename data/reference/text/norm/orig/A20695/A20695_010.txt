Though Sinne offending daily doth torment me,
Yet Grace amending, since I do repent me,
At my lives ending will I hope present me cleare to thy mercy.

The wound Sinne gave me was of Death assured,
Did not Grace save me, whereby it is cured:
So thou wilt have me to thy love invred, free without merit.

Sinnes stripe is healed, and his sting abated,
Deaths mouth is sealed, and the Grave amated,
Thy Love revealed, and thy Grace related gives me this spirit.
