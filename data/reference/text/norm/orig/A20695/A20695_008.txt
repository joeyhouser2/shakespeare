2 Sorrow to see my sorrowes cause augmented, and yet lesse sorrowfull were my sorrowes more:
Griefe that my griefe with griefe is not prevented, for griefe it is must ease my grieved sore.
Thus griefe and sorrow cares but how to grieve,
For griefe and sorrow must my cares relieve.

3 If any eye therefore can spare a teare to fill the well-spring that must wet my cheekes,
O let that eye to this sad feast draw neere, refuse me not my humble soule beseekes:
For all the teares mine eyes have ever wept
Were now too little had they all been kept.
