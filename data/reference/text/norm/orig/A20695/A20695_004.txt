Ile go to the woods, and alone, make my moane, oh cruell:
For I am deceiu'd and bereau'd of my life, my jewell.
O but in the woods, though Love be blinde,
He hath his spies, my secret haunts to finde.

Love then I must yeeld to thy might, might and spight oppressed,
Since I see my wrongs, woe is me, cannot be redrested.
Come at last, be friendly Love to me,
And let me not, endure this miserie.
