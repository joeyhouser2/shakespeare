Eyes, why did you bring unto me those graces,
Grac'd to yeeld wonder out of her true measure,
Measure of all joyes stay to phansie traces
Module of pleasure.

Reason is now growne a disease in reason,
Thoughts knit upon thoughts free alone to wonder,
Sense is a spie, made to do phansie treason,
Love go I under.

Since then eyes pleasure to my thoughts betray me,
And my thoughts reasons-levell have defaced,
So that all my powers to be hers, obey me,
Love be thou graced.

Grac'd by me Love? no, by her that owes me,
She that an Angells spirit hath retained
In Cupids faire skie, which her beauty showes me,
Thus have I gained.
