Thus wave we Christians still betwixt two aires;
Nor leave the world for God, nor God for it;
While these Turkes climing up united staires,
Above the Superstitions double wit;
Leave us as to the Jewish bondage heires,
A Saboth rest for selfe-confusion fit:
Since States will then leave warre, when men begin
For Gods sake to abhorre this world of sinne.
