Earth! Stand'st thou fast under this uglinesse?
And fal'st not downe to that infernall deepe,
Which feares (perchance) worse than it selfe to keepe?
Eyes! close your liddes: There is no more to do:
Yet know, you have seene that before you die,
Which no Age will beleeve; One worse than I.

Ah curst Mortality! So soone put out?
And have I lost the glory of Revenge,
If Fame find greater, as she goes about?
This blood, that bloody throte should have devour'd:
Rage lack'd in this. Where is the place for scorne;
Since woes be dead in him, as soone as borne?
Flesh is too brittle mould for brave excesse.
Yetlet these scraps give nourishment to Fame;
Since Love, and Rage this modell may expresse.
