Atlas upon his shoulders bare the skye,
The loade was heavy, but the loade was faire:
His sense was ravish'd with the melodie,
Made from the motion of the highest sphere.

Not Atlas I, nor did I heaven beare,
Caelica, 'tis true, once on my shoulder sate,
Her eyes more rich by many characts were
Than Starres or Planets, which men wonder at:
Atlas bare heaven, such burdens be of grace,
Caelica in heaven, is the Angels place.
