Hence strive the Schooles, by first, and second kinds
Of substances, by essence, and existence;
That Trine, and yet Unitednesse divine
To comprehend, and image to the sense;
As do the misled superstitious minds,
By this one rule, or Axiom taken thence;
Looke where the Whole is, there the Parts must be,
Thinke they demonstrate Christs ubiquity.
