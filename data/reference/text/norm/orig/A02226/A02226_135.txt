For we in such Kings (as cleare Mirrors) see,
And reade the heavenly glory of the good;
All other Arts, which borne of evill be,
By these are neither taught, nor understood,
Who, in the wombe of Gods true Church their (mother
Learne they that know him well, must know no other.

Which God this People worship in their King
And through obedience travaile to perfection;
Studying their wills under his will to bring,
Yeeld trust, and honour both, to his direction:
"And when they do from his example swarue,
"Beare witnesse to themselves they ill deserve.
