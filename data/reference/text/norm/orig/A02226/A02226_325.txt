Nourisht in Court, where no Thoughts peace is nourisht,
Us'd to behold the Tragedies of ruine,
Brought up with feares that follow Princes fortunes;
Yet am I like him that hath lost his knowledge,
Or never heard one storie of Misfortune.
My heart doth fall away: feare falls upon me.
Tame Rumors, that have been mine old acquaintance
Are to me now (like Monsters) feare, or wonder.
My Love beginnes to plague me with Suspitions.
My Mothers promises of my aduancement;
The name of Mustapha so often murmur'd,
With whose name ever I have been reioyced,
Now makes my heart misgive, my spirits languish.
Man then is Augur of his own misfortune,
When his joy yeelds him arguments of anguish.
