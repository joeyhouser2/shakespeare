The Turkish government allowes no Law,
Mens lives and states depend on his behest;
We thinke Subiection there a seruile awe,
Where Nature finds both honour, wealth and rest,

Our Christian freedome is, we have a law,
Which even the Heathen thinke no Power should wrest;
Yet proves it crooked as power lists to draw,
The rage or grace that lurkes in Princes brests.
Opinion bodies may to shadowes give,
But no burnt Zone it is, where People live.
