Cvpid did pine, Venus that lou'd her sonne,
Or lackt her sport, did looke with heavy heart:
The Gods are cal'd, a Councell is begunne,
Delphos is sought, and Aesculapius art.

Apollo saith, Love is a Relative,
Whose being only must in others be;
As bodies do their shadowes keepe alive,
So Eros must with Anteros agree;
They found him out a mate with whom to play,
Love straight enioy'd, and pin'd no more away.

Caelica, this image figures sorth my heart,
Where Venus mournes, and Cupid prospers not,
For this is my affections overthwart,
That I remember what you have forgot;
And while in you my selfe I seeke to find,
I see that you your selfe have lost your minde.

When I would joy, as I was wont to do,
Your thoughts are chang'd, and not the same to me;
My love that lacks her play-fellow in you,
Seeks up and downe, but blinded cannot see.
The Boy hath stolne your thoughts some other way,
Where wantonlike they do with many play.
