Thy golden hayre, thy forehead faire,
Thy daintie browes, thy eyes so cleare,
Those pretie dimplets to them neere,
Do cause me thus to love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Thy comely cheekes like damask rose,
Y•mixt with Lillies I suppose,
Even parted by thy comely nose,
Would cause a man to love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Thy mouth from thence devided is,
By such proportion ofblis,
What treasure can be like to this,
that makes me thus to love thee?
Give me leave, etc.

Thine Amber breath, thy pretie chin,
Indimpled where it doth begin,
Doth make me thinke it were a sin,
If that I should not love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Thy Lilly neck, that piller deere,
Like Alablaster white and cleere,
Twixt upright shoulders doth appeare,
To make a man to love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Thy long smooth arme, thy silke softe hand,
I wish were to my neck a band-
So might I let thee understand,
how well that I do love thee.
Give me leave▪ etc.

Upon thy breasts more white then snow,
Two pretie pamplets evenly grow,
O• venus guifts the richest show,
to make a man to love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Thy middle sm•ll, that curdie rock,
That there lyeth hid under thy smock,
Do moove my spirits, I do not mocke,
Exceedingly to love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Thy hidden parts I recommend,
To his conceit who is thy friend,
Whose labour sure doth only tend,
in part and whole to love thee.
Give me leave▪ etc.

Thy brawned thigh, thy whirled knee,
Thy legge, thine anckle pretilie,
Do give such comfort unto me
that I of force must love thee.
Give me leave▪ etc.

Thy heele, thy foot, thy toes so straight,
That trip and tread with such a sleight,
Do with my sences all so fraight
that needs I must thus love thee.
Give me leave, etc.

Each comelie parte from top to toe,
Will breed my sillie heart much woe,
Unles it please thee for to show
that thou againe wilt love me.
Give me leave, etc.

Then said my love, sith that you say,
And do protest to love me aye.
My love to you Ile not denay,
in sorte as you do love me.
Give me leave, etc.

So had I leave to love my Lasse,
So had I leave to love her,
Now should I be too much an Asse.
If I would not then proove her,
Give me leave etc.
