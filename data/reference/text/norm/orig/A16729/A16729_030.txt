IN trueth is trust, distrust not then my trueth,
Let vertue live, I aske no greater love,
Of such regard repentance not ensueth,
And hope of heaven doth highest power proove.

In trueth somtime it was a sweete conceit,
To see how love and life did dwell together▪
But now in trueth there is so much deceit,
That trueth in deede is gone I know not whither.

Yer liveth trueth, and hath her secret love,
And love in trueth deserves to be regarded,
And loves regard in reason doth appeare,
Approved trueth can nevet be discharged:
Then try me first▪ and if that true you prove me,
In trueth you wrong me if you do not love me.
