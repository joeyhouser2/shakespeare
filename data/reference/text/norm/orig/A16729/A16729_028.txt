MY Mistresse all alone my seruice I did vowe,
She sware, as she a woman was, no love she did allow.
Alas, then grew my paine, it greu d me to the heart,
My sences then so sencelesse were, as that I felt no smart.
And standing in a maz, as Aspis on the charme,
She said and swore (to save my life) she wisht no good nor harme,
Alas, what bitter sweet, alas what pleasant paine.
What shivering heat, what chilling cold, did passe through every vain•
And when I would have sworne her heart would never moove,
By Jesus Christ she tooke that oath, that she did never love.
Alas what was I then? alas what am I now?
Too weake to love, too strong to die▪ quick, dead, I know not how.
