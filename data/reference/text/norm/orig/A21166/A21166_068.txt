With painted speech I list not prove, my cunning for to trie,
Nor yet will use to fill my pen, with guilefull flatterie:
With pen in hand, and hart in brest, shall faithfull promise make
To love you best, and serve you moste, by your great vertues sake.

And sure dame Nature hath you deckt, with giftes above the rest,
Let not Disdaine a harbour finde, within your noble brest:
For Love hath led his Lawe a like, to men of each degree,
so that the Begger with the Prince, shall Love as well as he.

I am no Prince, I must confesse, nor yet of Princes line,
Nor yet a brutish Begger borne, that feedes among the swine:
The fruite shall trie the tree at last, the blossomes good or no,
Then do not judge of me the worse, till you have tried me so.

As I deserve, so then reward, I make you judge of all,
If I be false in worde or deede, let Lightning thunder fall:
And furies Fell with franticke fittes, bereave and staie my breathe,
For an example to the rest, if I shall breake my faithe.
