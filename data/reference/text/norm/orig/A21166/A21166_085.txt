IN wealth we see some wealthy men, abounde in wealth most wealthely,
In wealth we see those men agayne, in wealth do live most wretchedly:
And yet of wealth having more store,
Then earst of wealth they had before.

These wealthy men do seeme to want, they seeme to want that most they have,
The more posses, the more they crave, the more they crave, the greater store:
That most they have, they thinke but skant,
Yet not content, woe be therfore.

The simple men that lesse wealth have, with lesser wealth we see content:
Content are they twixt wealth and scathe, a life to lead indifferent:
And thus of wealth, these men have more,
Then those of which we spoke before.
