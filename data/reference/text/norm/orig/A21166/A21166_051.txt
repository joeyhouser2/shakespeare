IF thou delight, in quietnesse of life,
Desire to shun, from braules, debate and strife:
To live in love with God, with friend and fo,
In rest shalt sleepe, when others can not so.

Give eare to all, yet do not all beleeve,
And see the ende, and then do sentence give:
But say for truth, of happie lives assinde,
The best hath he, that quiet is in minde.
