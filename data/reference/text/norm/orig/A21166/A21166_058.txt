Where griping grief the hart would wound, and doleful dumpes the minde oppresse,
There Musick with her silver sound, is wont with speede to geve redresse
Of troubled mindes for every sore, sweete Musicke hath a salve in store,
In joy it makes our mirth abound, in grief it cheeres our heavy sprites,
The carefull head relief hath found, by Musickes pleasaunt sweete delites,
Our senses, what should I say more, are subiect unto Musickes lore.
The Gods by Musicke hath their pray, the foule therein doth joy,
For as the Roman Poets say, in seas whom Pirates would destroy:
A Dolphin saude from death most sharpe, Arion playing on his Harpe,
Of heavenly gift, that turnes the minde like as starne doth rule the ship:
Oh Musicke whom the Gods assignde to comfort man, whon cares would nip,
Sith thou both man and beast doest move, what wisman then will thee reprove.
