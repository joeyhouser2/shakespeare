The fayth that fayles, must needes be thought untrue,
The frend that faignes, who holdeth not uniust:
Who likes that love, that chaungeth still for new,
Who hopes for truth, where troth is voyde of trust.
No faith, no frend, no love, no troth so sure,
But rather fayles, then stedfastly endure.

What head so stayed? that altereth not intent,
what thought so sure? that stedfast did remaine,
what witte so wise? that never needes repent:
what tongue so true? but sometime wontes to fayne,
what foote so firme? that never treades awrie,
what sooner dimde? then sight of clearest eye.

What hart so fixt? but soone enclines to chaunge,
what moode so milde? that never moved debate:
what faith so strong? but lightly likes to raunge,
what love so true? that never learnd to hate.
what life so pure? that lastes without offence,
what worldly mynde? but moves with ill pretence.

What knot so fast? that may not be untide,
what seale so sure? but fraude or force shall breake,
what prop of stay? but one tyme shrinkes aside,
what ship so stauche? that never had a leake.
what graunt so large? that no exception makes,
what hoped helpe? but frend at neede forsakes.

What seate so high? but low to ground may fall,
what hap so good? that never found mislike:
what state so sure? but subiect is to thrall,
what force prevayles? where Fortune list to strike.
what wealth so much? but time may tourne to want,
what store so great? but wasting maketh scant.

What profites hope? in depth of daungers thrall,
what trust in time? but waxeth worse and worse:
what helpes good hart, if Fortune frowne withall,
what blessing thrives agaynst heavenly helpelesse curse.
what winnes desire, to get and cannot gayne,
what bootes to wish, and never to obtaine.
