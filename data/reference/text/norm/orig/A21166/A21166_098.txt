O Heavenly God, O Father deare, cast downe thy tender eye,
Upon a wretche, that prostrate here, before thy face doth lye:
O poure thy precious oyle of grace, into my wounded hart,
O let the droppes of mercy swage, the rigour of my smart.

My fainting soule suppressed sore, with carefull clogge of sinne,
In humble sort submittes it selfe, thy mercy for to winne:
Graunt mercy then, O Saviour sweete, to me most wofull thrall,
Whose mournefull crie, to thee O Lord, doth still for mercy call.

Thy blessed will I have despised, upon a stubburne minde,
And to the sway of worldly thinges, my selfe I have inclinde:
Forgetting heaven, and heavenly powers, where God and Sainctes do dwell,
My life had like to tread the pathe, that leades the way to hell.

But now my Lord, my Lodestarre bright, I will no more do so,
To thinke upon my former life, my hart doth melt for wo:
Alas I sigh, alas I sobbe, alas I do repent,
That ever my licencious will, so wickedly was bent.

Sith thus therfore, with carefull plaint, I do thy mercy crave,
O Lord for thy great mercies sake, let me thy mercie have:
Restore to life the wretched soule, that els is like to dye,
So shall my voyce unto thy name, sing prayse eternally.

Now blessed be the Father first, and blessed be the Sonne,
And blessed be the holy Ghost, by whom all thinges are done:
Blesse me O blessed Trinitie, with thy eternall grace,
That after death my soule may have, in heaven a dwelling place.
