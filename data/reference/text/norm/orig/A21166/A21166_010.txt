ALL mortall menn this day reioyce, in Christ that you redemed hath,
By death with death sing we with voyce, to him that hath appeasde Gods wrath:
Due unto man for sinfull path, wherein before he went astray,
Give thankes to him with perfect faith, that for mankinde hath made this glorious day.

This day he rose from tombe againe, wherein his precious corse was layd,
Whom cruelly the Jewes had slaine, with bloudy woundes full ill arayd:
O man be now no more dismaid, if thou hencefoorth from sinne do stay,
Of death thou needest not to be afrayde, Christ conquered death for this his glorious day.

His death prevayled had no whit, as Paule the Apostle well doth write,
Except he had uprised it, from death to life by godlike might:
With most triumphant glittering light,
This daie his glory shined I say, and made us bright as sunne this glorious day.

O man arise with Christ therefore, since he from sin hath made thee free,
Beware thou fall in sinne no more, but rise as Christ did rise for thee:
So mayest thou him in glory see, when he at day of doome shall say,
Come thou my child and dwell with me, God graunt us all to see that glorious day.
