What grieves my bones, and makes my body faint?
What prickes my flesh, and teares my head in twaine?
Why do I wake, when rest should me attaint?
When others laugh, why do I live in paine?
I tosse, I turne, I chaunge from side to side,
And stretch me oft, in sorrowes linckes betide.

I tosse, as one betost in waves of care,
I turne, to flie the woes of loathsome life?
I chaunge, to spie if death this corpes might spare,
I stretch to heaven, to rid me of this strife.
Thus do I stretch, and chaunge, and tosse, and turne,
While I in hope of heaven, my life do burne.

Then hold thee still, let be thy heavinesse,
Abolish care, forget thy pining woe:
For by this meanes, sone shalt thou find redresse,
When oft betost, hence thou to heaven must go.
Then tosse and tourne, and tumble franke and free,
O happie thrise, when thou in heaven shalt be.
