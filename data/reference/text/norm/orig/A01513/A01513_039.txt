I Cast mine eye and saw ten eies at once,
All seemely set upon one lovely face:
Two gaz'd, two glanc'd, two watched for the nonce,
Two winked wyles, two fround with froward grace▪
Thus every eye was pitched in his place.
And every eye which wrought each others wo,
Said to itself, alas why lookt I so?
And every eye for jelouse love did pine,
And sigh'd and said, I would that eye were mine.
