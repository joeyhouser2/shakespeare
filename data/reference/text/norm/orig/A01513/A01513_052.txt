Amid my Bale I bath in blisse,
I swim in heaven, I sink in hell:
I find amends for every misse,
And yit my moane no tonge can tell.
I live and love, what would you more?
As never lover liu'd before.

I laugh sometimes with little lust,
So jest I oft and feele no joye:
Mine ease is builded all on trust,
And yit mistrust breedes mine anoye.
I live and lack, I lack and have:
I have and misse the thing I crave.

These things seeme straunge, yit ar they trew
Beleeve me (sweete) my state is such:
One pleasure which I would eschew,
Both slakes my grief, and breedes my gruch.
So doth one pain which I would shoon
Renew my joyes where grief begoon.

Thon like the Larke that past the night
In heavy sleepe with cares opprest:
Yit when she spies the pleasaunt light,
She sends sweete notes from out her brest.
So sing I now because I think
How joyes approch, when sorrowes shrink.

And as faire Philomene ageine
Can watch and singe when other sleepe:
And taketh pleasure in her payne,
To wray the woo that makes her weepe.
So sing I now for to bewray
The lothsome life I lead alway.

The which to thee (deare wench) I write,
That know'st my mirth, but not my moane:
I pray God graunt thee deepe delight,
To live in joyes when I am gone.
I cannot live, it will not be:
I dye to think to part from thee.
