Lo Lady if you had but halfe like care for me,
That worthy Bradamant had then her own Ruggier to see:
My readie will should be so prest to come at call,
You should have no such sight or dreame to trouble you withall.
Then when you list commaund, and I will come in hast,
There is no hap shall hold me backe, good will shall roon so fast.
