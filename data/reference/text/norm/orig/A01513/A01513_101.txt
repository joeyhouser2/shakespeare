IF thou sitte here to viewe this pleasant garden place,
Think thus: at last will come a frost, and all these floures deface.
But if thou sitte at ease to rest thy wearie bones,
Remember death brings finall rest to all oure greevous grone•.
So whether for delyght, or here thou sitte for ease,
Thinke still upon the latter day, so shalt thou God best please.
