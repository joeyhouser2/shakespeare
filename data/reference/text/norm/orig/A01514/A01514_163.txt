THE stately Dames of Rome, their Pearles did weare.
About their neckes to beautifie their name:
But she (whome I do serve) her pearles doth beare,
Close in her mouth, and smiling shewe, the same.
No wonder then, though eu'ry word she speakes,
A Jewell seeme in judgement of the wise,
Since that her sugred tongue the passage breakes,
Betweene two rockes, bedeckt with pearles of price.
Her haire of golde, her front of Juory,
(A bloody heart within so white a breast)
Her teeth of Pearle lippes Rubie, christall eye,
Needes must J honour her above the rest:
Since she is fourmed of none other moulde,
But Rubie, Christall, Ivory, Pearle, and Golde.
