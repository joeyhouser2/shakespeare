Whilst by her eies pursude, my poore hart flue it
Into the sacred bosome of my dearest,
She there in that sweete Sanctuarie slew it,
When it had hop'd his safetie to be nearest.

My faith of priviledge could no whit protect it,
That was with bloud, and three yeres witnes signed,
Whereby she had no cause once to suspect it:
For well she saw my love, and how I pined.

Yet no hopes letter would her brow reveale me,
No comforts hue, which falling spirits erecteth;
What bootes to lawes of succour to appeale me?
Ladies and tyrants never lawes respecteth.
Then there I die, where I had hope to liven;
And by her hand that better might have given.
