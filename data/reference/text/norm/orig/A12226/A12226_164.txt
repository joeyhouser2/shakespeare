GO wayling verse the infant of my love,
Minorua like, brought foorth without a mother:
That beares the image of the cares I prove;
Witnesse your fathers griefe exceeds all other.

Sigh out a Storie of her cruell deedes
With interrupted accents of dispaire,
A monument that whosoever reedes,
May justly praise and blame my loveles Faire.

Say her disdaine hath dried up my blood,
And sterued you in succours still denying,
Presse to her eyes, importune me some good,
Waken her sleeping crueltie with crying,
Knock at her hard hart: say, I perish for her,
And feare this deed will make the world abhor her.
