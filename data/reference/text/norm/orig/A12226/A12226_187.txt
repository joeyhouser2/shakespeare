MY cares drawes on my everlasting night,
And horrors sable clowds dims my lives sunne;
That my lives sunne, and thou my worldly light,
Shall rise no more to me, my daies are donne.

Ile go before unto the myrtle shades,
To attend the presence of my worldes deare,
And dresse a bed of flowers that never fades,
And all things fit against her comming there.

If any aske, why that so soone I came?
Ile hide her fault, and say, it was my lot,
In life and death Ile tender her good name,
My life and death shall never be her blot:
Although the world this deed of hirs may blame,
The Elisian ghoasts shall never know the same.
