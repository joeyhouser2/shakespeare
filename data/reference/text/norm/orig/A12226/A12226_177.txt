MY Cinthia hath the waters of mine eies,
The reddie handmaides on her grace attending,
That never falls to ebbe nor ever dies,
For to their flow she never grants an ending.

The Ocean never doth attend more duly
Upon his soveraigne, the night wandring Queene;
Nor ever hath his impost paid more truly,
Than mine to my soules Queene hath ever been.

Yet her hard rocke firme fixt for ay removing,
No comfort to my cares she ever giveth;
Yet had I rather languish in her loving,
Than to imbrace the fairest she that liveth.
I feare to find such pleasure in my raigning,
As now I tast in compasse of complaining.
