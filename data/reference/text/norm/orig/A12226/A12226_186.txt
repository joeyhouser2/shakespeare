IF this be Love to drawe a wearie breath,
To paint on flods till the shore crie to the aire,
With prone aspect still treading on the earth,
Sad horror, pale griefe, prostrate dispaire:

If this be Love, to warre against my soule,
Rise up to waile, lie downe to sigh, to grieve me,
With ceaseles toyle Cares restlesse stones to roule,
Still to complaine and mone, whilst none relieve me:

If this be Love, to languish in such care,
Loathing the light, the world, my selfe, and all.
With interrupted sleepes fresh griefes repaire,
And breath out horror in perplexed thrall:
If this be Love, to live a living death;
Loe then love I, and draw this wearie breath.
