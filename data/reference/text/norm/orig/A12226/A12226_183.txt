Happie in sleepe, waking content to languish,
Imbracing cloudes by night, in day time mourne:
All things I loth save her and mine own anguish,
Pleasd in my heart mooved to live forlorne.

Nought do I crave but love, death, or my Ladie,
Horce with crying mercie, (mercie yet my merit)
So many vowes and praiers ever made I,
That now at length to yeeld meere pittie were it.

Yet since the Hidra of my cares renewing,
Revives still sorrowes of her fresh disdaining,
Still must I go the Summer winds pursuing,
And nothing but her love and my harts paining.
Weep howrs, grieve daies, sigh months, and still mourn yearly,
Thus must I do because I love her dearelie:
