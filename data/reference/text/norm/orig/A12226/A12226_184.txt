IF Beautie bright be doubled with a frowne,
That Pitie cannot shine through to my blisse,
And Disdaines vapors are thus overgrowen,
That my lives light to me quite darkened is.

Why trouble I the world then with my cries,
The aire with sighs, the earth below with teares,
Since I live hatefull to those ruthfull eyes,
Vexing with my untuned mone her daintie eares.

If I have lou'd her dearer than my breath,
My breath, that cals the heavens to witnes it.
And still hold her most deare until my death:
And if that all this cannot moove one whit;
Yet let her say that she hath done me wrong,
To use me thus and know I lou'd so long.
