Hear then, but then with wonder hear; see, but admiring see;
No mortal gifts, no earthly frutes now here discerned be:
See, do you see this face: a face, nay image of the skyes,
Of which, the two life-giving lights are figured in her eyes:
Heare you this soule-inuading voyce, and count it but a voyce,
The very essence of their tunes, when Angels do reoyce.
