Oft and in vaine my rebels thoughts have ventred,
to stop the passage of my vanquisht hart,
And close the way, my friendly foe first entred,
Striving thereby to free my better part.

Whilest garding thus the windowes of my thought,
Where my harts-thiefe to vex me made her choice,
And thether all my forces to transport,
Another passage opens at her voice.

Her voice betraies me to her hand and eie,
My freedomes-tyrant glorying in her art:
But (ah) sweete foe, small is the victorie
With three such powers to plague one silly hart.
Yet my soules sovereigne, since I must resigne,
Raigne in my thoughts, my love and life are thine.
