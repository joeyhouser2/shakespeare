Yet worse than worse, I say thou art a Thiefe. A thiefe?
Now God forbid: a thiefe, and of worst thieves a thiefe;
Thieves steale for need, and steale for goods, which pain recovers
But thou, rich in all joyes, dost rob my goods from me,
Which cannot be restorde by time nor industrie:
Of foes the spoyle is evill, farre more of constant lovers.
