Since the first looke that led me to this error,
To this thoughts-maze to my confusion tending;
Still have I liude in griefe, in hope, in terror,
The circle of my sorrowes never ending.

Yet cannot have her Love that holds me hatefull,
Her eies exacts it, though her heart disdaines me,
See what reward he hath that serves th'ngratefull,
So long and pure a faith no favour gains me.

Still must I whet my young desires abated,
Upon the flint of such a heart rebelling,
And all in vaine her pride is so imated,
She yeelds no place at all for pities dwelling,
Oft have I told her that my Soule did love her,
And that with teares; yet all this will not move her.
