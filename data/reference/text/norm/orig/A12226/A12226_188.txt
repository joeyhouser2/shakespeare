The Starre of my mishap imposd my paining
To spend the Aprill of my yeares in crying,
That never found my fortune but in wayning,
With still fresh cares my bloud and bodie trying.

Yet her I blame not, though she might have blest me
But my desiers wings so high aspiring;
Now melted with the Sunne that hath possest me,
Downe do I fall from of my high desiring.

And in my fall do crie for mercie speedie,
No piteous eye lookes backe upon my mourning;
No helpe I finde, when now most favour neede I;
My Ocean teares drowne me, and quench my burning,
And this my death must christen her anew,
Whiles faith doth bid my cruell Faire adieu.
