Raising my hope on hills of high desire,
Thinking to scale the heaven of her hart,
My slender meane presumes too high a part:
For disdaines thunderbolt made me retire,

And threw me downe to paine in all this fire.
Where lo I languish in so heavie smart,
Because th'attempt was far above my Art,
Her state brooks not poore soules should come so nie her.

Yet I protest my high aspiring will,
Was not to dispossesse her of her right,
Her Soveraigntie should have remained still,
I only sought the blisse, to have her sight:
Her sight contented thus to see me spill,
Fram'd my desires fit for her eies to kill.
