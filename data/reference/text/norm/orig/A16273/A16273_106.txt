When the dogge
Full of rage
With his irefull eyes
Frownes amidst the skies:
The Sheepheard to asswage
The furie of the heate,
Him selfe doth safely seate
By a Fount
Full of faire,
Where a gentle breath
Mounting from beneath,
tempereth the ayre.
There his flocks
Drinke their fill,
And with ease repose,
While sweet sleepe doth close
Eyes from toyling ill,
But I burne,
Without rest,
No defensive power
Shields from Phoebus lower,
sorrow is my best.
Gentle Love
Lower no more,
If thou wilt inuade
In the secret shade,
Labour not so sore
I my selfe
And my flocks,
They their Love to please,
I my selfe to ease,
Both leave the shadie Oakes,
Content to burne in fire,
Sith Love doth so desire.
