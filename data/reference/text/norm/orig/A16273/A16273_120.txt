Since thou to me wert so unkinde,
My selfe I never loved, for
I could not love him in my minde,
Whom thou (faire Mistresse) do'st abhorre.

If viewing thee, I sawe thee not,
And seeing thee, I could not love thee:
Dying, I should not live (God wot)
Nor living, should to anger moove thee.

But it is well that I do finde
My life so full of torments, for
All kinde of ills do fit his minde
Whom thou (faire Mistresse) do'st abhorre.

In thy oblivion buried now
My death I have before mine eyes:
And heere to hate my selfe I vow,
As (cruell) thou do'st me despise.

Contented ever thou didst finde
Me with thy scornes, though never (for
To say the trueth) I joyed in minde,
After thou didst my love abhorre.
