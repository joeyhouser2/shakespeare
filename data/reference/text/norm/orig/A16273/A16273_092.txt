AS to the blooming prime,
Bleake Winter being fled:
From compasse of the clime,
Where Nature lay as dead,
The Rivers dull'd with time,
The greene leaves withered,
Fresh Zephyri (the Westerne brethren) be:
So th'honour of your favour is to me.
For as the Plaines revive,
And put on youthfull greene:
As plants begin to thrive,
That disattir'd had been:
And Arbours now alive,
In former pompe are seene.
So if my Spring had any flowers before:
Your breathes Favonius hath encreast the store.
