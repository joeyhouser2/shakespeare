Loves Queene long wayting for her true-Love,
Slaine by a Boare which he had chased,
Left off her teares, and me embraced,
She kist me sweete, and call'd me new-Love.
With my silver haire she toyed,
In my stayed lookes she joyed.
Boyes (she said) breede beauties sorrow:
Olde men cheere it even and morrow.
My face she nam'd the seate of favour,
All my defects her tongue defended,
My shape she prais'd, but most commended.
My breath more sweete then Balme in savour.
Be old man with me delighted,
Love for love shall be requited.
With her toyes at last she wone me:
Now she coyes that hath undone me.
