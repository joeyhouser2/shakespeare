THE silly Swaine whose love breedes discontent,
Thinks death a trifle, life a loathsome thing,
Sad he lookes, sad he lyes:
But when his Fortunes mallice doth relent,
Then of Loves sweetnes he will sweetly sing,
thus he lives, thus he dyes.
Then Tityrus whom Love hath happy made,
Will rest thrice happy in this Mirtle shade.
For though Love at first did greeve him:
yet did Love at last releeve him.
