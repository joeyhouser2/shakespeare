Swell Ceres now, for other Gods are shrinking,
Pomona pineth,
Fruitlesse her tree:
Faire Phaebus shineth
Only on me.
Conceite doth make me smile whilst I am thinking,
How every one doth reade my storie,
How every bough on Ceres lowreth,
Cause heaven plenty on me powreth,
And they in leaves do only glorie,
All other Gods of power bereaven,
Ceres only Queene of heaven.

With roabes and flowers let me be dressed,
Cinthia that shineth
Is not so cleare:
Cinthia declineth
When I appeare.
Yet in this Isle she raignes as blessed,
And every one at her doth wonder,
And in my eares still fond fame whispers
Cinthia shall be Ceres Mistres,
But first my Carre shall rive in sunder.
Helpe Phaebus helpe, my fall is suddaine:
Cinthia, Cinthia must be Soveraigne.
