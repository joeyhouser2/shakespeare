Melampus, when will Love be void of feares?
When Jealousie hath neither eyes nor eares.
Melampus, when will Love be throughly shrieved?
When it is hard to speake, and not beleeved.
Melampus, when is Love most malecontent?
When Lovers range, and beare their bowes unbent.
Melampus, tell me, when takes Love least harme?
When Swaines sweete pipes are puft, and Trulls are warme.
Melampus, tell me, when is Love best fed?
When it hath suck'd the sweet that ease hath bred.
Melampus, when is time in Love ill spent?
When it earnes meede, and yet receaves no rent.
Melampus, when is time well spent in Love?
When deedes win meedes, and words Loves works do prove.
