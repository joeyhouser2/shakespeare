Prometheus, when first from heaven hie,
He brought downe fire, ere then on earth unseene:
Fond of delight, a Satyre standing by,
Gave it a kisse, as it like sweete had been.

Feeling forth-with the other burning power,
Wood with the smart, with shoutes and shrikings shrill:
He sought his ease in River, field, and bower,
But for the time his greefe went with him still.

So silly I, with that unwonted sight,
In humane shape, an Angell from above:
Feeding mine eyes, th'impression there did light,
That since I runne, and rest as pleaseth Love.
The difference is, the Satires lips, my hart:
He for a while, I evermore have smart.
