MY hart and tongue were twinnes, at once conceaved,
The eldest was my hart, borne dumbe by destinie:
The last my tongue, of all sweet thoughts bereaved,
Yet strung and tun'd, to play harts harmonie.
Both knit in one, and yet a-sunder placed.
What hart would speake, the tongue doth still discover:
What tongue doth speake, is of the hart embraced,
And both are one, to make a new-found Lover.
New-found, and only found in Gods and Kings,
Whose words are deedes, but deedes nor words regarded:
Chast thoughts do mount, and flie with swiftest wings,
My love with paine, my paine with losse rewarded.
Engrave upon this tree Daphnes perfection:
That neither men nor Gods can force affection.
