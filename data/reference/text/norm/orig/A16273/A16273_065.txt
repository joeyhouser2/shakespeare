AH trees, why fall your leaves so fast?
Ah Rocks, where are your roabes of mosse?
Ah Flocks, why stand you all agast?
Trees, Rocks, and Flocks, what, are ye pensive for my losse?

The birds me thinks tune naught but moane,
The winds breath naught but bitter plaint:
The beasts forsake their dennes to groane,
Birds, winds, and beasts, what, doth my losse your powers attaint?

Floods weepe their springs above their bounds,
And Eccho wailes to see my woe:
The roabe of ruthe doth cloath the grounds,
Floods, Eccho, grounds, why do ye all these teares bestow?

The trees, the Rocks and Flocks replie,
The birds, the winds, the beasts report:
Floods, Eccho, grounds for sorrow crie,
We greeve since Phillis nill kinde Damons love consort.
