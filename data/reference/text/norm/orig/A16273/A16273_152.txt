O Gentle Love, ungentle for thy deede,
thou makest my hart,
a bloodie marke,
With piercing shot to bleede.

Shoote soft sweete Love, for feare thou shoote amisse,
for feare too keene,
thy arrowes been:
And hit the hart, where my beloved is.

Too faire that fortune were, nor never I
shall be so blest,
among the rest:
That love shall ceaze on her by simpathy.

Then since with Love my prayers beare no boote,
this doth remaine,
to ease my paine,
I take the wound, and die at Venus foote.
