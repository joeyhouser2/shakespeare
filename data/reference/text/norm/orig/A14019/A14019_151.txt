Long did I love, and likte her passing well
Whose beautie bred the thraldom of my thought,
Long did I sue to her for to expell
The foule disdain that beauties beames had wrought:
Long did I serve, and Long I would have doon,
My minde was bent a thorow race to roon.
Long when I had looude, sude, and served so.
As mought have likte as brave a Dame as she,
Her Friend she forced not but let him go,
She looude at least besides him two or three:
Her common cheare to erie one that sude,
Bred me to deeme she did her Friend delude.
Great was my griefe at first to be refusde
That Long had looude with true unfained hart,
But when I sawe I had been long abusde
I forcde the lesse from such a Friend to part:
Yet ere I gave her up I gainde a thing
That griefe to her, and ease to me did bring.
