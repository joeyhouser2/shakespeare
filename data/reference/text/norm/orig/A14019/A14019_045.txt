BY fortune came a Foxe,
where grue a loftie Vine,
I will no Grapes (quoth he)
this yarde is none of mine:
The Foxe would none bicause that he
Perceiude the highnesse of the Tree.
¶ So men that Forlie are,
and long their lust to have,
But cannot come thereby,
make wise they would not crave:
Those subtill Marchants will no Wine
Bicause they cannot reach the Vine.
