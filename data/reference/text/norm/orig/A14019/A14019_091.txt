What (Philomela) meanes this fond intent
To hatch thy broode in fell Medaeas lap?
What? doste thou hope her rigor will relent
Towarde thy Babes, that gave her own no pap?
But slue them all at once, and at a clap?
I wote not what thou meanste: unlesse that she
Should kill thy Brats, too make the Mother free.
