The countnance of this Noble Cowntesse marck
When she thy Verse with eie that Saphire like
Doth shine suruayes, let be thy only carck
To note her Lookes: and if she ought mislike
Say that thou shouldst have hid it from her sight,
Thy Authour made the best for her delight,
The woorst he willde in covert scrole to lurke
Until the Beare were overlickt afresh,
For why in deepe this hastie hatched wurke
Resembleth much the shapelesse lumpe of flesh
That Beares bring forth: So when I lick thee over
Thou shalt (I trust) thy perfite shape recover.
