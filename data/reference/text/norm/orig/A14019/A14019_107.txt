J Dido and the Queene of Carthage ground,
Whose lims thou seest so lively set to sight:
Such one I was, but never to be found
So farre in love as Vergill seemes to wright,
I liude not so in lust and fowle delight.
For neither he that wandring Duke of Troie
Knewe me, nor yet at Lybie lande ariude:
But to escape Jarbos that did noie
Me sore, of lyfe my Carcasse I depriude,
To keepe my Hest that he would tho have riude.
No storme of love, or dolour made me die,
I slue my selfe to save my Sheete of shame
Wherein good Sycheus wrapped me perdie:
Then Vergill then the greater be thy blame,
That so by love dost breede my fowle defame.
