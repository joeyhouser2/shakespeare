Like as the waxe doth quench, and feed the flame,
•o love to men gives both despaire and life.
As luie finds fit meanes whereby to climbe,
So love sorts out his subiect where him list▪
As fire with violence consumeth wood,
So scorne with crueltie doth murder love.
As young vines yeeld most wine, but old brings best,
So young love speaketh much, but old doth most.
Like as affection is in lovers restlesse.
So being perfect, it is likewise endlesse.
As fancie must be cured by affection,
So love is only remedied by love.
