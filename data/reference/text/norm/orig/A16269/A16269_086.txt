The Tyrant Dionysius, by much drinke,
Lost utterly the benefit of sight.
Aruntius in his beastly drunkennes,
With his own daughter incest did commit.
Ptolomie slew his father and his mother,
Through wine and women, dying like a beast.
Geta the Emperour three dayes feasting sate,
Seru'd by the order of the Alphabet.
Men given to belly-seruice, Plato saith,
Deserve no better name, than brutish beasts.
Excesse (saith Tullie) is a testimonie
Of soules incontinence, and base desires.
