AS the hearb Rew is bitter in our taste,
•o deaths remembrance fearefull is to many.
As sleepe deprives the memorie of paines,
So sleepe of death ends all our wretchednes.
As all small currents runne into the sea,
So all mens toiles are swallowed up in death.
As borrowed money must be paid againe,
So what life owes, must be by death discharg'd.
As we are merrie at our childrens birth,
So should we not grieve vainly at their death.
As darknesse doth obscure the fairest day,
So death laies hold upon the forward'st life.
