Pvblicola wonne fame for leading armes:
And Solon by his civill actions.
The fame Milciades got at Marathon,
Would not permit Thems stocles to sleepe.
Fabius did by his vertues get such fame,
As Maximus was added to his name.
Lysimachus was famous through the world,
Because he kill'd a Lyon in his youth.
Seneca saith; Fame should be followed,
Rather than coveted by fond desire.
No man (saith Cicero) is learn'd and wise,
But fame must needs attend his actions.
