Papyrius Cursor punisht Fabius,
Because uniustly he commenced warre.
The Emperour A•relius gave strict charge,
That no man should abuse himselfe in warre.
The worthie Souldiour Bell•zarius,
Would never warre but on some speciall cause.
Traiane was never vanquished in warre,
Because he would not meddle, but in right.
Warre makes men cruell, so saith Sen••a:
But peace provo•eth them to gentlenes.
Plato affirmed, warre was excellent
When it did ha•me to none but enemies.
