Pompey let burne his finger in the fire,
Rather than he would be too free of tongue.
V•ysse• in his you•h refrain'd from speech,
Because in yeares he would direct his tongue.
Great Alexander, gave Cherillus coine
To hold his peace, and to forbeare to write.
Antig•nus this lesson taught his sonne,
First to learne silence, then to practise speech.
Ze•o repr•oved one that prated much,
And said, his eares were founded on his tongue▪
The to•gue (saith Aristotle) blabs the mind,
And fooles or wise men soone thereby we find.
