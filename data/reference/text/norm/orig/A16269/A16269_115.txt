AS lowlinesse of heart brings downe heavens grace,
So humble words can proudest tearmes deface.
As salt doth season every kind of meat,
So lowlinesse doth shew all vertues best.
As vallies sertilnesse the hills exceeds,
So humble lowlinesse shewes fairest deeds.
As wine in lowest vaults is best preseru'd,
So grace in humble minds is best discern'd.
As proud presumption seekes his own decay,
So lowlinesse to blisse directs the way.
As ignorance most scorneth to be taught,
So humblenesse desireth still to learne.
