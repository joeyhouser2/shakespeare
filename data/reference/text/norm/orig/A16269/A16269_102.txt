Narsetes that renowmed Generall,
By slaunders was dismissed from his charge.
When Scipio was by slaunder highly wrong'd,
His discreet answere soone acquited him.
Calisthenes, Parmenio, and Philotae•.
By slaundrous accusations lost their lives.
Augustus pardon'd one that would have slaine him,
But banisht him that falsely slaundred him.
Diogenes affirmed, the slaunderer
Was worse than any wild or furious beast.
Seneca saith, O• theeves men may beware,
But hardly shall they scape the slaunderer.
