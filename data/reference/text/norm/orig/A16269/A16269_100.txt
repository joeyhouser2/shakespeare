Caligula did covet his own ease,
And yet disdain'd others should do the like.
Anthonie causde the head of Cicero
In scorne, be set before him at his meat.
Plato, Xenophon, and Demosthenes,
Against each other were contemptuous.
Geta and Antoninus, being brethren,
Slew one the other through their privat scorne.
Among all perturbations, Tullie saith,
Disdaine is most iniurious to it selfe.
And Fabius Maximus holds like conceit,
Affirming, nothing worser than contempt.
