AS fire supprest, is much more forcible,
So griefes conceal'd, urge greater passions.
As streames restrain'd, breake through or over-flow,
So sorrow smoother'd, growes to greater woe.
As tendrest wood is most annoyed of wormes,
So feeblest minds doth sorrow most afflict.
As clouds do rob us of faire heavens beautie,
So care bereaves us of our speeches libertie.
As the sweet rose doth grow among the bryars,
So oft in sorrowes some content is found.
As discreete Pylots do for stormes prepare,
So in our joy let us provide for care.
