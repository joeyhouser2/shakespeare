AS sinne is soonest entertain'd in youth,
So is it hardly shaken off in age.
As gentle mould is apt for any print,
So youth receives what-e're impression.
As until'd fields bring nothing •orth but weeds,
So untaught youth yeelds all but vanitie.
As freshest flowres the canker soonest eats,
So youthfull heads are quickly caught by vice.
As unripe apples fall not but by force,
So unconstrain'd, •outh hardly yeelds to die.
As youngest nettles are not free from stings,
So wisest youth hath imperfections.
