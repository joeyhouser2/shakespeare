AS Rats and Myce do feed upon our meat,
So slaunderers feed on flesh of other men.
As divers meats do hurt digestion,
So changeable reports begetteth slaunder.
As Princes armes reach very farre in length,
So slaunder stretcheth unto following times.
As deepe incisions are for festred sores,
So mightie meanes must cure up slaunders wounds.
As vultures prey upon dead carion,
So slaunderers feed upon mens living names.
As Somners live by peoples daily sinnes,
So slaunders live by killing mens good fame.
