Zaleucus to the Locrians made a law,
To loose their eyes that sinn'd in foule desires.
Appius was banished the cittie Rome,
For leud affection to Virginia.
Marke Anthonie disgrac'd his former fame,
By not restraining his affections.
King Alexander hated to the death
In men or women loose and leud desires.
Fancie (saith Aristotle) often makes
A frenzie in their soules are led thereby.
Desire (saith Socrates) no limits holds,
And therefore hardly can be mastred.
