AS vapours in ascending soone are lost,
So prides presuming but confounds it selfe.
As great fires hazard simple cottages,
So pride in poore men is most perillous.
As winds blow sternely being neere to cease,
So pride is loftiest, neere destruction.
As Cadar trees unfruitfull are and stiffe,
So proud men helpe not any, nor themselves.
As pride is the beginner of all vice,
So the destruction is it of all vertue.
As still the dropsie covets after drinke,
So pride is never pleased but with pride.
