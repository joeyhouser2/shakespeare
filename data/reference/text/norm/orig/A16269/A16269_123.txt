Philostrates endured all his paines,
To th'admiration of his enemies.
Marius the Romane said, he felt no paine
In all his hurts, if but one friend were by.
Sextus Pompeius could abide no paine,
No, not so much as feele his head to ake.
The Spartanes for their pleasures, made strict lawes,
Shewing, what paine to each one did belong.
Cicero said, No paine could touch the mind,
That was but rampierd▪in with •usterance.
And Aristotle held the same opinion,
Firme resolution could subdue all paine.
