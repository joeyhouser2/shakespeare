AS kings do rule by counsell and aduise,
So tyrants by their will, and not by law.
As want of riches makes a tyrant prince,
So great abundance heapes up wickednes.
As boistrous winds do shake the highest towers,
So blood and death cuts off proud tyrannie.
As enuie shooteth at the fairest markes,
So tyrants levell at tr••• princes lives.
As forest tempests are in largest seas,
So greatest evill ensues on tyrannie.
As trees are alwaies weakest toward the top.
So falleth Tyrants in their chiefest height.
