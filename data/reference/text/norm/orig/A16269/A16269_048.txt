Philip, when any made complaint to him,
Stopt one eare, till the other part were heard.
Aristides so loved Equitie,
That he of all men was sir named IVST.
Junius the Consull so respected right,
As his own sonnes he did condemne to death.
Cato Censorius was so just and firme,
As none durst moove him in a naughtie cause.
Justice (saith Seneca) is the law of God,
And bond of all humane societie.
Devotion and good will (Lactantius saith)
Joynes us to God, as justice doth to men.
