Nestor rehearst his honourable deed•,
That Hectors combat might be und•••ane.
Leonidas to honour E•tich•s,
Led him from forth the daunger of the fight▪
Pericles being requested to sw•are false,
Replyed: That honour would not suffer him.
Agesilaus urg'd to give sentence wrong,
Said: But for honour he could easily do it.
Parmenides, his schollers did instruct:
No wound was comparable to dishonour.
Cleobulus condemn'd that citie quite,
Where honour was not held in high esteeme.
