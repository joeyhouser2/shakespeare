AS daunger waiteth at the heeles of pride,
So every pleasure hath a following paine.
As where mishaps do flow, there love doth ebbe,
So where friends faile, the heart feeles no like paine.
As sicke men with least anguish are disturb'd,
So to vexe troubled minds, augments their paine.
As Sun-shine daies of fortune getteth friends,
So paine or perill looseth them as soone.
As miserie a med'cine hardly finds,
So inward paines, are not with pratinge cur'd.
As he beares sorrow best that hides it most,
So who knowes patience, stands prepar'd for paine.
