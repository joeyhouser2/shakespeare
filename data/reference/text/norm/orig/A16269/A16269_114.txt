Brave Coriolanus being banisht Rome,
•oucht with his fault, went forth, and dide in teares.
The Romane matrons for old Brutus death,
For one whole yere did nothing else but mourne.
The wife of Lepidus, her misbehaviour,
In teares and anguish did abridge his daies.
Crassus was never seene in all his life
But once to smile; but many times to mourne.
Sen•e• saith, That mightie men by power
Revenge themselves; the weaker, by their teares.
The broken heart (saith Tullie) hath moist eyes,
When often-times it faignes forth merrie lookes.
