When Alexander gave great gifts away,
Being askt, What for himselfe he kept? replied, Hope.
Caesar continually wasled with hope,
That he should governe many Monarchies.
Androclidas derided being lame,
Said; Then in fight I hope I shall not flie.
Pindarus calleth hope, The nource of age:
And Thales said, Hope was a common helpe.
Learned men differ from the ignorant
(As Bias saith) but only by their Hope.
Hope (as Simonides the Poet saith)
Is the sole guide and governour of men.
