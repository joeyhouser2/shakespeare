Philip was told, that men enuied him:
Quoth he; I care not, I have given no cause.
Caligula had such an enuious heart,
As he repin'd at all mens happinesse.
Th'Athenians so enuied Themistocles,
As they made verses in reproch of him,
Pompey and Caesars enuie to each other,
Can scant be matcht in any histories.
Plutarch saith, enuie is damn'd sorcerie,
And therefore witht it generally abhorr'd.
Thucidides graunts, wise men may be enuied,
Only to make their vertues shine the more.
