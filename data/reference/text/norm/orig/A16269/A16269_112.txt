Caesar did never feare a merrie looke.
But doubted sad men to have wicked thoughts.
The Spartanes carried commendation,
Because they scorn'd to beare iniurious thoughts.
Puluillus being told, his sonne was dead,
Made answere: Therefore he would take no thought.
When Scipio read the bookes of Xenophon,
He said: They counsail'd him from taking thought.
Thoughts do afflict the mind, saith Cicer•,
And makes it subiect to no certaintie.
Saith Aristotle, They need Physicke most,
That do devoure their health by fretfull thoughts.
