AS falls the tree, so prostrate still it lyes:
So speedeth life, in living, as it dyes.
As men by life in bondage soone are brought,
Even so by death is freedome soonest wrought.
As si•e burnes fiercely, being still supplyed,
So life postes swiftly when it least is spyed.
As sharpe frosts easily nip forward springs,
So life to end it, hath too many things.
As Easterne winds doth towardly blossoms blast,
So inward cares makes life to finish fast.
As life is only by the gift of grace.
So death by nature taketh time and place.
