Scevola in the greatnesse of his mind,
Entred Porsennaes Tent to murder him.
Queene Tomiris to shew her dauntlesse mind,
With Cyrus blood, reveng'd her deare sonnes death.
Zenobia told Aurelian in the field,
He was not able to subdue her mind.
Lucius Dentatus, never matcht for mind,
Came eighteene times a conquerour from field.
Cicero saith, the goodnesse of the m•nd,
Is most discern'd in pardoning iniuries.
Socrates said, His quietnes in mind
Was cause he never sickned till his death.
