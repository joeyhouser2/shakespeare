AS beautie lookes like flowers in the spring,
So chastitie is like the starres of heaven.
As Violets smell sweet in any sente,
So chastitie shines bright in every eye.
As water-drops will pearce the hardest flint,
So chast resolve o'recomes the proudest lust.
As glasses broke, can never be repaird,
So chastitie once lost, is ne're restor'd.
As lust and libertie doth shorten life,
So chastitie makes endlesse live the soule.
As champions by their manhood are best knowne,
So is good life by spotlesse chastitie.
