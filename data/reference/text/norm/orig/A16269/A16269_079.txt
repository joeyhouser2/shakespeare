AS fire, the more it hath, the fiercer burnes,
•o covetous minds do alwaies crave for more.
As Bees do flocke unto a hony dew,
So covetous men still haunt the sente of gaine.
As greatest fish devoure the smaller frie,
So covetous wretches feed upon the poore.
As glurtons from them-selves can nothing spare,
So misers will let nothing passe their purse.
As without waves we never see the sea,
So covetous men are never free from cares.
As clouds do• somtime hide the Sunnes cleare light,
So covetousnes deprives the light of grace.
