Comodus not well tutor'd in his youth,
Did afterward proove a most wicked Prince.
Neroe• unbridled youth, made him to fall
To greater leudnesse than was ever heard.
Cato would to his sonnes be schoole master,
Because he would not have their youth infected.
Scemides and her sonne were cast in Tyber,
For bringing up the gulfe of shame to Rome.
Youth well instructed, saith Euripides,
Doth after make his age more honourable.
Pythagoras bad, tutor so young youth,
The sinnes of age be not imposde on thee.
