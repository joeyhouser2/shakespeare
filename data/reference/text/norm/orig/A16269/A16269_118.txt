AS courage addeth wings to brave desire,
So bloodie shewes doth quench incensedire.
As it is valour to be conquerour,
So wisdome maketh use of victorie.
As courage keepes the mind from base assaults,
So cowardise infects it with all faults.
As courage is esteem'd a wise mans coat,
So cowardise is follies cognisance.
As Faulconers do in Faulcons most delight,
So mightie men reioyceth in their might.
