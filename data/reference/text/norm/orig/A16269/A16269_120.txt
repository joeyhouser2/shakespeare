AS no estate can stable stand for aye,
So every pleasure hath his ending day.
As small brookes swell and are enrag'd with raine,
So sight of pleasure trebleth every paine.
As weeds expeld, the corne doth better thrive,
So care being kild, pleasure bides long alive.
As greatest griefes do make the least not seene,
So huge delights cause meane ones vanish cleane.
As greatest light, is in the largest skie,
So that delights, is furthest from the eye.
As sad minds brooke no merrie companie,
So sorrow is to pleasure enemie.
