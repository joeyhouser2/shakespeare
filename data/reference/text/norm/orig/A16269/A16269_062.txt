What-e're mishap Darius did befall,
Yet in his mind he was not moou'd at all.
Marcus 〈◊〉 said, that pati•nce
Had holpe him more than any thing be side.
Sylla endur'd revilings patiently,
And mildly yeelded his Dictator-ship.
Mauritius moou'd not at his childrens death,
Although he saw them slaine before his face.
When Socrates was councel'd to revenge,
Said: If an Asse strike, shall I strike againe?
In patience conflict, saith Euripides:
The vanquisht do exceed the vanquisher.
