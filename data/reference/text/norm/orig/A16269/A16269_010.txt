AS hell was made to punish sinnes proud guilt,
Even so was heaven for comfort of the just.
As base clothes ill become a princes court,
So none can enter heaven but purely clad.
As travailers desire their native homes,
So should all soules long for their heavenly home.
As hells obscuritie excells all darke,
So nothing with heavens brightnesse may compare.
As Jacob for his Rachell thought sower sweet,
So we for heaven ought deeme all else but vile.
As all joyes in this life are fraile and vaine,
So none but heavens joyes are perfect gaine.
