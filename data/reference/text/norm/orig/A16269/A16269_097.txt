AS poyson sweetly mixt is sooner ta'ne,
So fancie close conceal'd, is soonest fier'd.
As Chrysolites are prooved in the fire,
So is affection in enforc'd restraint.
As cities wanting Magistrates, decay:
Even so desire ungovern'd, hurts it selfe.
As all the world were darke but for the Sunne,
So life, but for affection, were unsure.
As steele brings fire from the hardest flint,
So fancie mollisies the stearnest mind.
As Almond trees in age do beare most fruit,
So yeares doth best approove affections.
