Scipi• did banish from his campe, all such
As could be toucht with sloth or idlenes.
The wise men thus did answere Alexander,
If he were idle, long he could not live.
Because the Sabies did abound in wealth,
They gave them selves to nought but idlenes.
Metellus being arriu'd in Africa,
Dismist all meanes might offer idlenes.
Tully saith, Men were borne to do good workes,
As a pre•eruative gainst idlenes.
