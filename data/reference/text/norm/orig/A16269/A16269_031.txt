AS no content is like the sweetes of love,
So no despaire can match with jealousie.
Love, as it is divine with loyaltie,
So is it hellish, wrapt in jealousie.
As from small brookes great rivers do arise,
So huge distemper springs from jealousie.
As Crowes do deeme their brood the fairest birds,
So jealous men their own choise most commend.
As shippes in tempests by the winds are tost,
So fond conceits do hurrie jealous heads.
As kindnesse doth delight in companie,
So is it poyson to mad jealousie.
