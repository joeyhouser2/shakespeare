BY follie Nicias was ta'ne alive,
Dismayed only with the Moones ecclipse.
Oemilius tearmed Perses but a foole,
To be dismay'd because of vanquishing.
Cleander, who would needs betray his lord,
Lost all his hopes, and proou'd him selfe a foole.
