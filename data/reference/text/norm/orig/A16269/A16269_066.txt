IS•rina, sometimes Queene of Scithia,
With her sweet voice, made calme the rough swolne seas.
Romane Cornelia was so eloquent,
That to her they did daily sacrifice.
Lachis of Athens, to her country-men
Appointed lawes for civill government.
Queene Parisatis caus'd the Persian kings,
To have their buriall in rich tombes of gold.
Plato held women in a familie,
As needfull as a kingdomes governour.
If nature do desire her selfe to see,
(Saith Plutarch) women then her glasse may be.
