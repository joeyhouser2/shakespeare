Parmenio wild his king to breake his faith,
I would (quoth he) were I Parmenio.
Lysander made no reckoning of his faith,
And therefore was by every one reproou'd.
Attilius sent to Rome upon his faith,
Boldly return'd, although it cost his life.
Rastrix the Duke of Cleveland, breaking faith,
Was therefore dispossest of both his eyes.
Let none speake ill of us, said Pittacus,
To whome we stand obliged by our faith.
Keepe faith (saith Cicero) with enemies
What ere mishap do follow thereupon.
