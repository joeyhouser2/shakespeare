Pausanias lou'd his wife with such firme love,
As no description well could set it downe.
Prediccas for his love to Alexander,
Refused mightie wealth in Macedon.
The Emperour Claudius would not love or hate,
But as he wa• thereto by others led.
Scipio so lou'd the Poet Ennius,
That being dead, he kept his picture still.
Zeno, although a Stoicke, yet did yeeld,
That love in young men was most requisite.
Cicero not gain-said wise men to love
So they might love without deepe cares and sighes.
