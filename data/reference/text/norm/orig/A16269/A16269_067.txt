AS breath on steele, as soone is off as on,
So climbers are as quickly downe as up.
As nothing in substance is more light than aire,
So nothing can out-go ambitious thoughts.
As winds being up, do blow more violent,
So proud usurpers tyrannise in height.
As bad men grieve at good mens happinesse,
So high aspirers grudge at lowlines.
As powder fier'd, is but a suddaine flash,
Even so ambition is no more than smoke.
As B•ts do flutter, not directly flie,
So climbers aime at much, and misse of all.
