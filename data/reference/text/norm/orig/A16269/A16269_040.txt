Ulysses lou'd so deare his native land.
As for it, he refus'd to be immortall.
Aglaurus to redeeme his countries peace,
From Athens walls himselfe threw headlong downe.
Faire Iphigenia for her kingdomes good,
Made willing sacrifice of her own blood.
King Codrus, rather than his realme should perish,
Gladly did runne upon his foe-mens swords.
Xenophon did thinke them unworthie life,
That made no conscience of the common-wealth.
He that denyes to die in countries cause,
Deserves (saith Tullie) hate of all good men.
