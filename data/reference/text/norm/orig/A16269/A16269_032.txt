The Persians were so jealous of their wives▪
As but in waggons they ne're went abroad.
Phanius lockt up his wife through jealousie,
Whereby she compast what she could not else.
Procris was slaine through her own jealousie,
Hid in a bush to watch her husbands walke.
Argus, albeit he had an hundred eyes,
Yet could not keepe from Jo, Jupiter.
Cicero calleth Jealousie, a feare
Of loosing that belongs to ones own selfe▪
Chrysippus holds, that jealousie ill brookes
A partner in the thing it most esteemes.
