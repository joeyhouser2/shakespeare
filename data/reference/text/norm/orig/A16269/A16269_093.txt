Like as the day cannot prevent the night,
So vaine it is against the fates to fight.
As with the worst, fate spareth not the best,
So faults are easier lookt in, than redrest.
Even as the starres and sands have wondrous date,
So are our lives subiect to nought but fate.
As cities are o're-come by batterie,
So all on earth must yeeld to destinie.
As lookes of love oft shadow inward hate,
So times faire hope is shortned soone by fate.
As flowers in morning fresh, oft fade ere night,
So fate cuts off what goodliest seemes in sight.
