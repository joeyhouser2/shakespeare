Mistrust misdemes amisse, whereby displeasure growes,
And time delaied, finds frends afraied, their faith for to disclose:
Suspect that breede the thought, and thought to sighes conuarte,
And sighs have sought a floud of teares, where sobbs do seke the hart.

Thus harte that meanes no harme, must feede on sorrowes all,
Until such tyme as pleaseth the judge, the truth in question call:
Though cause of greate mistrust, before that judge appeare,
My truthe and mercie of my judge, I trust shall set me cleare.

Report these rimes at large, my truthe for to detecte,
Yet truthe in tyme shall trie it self, and drive awaie suspecte:
Beleve not every speache nor speake not all you heare,
For truthe and mercie of the judge, I trust shall set me cleare.
