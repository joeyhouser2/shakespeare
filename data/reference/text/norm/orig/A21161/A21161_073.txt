Shall I no waie winne you, to graunt my desire?
What woman, will graunt you, the thyng you require:
You only to love me, is all that crave,
You only to leave me, is all I would have.
My deare alas now saie not so,
To love you best, I must saie no:
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
The swifter I followe, then you fly awaie,
Swift hauks in their fliyng, oft times misse their pray
Yet some killeth dedly, that flie to the marke:
You shall touche no feather, thereof take no carke.
Yet hope shall further my desire,
You blowe the coales, and raise no fire.
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt,
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
To love is no daunger where true love is ment,
I will love no ranger, lest that I repent:
My love is no ranger, I make God avow,
To trust your smoth saiyngs, I sure knowe not how.
Moste truthe I meane, as tyme shall well trie,
No truthe in men, I oft espie.
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not.
I will not:
Make me your man,
Be shrewe me than.
Some women maie saie naie, and meane love moste true,
Some women can make fools, of as wise men as you,
In tyme I shall catche you, I knowe when and where:
I will sone dispatche you, you shall not come there.
Some speds at length, that oft have mist,
I am well armed, come when you list.
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrewe me than.
Yet worke your kinde kindly, graunt me love for love,
I will use you frendly, as I shall you prove:
Moste close you shall finde me, I this do protest,
Then sure you shall binde me, to graunt your request.
O happie threde now have I sponne,
You syng before the conquest wonne.
Why then, will you swarne,
even as you deserve:
Love still,
I will,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
come to me than.
