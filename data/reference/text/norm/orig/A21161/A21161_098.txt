O Heavenly God O Father dere, cast doune thy tender eye,
Upon a wretche that prostrate here, before thy trone doeth lye:
O powre thy precious oyle of grace, into my wounded harte,
O let the dropps of mercie swage, the rigour of my smarte.

My fainting soule suppressed sore, with carefull clogge of sinne,
In humble sort submitts it self, thy mercie for to winne:
Graunt mercie then O saviour swete, to me moste wofull thrall,
Whose mornfull crie to thee O Lorde, doeth still for mercie call.

Thy blessed will I have despised, upon a stubborne minde:
And to the swaie of worldly thyngs, my self I have enclinde:
Forgettyng heaven, and heavenly powers, where God and saincts do dwel
My life had likt to tread the path, the leads the waie to hell.

But now my lorde, my lode starre bright I will no more do so:
To thinke upon my former life, my harte doeth melt for woe.
Alas I sigh, alas I sobbe, alas I do repent:
That ever my licencious will, so wickedly was bent.

Sith thus therefore with yernfull plain, I do thy mercie crave
O Lorde for thy greate mercies sake, let me thy mercie have:
Restore to life the wretched soule, that els is like to dye,
So shall my voyce unto thy name, syng praise eternally.

Now blessed be the Father first, and blessed be the Sonne,
And blessed be the holie Ghoste, by whom all thyngs are doen:
Blesse me O blessed Trinitie, with thy eternall grace,
That after death my soule maie have, in heaven a dwellyng place.
