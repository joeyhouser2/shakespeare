LO heare the man that must of love complaine,
Lo heare that seas that feeles no kinde of blisse:
Lo here I seke for joye, and finde but paine,
Lo what despite can greater be then this?
To freze to death, and stande yet by the fire,
And she that shonneth me moste, I do desire.

But shall I speake alas, or shall I die,
By death no helpe, in speache some helpe doeth lie:
Then from that breast, remove a Marble minde,
As I see cause, so are ye like to finde.
I yelde my self, what would you more of me,
You yelde, but for to winne and conquer me,
Sa•e and kill not madame,
Forsake your sute for shame,
No no no no, not so.

O happie man, now vaunt thy self,
That hath this conquest gainde:
And now doeth live in greate delight,
That was so lately painde.
Triumph, triumph, triumph, wholovers be
Thrise happie is that woyng,
That is not long a doyng,
Triumph, triumph, triumph, that hath like victorie.
