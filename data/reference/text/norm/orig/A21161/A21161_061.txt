When first mine eyes did vew, and marke thy beautie faire for to behold,
And when mine eares, gan first to harke, the pleasant words that thou me told
I would as then I had been free, fron eares to heare, and eyes to se

And when my hands did handle oft, that might thee kepe in memorie,
And when my feete had gone so softe, to finde and have thy companie,
I would each hande a foote had been, and eke each foote, a hand so seen.

And when in minde I did consent, to followe thus my fansies will,
And when my harte did first relent, to tast such baite my self to spill,
I would my harte had been as thine, or els thy harte as soft as mine.

Then should not I such cause have found, to wish this monstrus sight to se,
Ne thou alas that madest the wounde, should not deny me remedy,
Then should one will in bothe remain, to graunt one hart which now is twaine.
