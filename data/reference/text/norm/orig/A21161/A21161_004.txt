Sweete were the joyes, that both might like and last,
Strange were the state, exempt from all distresse:
Happy the lyfe, that no mishap should tast,
Blessed the chaunce, might never change successe.
Were such a lyfe to leade, or state to prove,
Who would not wyshe, that such a lyfe were love.

But O the sowry sauce of sweete unsure,
When pleasures flye, and flee with wast of winde:
The trustlesse traynes that hoping hartes allure,
When sweete delightes do but allure the minde.
When care consumes and wastes the wretched wight,
Whyle fancy feedes, and drawes of her delight.

What lyfe were love, if love were free from payne?
But O that payne, with pleasure matcht should meete:
Why dyd the course of nature so ordayne,
That sugred sowre, must sause the bitter sweete.
Which sowre from sweete, might any meanes remove.
What happe, what heaven, what lyfe, were lyke to love.
