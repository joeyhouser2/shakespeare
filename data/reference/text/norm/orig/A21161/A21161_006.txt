When MAY is in his prime, then MAY each hart reioyce,
When MAY bedeckes each branch with greene, each bird streines forth his voyce.
The lively sappe creepes, up into the bloming thorne,
The flowres, which cold in prison kept, now laughes the frost to scorne.
All natures Impes triumphes, whyles joyfull May doth last,
When MAY is gone, of all the yeere the pleasant time is past.

MAY makes the cherfull hue, MAY breedes and bringes newe blood,
MAY marcheth throughout every limme, MAY makes the mery moode.
MAY pricketh tender hartes, their warbling notes to tune,
Ful strange it is, yet some we see, do make their MAY in June.
Thus thinges are straungely wrought, whyles joyfull MAY doth last,
Take MAY in time, when MAY is gone, the pleasant time is past.

All ye that live on earth, and have your MAY at will,
Reioyce in MAY, as I do now, and use your MAY with skill.
Use MAY, whyle that you may, for MAY hath but his time,
When all the fruite is gone, it is to late the tree to clime.
Your liking and your lust, is freshe whyles MAY doth last,
When MAY is gone, of all the yeere the pleasaunt time is past.
