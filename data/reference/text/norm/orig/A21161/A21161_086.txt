MY meanyng is to worke, what wondes love hath wrought,
Wherwith I muse why men of wit, have love so derely bought:
For love is worse then hate, and eke more harme hath doen,
Record I take of those that rede, of Paris Priams sonne.

It semed the God of slepe had mazed so much his witts,
When he refused witt for love, which cometh but by fitts:
But why accuse I hym, whom yearth hath covered long,
There be of his posteritie alive, I do hym wrong.

Whom I might well condempne, to be a cruell judge:
Unto my self who hath the crime, in others that I grudge.
