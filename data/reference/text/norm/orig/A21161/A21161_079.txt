IN quest of my relief I finde distresse,
In recompence of love, moste depe disdaine:
My langour is such, words maie not expresse,
A shower of teares, my watrishe eye doeth raine.
I dreame of this, and do devine of wo,
I wander in the thoughts, of my swete fo.

I would no peace, the cause of warre I flie,
I hope, I feare I burne, I chill in froste:
I lye alowe, yet mounts my minde on hie,
Thus doubtfull stormes, my troubled thoughts have toste,
And for my paine, this pleasure do I prove.
I hate my self, and pine in others love.

The worlde I graspe, yet holde I nought at all,
At libertie, I seme in prison pent:
I taste the sweete, more lower then bitter gall,
My shipp semes sounde, and yet her ribbs be rent.
And out alas, on Fortune false I crie,
Looke what I crave, that still she doeth denie.

Bothe life and death, be equall unto me,
I do desire to die, yet crave I life,
My witts with sondrie thoughts do disagre,
My self am with my self at mortall strife.
As warmth of sonne doeth melte the silver Snowe,
The heate of love, beholde consumes me so.
