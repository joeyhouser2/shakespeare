With painted speache I list not prove, my cunnyng for to trie,
Nor•et will use to fill my penne, with gilefull flatterie:
With pen in hand and harte in breast, shall faithfull promise make
To love you best, and serve you moste, for your great vertues sake.

And since dame Nature hath you deckt, with gifts above the rest,
Let not disdaine a harbour finde, within your noble brest:
For love hath ledd his lawe alike, to men of each degree,
So that the begger with the prince, shall love as well as he.

I am no prince I must confesse, nor yet of princes line,
Nor yet a brutishe begger borne, that feeds among the Swine:
The fruite shall trie the tree at last, the blossomes good or no,
Then do not judge of me the worse, till you have tried me so.

As I deserve, so then reward, I make you judge of all,
If I be false in worde or deede, let lightnyng thunder fall:
And furies fell with franticke fitts, bereve and stopp my breathe,
For an example to the rest, if I shall breake my faithe.
