I Would to God I were Acteon, that Diana did disguise,
To walke the Forest up and doune, whereas my ladie lies:
An Harte of heere and hewe, I wishe that I were so,
So that my Ladie knewe me, only and no mo.
The shalyng Nutts and Maste, that falleth from the tree,
Should well suffice for my repast, might I my ladie see:
It should not greve me, there in frost, to lye upon the grounde,
Delite should easly quite the coste, what evill so that I founde.
Sometyme that I might saie, when I sawe her alone,
Beholde, see yonder slave aldaie, that walketh the woodds alone,
