IF pleasures be in painefulnesse? in pleasures doth my body rest,
If joyes accorde with carefulnesse? a joyfull hart is in my brest:
If prison strong be libertie? in libertie long have I been,
If joyes accord with miserie? who can compare a lyfe to mine.
Who can unbind that is sore bound? who can make free that is sore thrall
Or how can any meanes be found? to comfort such a wretch withall?
None can, but he that hath my hart, conuert my paines to comfort then,
Yet since his seruant I became, most like a bondman have I been:
Since first in bondage I became, my woord and deede was ever such,
That never once he could me blame, except from loving him too much.
Which I can judge no just offence, nor cause that I deserud disdayne,
Except he meane through false pretence, through forged loved to make a traine
Nay nay alas, my fained thoughts my frended and my fained ruth
My pleasures past my present plaints, shew wel I meane but to much truth:
But since I can not him attaine, against my will I let him go,
And lest he glorie at my paine, I wyl attempt to cloke my woe.
Youth, learne by me, but do not prove, for I have proved to my paine,
What greevous greefes do grow by love, and what it is to love in vaine.
