The faithe that failes, must nedes be thought untrue,
The frende that faines, who holdeth not uniust,
Who likes that love, that chaungeth still for newe:
Who hopes for truthe, where trothe is voide of trust,
No faithe, no frende, no love, no trothe so sure,
But rather failes then stedfastly endure.

What head so staied? that altereth not intent,
What thought so sure? that stedfast doeth remaine,
What witte so wise? that never nedes repent:
What tonge so true? but sometyme wonts to faine,
What foote so firme? that never treads awrie,
What soner dimde? then fight of clerest eye.

What harte so fixt? but some enclines to change,
What moode so milde? that never moved debate:
What faithe so strong? but lightly likes to range,
What love so true? that never learnde to hate.
What life so pure? that lasts, without offence,
What worldly mynde? but moves with ill pretence.

What knot so fast? that maie not be untide,
What seale so sure? but fraude or forse shall breke:
What prop of staye? but one tyme shrinks aside,
What ship so stauche? that never had a leke.
What graunt so large? that no exception maks,
What hoped helpe? but frende at nede forsaks.

What seate so high? but lowe to grounde maie fall,
What hap so good? that never founde mislike:
What state so sure? but subiect is to thrall
What force prevailes? where Fortune liste to strike.
What wealth so much? but tyme maie turne to want,
What store so greate? but wastyng maketh skant.

What profites hope in depth of dangers thrall,
What ruste in tyme, but waxeth worse and worse:
What helpes good harte, if Fortune froune withall,
What blessyng thrives, gainst heavenly helples curse.
What winnes desire, to get and can not gaine,
What botes to wishe and never to obtaine.
