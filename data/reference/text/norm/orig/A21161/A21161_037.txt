TO seeme for to revenge eache wrong in hastie wise,
By proofe we see of guiltlesse men, it hath not bene th• guise.
In slaunders lothsome brute, where they condemned be,
With ragelesse moode they suffer wrong, where truth shall trie then free.
These are the patient panges, that passe within the brest
Of those, that feele their cause by mine, where wrong hath right opprest.
I know how by suspect, I have bene judgd awrie,
And graunted g•ltie in the thing, that cleerely I denie:
My faith may me defend, if I might loved be,
God judge me so, as from the guilt I know me to be free.
I wrote but for my selfe, the griefe was all mine own,
As, who would prove excremitie, by proofe it might be knowne.
Yet are there such, that say, they can my meaning deeme,
Without respect of this olde trothe, things prove not as they seeme,
Whereby it may befall, in judgement to be quicke,
To make them selves suspect therewith, that needed not to kicke.
Yet in resisting wrong, I would not have it thought
I do amisse, as though I knew by whome it might be wrought.
If any such there be, that heerewithall be vext,
It were their vertue to beware, and deeme me better next.
