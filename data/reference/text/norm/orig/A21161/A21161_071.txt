How can the tree but wast, and wither awaie,
That hath not sometyme comfort of the Sonne:
How can that flower but fade, and sone decaie,
That alwaies is with darke clouds over ronne.
Is this a life, naie death you maie it call,
That feeles each paine, and knoweth no joye at all.

What foodles beast can live long in good plight,
Or is it life, where sences there be none:
Or what availeth eyes without their light?
Or els a tonge, to hym that is alone.
Is this a life? naie death you maie it call,
That feeles each paine, and knowes no joye at all.

Whereto serve eares, if that there be no sounde,
Or such a head, where no devise doeth growe:
But all of plaints, since sorrowe is the grounde,
Whereby the harte doeth pine in deadly woe.
Is this a life, naie death you maie it call,
That feeles each paine, and knows no joye at all.
