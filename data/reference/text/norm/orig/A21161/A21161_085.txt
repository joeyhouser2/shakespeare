Even as the waxe doeth melt, or dewe consume awaie,
Before the Sonne, so I behold through careful thoughts decaie:
For my best lucke leads me, to such sinister state,
That I do wast with others love, that hath my self in hate.
And he that beats the bushe, the wished birde not getts,
But such I see as sitteth still, and holds the foulyng netts.

The Drone more honie sucks, that laboureth not at all,
Then doeth the Bee, to whose most pain, least pleasure doth befall:
The Gardner sowes the seeds, whereof the flowers do growe,
And others yet do gather them, that tooke lesse paine I knowe.
So I the pleasaunt grape have pulled from the Vine,
And yet I languish in greate thirst, while others drinke the wine.

Thus like a wofull wight, I wove my webb of woe,
The more I would wede out my cares, the more they seme to grow
The which betokeneth hope, forsaken is of me,
That with the carefull culuer climes, the worne and withered tree.
To entertaine my thoughts, and there my happe to mone,
That never am lesse idle lee, then when I am alone.
