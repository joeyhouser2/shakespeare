I had rather love though in vaine that face,
Then have of any other grace.
I being forc'd to carrie Venus shield,
Had rather beare a Phoenix for my crest,
Rather then any bird within the field,
Love tels me that her beautie is the best:
Though some desire faire Vestas Turtle-dove,
In my Birds bosome resteth perfect love.

Vaine is that blind unskilfull herauldrie,
That will not cause my bird that is so rare,
Face all the world for her rarietie,
Then who with her for honor may compare?
Have we one like her for her pride of beautie,
Of all the feathered Quier in the aire?
Any but unto her do owe their dutie:
Other may blaze, but I will alwaies say,
Grace whom thou list, she beares the palme away.
