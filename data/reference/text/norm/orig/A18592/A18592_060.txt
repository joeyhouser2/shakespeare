Seeing you have mine, let me have thine.
Seeing my passions are so penetrable,
You of all other should be pittifull,
Have mind of me, and you'le be favourable,
Mine hart doth tell me you are mercifull,
Let my harts love be alwayes violable,
Me have you found in all things dutifull,
Have me in favour, and thy selfe shalt see,
Thine and none others, will I all wayes be.
