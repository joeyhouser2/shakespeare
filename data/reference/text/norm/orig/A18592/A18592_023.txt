Devout obedience on my knees I profer,
Delight matcht with delight, if thou do crave it,
Denie not gentle Phoenix my sweet offer,
Despaire not in my love, for thou shalt have it,
Damne not the soule to woe if thou canst save it:
Doves pray devoutly, O let me request,
Delicious love to build within thy nest.
