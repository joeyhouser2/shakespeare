Though place be far, my heart is nar.
Though thou my Dove from me be separated,
Place, nor the distance shall not hinder me,
Be constant for a while, thou maist be thwarted,
Far am I not, Ile come to succour thee.
My heart and thine, my sweet shall nere be parted,
Heart made of love, and true simplicitie:
Is not Love lawlesse, full of powerfull might?
Nar to my heart that still with Love doth fight.
