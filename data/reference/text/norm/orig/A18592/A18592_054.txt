Vaine is the love encountred with denayes,
That yeelds but griefe, where grace should rather grow,
Face full of furie, voide of curteous praise:
Then since all love consists of weale and woe,
Have still in mind, that love deserves the best,
Of hea•ts the touchstone, inward motions loving,
Any that yeelds the fruite of true-loves rest,
Other I love unworthie of commending,
Grac'd with bare beautie, beautie most offending.
