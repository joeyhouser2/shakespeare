If you me just have knowne,
Then take me for your own.
If you be faire, why should you be unkind?
You have no perfect reason for the s••e,
Me thinkes it were your glorie for to find
Just measure at my hands, but you to blame,
Have from the deepest closet of your heart,
Knowne my pure thoughts, and yet I pine in smart.

Then in the deepest measure of pure love,
Take pittie on the sad sicke pining soule,
Me may you count your unknowne Turtle-Dove,
For in my bosomes chamber, I enroule
Your deepe love-darting eie, and still will be
Own of your own, despight extremitie.
