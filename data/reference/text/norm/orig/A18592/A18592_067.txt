You are the first, in whom I trust.
You in your bosome having plac'd a light,
Are the chiefe admirall unto my Fleet,
The Lanthorne for to guide me in the night,
First to the shore, where I may set my feet
In safegard, void of Dangers cruell spight,
Whom in disgrace Love and fel Enuie meet,
I muster up my spirits, and they flie;
Trust of thy faith controules mine enemie.
