Within thy brest, my hart doth rest.
Within the cir•uit of a Christall spheare,
Thy eyes are plast, and underneath those eyes,
Brest of hard flint, eares that do scorne to heare
My dayes sad gronings, and night waking cries,
Hart sore sicke passions, and Loves agonies,
Doth it become thy beauty? no, a staine
Rests on thy bright brow wrinckled with disdaine.
