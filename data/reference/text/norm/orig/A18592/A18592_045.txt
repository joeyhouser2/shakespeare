My life you save, if you I have.
My eyes, my hand, my heart seeke to maintaine
Life for thy love, therefore be gracious,
You with your kindnesse have my true heart slaine,
Save my poore life, and be not tyrannous,
If any grace do in thy breast remaine,
You women have bene counted amorous;
I pine in sadnesse, all proceeds from thee,
Have me in liking through thy clemencie.
