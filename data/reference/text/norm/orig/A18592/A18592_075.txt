I have no love, but you my Dove.
I pine in sadnesse, and in sad songs singing
Have spent my time, my ditties harsh and ill,
No sight but thy faire sight would I be seeing:
Love in my bosome keepes his castle still,
But being dissevered I sit alwayes pining,
You do procure me Niobes cup to fill,
My dutie yet remembred I dare prove,
Doves have no power for to exchange their Love,
