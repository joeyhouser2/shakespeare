My selfe and mine, are alwayes thine.
My care to have my blooming Rose not wither,
Selfe-loving Enuie shall it not denie,
And that base weed thy growth doth seeke to hinder,
Mine hands shall pull him up immediatly,
Are they not enuious monsters in thine eie,
Alwayes with vaine occasions to inclose
Thine ever growing beautie, like the Rose?
