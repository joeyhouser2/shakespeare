Twelve men in number entred the vale of Avalon:
Joseph of Arimathea was the chiefest we confesse,
Josue the sonne of Joseph his father did attend on,
With other ten, these Glaston did possesse,
Hilarius the Nephew of Joseph first begate
Josue the Wise: Josue Aminadab,
Aminadab Castellors had by fate:
Castellors got Manael that lovely Lad,
And Manael by his wife had faire-fac'd Lambard▪
With another deare sonne surnamed Urlard;
And Lambard at the length begot a sonne,
That had I•rene bo•ne of his wife,
Of this Igrene, Uter the great P•ndragon
Begot King Arthur famous in his life▪
Where by the truth this Pedigree d•th end,
Arthur from Josephs loynes did first descend.
Peter Cousin to Joseph of Arimathea,
Being sometimes King of great Arcadia,
Begat Erlan that famous worthy Prince,
And Erlan gat Melianus, that did conuince
His neighbour foes, Melianus, did beget
Edor, and Edor Lothos name did set,
That tooke to wife the sister of King Arthur▪
A Virgine faire, chaste, lovely, and most pure,
Of whom this Lotho had foure lovely boyes,
Their fath•rs comfort and their mothers joyes▪
Walwanus, Agranaius, Garelus and Guerelise,
That in their countrey much did soveragnize:
All which were men of great authoritie▪
And famous in the land of Britaine.
