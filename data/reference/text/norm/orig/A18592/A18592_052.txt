I had rather love, though in vaine that face,
Then have of any other grace,
I now do wish my love should be relieved,
Had I my thoughts in compasse of my will,
Rather then live and surfeit being grieved,
Love in my breast doth wondrous things fulfill,
Though loves unkindnesse many men do kill,
In her I trust, that is my true sworne lover,
Vaine he doth write that doth her vertues smother.

That she is faire, Nature her selfe alloweth,
Face full of beauty, eyes resembling fire,
Then my pure hart to love thy hart still voweth,
Have me in favour for my good desire,
Of holy love, Loves Temple to aspire;
An• but thee my thoughts will here require,
Other sweet motions now I will conceale
Grace these rude lines that my hearts thoughts reveale,
