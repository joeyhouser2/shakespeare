Lvte arise and charme the aire,
Until a thousand formes she beare,
Coniure them all that they repaire,
Into the circles of her eare,
Ever to dwell in concord there,

By this thy tunes may have accesse,
Even to her spirit whose flowring treasure,
Doth sweetest Harmonie expresse,
Filling all eares and hearts with pleasure
On earth, obseruing heavenly measure,
Right well can she Judge and defend them,
Doubt not of that for she can mend them.
