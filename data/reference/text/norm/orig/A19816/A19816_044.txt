Reade in my face a volume of dispayres,
The wailing Iliads of my tragicke woe;
Drawne with my blood, and printed with my cares,
Wrought by her hand that I have honour'd so.
Who whilst I burne, she sings at my soules wrack,
Looking aloft from Turret of her pride:
There my soules Tyrant joyes her, in the sack
Of her own seate, whereof I made her guide.
There do these smoakes that from affliction rise,
Serve as an incense to a cruell Dame;
A sacrifice thrice-gratefull to her eyes,
Because their powre serve to exact the same.
Thus ruines she (to satisfie her will,)
The Temple, where her name was honour'd still.
