O Whether (poore forsaken) wilt thou go,
To go from sorrow, and thine own distresse,
When every place presents like face of woe,
And no remove can make thy sorrowes lesse?
Yet go (forsaken,) leave these woods, these playnes,
Leave her and all, and all for her that leaves
Thee and thy love forlorne, and both disdaines;
And of both, wrongfull deemes, and ill conceaves.
Seeke out some place, and see if any place
Can give the least release unto thy griefe:
Conuay thee from the thought of thy disgrace,
Steale from thy selfe, and be thy cares own thiefe.
But yet what comfort shall I heereby gaine?
Bearing the wonnd, I needs must feele the paine.
