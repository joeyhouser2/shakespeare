GO wayling verse, the Infants of my love,
Minerua-lyke, brought foorth without a mother:
Present the Image of the cares I prove,
Witnes your Fathers griefe exceedes all other.
Sigh out a story of her cruell deedes,
With inter-rupted accents of dispaire:
A monument that whosoever reedes,
May justly praise, and blame my lovelesse Fayre.
Say her disdaine hath dryed up my blood,
And starved you, in succours still denying:
Presse to her eyes, importune me some good,
Waken her sleeping pitty with your crying,
Knock at her hard hart, beg till you have mou'd her,
And tell th'unkind, how decrely I have lou'd her.
