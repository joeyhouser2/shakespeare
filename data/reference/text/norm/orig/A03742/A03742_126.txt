Clinking of fetters would such musick crave.
•tink, and close ayre away my life it weares.
Poore innocence is all the hope I have.
Rain, winde, or wether judge I by mine eares.
Malice assaultes, that righteousnesse should have.
Sure am I, Brian, this wound shall heale again:
But yet alas, the skarre shall still remain.
