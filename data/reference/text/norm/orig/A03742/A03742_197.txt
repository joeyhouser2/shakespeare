Death and the king, did as it were contend,
Which of them two bare Denny greatest love.
The king to shew his love gan farre extende,
Did him aduaunce his betters farr• above.
Nere place, much welth, great honor eke him gave,
To make it known what powre gret princes have.
But when death came with his triumphant gift,
From worldly cark he quite his weried ghost,
Free from the corps, and straight to heaven it li•t,
Now deme that can who did for Denny most.
The king gave welth but fading and unsure,
Death brought him blisse that ever shall endure.
