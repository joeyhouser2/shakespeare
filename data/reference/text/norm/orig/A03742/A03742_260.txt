AH libertie now have I learnd to know,
By lacking thee what Jewell I possest,
When I received first from Cupids bow
The deadly wound that festreth in my brest.
So farre (alas) forth strayed were mine eyes,
That I ne might refraine them backe, for lo:
They in a moment all earthly thinges despise,
In heavenly sight now are they fixed so.
What then for me but still with mazed sight,
To wonder at that excellence divine:
Where love (my freedome having in despight)
Hath made me thrall through errour of mine eyen.
For other guerdon hope I not to have,
My foltring toonge so basheth ought to crave.
