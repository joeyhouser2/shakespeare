What harder is then stone• what more then water soft?
Yet with soft water drops• hard stones be pers•d softe.
What geves so strong impulse,
That stone ne may withstand•
What geves more weake repulse.
Then water p•est with hand:
Yet weke though water be,
It holowith hardest flint:
By proofe wherof we see,
Time geves the greatest dint.
