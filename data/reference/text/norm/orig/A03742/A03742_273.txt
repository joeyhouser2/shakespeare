What one art thou, thus in torn weed yclad?
Uertue, in price whom auncient sages had.
why, poorely rayd? For fading goodes past care.
Why doublefaced? I marke ech fortunes fare.
This bridle, what? Mindes rages to restrain.
Tooles why beare you? I love to take great pain•
Why, winges? I teache above the starres to flye.
Why tread you death? I only cannot dye•
