IN court to serve decked with fresh aray,
Of sugred meates feling the swete repast:
The life in bankets, and sundry kindes of pla•,
Amid the presse of worldly lookes to waste,
Hath with it joynde oft times such bitter taste.
That who so joyes such kinde of life to hold,
In prison joyes fettred with cheines of gold.
