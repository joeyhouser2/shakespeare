The Cowerd oft whom deinty viandes fed,
That bosted much his ladies eares to please,
By helpe of them whom under him he led
Hath reapt the palme the valiance could not cease.
The unexpert that shores unknowen neare sought,
whom Neptune yet apaled not with feare:
In wandring shippe on trustles seas hath tought
The skill to fele that time to long doth leare.
The s•orting knight that sco•neth Cupides kinde,
With fained chere the pained cause to brede:
In game unhides the leden sparkes of minde,
And gaines the gole, where glowing flames shold spede,
Thus I see proofe that trouth and manly hart
May not availe, if fortune chaunce to start.
