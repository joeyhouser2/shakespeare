What shall I say for that is not in sight?
The hidden partes I judge them by the rest.
And if I were the forman of the quest,
To geve a verdite of her beauty bright,
For geve me Phebus, thou shouldst be dispossest,
Which doest usurpe my ladies place of right.
Here will I cease lest enuy cause dispite.
But nature when she wrought so faire a wight,
In this her worke she surely dyd entende,
To frame a thing that God could not amende.
