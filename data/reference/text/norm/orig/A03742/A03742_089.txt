The enmy of life, decayer of all kinde,
That with his cold withers away the grene:
This other night, me in my bed did finde:
And offerd me to ryd my fever clene.
And I dyd graunt: so did dispaire me blinde.
He drew his bow•, with arrowes sharpe and kene:
And strake the place, where love had hit before:
And drave the first dart deper more and more.
