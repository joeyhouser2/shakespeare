Who thinks that sorrowes felte, desires hidden,
Or humble faith in constant honor arm'd,
Can keepe love from the friut that is forbidden,
Who thinks that change is by entreatie charm'd▪
Looking on me let him know loves delights
Are treasures hid in caves, but kept by Sprights.
