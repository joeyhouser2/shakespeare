Earth with her flowers shall sooner heau'n adorne,
Heaven her bright stars through earths dim globe shall move,
Fire heate shall loose and frosts of flames be borne,
Ayre made to shine as blacke as hell shall prove:
Earth, heaven, fire, ayre, the world transform'd shall vew,
E're I prove false to faith, or strange to you.
