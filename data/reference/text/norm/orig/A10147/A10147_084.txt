Who sees the yll, and seekes to shun the same,
Shall doutlesse win at length immortal fame:
For wisdome, vice and vertue doth perceave,
She vertue takes, but vice she seekes to leave.
A wise man knowes the state of each degree,
The good be praysde, the evill dishonord be:
He sees the good, the evill he doth espye,
He takes the good, the evill he doth deny•:
He folowes good, the evill he doth eschue,
He leapes the lake, when others stay to vew▪
His honor stands, his same doth ever last,
Upon the earth when breathing breath is past:
As Solomon whose wisdome recht unto the lofty skye,
And David King, their prayses live (though bodies t•mbed lye)
They saw the good, the evill they did eschue,
Their honor lives, the proofe affirmes it true:
Then sithe examples playnly, showes the same,
Their prayses live who seekes to merit fame.
