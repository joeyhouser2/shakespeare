TO seeme for to revenge, each wrong in hastie wise,
By proofe we see of gyltlesse men, it hath not bin the guise:
In slaunders lothsome brute, when they condemned be,
With rageles moodes they suffer wronge, when truth shall try them free:
These are the pacient panges, that pas within the brest
Of those that feele their case by mine, where wrong hath right opprest:
I know how by suspect, I have been judged away,
And graunted gyltie in the thing, that clearly I denye.
My fayth may me defende, if I might levid be,
God judge me so, as from that gylte I know me to be free:
I wrought but for my freend, the greefe was all mine own,
As if the troth were truely tryde, by prooft it might be knowne.
Yet are there such that say, they •an my meaning deeme,
Without respect to this olde troth• things prove not as they seeme:
Wherby it may befall, in judgment to be quicke,
To make them be suspecte therwith, that needeth not to kicke:
Yet in resisting wrong, I would not have it thought,
I do accuse as though I knew, by whom it may be wrought:
If any such there be, that here withall be vext,
It were their vertue to beware, and deeme me better nexte.
