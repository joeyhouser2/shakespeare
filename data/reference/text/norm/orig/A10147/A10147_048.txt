I Spake when I ment not, in speeding to gayne,
I sought, when I sped not, but travaild in vayne:
I found where I feard not, would writh with the wind,
I loste where I lou'd not, nor for••d to finde.
Nothing in which, truth is not trustie,
But double is such, and beauty but rustie:
I coole with the colde, I leve that I like not,
I know not the olde, that rotteth and ripes not.
I savor no such, that fondly doth favor,
I care not to much, for such sory savor:
I taste or I try, in parte or in all,
I care not a flye, the losse is but small.
I labor at leasure, I pricke without payne,
In using for pleasure, beates in my brayne:
I spare not in byrding, to beat well the bush,
Nor leave not in stryking, as long as they rush.
I try ere I trust, nought wasting but winde,
Before I finde just, they know not my minde:
I jet not with Geminie, nor tarry not with Tawre
In bluttring who bleares me? I leave them with Law••.
For fier who fyndeth, in burning to bight,
The wise man he warneth, to leape from the light:
For seeing the weede, and losing from bandes,
The plowing in Sea, and sowing in Sandes.
