Not she for whom prowde Troy did fall and burne,
The Greekes eke slaine, that bluddy race did runne:
Nor she for spight that did Acteon turne,
Into an Hart her beauty coye did shunne:
Nor she whose blud upon Achilles Tombe,
Whose face would tame a Tygars harte:
Nor she that wan by wise of Paris dome.
Th'apple of Golde for Beauty to her parte:
Nor she whose eyes did pearce true Troylus brest,
And made him yeeld, that knew in love no law,
Might be compared to the fayrest and the best,
Whom Nature made to keepe the rest in awe:
For Beauties sake, sent downe from Jove above,
Thrise happy is he, that can attayne her love.
