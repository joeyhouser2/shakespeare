Some women fayne that Paris was,
The falsest lover that could be:
Who for his (life) did nothing passe,
As all the world might playnly see:
But ventred life and limmes and all,
To keepe his freend from Greekish thrall:
With many a broyle he dearely bought,
His (Hellen) whom he long had sought.
For first (Dame Venus) graunted him,
A gallant gifte of Beauties fleece:
Which boldely for to seeke to win,
By surging Seas he sayld to Greece:
And when he was arrived theare,
By earnest sute to win his Deare:
No greater paynes might man endure,
Then Paris did, for Hellen sure.
Besides all this when they were well,
Both he, and she, arry•'d at Troy:
Kinge Menelaus wrath did swell,
And swore, by sword, to rid their joyes:
And so he did for ten yeres space,
He lay before the Troyans face:
With all the hoste that he could make,
To be reveng'd for Hellens sake.
Loe? thus much did poore Paris bide,
Who is accounted most vatrue:
All men be false it hath bin said,
They thinke not what they speake (say you)
Yes Paris spoke, and sped with speede,
As all the heavenly Gods decreed:
And proou'd himselfe a Lover just,
Till stately Troy was turnd to dust:
I do not reade of any man,
That so much was unfaythfull found:
You did us wrong, t'accuse us than,
And say our freendship is not sound:
If any fault be found at all,
To womens lot it needes must fall:
If (Hellen) had not bin so light,
Sir Paris had not died in fight.
The falsest men I can excuse,
That ever you in stories reade:
Therfore all men for to accuse,
Me thinkes it was not well decreede:
It is a signe you have not tride,
What stedfastnesse in men doth bide:
But when your time shall try them true,
This judgment then, you must renue.
I know not every mans devise,
But commonly they stedfast are:
Though you do make them of no price,
They breake their vowes but very rare:
They will performe their promis well,
And specially where love doth dwell:
Where freendship doth not justly frame,
Then men (forsooth) must beare the blame.
