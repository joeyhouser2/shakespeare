You graves of grisly ghosts
Your charge fron coffins send
Fron roring rout in Plutoes costs
You Furies up ascend.

You trampling steades of Hell
Come teare a wofull wight,
Whose haples hap no tonge can tell
Ne pen can well endight.

I hate this lothsome life
O Atropos draw nie,
Untwist the thred of mortall strife
Send death and let me die.

For Beauties taynted trope
Hath made my cares assay,
And ficklenes with her did cope:
To fordge my whole decaye.

My fayth alas I gave
To wight of Cressids kinde,
For stedfast love I love did crave
As curtesy doth binde.

She likewise troth doth plight
To be a constant love,
And prove her self even maugre spight
A faythfull turtle Dove.

Ne will, no wo, or smart
Could minde from purpose fet,
But that I had a Jasons harte
The golden fleese to get.

Ne for my part I swere
By all the Gods above,
I never thought on other fere
Or sought for other love.

In her the like consente
I saw ful oft appear,
If eyes be judge of that it mente
Or eares have power to heare.

Yet woordes be turnd to winde
A new found gest hath got

Her freend that ment her well
Out of conceyt is quite,
While other beares away the bell
By hitting of the white.

In this our wavering age
So light are womens mindes.
As Aspen leafe that stil doth rage
Though aeole calme his windes.

No place hath due desart
No place hath constancy
In everi mood their mindes back start
As dayly we may see.

Yet women do reward

You gallant youths therfore
In time beware by me
Take heed of womens subtil lore,
