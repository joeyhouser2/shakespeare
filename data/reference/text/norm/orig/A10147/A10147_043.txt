Aske what love is? it is a passion,
Begun with rest, and pampred up in play:
Planted on sight, and nourished day by day,
With talke at large, for hope to graze upon,
It is a short joy, long sought, and soone gon:
An endles maze, wherin our willes do stray:
A gylefull gaine, repentance is the pay.
A great fier bred of small occasion,
A plague to make, our fraylty to us knowen,
Where we therby, are subiecte to their lay:
Whose fraylty ought, to leave until our stay,
In case our selves, this custome had not knowen.
Of hope and health, such creatures for to pray,
Whose glory resteth cheefly on denaye.
