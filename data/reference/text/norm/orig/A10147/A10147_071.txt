The greedy man, whose hart with hate doth swell,
Because he sees his neyghbors good estate:
Lives uncontent, with what might serve him well,
And eftsoones seemes to blame sufficient fate:
This grudging gluton glut, with goulden gayne,
To serve his use, although he hath enough:
Repines at that, which others get with payne,
So that himselfe therby, he doth abuse:
He thinkes that much, which passeth by his claw,
And findes a fault for it through luckles hap:
Although the matter valueth scarce a straw,
He deemes it small of gaine, that gives no sap.
He thinkes his store, shall serve his sen•lesse corse,
Or that no death at all, he deemes there be:
Els would he to his conscience have remorse,
And seeke to live content with his degree:
For what avayles, to horde up heapes of drosse,
Or seeke to please unsaciate fond desire:
Considering that, tis subiect unto losse,
And we (therby yll got) deserves Hell fire:
From which O Lord conduct us with thy hand,
And give us grace to live unto thy prayse:
Preserve our Queene his subiects and her land,
And graunt in peace, she raigne here Nestors dayes.
