Though Fortune cannot favor
According to my will:
The proofe of my behavor:
Shall be to love you still.

Entending not to chaunge,
Whiles that my life doth last:
But still in love to raunge:
Till youth and age be past.

Though I be far you fro,
Yet in my fantacie:
I love you and no mo:
Thinke this assuredly.

Your own both true and juste,
Alwayes you shall me finde:
Wherfore of right you must,
Have me likewise in minde.

And do not me forsake,
Though I do tarry longe:
But take me for your make,
I will not chaunge my songe.

Though absence now a while,
Do part us thus in twayne:
Thinke neither craft nor gyle,
For I will come agayne

The same man that I went.
Both in my woorde and deede:
Though some men do relent,
And grudge that I should speed.

But if you do remayne,
And do not fro me starte:
My hart you do attayne,
Till death us two depart.

And thus farewell adew,
And play an honest parte:
And chaunge me for no new,
Seeing that you have my hart.
