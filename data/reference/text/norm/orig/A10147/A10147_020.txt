Like as the Hauke is led by lure, to draw from tree to tree,
So is my hart through force of love, where ever my body be
The Hauke to pray doth double wing, her flight is fled in vayne
I make my flight in waste of winde, my hope receyveth no gayne.
Haukes that be high it hurtes to light, two flightes without reward
My flight is two, and three againe, alas Mistresse regarde:
The Hauke brought low, is soone made high, by feeding on warme foode
Your mouthes breath settes me aloft, there is nothing so good.
Good Lady then strain forth the strings, whose tune may me revive
And with straung tongue do not prolong, my joyes thus to deprive.
Within your brest my hart is hid, your will and it is one,
Regard my smart, the cure is yours, and losse, when I am gone.
Thus all your own, I recommend me wholly to your grace,
As seemeth you best for to reward, my plight and wofull case.
Which plight if you do counterpaise, with joyes, as doth belonge,
My hart for joy would tune accorde, to singe some pleasant songe.
