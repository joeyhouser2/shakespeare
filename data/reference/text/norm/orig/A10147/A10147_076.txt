Who goes to gaze of every gallant girle,
And castes his eyes at every glauncing gloze:
Whose masking minde, with every motion moou'd,
In fine shall finde, his fancy fraught with woes.

For pleasure spent, is but a wishing vayne,
By crooked chaunce, depriude of brave delight:
Cut of by care, a heape of hurtfull harmes,
Our gaze ungaynd, which whilome pleasde our sight.

Our baunts do vade, our pleasures passe away,
Our sugerest sweetes, reapes sorowing sobs in fine:
Our braggest boast, of beauties bravest blaze,
To forowed browes, doth at the length resigne.

Our foolish fancy filde, with filthy vice,
Pursues his hurt, unto anothers harmes:
A hovering hart, with every gloze enticed,
gaynes lothsome-love, whence nought but sorow swarmes.

Leave then to gaze, of every glauncing gloze,
Contemne the sleights, of beauties sugerest bate:
Whose outward sheath, with colours brave imbost,
Shuns cruell craft, and enuious hurtfull hate.
