The heat is past that did me fret,
The fier is out that nature wrought
The plantes of youth that I did set,
Are dry and dead within my thought
The Frost hath slayne the kindly sap,
That kept the hart in lively state:
The sodayne storme and thunder clap:
Hath turned love, to mortall hate.

The myst is gon that bleard mine eyes,
The lowring cloudes I see appeare,
Though that the blinde eate many flyes,
I would you knew, my sight is cleare:
Your sweete deceyving flattryng face
Did make me thinke that you were white:
I muse how you had such a grace:
To seeme a Hauke, and be a kyte.

Where precious ware is to be solde,
They shall it have, that giveth most:
All things we see, are woon with Golde,
Few things is had, where is no cost.
And so it fareth now by me,
Because I preace to give no gyftes:
She takes my sute unthankfully,
And drives me of with many dryftes.

Is this th'end of all my sute,
For my good will, to have a skorne?
Is this of all my paynes the frute,
To have the chafte in steade of corne?
Let them that lyst, posses such drosse,
For I deserve a better gayne:
Yet had I rather leave with losse,
Then serve and sue, and all in vayne.
