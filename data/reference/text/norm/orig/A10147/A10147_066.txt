MY fancy feedes, upon the sugred gaule,
My witlesse will, unwillingly workes my woe:
My carefull choyse, doth choose to keepe me thraule,
My franticke folly, fawns upon my foe:
My lust alluers, my lickering lyppes to taste,
The bayte wherin, the subtill hooke is plaste.

My hungry hope, doth heape my heavy hap,
My sundry sutes, procure my more disdayne:
My steadfast steppes, yet slyde into the trap,
My tryed truth, entangleth me in trayne:
I spye the snare, and will not backward go,
My reason yeeldes, and yet sayth ever, no.

In pleasant plat, I tread upon the snake.
My flamyng thirst, I quench with venomd Wine:
In dayntie dish, I do the poyson take,
My hunger biddes me, rather eate then pine:
I sow, I set, yet fruit, ne flowre I finde,
I pricke my hand, yet leave the Rose behinde.
