TO darkesome cave, where crawling wormes remayn.
Thou worldly wretch, resigne thy boasting breath:
Yeeld up thy pompe, thy corps must passe agayn,
From whence it came, compeld by dreadfull death.
Oh sicknesse sore, thy paines do pearce my hart,
Thou messenger of death, whose goryng gripes me greve:
Permit a while, me loth yet to depart
From freends and goods, which I behinde must leave.
Ah silly soule, entis'de with worldly vayne,
As well as thou, thy freends must yeeld to death:
Though after thee, a while they do remayne,
They shall not still, continue on the earth.
What must I then neede, shrine in gastly grave?
And leave what long, I got with tedious toyle:
Prolong me yet, and let me licence have,
Till elder yeeres, to put your Brutes to foyle.
O foolish man, allurde by lewd delight,
Thy labors lost, these goods they are not thine:
But as (thou hadst) so others have like right,
(Of them) when thou, shalt up thy breath resigne.
Then farewell world, the Nurse of wicked vice,
Adue vile drosse which mooves mens mindes to ill▪
Farewell delights, which did my youth entice,
To serve as slave, unto unsatiate will.
