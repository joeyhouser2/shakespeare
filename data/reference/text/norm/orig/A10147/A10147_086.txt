Try, ere thou trust, unto a fawning freend,
Give no regard, unto his sugered wordes,
Make your account to leese, what you him lend,
For collourd craft, the smoothest speech affordes.

My selfe have tried, the trust of tatling tungs
Who paynt their prates, as though they would performe:
(The more my greefe) for they (which) whilome clungs,
Like Bees (goods lost) sole left me in the storme,

Where I was fayne, in worldly woes to wave,
And seeke releefe, of former freends, no •je:
Perforce constraynd, to seeke my selfe to save,
Or els unhelp'd, sance succor still to lye.

I made my mone, the greater was my greefe,
To him which was, as seruant to my state:
But what prevayld, by proofe I found him cheefe,
Who not of me, but on my wealth did wate.

Donec eris foelix, multos numerabis amicos,
Temporasi fuerint nubila, solus eris.
