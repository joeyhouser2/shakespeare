— False suspition of another is
A sure condemning of our own amis.
Mistrust doth treason in the truest raise,
Suspitious Romulus stain'd his walles first rear'd
With brothers bloud, whom for light leape he feard,
The jealous cuckold weares th'infamous horne,
So not in brotherhood, jealousie may be borne.
Rivalles in love will be suspitious quickly.
The Marchant traffiking abroad, suspects his wife at home
A youth will play the wanton, and a wanton prove a mome.
