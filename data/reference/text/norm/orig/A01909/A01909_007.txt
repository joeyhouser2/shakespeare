Here lyved a Ladye fayre of late, that Claudia men dyd call:
Of goodly forme, yea such a one, as farre surmounted all.
The stately Dames, that in this Courte, to showe them selves do lye,
There was not one in all the Crewe: that coulde come Claudia nye.

This said, in haste she hieth in, and there doth vengeaunce call,

This dolfull chaunce, when Paustus heard lamentynge lowde he cryes,

No Man knowes where, he is becom, some saye the wooddes he tooke,
Intendynge there to ende his lyfe, on no Man more to looke:
The Courte lamentes, the Princesse eke her selfe doth weepe for woe,
Loe, Faustus fled, and Claudia deade.
