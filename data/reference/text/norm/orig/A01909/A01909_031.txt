Not from the hye Cytherion Hyll nor from that Ladies throne
Fron whens flies forth the winged boy that makes some sore to grone.
But nearer hence this token coms, from out the Dongeon deepe,
Where never Plutto yet dyd raygne nor Proserpyne dyd sleepe.
Wheras thy faithful Seruaunt lives. whom duetie moves aryght,
To wayle that he so long doth lacke, his own deare Maystres syght.
