When Brutysh broyle, and rage of war in Clownysh harts began
When Tigres stoute, in Tanners bonde unmusled all they ran,
The Noble Sheffeyld Lord by byrth and of a courage good,
By clubbish hands, of crabbed Clowns there spent his Noble blud.
His noble byrth avayled not, his honor all was vayne,
Amyd the prease, of Mast ye Curres, the valyant Lorde was slayve.
And after such a sorte (O ruth,) that who can teares suppresse.
To thynke the Dunghyll Dogs shuld dawnt the Floure of worthynes.
Whyle as the ravenyng Wolves he prayed his gylteles lyfe to save.
A bluddy Butcher byg and blunt, a vyle unweldy knave
With beastly blow of boysterous byll at hym (O Lorde) let dryve,
And cleste his head, and said therwith shalt thou be lefte alyve?
O Lorde that I had present ben, and Hectors force withall,
Before that from his Carlysh hands, the cruell Byll dyd fall.
Then shulde that peasaunt vyle have felt the clap upon his Crowne,
That shuld have dazed his dogged hart from dryvyng Lordes adowne.
Then shuld my hands have saved thy lyfe good Lord whon deare I loved
Then shuld my hart in doutfull case, full well to the ben proved,
But all in vayne thy death I wayle, thy Corps in earth doth lye.
Thy kyng and Countrey for to serve thou dydste not feare to dye.
Farewel good Lord, thy deth bewayle all such as well the knewe,
And everye man laments thy case: and Googe thy death doth rewe.
