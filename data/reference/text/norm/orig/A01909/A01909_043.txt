This Mirrour left of this thy Byrde I fynde,
Hath not such force, to enter in the Hert,
To roote away, Unthankefulnes of minde,
As others have, the Vertues to peruert, (so prone we are to Vice:)
The Tenche by kynd, hath Salve for every Soore,
And heales the maymed Pike in his dystresse,
The Churlysh Pike fo Gentlenes therfore,
In his rewarde, doth Crueltie expresse.
His murdring mynde, his fylthy spotted fayth,
When hungre prickes to fyll his gredye Jawes,
He grypes his poore Chyrurgion unto death.
Who late to hym of lyfe was only cause.
Thy Merlians have fewe Ayryes in our ground
But Pikes have Spawnes good stoore in every Pound
