The greatest vyce that happens unto men,
And yet a vyce, that many comon have,
As aunci•nt Wryters waye with sobre Pen,
Who gave their doome, by force of wysdom grave,
The sorest mayme, the greatest evyll sure,
The vylest plague that Students can sustayne,
And that which moste doth ygnoraunce procure.
My Holmeden is to have a ronnyng Brayne,
For who is he that leades more restles lyfe,
Or who can ever lyve more yll bestead?
