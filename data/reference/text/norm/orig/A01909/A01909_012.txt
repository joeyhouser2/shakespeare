Wan Mars had moved mortall hate and forced sumysh heate
And hye Bellona had decreed, to syt with Sworde in Seate,
The Scottes untrue with fyghtynge hande, their promys to denye,
Assembled fast, and England thought, the trothe with them to trye.
Chose Musclebroughe their fyghtynge place amyd those barrayne fyelds
Their breche of fayth, there not to try with trothe, but trotheles Shyeldes
In battayle brave, and Armye strong Encamped soore they laye,
Ten Scottes to one (a dredeful thyng a dolfull fyghtyng daye.)
That Englysh men were all agaste, with quakyng staves in hande.
To se their enemyes lye so neare, and death with them to stande.
No other remedye there was, but fyght it out or flye.
And who shuld fyrst the Onset gyve, was sure therin to dye.
Thus all dismayde, and wrapt in feare with doutfull mynde they stande,
If best it be, with flyght of foote, to stryve or fyght of hande.
Till at the length, a Captayn stoute. with hawtye mynde gan speake.
O Towards all, and maydly men of Courage faynt and weake,
Unworthye com of Brutus race, is this your manhode gon,
And is there none you Dastardes all. that dare them set upon.
Then Shelley all inflamed with heate with heate of valyaunt mynde,
No Towardes we, nor maydly men, ne yet of Dastards kynde,
I wold you wyste dyd ever com, but dare be bolde to trye,
Our manhode heare, thoughe nought appeare but deth to all mens eye
And with these wordes (O noble hart) no longer there he stayde,
But forth before them all he sprang as one no whyt dismayed
With charged staffe on fomyng horse his Spurres with heeles he strykes,
And forewarde ronnes with swyftye race, among the mortall Pykes
And in this race with famous ende, to do his Countrey good,
Gave Onset fyrst upon his Foes, and lost his vitall blud.
