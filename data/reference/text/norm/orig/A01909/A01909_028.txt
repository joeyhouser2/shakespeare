Swete muse tell me, where is my hart becom.
For well I feele, it is from hence a way,
My Sences all, doth sorrow so benumme:
That absent thus, I can not lyve a Day.
I know for troth, there is a specyall Place.
Where as it most, desyreth for to be:
For Oft it leaves, me thus in Dolfull case,
And hether commes, at length a gayne to me?
Woldest thou so fayne, be tolde where is thy Hart•
Sir Foole in place, where as it shuld not be:
Tyed up so fast, that it can never starte?
Till Wysdom get, agayne thy Lybertye:
In place where thou, as safe maist dwel swet daw?
As may the harte, ly by the Lyons paw:
And where for thee, as much be sure they passe:
As dyd the master, ons for Esops Asse.
