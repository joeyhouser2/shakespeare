O Ragyng Seas, and myghty Neptunes rayn• ▪
In monstrous Hylles, that throwest thy selfe so hye,
That with thy sludes, doest beate the shores of Spayne:
And breake the Clyves, that dare thy force enuie.
Cease now thy rage, and laye thine Ire a syde,
And thou that hast, the governaunce of all,
O myghty God, graunt Wether Wynd and Tyde,
Till in my Countreye Coast, our Anker fall.
