The Muses joye, and well they may to se,
So well their laboure com to good successe,
That they sustayned long agoe in the,
Minerua smyles, Phebus can do no lesse,
But over all, they chyefly do reioyse,
That leavyng thyngs, which are but fond and vayne,
Thou dyddest chuse, (O good and happy choyse)
In sacred Scoles, thy luckye yeares to trayne,
By which thou hast obtaynde (O happy thyng)
To learne to lyve, whyle other wander wyde,
And by thy lyfe, to please the immortall kyng,
Then which so good, nothyng can be applyed,
Lawe gyves the gayne, and Physycke fyls the Purse,
Promotions hye, gyves Artes to many one,
But this is it, by which we scape the Curse,
And have the blys of God, when we be gone.
Is this but only Scriptures for to reade?
No, no. Not talke, but lyfe gyves this in deade.
