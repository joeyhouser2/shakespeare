NO vayner thing there can be found amyd this vale of stryfe,
As Auncient men reporte have made then truste uncertayne lyfe.
This trwe we dayly fynde, by proofes of many yeares,
As answered well, unto thy vertuous fame,
The gentlenes that at thy handes I founde
In staungers house, all unaquaynted I,
Good S. hath my Hart to the so bounde,
That from the can it not be forced to flye,
In pledge wherof, my seruyce here I gyve
If thou so wylte, to serve the whylst I lyve.
