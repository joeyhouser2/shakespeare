AS ofte as I remembre with my self,
The Fancies fonde, that flame by foolysh Love,
And marke the Furyes fell, the blynded elfe
And Venus she that raynes so sore above,
As ofte as I do se the wofull state,
Of Lovers all, and eake their myserye,
The ones desyryng mynde the others hate,
Trothe with the one, with the other Trecherye,
So ofte saye I, that blessed is the wyght,
Yea Nevell blest, and double blest agayne,
That can by reason rule his mynde a ryght,
And take such foolysh fadynge toyes for vayne.
