The Paynes that all the Furyes fell can cast fron Lymbo lake,
Each Torment of those Hellish brains where crawleth mani a snake,
Each mischiefe that therrin doth lye each smart that may be founde,
Flye fron those feendish clawe• a whyle with flames breake up the grounde,
Lyght here upon this cursed hand, make here your dwellyng place,
And plague the part, the durst presume his Mayster to disgrace.
Which thrust amongst a nombre of: so many princely names,
And where thy Maistres had her place amongst the chiefest Dames,
Durste thus presume to leve her there and drawe a straunger wyght,
And by thine own unhappy draught torment my pauled Spryght.
