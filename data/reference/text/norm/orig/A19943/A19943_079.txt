I Have entreated, and I have complained,
I have disprays'd, and prayse I like wise gave,
All meanes to win her Grace I tryed have,
And still I love, and still I am disdained.
So long I have my Tongue and Pen constrained,
To praise, dispraise, complaine, and pitty crave,
That now, nor Tongue, nor Pen, to me her slave
Remaines, whereby her Grace may be obtained.
Yet you (my Sighs) may purchace me releefe,
And ye (my Teares) her rocky hart may move;
Therefore my sighes sigh in her eares my greefe,
And in her Hart my Teares imprint my Love.
But cease vaine sighes, cease cease ye fruitles teares,
Teares cannot pierce her Hart, nor sighes her Eares.
