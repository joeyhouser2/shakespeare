IF my harsh humble stile, and Rimes ill dressed,
Arrive not to your worth and beautie glorious
My Muses shoulders are with weight oppressed,
And heavenly beams are o're my sight victoriou•
If these dimme colours have your worth expressed
Laide by Loves hand, and not by Arte laborious
Your Sun-like raies have my wits haruest blesse•
Ennabling me to make your praise notorious.
But if alas! (alas the heavens defend it,)
My lines your eies, my love your hart displeasing
Breed hate in you, and kill my hope of easing;
