SWEETE, if you like and love me still,
And yeelde me love for my good will.
And do not from your promise start,
When your faire hand gave me your hart.
If deere to you I be,
As you are deere to me.
Then yours I am, and will be ever,
Nor time, nor place my love shall sever,
But faithfull still I will persever,
Like constant marble stone,
Loving but you alone.

But if you favour moe than me,
(Who love thee still, and none but thee.)
If others do the Haruest gaine
That's due to me for all my paine.
If that you love to range,
And oft to choppe and change:
Then get you some new-fangled Mate,
My doating Love shall turne to Hate,
Esteeming you (though too too late)
Not worth a pebble stone,
Loving not me alone.
