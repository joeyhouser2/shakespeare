Your Presence breedes my anguish,
Your absence makes me languish:
Your sight with woe doth fill me,
And want of your sweete sight alas doth kill me.

If those deere Eyes that burne me,
With milde aspect you turne me,
For life my weake hart panteth:
If frowningly, my Spirit and Life-blood fainteth.

If you speake kindly to me,
Alas, kind words undoo me:

Thus Sunne, nor shade doth ease me,
Nor speach, nor silence please me:
Favours and frownes annoy me,
Both want and plentie equally destroy me.
