Jo•ntly to poure on me their worst disgrace;
So I be graeious in your heavenly Face,
I wey not Fates, nor Starres, nor Fortunes yre.
T'is not the influence of Heavens Fire,
Hath power to make me blessed in my Race,
Nor in my happinesse hath Fortune place,
Nor yet can Fate my poore lifes date expyre.
T'is your faire Eyes (my Starres) all blisse do give,
Tis your disdaine (my Fate) hath power to kill,
T'is you (my Fortune) make me happy live,
Though Fortune, Fate, and Stars conspyre mine ill.
Then (blessed Saint) into your favour take me,
•ortune, nor Fate, nor Stars can wretched make me.
