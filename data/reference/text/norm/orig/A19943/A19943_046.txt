Dare not in my masters bosome rest,
That flaming Etna would to Ashes burne me:
•or dare I harbour in his Mistris brest,
•he frosty Clymate into yce would turne me:
So, both from her and him I do retyre me,
Lest th'one should freeze me, and the other fire me.

Wing'd with true Love, I flie to this sweet Brest,
Whose Snow, I hope, will cool but t'yce not turn me:
Where fire and snow, I trust, so tempred rest,
•s gentle heate will warme, and yet not burne me:
But (O deere Brest) from thee Ile ne're retire me,
Whether thou cool, or warm, or freeze, or fire me.
