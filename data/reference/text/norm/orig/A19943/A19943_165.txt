When as the Sun eclipsed is, some say,
It thunder, lightning, raine, and wind portende•
And not unlike but such things happen may,
Sith like effects my Sun eclipsed sendeth.
Witnes my throat made hoars with thundring crie
And hart with loves hot flashing lightnings fire
Witnes the showers which stil fal from mine eie
And brest with sighs like stormy winds neare rive•
Shine then once againe, sweete Sun on me,
And with thy beames dissolve clouds of dispair
Whereof these raging Meteors framed be,
In my poore hart by absence of my faire,
So shalt thou prove thy Beames, thy heate, thy ligh•
To match the Sun in glory, grace, and might.
