Only (sweet love) afforde me but thy hart,
Then close thine eies within their ivory cover
That they to me no beame of light impart,
Although they shine on all thy other lovers.
As for thy lip of ruby, cheeks of rose,
Though I have kist them oft with sweet Content

If thy sweet will will bar me, I assent.
Let me not touch thy hand, but through thy glove,
Nor let it be the pledge of kindnes more;
Keepe all thy beauties to thy selfe, sweet love,
I aske not such bold favours as before.
I beg but this, afforde me but thy hart,
