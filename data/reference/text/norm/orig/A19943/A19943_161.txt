Pale Death himselfe did Love my Philomel,
When he her Vertues and rare beutie saw:
Therefore he sicknesse sent, which should expell,
His Rivall life, and my Decre to him draw.
But her bright beauty dazeled so his Eyes,
That his dart life did misse, though her it hitt:
Yet not therewith content, new meanes he tries,
To bring her unto Death, and make life flitt.
But Nature soone perceiving, that he meant
To spoyle her only Phoenix, her chiefe pride:
Assembled all her force, and did prevent
The greatest mischiefe that could her betide.
So both our lives and loves Nature defended,
For had she dide, my love and life had ended.
