Bvt if my Lines may not be held excused,
Nor yet my Love finde favour in your Eyes,
But that your Eyes as Judges shall be used,
Even of the fault which fron themselves doth rise,
Yet this my humble suite do not despise,
Let me be judged as I stand accused,
If but my fault my doome do equalise,
What er'e it be, it shall not be refused.
And since my Love already is expressed,
And that I cannot stand upon deniall,
I freely put my selfe upon my triall,
Let Justice doome me as I have confessed.
For in my Doome if Justice be regarded,
My Love with Love againe shall be rewarded.
