What can I now suspect? or, what can I feare any longer?
Oft did I feare, oft hope, whil'st life in Sidney remained.
Of nothing can I now despaire, for nought can I hope for;
This good is in misery, when great extreamitie grieves us,
That neither hope of good, nor feare of worse can affright us.
And can I then complaine, when no complaint can availe me?
How can I seeme to be discontent, or what can I weepe for?
He lives eternall, with endlesse Glorie bedecked:
Yea still on earth he lives, and still shall live by the Muses.
