OF Atreus Sonnes faine would I write,
And faine of Cadmus would I sing:
My Lute is set on Loves delight,
And only Love sounds eu'ry string.

Of late my Lute I alt'red quite,
Both frets and strings for tunes above,
I sung of fierce Alcides might,
My Lute would sound no tune but Love,
Wherefore ye worthles all farewell,
No tune but Love my Lute can tell.
