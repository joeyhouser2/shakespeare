The wretched life I live
In my weake Sences such confusion maketh,
That like th'accursed Rabble
That built the Towre of Babble,
My wit mistaketh,
And unto nothing a right name doth give.

I terme her my deere love, that deadly hates m•,
My cheefest Good, her that's my cheefest evill;
Her Saint and Goddesse, who's a Witch, a Devill;
Her my sole Hope, that with despaire amates me,
My Balme I call her, that with poyson fills m••
And her I terme my life, that daily kills me.
