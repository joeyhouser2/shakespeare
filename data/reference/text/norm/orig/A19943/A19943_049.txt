O Hand of all handes living,
The softest, moistest, whitest,
•ore skild then Phoebus on a Lute in running;
•ore then Minerua, with a Needle cunning;
Then Mercury more wily,
In stealing Harts most sliely.
Since thou, deere Hand, in theft so much delightest,
Why fall'st thou now a giving?
Ay me! thy gifts are thefts, and with strange Art,
In giving me thy Glove, thou stealst my Hart.
