IF Love conioyn'd with worth and great desarte,
Merit like love in every noble minde:
Why then do I you still so cruell finde,
To whom you do such praise of worth imparte?
And if (my Deere) you speake not from your harte,
Two haynous wrongs you do together binde:
To seeke with glozing words mine eies to blinde,
And yet my Love with hateful deedes to thwarte.
To want what one deserves, engreeves his paine,
Because it takes away all selfe-accusing,
And under kindest words to maske disdaine,
Is to a vexed Soule too much abusing.
•hen ift be false, such glosing words refraine,
〈◊〉 true, O then let worth his due obtaine!
