What mou'd me then? say Love, for thou canst tel;
Of thee I learn'd this skill, if skill I have:
Thou knowst the Muse, whose help I alwais crave
Is none of those that on Parnassus dwell.
My Muse is such as doth them all excell,
They all to her alone their cunning gave,
To sing, to dance, to play, to make so brave;
Thrice threefold Graces her alone befell.
From her do flow the streames that water me,
Hers is the praise, if I a Poet be;
Her only looke both will and skill doth give.
What maruaile then if I those lawes refuse,
Which other Poets in their making use,
Since by her lookes I write, by which I live?
