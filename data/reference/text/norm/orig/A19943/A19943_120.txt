MY wanton Muse that whilome wont to sing,
Faire Beauties praise and Venus sweet delight,
Of late had chang'd the tenor of her string,
To higher tunes then serve for Cupids fight:
Shril Trumpets sound, sharpe Swords and Lance strong
Warre, bloud, and death, were matter of her song

The God of Love by chance had heard thereof,
That I was prou'd a Rebell to his Crowne,
Fit words for Warre, quoth he, with angry skoff,
A likely man to write of Marses frowne:
Well are they sped whose praises he shall write,
Whose wanton Pen can nought but Love indit

This said, he whiskt his parti-coulor'd wings,
And down to earth he comes more swift then thog
Then to my hart in angry haste he flings,
To see what change these news of wars had wroght
He pries, and lookes, he ransacks eu'ry vaine,
Yet findes he nought, save love, and Lovers pain

Then I that now perceiu'd his needles feare,
With heavy smile began to pleade my cause:
In vayne (quoth I) this endlesse greefe I beare,
In vaine I strive to keepe thy greevous Lawes,
If after proofe so often trusty found,
Uniust Suspect condemne me as unsound.

Is this the guerdon of my faithfull hart?
Is this the hope on which my life is staide?
Is this the ease of never-ceasing smart?
Is this the price that for my paines is paid?
Yet better serve fierce Mars in bloody field,
Where death, or conquest, end or joy doth yeeld.

Long have I seru'd: what is my pay but payne?
Oft have I sude: what gaine I but delay?
My faithfull love is quited with disdaine,
My greefe a game, my pen is made a play:
Yea, Love that doth in other favour find,
In me is counted madnes out of kind.

And last of all, but greevous most of all,
Thy selfe, sweet Love, hath kild me with suspect;
Could Love beleeve, that I from Love would fall?
•s warre of force to make me Love neglect?
No, Cupid knowes, my mind is faster set,
Then that by war I should my Love forget.

My Muse indeed to War enclines her minde,
The famous Actes of worthy Brute to write:
To whom the Gods this Ilands rule assignde,
Which long he soughtby seas throgh Neptunes spight
With such conceits my busie head doth swel,
But in my hart nought els but Love doth dwell.

And in this warre thy part is not the least,
Heere shall my Muse Brutes noble Love declare:
Heere shalt thou see the double Love increast,
Of fayrest Twins that ever Lady bare:
Let Mars triumph in Armour shining bright,
His conquerd Armes shall be thy triumphs light.

As he the world, so thou shalt him subdue,
And I thy glory through the world will ring:
So be my paines, thou wilt vouchsafe to rue,
And kill despayre: With that he whisk't his wing,
And bade me write, and promist wished rest,
But sore I hope false hope will be the best.
