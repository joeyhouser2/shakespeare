How can my Love in equitie be blamed,
Still to importune though it ne'r obtayne;
Since though her face and voice will me refraine,
Yet by her Voyce and Face I am inflamed?
For when (alas) her face with frownes is framed,
To kill my Love, but to revive my payne;
And when her voice commands, but all in vayne,
That love both leave to be, and to be named.
Her Syren voyce doth such enchantment move,
And thogh she frown, eu'n frowns so lovely make her.
That I of force am forced still to love;
Since then I must, and yet can not forsake her,
My fruitles praiers shall cease in vaine to move her,
But my devoted Hart ne're cease to move her.
