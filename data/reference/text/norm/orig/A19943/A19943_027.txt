SORROW seldome killeth any,
Sodaine Joy hath murthered many.
Then (Sweete) if you would end me,
Tis a fond course with lingring griefe to spend met.
For, quickly to dispatch me,
•our only way is, in your armes to catch me,
And give me a sweete Kisse:
•or such excessive and unlookt for blisse,
Would so much over-joy me,
As it would strait destroy me.
