IF your fond Love want worth and great desarte,
Then blame your selfe, if you me cruell finde:
If worth alone move every noble minde,
Why to no worth should I my love imparte?
And if the lesse to greeve your wounded harte,
I seeke your dazled eies with words to blinde,
To just disfavour I great favor binde,
With deeds, and not with words your love to thwarte
The freeing of your minde from selfe-accusing,
By granting your deserts should ease your paine
And since your fault's but love, t'were some abu• sin
With bitter words t'enuenom just disdaine.
Then ift be true, all glozing I refraine;
If false, why should no worth, worths due obtaine
