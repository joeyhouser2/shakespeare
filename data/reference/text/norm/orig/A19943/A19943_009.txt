SWEETE, I do not pardon crave,
Till I have,
By deserts, this fault amended:
This, I only this desire,
That your ire
May with penance be suspended.

Not my Will, but Fate did fetch
Me poore wretch,
Into this unhappy error.
Which to plague, no Tyrants minde
Paine can finde,
Like my hearts selfe-guiltie terror.
