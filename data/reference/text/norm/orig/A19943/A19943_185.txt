Were I as base as is the lowly playne,
And you (my Love,) as high as heau'n above,
Yet should the thoughts of me your humble swaine,
Ascend to Heaven, in honour of my Love.
Were I as hight as Heau'n above the playne,
And you (my Love) as humble and as low
As are the deepest bottoms of the Mayne,
Wherso'ere you were, with you my Love should go
Were you the Earth (deere Love) and I the skies,
My love should shine on you like to the Sun,
And looke upon you with ten thousand Eyes,
Till heau'n wax't blind, and til the world were dun
Whereso'ere I am, below, or els above you,
Whereso'ere you are, my hart shall truly love you
