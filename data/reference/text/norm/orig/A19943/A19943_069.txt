Let not (sweet Saint) let not these lines offend you,
Nor yet the Message that these lines imparte;
The Message my unfained Love doth send you,
Love, which your selfe hath planted in my harte.
For beeing charm'd by the bewitching arte
Of those inveagling graces which attend you,
Loves holy fire makes me breathe out in parte,
The never-dying flames my brest doth lend you.
Then if my Lines offend, let Love be blamed.
And if my Love displease, accuse mine Eies,
If mine Eies sinne, their sinnes cause only lies
On your brite eies, which have my hart inflamed
Since eies, love, lines, erre then by your direction;
Excuse mine Eies, my Lines, and my Affection.
