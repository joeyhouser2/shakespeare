O Why did Fame my Hart to Love betray,
By telling my Deares vertue and perfection?
Why did my Traytor Eares to it conuay,
That Syren-song cause of my Harts infection,
Had I bene deafe, or Fame her gifts concealed,
Then had my Hart been free from hopeles Love:
Or were my state likewise by it revealed,
Well might it Philomel to pitty move.
Then shold she kno how love doth make me languish,
Distracting me twixt hope and dreadfull feare:
Then shold she kno my care, my plants, and anguish
All which for her deere sake I meekely beare.
Yea I could quietly deaths paynes abide,
So that she knew that for her sake I dide.
