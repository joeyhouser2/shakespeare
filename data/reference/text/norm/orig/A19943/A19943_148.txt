Begin, and halfe is done, yet halfe undone remaines,
Begin that half, and all is done, and thou art easd of pains
The second halfe is all, when halfe thereof is dun,
The other halfe is all again, new work must be begun
Thus he that stil begins, doth nothing but by halves,
And things half done, as good undone, half oxen are but calves.
