YE walles that shut me up from sight of men,
Inclosde wherein, alive I buried lie.
And thou, sometime my bed, but now my den,
Where, smothred up the light of Sunne, I flie.
O shut your selves, ech chinke and crevis straine,
That none but you may heare me thus complain.

My hollow cries that beate thy stony side,
Vouchsafe to beate, but beate them backe againe,
That when my griefe hath speech to me denide,
Mine eares may heare the witnes of my paine.
As for my Teares, whose streames must ever last
My silent cowch shall drinke them up as fast.
