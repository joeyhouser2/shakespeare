When you that Scroule restor'de me,
With grateful words, kind grace, and smiling merrily
My brest did swell with joy, supposing verily,
You, answer did afford me.
But finding only that I writt,
I hop't to finde my Hart in it:
But you my hope abused had,
And poison of Dispaire in stead thereof infused had

Why, why did you torment me,
With giving back my humble Rymes so hatefully
You should have kept both hart and paper gratefully;
Or both you should have sent me.
Hope you my Hart thence to remove
By scorning me, my Lines, my Love?
No, no; your hope abused is,
Too deepe to be remou'd it in your Brest infused is

O shall I hide or tell it?
Deere with so spotlesse, zealous, firme Affection,
I love your Beauty, Vertue, and perfection,
As nothing can expell it.
Scorne you my Rimes, my Love despight?
Pull out my Hart, yea kill me quite
Yet will your hate abused be,
For in my very soule, your love and lookes infused be.
