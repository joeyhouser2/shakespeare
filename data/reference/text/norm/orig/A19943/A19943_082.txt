Like a Sea-tossed Barke with tackling spent,
And Starres obscur'd his watry jornies guide
By lowd tempestuous windes and raging tide,
From wave to wave with dreadfull fury sent,
Fares my poore Hart; my Hart-strings being rent,
And quite disabled your fierce wrath to bide,
Since your faire eies my Stars thenselves do hide
Clouding their light in frownes and discontent.
For from your frowns do spring my sighes and teares
Teares flow like seas, and sighes like winds do bloe
Whose joyned rage most violently beares
My Tempest-beaten hart from woe to woe.
And if your Eyes shine not that I may shun it,
On Rocke, despaire, my sighes, and teares will run it
