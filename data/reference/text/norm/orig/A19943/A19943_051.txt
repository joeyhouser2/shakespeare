Praise you those barren Rimes long since composed?
Which my great Love, her greater Cruelty,
My constant faith, her false Inconstancy,
My praiseles stile, her o're-praisd worth disclosed.
O if I lou'd a scornefull Dame so deerely;
If my wilde yeeres did yeeld so firme affection;
If her Moon-beams, short of your Suns perfection,
Taught my hoars Muse to sing (as you say) cleerly
How much, how much should I love and adore you,
(Divinest Creature) if you deign'd to love me:
What beauty, fortune, time should ever move me
In these staid yeeres to like aught els before you?
And O! how should my Muse, by you inspired,
Make Heaven and Earth resound your praise admired.
