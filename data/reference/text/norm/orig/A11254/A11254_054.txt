Praisd be Dianas faire and harmles light,
Praisd be the dewes, wherwith she moists the ground;
Praisd be her beames, the glorie of the night,
Praisd be her powre, by which all powres abound.

Praisd be her Nimphs, with whom she decks the woods,
Praisd be her knights, in whom true honor lives,
Praisd be that force, by which she moves the floods,
Let that Diana shine, which all these gives.

In heaven Queene she is among the spheares,
In ay she Mistres like makes all things pure,
Eternitie in her oft chaunge she beares,
She beautie is, by her the faire endure.

Time weares her not, she doth his chariot guide,
Mortalitie belowe her orbe is plaste,
By her the vertue of the starrs downe slide,
In her is vertues perfect image cast.

A knowledge pure it is her worth to kno,
With Circes let them dwell that thinke not so.
