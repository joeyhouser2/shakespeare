Sought by the world, and hath the world disdain'd,
Is she, my hart, for whom thou doost endure,
Unto whose grace, sith Kings have not obtaind,
Sweete is thy choise, though losse of life be sowre:
Yet to the man, whose youth such pains must prove,
No better end, than that which comes by Love.

Steere then thy course unto the port of death,
Sith thy hard hap no better hap may finde,
Where when thou shalt unlade thy latest breath,
Enuie her selfe shall swim to save thy minde,
Whose bodie sunke in search to gaine that shore,
Where many a Prince had perished before.

And yet my hart it might have been foreseene,
Sith skilfull medcins mends each kinde of griefe,
Then in my breast full safely hadst thou been,
But thou my hart wouldst never me beleeve,
Who tolde thee true, when first thou didst aspire,
Death was the end of every such desire.
