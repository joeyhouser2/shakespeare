Though neither tears nor torments can be thought,
Nor death it selfe too deere to be sustaind,
To win those joyes so woorthie to be sought,
So rare to reach, so sweete to be obtaind.

Yet earnest Love, with longing to aspire,
To that which hope holds in so high regarde,
Makes time delaid, a torment to desire,
When Love with hope forbeares his just rewarde.

Then blessed hope haste on thy happie daies,
Save my desire, by shortning thy dealaies.
