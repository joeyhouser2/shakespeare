What else is hell, but losse of blisfull heaven?
What darknes else, but lacke of lightsome day?
What else is death, but things of life bereaven?
What winter else, but pleasant springs decay?

Unrest what else, but fancies hot desire,
Fed with delay, and followed with dispaire?
What else mishap, but longing to aspire,
To strive against, earth, water, fire and aire?

Heaven were my state, and happie Sunneshine day,
And life most blest, to joy one howres desire,
Hap, blisse, and rest, and sweete springtime of May,
Were to behold my faire consuming fire.

But loe, I feele, by absence from your sight,
Mishap, unrest, death, winter, hell, darke night.
