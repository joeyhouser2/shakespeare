For pittie pretie eies surcease,
To give me warre, and graunt me peace,
Triumphant eies, why beare you Armes,
Against a hart that thinks no harmes.
A hart alreadie quite appalde,
A hart that yeelds, and is enthrald,
Kill Rebels prowdly that resist,
Not those that in true faith persist.
And conquered serve your Deitie,
Will you alas commaund me die?
Then die I yours, and death my crosse,
But unto you pertains the losse.
