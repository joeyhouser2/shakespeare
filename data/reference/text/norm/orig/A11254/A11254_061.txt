Who plucks thee down fron hie desire poor hart?
Who comforts thee in depth of thy distresse?
Amid contents, who breeds thy secret smart?
Who seekes the meane, thy sorrowes may be lesse?

Who calls thy wits togither to their worke:
Who warnes thy will, to follow warie wit?
Who lets thee see in love what sorrowes lurke?
Who makes thee feele the force of fancies fit?

Who taught thee first to trie before thou trust?
Who bids thee keepe a faithfull tried freend?
Who wils thee say, love wantons he that lust?
Who winnes the wish, that hath a happie end?

Care then to keepe, that faithfull friend in store,
Whose love commands, that thou shalt care no more.
