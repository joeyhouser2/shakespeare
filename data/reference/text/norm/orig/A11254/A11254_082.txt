Onight, O jelious night, repugnant to my pleasures,
O night so long desir'd, yet crosse to my content,
There's none but only thou that can performe my pleasures,
Yet none but only thou that hindereth my intent.

Thy beams, thy spiteful beams, thy lamps that burn to brightly,
Discover all my traines, and naked lay my drifts,
That night by night I hope, yet faile my purpose nightly,
Thy enuious glaring gleame defeateth so my shifts.

Sweet night withhold thy beams, withhold them til to morow,
Whose joyes in lack so long, a hell of torments breedes,
Sweete night, sweete gentle night, do not prolong my sorow,
Desire is guide to me, and Love no Loadstar needes.

Let Sailers gaze on stars and Moone so freshly shining,
Let them that misse the way be guided by the light,
I knowe my Ladies bowre, there needes no more divining,
Affection sees in darke, and Love hath eies by night.

Dame Cinthia couch awhile, holde in thy hornes for shining,
And glad not lowring night, with thy too glorious raies,
But be she dim and darke, tempestuous and repining,
That in her spite, my sport may worke thy endles praise.

And when my will is wrought, then Cinthia shine good Ladie,
All other nights and daies, in honour of that night,
That happie heavenly night, that night so darke and shadie,
Wherein my Love had eies, that lighted my delight.
