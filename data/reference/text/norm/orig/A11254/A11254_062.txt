Those eies that holds the hand of every hart,
Those hands that holds the hart of every eie,
That wit that goes beyond all natures Art,
That sence too deepe, for wisdome to discrie,
That eie, that hand, that wit, that heavenly sence,
All these doth show my Mistres Excellence.

Oh eies that perce into the purest hart,
Oh hands that hold, the highest harts in thrall,
Oh wit that weyes the deapth of all desart,
Oh sence that showes, the secret sweete of all,
The heaven of hevens, with hevenly powrs preserve thee,
Love but thy selfe, and give me leave to serve thee.

To serve, to live, to looke upon those eies,
To looke, to live, to kisse that heavenlie hand,

To sound that wit, that doth amaze the wise,
To know that sence, no sence can understand,
To understande that all the world may know,
Such wit, such sence, eies, hands, there are no moe.
