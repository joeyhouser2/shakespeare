Fvll fraught with unrecomptles sweete,
Of your faire face that stole mine eie,
No gladsome day my lookes did greete,
Wherein I wisht not willingly;
Mine eies were shut I might not see,
A Ladie of lesse maiestie.

What most I like, I never minde,
And so on you have fixt my thoughts,
That others sights do make me blinde,
And what I see but you is noughts;
By use and custome thus you see,
Another nature lives in me.

The more I looke, the more I love,
The more I thinke, the more I thrive,
No obiect can my looke remove,
No thought can better thoughts revive,
For what I see or thinke, I finde,
Exceedeth sight or thought of minde.

Since then your lookes, have stolne mine eies,
And eies content to nourish love,

And love doth make my thoughts arise,
And thoughts are firme, and will not move,
Vouchsafe to knit by powre unknowne,
Our eies, our loves, our thoughts in one.
