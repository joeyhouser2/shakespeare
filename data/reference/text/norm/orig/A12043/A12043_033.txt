Fvll many a glorious morning have I seene,
Flatter the mountaine tops with soveraine eie,
Kissing with golden face the meddowes greene;
Guilding pale streames with heavenly alcumy:
Anon permit the basest cloud•s to ride,
With ougly rack on his celestiall face,
And from the for-•orne world his visage hide
Stealing un•eene to west with this d•sgrace:
Even so my Sunne one early morne did shine,
With all triumphant splendor on my brow,
But out a•ack, he was but one houre mine,
The reg•on cloude hath mask'd him from me now.
Yet him for this, my love no whit disdaineth,
Suns of the world may staine, when heavens sun stainteh.
