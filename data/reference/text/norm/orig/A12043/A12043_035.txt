Roses have thornes, and silver fountaines mud,
Cloudes and eclipses staine both Moone and Sunne,
And loathsome canker lives in sweetest bud.
A•l men make faults, and even I in this,
Autho•izing thy trespas with compare,
My selfe corrupting saluing thy amisse,
Excusing their fins more then their sins are:
For to thy sensuall fault I bring in sence,
T•y aduerse party is thy Aduocate,
A•d gainst my selfe a lawfull plea commence,
Such ci•ill war is in my love and hate,
That I an accessary needs must be,
To that sweet theefe which sourely robs from me,
