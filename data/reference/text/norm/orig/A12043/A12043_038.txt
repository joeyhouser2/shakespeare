While thou dost breath that poor'st into my ver•e,
Thine own sweet argument, to excellent,
For every vulgar paper to rehearse:
Oh give thy selfe the thankes if ought in me,
Worthy perusal stand against thy sight,
For who's so dumbe that cannot write to thee,
When thou thy selfe dost give inuention light?
Be thou the tenth Muse, ten times more in worth
Then those old nine which rimers innocate,
And he that calls on thee, let him bring forth
Eternal numbers to out-live long date.
If my slight Muse do please these curious daies,
The paine be mine, but thine shall be the praise.
