IN loving thee thou know'st I am forsworne,
But thou art twice forsworne to me love swearing;
In act thy bed-vow broake and new faith torne,
In vowing new hate after new love bearing:
But why of two othes breach do I ac•use thee,
When I breake twenty: I am periur'd most,
For all my vowes are othes but to misuse thee:
And all my honest faith in thee is lost.
For I have sworne deepe o•hes of thy deepe kindnesse:
Othes of thy love, thy truth, thy constancie,
And to inlighten thee gave eyes to blindnesse,
Or made them swere against the thing they see.
For I have sworne thee faire: more periurde eye,
To swere against the truth fo foule a lie.
