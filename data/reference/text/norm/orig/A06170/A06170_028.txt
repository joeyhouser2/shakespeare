No glory makes me glorious or glad,
Nor pleasure may to pleasure me dispose,
Ne comfort can revive my sences sad,
Nor hope enfranchise me with one repose.
Nor in her absence tast I one delight,
Nor in her presence am I well content,
Was never time gave tearme to my dispight,
Nor joy that dried the teares of my lament:
Nor holde I hope of weale in memorie,
Nor have I thought to change my restlesse griefe,
Nor doth my conquest yeelde me soverainetie,
Nor hope repose, nor confidence, reliefe.
For why she sortes her frownes and favoures so,
As when I gaine or loose I cannot know:
