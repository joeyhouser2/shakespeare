My fraile and earthly barke, by reasons guide,
(Which holdes the helme, whilst will doth weilde the saile:)
By my desires (the windes of bad betide,)
Hath saild these wordly Seaes with small availe.
Vaine obiectes serve, for dreadfull Rockes to quaile,
My brittle boate from haven of life that flies
To haunt the Sea of mundane miseries:
My sowle that drawes Impressions from above,
And viewes my course, and sees the windes aspire,
Bids reason watch to scape the shoales of love:
But lawles will enflamd with endlesse ire
Doth steerem poope whilest reason doth retire.
The streames increase loves waves my barcke do fill,
Thus are they wrackt that guide their course by will.
