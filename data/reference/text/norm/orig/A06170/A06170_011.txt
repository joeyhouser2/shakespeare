The rumor runnes that heere in I sis swimme,
Such stately Swannes so confident in dying;
That when they feele them selves neere Lethes brimme,
They sing their fatall dirge when death is nighing.
And I like these that, feele my woundes are mortall,
Contented dye for her whom I adore:
And in my joyfull himnes do still exhort all,
To die for such a Saint or love no more.
Not that my torments, or her tiranie.
Inforce me to enioyne so hard a taske,
But for I know, and yeeld no reason why,
But will them trie that have desire to aske.
As love hath wreathes his pretty eies to feele,
So lovers must keepe secret what they feele
