I hope and feare, I pray and hould my peace,
Now freeze my thoughtes and straight they frie againe,
I now admire and straight my wounders cease,
I loose my bondes and yet my selfe restraine:
This likes me most that leaves me discontent,
My courage serves and yet my heart doth faile,
My will doth clime whereas my hopes are spent,
I laugh at love, yet when he comes I quaile.
The more I strive, the duller bide I still,
I would be thrald, and yet I freedome love,
I would redresse, yet hourly feede mine ill,
I would repine, and dare not once reprove,
And for my love I am bereft of power,
And strengthlesse strive my weaknes to devoure.
