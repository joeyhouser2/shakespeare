Faire eyes whilest fearefull I your faire admire,
By unexpressed sweetnes that I gaine,
My memory of sorrow doth expire,
And faulcon like I tower joyes heavens amaine
But when your sonnes in Oceans of their glory,
Shut uppe their day-bright shine, I dye for thought:
So passe my joyes as doth a new plaid storie,
And one poore sigh breaths all delight to nought.
So to my selfe I live not, but for you,
For you I live, and you I love, but none else:
Oh then faire eyes whose light I live to viewe,
Or poore forlorne despis'd to live alone els,
Looke sweete since from the pith of contemplation,
Love gathereth life, and living, breedeth passion.
