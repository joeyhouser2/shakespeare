How languisheth the Primrose of loves garden?
How trill her teares th'Elixar of my sences:
Ambitious sicknes, what doth thee so harden,
Oh spare and plague thou me for her offences.
Ah Roses, loves faire Roses do not languish,
Blush through the milk-white vaile that holdes you covered:
If heate or colde may mitigate your anguish,
He burne, He frize, but you shall be recovered.
Good God would beautie marke now she is crased,
How but one shower of sicknesse makes her tender:
Her Judgmentes then to marke my woes amazed,
To mercy should opinions fort surrender:
And I (oh would, I might, or would she ment it,)
Should herrie love, who now in hart lament it.
