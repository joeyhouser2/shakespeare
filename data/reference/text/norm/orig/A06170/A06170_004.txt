In fancies world an Atlas have I been,
Where yet the Chaos of my ceaslesse care:
Is by her eies unpitied and unseene,
In whom all giftes but pity planted are.
For mercie tho still cries my moane-clad muse,
And every paper that she sendes to beautie:
In tract of sable teares bringes wofull newes,
Of my true heartkinde thoughts and loyall duetie.
But ah the stringes of her hard heart are strained,
Beyond the harmonie of my desires:
And tho the happie heavens themselves have pained,
To tame her heart whose will so farre aspires,
Yet she who claimes the title of worldes wonder,
Thinkes all desartes to bace to bring her under.
