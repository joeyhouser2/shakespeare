Burst burst poore heart thou hast no longer hope,
Captive mine eyes unto eternall sleepe,
Let all my sences have no further scope,
Let death be lord of me and all my sheepe.
For Phillis hath betrothed fierce disdaine:
That makes his mortall mantion in her heart,
And though my tonge have long time taken paine,
To sue devorse and wed her to desart.
She will not yeeld, my wordes can have no power,
She scornes my faith, she laughes at my sad layes,
She filles my soule with never ceasing sower,
Who filt the world with volumes of her praise:
In such extreames what wretch can cease to crave
His peace from death, who can no mercy have.
