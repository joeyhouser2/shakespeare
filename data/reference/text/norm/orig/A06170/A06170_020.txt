Thou tiranizing Monarcke that dost tire,
My love-sicke heart through those assaulting eyes,
That are the lampes which lighten my desire,
If nought but death furie may suffise:
Not for my peace, but for thy pleasure be it,
That Phillis, wrathfull Phillis that repines me,
All grace but death, may daine to come and see it,
And seeing greeve, at that that which she assignes me.
This only boone for all my mortall bane,
I crave and crie for, at thy mercye seate;
That when her wrath a faithfull heart hath slaine,
And soule is fled, and body rest of heate:
She might perceive how much she might command,
That had my life, and death, within her hand.
