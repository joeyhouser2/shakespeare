Resembling none, and none so poore as I,
Poore to the world, and poore in each esteeme,
Whose first borne loves, at first obscurd did die,
And bred no fame but flame of bace misdeeme.
Under the Ensigne of whose tyred pen,
Loves legions forth have maskt, by others masked:
Thinke how I lyve wronged by ill tonged men,
Not Maister of my selfe, to all wrongs tasked.
Oh thou that canst, and she that may do all things,
Support these languishing conceits that perrish,
Looke on their growth: perhaps these sillie small things
May winne this worldly palme, so you do cherrish.
Homer hath vowd, and I with him do vowe this,
He will and shall revive, if you alowe this.
