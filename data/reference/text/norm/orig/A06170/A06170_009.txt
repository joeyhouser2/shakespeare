No starres her eyes to cleere the wandering night,
But shining sunnes of true divinitye:
That make the soule conceive her perfect light:
No wanton beauties of humanitie
Her prettie browes, but beames that cleare the sight
Of him that seekes the true Philosophie:
No Corrall is her lippe, no rose her faire,
But even that crimson that adornes the Sunne
No Nimph is she, but mistresse of the ayre,
By whom my glories are but newe begunne,
But when I touch and tast as others do,
I then shall wright and you shall wonder to.
