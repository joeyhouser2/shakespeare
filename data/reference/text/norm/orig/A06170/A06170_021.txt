Some praise the lookes, and others praise the lockes,
Of their faire Queenes, in love with curious wordes:
Some laud the breast where love his treasure locks,
All like the eie that life and love affordes.
But none of these fraile beauties and unstable
Shall make my pen ryot in pompous stile:
More greater giftes shall my grave muse enable,
Whereat severer browes shall never smile.
I praise her honny-sweeter eloquence,
Which from the fountaine of true wisdome floweth:
Her modest meane that matcheth exelence,
Her matchlesse faith which from her ver tue groweth:
And could my stile her happie vertues equale,
Time had no power her glories to enthrale.
