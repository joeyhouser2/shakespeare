The Harpye birdes, that did in such despight
Greive and ann•y old Phinëus so sore,
Were chas'd away by Calais in fight
And by his brother Zeth for evermore;
Who follow'd them, until they hard on hye
A voyce, that said, Ye Twinnes No further fly.
Phineus I am, that so tormented was;
My Laura here I may an Harpye name;
My thoughtes and lustes be Sonnes to Borëas,
Which never cea'st in following my Dame,
Till heau'nly Grace said unto me at last,
Leave fond Delightes, and say thy love is past.
My love is past I say, and sing full glad;
My time, alas, mispent in Love I rewe,
Wherein few joyes, or none at all I had,
But stoare of woes: I found the prover be true,
For eu'ry pleasure that in Love is found,
A thousand woes and more therein abound.
