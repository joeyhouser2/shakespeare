My joyfull muse shall never make an end

Nor t•o my peane shall ever cease to write
Of ought, wherin sweete Venus takes delite.
My temples hedged in with Myrtle bowes
Shall set aside Apolloes Lawrell tree,
As did Anchises sonne, when both his browes
With Myrtle he beset, to honour thee:
Then will I say, the Rose of flowres is best.
And silver Dooves for birdes excell the rest.
Ile praise no starre but Hesperus alone,
Nor any hill but Erycinus meunte,
Nor any woodde but I daly alone,
Nor any spring but Acidalian founte,
Nor any land but only Cyprus shoare,
Nor Gods but Love, and what would Venus more?
