Iwenis que castus crimine incestae jacet,
Pudicus, insons.
Diana, since Hippolytus is deade,
Let me enioy thy favour, and his place:
My might through will shall stand thee in some steade,
To drive blinde Love and Venus from thy chase:
For where they lately wrought me miekle woe,
I vow me nowe to be theire mortall foe.
And do thou not mistrust my chastetie,
When I shall raunge amidst thy virgine traine:
My raynes are chastned so through miserie,
That Love with me can nere prevaile againe:
" The childe, whose finger once hath felt the fire,
" To playe therewith will have but sinale desire.
Besides, I vow to heare a watchful eye,
Discou'ring such, as passe along thy grove;
If Juppiter him selfe come loytring by,
Ile call thy crew, and bid them fly from Jove;
For if they stay, he will obtaine at last,
What now I loathe, because my love is past.
