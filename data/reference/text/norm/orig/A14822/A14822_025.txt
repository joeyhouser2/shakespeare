IF Poets have done well in times long past,
To glose on trifling toyes of little price:
Why should not I presume to fame as fast,
Espying forth a ground of good devise?
A Sacred Nimph is ground whereon ile write,
The fairest Nimph that ever yet saw light.
And since her song hath fild mine eares with joye,
Her vertues pleas'd my minde, her face mine eye,
I dare affirme what some will thinke a toy,
She Phoenix is, though not of Arabie;
And yet the plumes about her neck are bright,
And Sol him selfe in her hath chief• delight.
You that will know why Sol afoordes her love,
Seeke but the cawse why Peakocks draw the place,
Where Juno sitts; why Venus likes the Dove;
Or why the Owle befitts Mineruaes grace;
Then if you grudge, that she to Sol belonge,
Marke but her face, and heare her skill in songe.
