MY humble sute hath set my minde on pride,
Which pride is cause thou hast me in disdaine,
By which disdaine my woundes are made so wide,
That widenesse of my woundes augmentes my paine,
Which Paine is cause, by force of secreate jarres,
That I sustaine a brunt of private Warres.
But cease deere Dame to kindle further strife,
Let Strifes have ende, and Peace enioy their place;
If Peace take place, Pitie may save my life,
For Pitie should be show'ne to such as trace
Most daung'rous wayes, and tread their stepp's awry,
Or live in woes: and such a one am I.
Therefore My Deere Delight regard my Love,
Whome Love doth force to follow Fond Desire,
Which Fond Desire no counsell can remove;
For what can counsell do, to quench the fire
That fires my hart through fancies wanton will?
" Fancie by kind with Reason striveth still.
