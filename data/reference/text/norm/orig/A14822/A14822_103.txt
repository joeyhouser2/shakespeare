Ecce puer Veneris fert eversam que phraretram,
Et fractos arcus, and sine luce facem.
Aspice demissis ut eat miserabilis alis,
Pectora que infesta tondat aperta manu. etc.
Net minus est confusa Venus. etc.,
Quàm iwenis rupit cum ferus inguen aper,
What ayles poore Venus nowe to sit alone
In funerall attyre, her woonted hew
Nuite chang'd, her smile to teares, her myrth to mean:
As though Adonis woundes nowe bled anew,
Or she wish young Julus late return'd
From seeing her Aeneas carkas burn'd.
Alack for woe, what ayles her little Boy,
To have his tender cheekes besprent with teares,
And sit and sighe, where he was wonte to toy?
How happes, no longer he his quiver weares,
But breakes his Boe, throwing the shivers by,
And pluckes his winges, and lettes his fyrebrand dye?
No, Dame and Darling too, ye come to late,
To winne me now, as you have done tofore;
I live secure, and quiet in estate,
Fully resolu'd from loving any more:
Go pack for shame from hence to Cyprus Ile,
And there go play your prankes an other while.
