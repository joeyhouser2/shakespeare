" Love hath delight in sweete delicious fare;
" Love never takes good Counsell for his frende;
" Love author is, and cause of ydle care;
" Love is distraught of witte, and hath no end;
" Love shoteth shaftes of burning hote desire;
" Love burneth more then eyther flame or fire;
" Love doth much harme through Jealosies assault;
" Love once embrast will hardly part againe;
" Love thinkes in breach of faith there is no fault;
" Love makes a sporte of others deadly paine;
" Love is a wanton Childe, and loves to brall;
" Love with his warre bringes many soules to thrall.
These are the smallest faultes that lurke in Love,
These are the hurtes which I have cause to curse to curse,
These are those truethes which no man can disprove,
These are such harmes as none can suffer worse.
All this I write, that others may beware,
Though now my selfe twise free from all such care.
