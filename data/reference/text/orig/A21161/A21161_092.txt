MIſtruſt miſdemes amiſſe, whereby diſpleaſure growes,
And time delaied, finds frēds afraied, their faith for to diſcloſe:
Suſpect that breede the thought, and thought to ſighes conuarte,
And ſighs haue ſought a floud of teares, wher ſobbs do ſeke yᵉ hart.

Thus harte that meanes no harme, muſt feede on ſorrowes all,
Vntill ſuche tyme as pleaſeth the iudge, the truth in queſtion call:
Though cauſe of greate miſtruſt, before that iudge appeare,
My truthe and mercie of my iudge, I truſt ſhall ſet me cleare.

Report theſe rimes at large, my truthe for to detecte,
Yet truthe in tyme ſhall trie it ſelf, and driue awaie ſuſpecte:
Beleue not euery ſpeache nor ſpeake not all you heare,
For truthe and mercie of the iudge, I truſt ſhall ſet me cleare.
