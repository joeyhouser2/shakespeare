I Would to God I were Acteon, that Diana did diſguiſe,
To walke the Foreſt vp and doune, whereas my ladie lies:
An Harte of heere and hewe, I wiſhe that I were ſo,
So that my Ladie knewe me, onely and no mo.
The ſhalyng Nutts and Maſte, that falleth from the tree,
Should well ſuffice for my repaſt, might I my ladie ſee:
It ſhould not greue me, there in froſt, to lye vpon the grounde,
Delite ſhould eaſly quite the coſte, what euill ſo that I founde.
Sometyme that I might ſaie, when I ſawe her alone,
Beholde, ſee yonder ſlaue aldaie, that walketh the woodds alone,
