SHall I no waie winne you, to graunt my deſire?
What woman, will graunt you, the thyng you require:
You onely to loue me, is all that craue,
You onely to leaue me, is all I would haue.
My deare alas now ſaie not ſo,
To loue you beſt, I muſt ſaie no:
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
doe ſtill,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
beſhrewe me than.

Yet ſome killeth dedly, that flie to the marke:
You ſhall touche no feather, thereof take no carke.
Yet hope ſhall further my deſire,
You blowe the coales, and raiſe no fire.

To loue is no daunger where true loue is ment,
I will loue no ranger, leſt that I repent:
My loue is no ranger, I make God auow,
To truſt your ſmoth ſaiyngs, I ſure knowe not how.
Moſte truthe I meane, as tyme ſhall well trie,
No truthe in men, I oft eſpie.

then plaie on the bitt.

Some women maie ſaie naie, and meane loue moſte true,
Some women can make fools, of as wiſe men as you,
In tyme I ſhall catche you, I knowe when and where:
I will ſone diſpatche you, you ſhall not come there.
Some ſpeds at length, that oft haue miſt,
I am well armed, come when you liſt.

Yet worke your kinde kindly, graunt me loue for loue,
I will vſe you frendly, as I ſhall you proue:
Moſte cloſe you ſhall finde me, I this doe proteſt,
Then ſure you ſhall binde me, to graunt your requeſt.
O happie threde now haue I ſponne,
You ſyng before the conqueſt wonne.
Why then, will you ſwarne,
euen as you deſerue:
Loue ſtill,
I will,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
come to me than.
