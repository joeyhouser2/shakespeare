THe ſainct I ſerue, and haue beſought full oft,
Vpon my knees, to ſtande my Goddes good:
With hope did holde, my head ſometyme aloft,
And fed my faunyng frende, with daintie foode.
But now I ſee, that words are nought but winde,
The ſweter meate, the ſowrer ſauce I finde.

Thus while I helde the Ele by the taile,
I had ſome hope, yet neuer wanted feare:
Of double dread, that man can neuer faile,
That will preſume to take the Wolfe, by the eare.
I ſnatche for ſothe, muche like to Eſops dogg,
I ſought for fiſhe, and alwaies caught a frogg.

Thus did I long bite, on the fomyng bitt,
Whiche found me plaie enough, vnto my paine:
Thus while I loued, I neuer wanted fitt,
But liued by loſſe, and ſought no other gaine.
But why ſhould I miſlike with Fortunes fetters,
Since that the like haue hapt vnto my betters.
