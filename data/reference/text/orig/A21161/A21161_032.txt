If Fortune were ſo blinde, to geue to one man what he will,

We wiſh, we ſearche, we ſtriue for all, and haue no more therin

In getting much, we get but care, ſuch brittle wealth to keepe,

When poore in weake and ſlender houſe, doe feare no loſſe of wealth,
And haue no further care but this, to keepe them ſelues in health.

Affection may not hide the ſword of ſway, in iudgement ſeat,

But if the minde in conſtant ſtate, affection quite doe leaue,
The higher ſtate ſhall haue their rights, the poore no wrong receaue.
