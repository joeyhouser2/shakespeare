IN dewe of Roses, steeping her louely cheekes,
Lycoris thus sate weeping.
Ah Dorus false, that hast my hart bereft me,
And now vnkinde hast left me.
Heare alas, oh heare me,
Aye me, aye me,
Cannot my beautie mooue thee?
Pitty, yet pitty me,
Because I loue thee.
Aye me, thou scorn'st the more I pray thee:
And this thou doo'st, and all to slay me.
Why doo then,
Kill me, and vaunt thee:
Yet my Ghoast
Still shall haunt thee.
