SHall I no waie winne you, to graunt my deſire?
What woman will graunt you, the thyng you require:
You onely to Loue me, is all that I craue,
You onely to leaue me, is all I would haue.
My deare alas, now ſaie not ſo,
To Loue you beſte I muſt ſaie no:
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
doe ſtill,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
beſhrewe me than.
The ſwifter I followe, then you flie awaie,
Swift hauks in their fliyng, oft tymes miſſe their praie,
Yet ſome killeth deadly, that flie to the marke:
You ſhall touche no feather, thereof take no carke.
Yet hope ſhall further my deſire,
You blowe the coales, and raiſe no fire,
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt,
I will,
doe ſtill,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beſhrewe me than.
To Loue is no daunger, where true Loue is ment,
I will Loue no ranger, leaſt that I repent:
My Loue is no raunger, I make God auow,
To truſt your ſmothe ſaiyngs, I ſure knowe not how.
Moſte truthe I meane, as tyme ſhall well trie,
No truthe in men, I ofte eſpie,
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
doe ſtill,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beſhrewe me than.
Some women maie ſaie naie, and meane Loue moſte true,
Some women can make fooles, of as wiſe men as you:
In tyme I ſhall catche you, I knowe when and where:
I will ſone diſpatche you, you ſhall not come there.
Some ſpeds at length, that ofte haue miſte,
I am well armde, come when you liſte.
Yet will I not flitt,
then plaie on the bitt.
I will,
doe ſtill,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beſhrewe me than.
Yet worke your kinde kindly, graunt me Loue for Loue,
I will vſe you frendly, as I ſhall you proue:
Moſte true you ſhall finde me, I this doe proteſt,
Then ſure you ſhall binde me, to graunte your requeſt.
O happie threede, now haue I ſpunne,
You ſyng before the conqueſt wonne.
Why then, will you ſwarue,
euen as you deſarue:
Loue ſtill,
I will,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
come to me than.
