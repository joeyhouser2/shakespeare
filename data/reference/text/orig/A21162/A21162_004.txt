Not plenties purſe, but poore eſtate, not happy hap, but troward fate:
Not wiſh at wil, but wāt of ioy, not harts good helth but harts annoy:
No fredomes vſe, but priſoners thrall, not coſtly ſeate, but loweſt fall:
Not weale I meane, but wretched wo, doth truely try, yᵉ feend frō foe:
And nowght but frowarde Fortune prooues, who fauning faines, or ſimply loues.
