LIke as the dolefull Doue, delights alone to bee,
And doeth refuſe the bloumed branche, chuſyng the leafleſſe tree.
Whereon wailyng his chaunce, with bitter teares beſprent,
Doeth with his bill, his tender breaſte ofte pearſe and all to rent.
Whoſe greeuous gronyngs tho: whoſe grips of pinyng paine,
Whoſe gaſtly lookes, whoſe bloudie ſtreams out flowyng frō ech vain.
Whoſe fallyng from the tree, whoſe pantyng on the grounde,
Examples bee of myne eſtate, tho there appere no wounde.
