AS Iuie kills the tree embracing it,
•o traitours murder with their smoothest lookes.
As euill sauours doe corrupt the aire,
So treachers doe pollute a common-weale.
As in faire weather greatest stormes arise,
So in mild seasons, treason is most bold.
As braunches too much loden quickly breake,
So traitors too farre trusted, doe most harme.
As too much rankenesse bends the stalkes of corne▪
So too much mildnesse whets the traitor on.
As hawkes are lost by soaring ouer-high,
So traitours perish in their chiefest hopes.
