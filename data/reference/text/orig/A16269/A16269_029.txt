AS the right Corall need no other grace,
So Artl•sse beautie best sets sorth the face.
As finest cloth will soonest catch a staine,
So fairest lookes may shadow minds most vaine.
As greatest feasts seldome can want fit friends,
So beauties house will hardly lacke resort.
As medlers with the fire are easily scorcht,
So they that gaze on beautie soone are caught.
As coldest Climates haue their Summer dayes,
So coolest thoughts are fierd at beauties blaze.
As that same Speare which harme must heale the wound,
So looke where beautie kills, it must reuiue.
