THe Persians were so iealous of their wiues▪
As but in waggons they ne're went abroad.
Phanius lockt vp his wife through iealousie,
Whereby she compast what she could not else.
Procris was slaine through her owne iealousie,
Hid in a bush to watch her husbands walke.
Argus, albeit he had an hundred eyes,
Yet could not keepe from Io, Iupiter.
Cicero calleth Iealousie, a feare
Of loosing that belongs to ones owne selfe▪
Chrysippus holds, that iealousie ill brookes
A partner in the thing it most esteemes.
