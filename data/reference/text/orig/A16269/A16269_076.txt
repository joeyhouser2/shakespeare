Cleopatra had her brothers companie,
Wronging thereby her husband & her selfe.
Thalestris trauail'd five and twentie dayes,
To lye one night with mightie Alexander.
Claudius of his owne sisters made no spare.
Sem•ramis in lust desir'd her sonne.
Nero slew Atticus the Romane Consull,
That he might haue accesse vnto his wife.
Aristotle saith, that lust mens bodies chaunge.
And likewise breedeth madnesse in their soules.
Hippocrates call'd lust the foule disease,
Than which could be no worse infection.
