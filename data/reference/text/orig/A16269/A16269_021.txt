AS greatest calmes oft turne to thunderclaps,
So sweetest hopes doe change to sowrest haps.
As in meane places may much wealth be hid,
So little hopes may mightie things expect.
As sadnes is the hearts chiefe punishment,
So hope is highest helpe in deepe distresse.
As one part of the body toiles for all,
So hope striues to accomplish all desires.
As euery mettall is of Sulphur made,
So euery pleasure doth from hope proceed.
As honest pastimes can no way offend,
So good mens hopes must needs haue happy end.
