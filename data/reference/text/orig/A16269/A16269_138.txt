HEctor said to his wife Andromache,
Grieue not my death, all men are borne to die.
Gorgias, askt in sicknesse how he far'd?
Said, Sleepe now yeelds me to his brother death.
Pindarus sleeping on a young lads breast,
Neuer awaked, but in that sort dyed.
Vespasian stood vp at the point of death,
And said, An Emperour should standing dye.
Plato thankt Nature, that she let him liue,
In such a time, as taught him well to die.
Thales will'd euery man amend his life,
Else he could haue no honour in his death.
