HErmocrates lying at the point of death,
Bequeath'd his goods to no one but him-selfe.
Euclio ••• his treasure in his house,
And durst not goe abroad for feare of robbing.
Caligula became so couetous,
That he would spare no meanes to compasse coine.
Demonica for gold sold Ephesus,
And after dyed vnder the weight thereof.
Socrates seeing one ignorant, yet rich,
Said: He was nothing but a golden slaue.
Diogenes would say to couetous men,
That he had rather be their sheepe, than sonne.
