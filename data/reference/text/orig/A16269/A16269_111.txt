AS white and blacke are contrarie in sight,
So words and thoughts are very different.
As fire and water neuer can agree,
Euen so mens words and thoughts doe disagree.
As courtiers cloakes are shifted very oft,
So are our thoughts neuer at certaine stay.
As light is welcome to perplexed minds,
So merrie thoughts doe banish sadnesse best.
As euery tree hath his peculiar fruit,
So euery man hath his owne priuat thought.
As merrie hosts care not for frowning guests,
So pleasant minds can brooke no pensiue thoughts.
