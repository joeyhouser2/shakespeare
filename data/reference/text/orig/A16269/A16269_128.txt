AS foolish questions merit silence best,
So kind demaunds require as kind replyes.
As no mishap can mooue a carelesse mind,
So no instructions can reforme a foole.
As wise men not esteem'd by outward shewes,
So any semblaunce satisfieth fooles.
As snow in Summer no man doth commend,
So none deemes honour requisite for fooles.
As spots disfigure any beauteous face,
So follie is the blemish of the mind.
As smoake at highest, soonest vanisheth,
So follie praised, quickliest perisheth.
