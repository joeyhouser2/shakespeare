CLaudius being giuen to feare, his mother said,
Nature begun, but had not finisht him.
Midas grew desperate by his fearefull dreames,
That to be rid of them, he slew him-selfe.
Aristodemus fear'd with howling dogges,
Tooke such conceit, that soone he ended life.
Nicias th'Athenian, through cowardly feare,
Lost many famous opportunities.
Tully saith, Much more euill is in feare,
Than in the thing that doth procure the feare.
Solon gaue instance to his country-men,
That shame did euermore attend on feare.
