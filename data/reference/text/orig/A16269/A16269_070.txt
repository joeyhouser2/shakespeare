TH•asillus teaching meanes of tyrannie,
Was first enforst to tast thereof himselfe.
Scyron threw others headlong in the sea,
Till Theseus did the like by him at length.
Caligula wisht Rome had but one head,
That at one stroke himselfe might smite it off.
Cyrus that neuer had enough of blood,
Had afterward his head all drown'd in blood.
The soules of tyrants, Plutarch doth affirme,
To be compos'd of crueltie and pride.
A tyrant seekes his priuate benefit
And no mans else, as Seneca maintaines.
