AS Bibulus in triumph rode through Rome,
His fate was with a tyle-stone to be slaine.
Aurelius sister, Lucia, by her needle
But prickt her breast, and dyde immediatly.
Cneius Rufferius, combing of his head,
One of the teeth bereft him of his life.
Methridates, suppos'd mens destinies
Consisted in the power of hearbs and stones.
Chilo of Lacedaemon did maintaine,
That men might comprehend what was to come.
Plato affirmed, That a good mans fate
Neuer to euill could be destinate.
