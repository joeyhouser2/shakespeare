AS Scales by poise are mounted vp and downe,
So too and fro conceits doe vrge the mind.
As tender trees bend euery way we please,
So gentle minds are easily ouer-rul'd.
As heauines fore-tels some harme at hand,
So minds disturb'd, presage ensuing ills.
As sickly bodies brooke not heat nor cold,
So crazed minds dislike of euery thing.
As working vessels are by vent kept sound,
So troubled minds by conference find ease.
