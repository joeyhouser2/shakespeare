AS pride makes enemies of perfect friends,
So liberalitie makes friends of foes.
As hollow spouts retaineth nought but aire,
So hollow hearts all bountie euer ha•e.
As Bees doe flocke vnto a honey dewe,
So multitudes flyes to a liberall mind.
As s••dowes hinders ripening of the fruits,
So couetousnes still holdeth bountie backe.
As H•nbane causeth death by sleepines,
So bountie is destroy'd by niggardnes.
As manhood is discern'd by cowardise,
So bountie is beheld by wretchednesse.
