PHerecides, for his contemning God,
Was eate with lice, and dyed miserably.
Lucian an Atheist, and denying God,
Was afterward in pieces torne with dogs.
Iustinian for his light regard of God,
Became a foole, and so in follie dyde.
Th' Athenians banished Protagoras,
Because his bookes question'd the deitie.
Socrates did confesse one onely God,
And tearn•'d the heathen Gods but vaniti•.
Plato, when he wrote any serious thing,
Began still, In the name of one sole God.
