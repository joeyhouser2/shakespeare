THey that slew Caesar in the Senate-house,
Perisht like traitours, neuer prospered.
The traitours that great •ompey did betray,
With death did Caesar iustly guerdon them.
Sylla to her owne father proou'd vntrue,
And therefore felt desert accordingly.
Lyciscus rotted aliue aboue the ground
For his vile treason to th'Orcmenians.
Th'Athenians would let none be buried,
That had been traitours to the countrey.
Tullie saith: Wise men not at any time
Will trust a traitour or a faithlesse man.
