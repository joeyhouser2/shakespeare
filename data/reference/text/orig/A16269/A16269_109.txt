AS Fowlers by their craft beguile the birds,
So wicked deeds corrupt plaine meaning minds.
As serpents food is onely on the earth,
So wicked mens delights, is ill done deeds.
As sheep-cloath'd Wolues do alwaies greatest spoile,
So painted deeds doe most of all deceiue.
As Circes witch-craft chaunged men to beasts,
So wicked deeds makes seeming men bruit beasts.
As braunches prosper not cut from the tree,
So all is vaine that swerues from honest course.
As little sparkes of •ire procure great harme,
So least ill deeds doe hardly find amends.
