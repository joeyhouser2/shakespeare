AS suddaine bleeding, argues ill ensuing,
So suddaine ceasing is fell feares renewing.
As leaking vessels cannot long endure,
So fearefull minds haue slender permanence.
As nettles haue no prickes, and yet doe sting,
So feares haue little motion, yet oft kill.
As salt ta'ne moderately doth •ellish meat,
So discreet feares doe often benefite.
As in calamitie good friends auaile,
So sound aduise aduantageth in feare.
As wrong suspitions are but mens disgrace,
So needlesse feares declare but want of wit.
