Caesar, by bountie to his followers,
Was call'd the liberall'st prince in all those times.
Archelaus gaue not to vnworthie men,
For that h• held not liberalitie.
Titus, remembring one day nothing giuen,
Said: O my friends, how haue we lost this day▪
Plato said, Niggards neuer can be good,
For all attendeth on the bountifull.
Phocylides will'd no man sleepe at night,
Till that day he could count some well-done deed.
