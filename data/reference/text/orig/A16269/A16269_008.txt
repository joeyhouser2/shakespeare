AS one poore drop is nothing to the sea,
So all we can is nothing in Gods sight.
As the bright Sunne defaceth candle-light,
So Gods great power controlleth all the world.
As Princes are to be both lou'd and fear'd,
So God the Prince of princes, must haue more.
As with great care a Pilot guides the ship,
So with great grace doth God direct the world.
As when the soule departs, the body dies:
So where God blesseth not, all things decay.
As mothers hugge their children in their armes,
So God enfolds his chosen with his grace.
