CAesar in his great fortunes gloried,
Yet by his wisdom all were brought to passe.
Antonius the Emperour was so wise,
He ne're repented what-•oe're he did.
Scipio, accus'd vniustly, by his wit
In making answere, wonne himselfe renowme.
The Senate did acquire Emilius Scaurus,
Onely because he answer'd wittily.
Plato in his Conuiuim doth affirme,
That wisdome is the onely gift in man.
Tullie tearmes wisdome, mistresse of this life:
Likewise, an Art instructing to liue well.
