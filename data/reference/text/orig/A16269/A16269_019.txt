AS raine makes euery ground bring forth encrease.
So faith of euery soule doth shew the fruits.
As honours fire doth kindle high desires,
So zealous faith lifts vp the lowest soule.
As night doth best the diamonds glory show,
So sharpe affliction best makes faith to grow.
As wisdome is the only way to weale,
So true discretion best directeth zeale.
As loue and hate cannot agree in one,
So without zeale, faith thinkes her selfe alone.
