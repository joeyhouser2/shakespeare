THe Kings of Persia, alwaies shewed themselues
More subiect to the law, than to their Lords.
Antiochus told his sonne Dem•trius,
That kingly rule was noble slauerie.
B•lus the sonne of Nemrod, was first king
That in this world had title of that name.
The Romane kings did vse to weare no crownes.
But alwaies bare their scepters in their hands.
Tully saith, then 'tis best to checke a prince,
When he forgets himselfe to be a prince.
S•••ates wil'd good kings preferre their friends,
And shewe some kindnesse to their enemies.
