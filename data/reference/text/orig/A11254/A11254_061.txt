WHo plucks thee down frō hie deſire poor hart?
Who comforts thee in depth of thy diſtreſſe?
Amid contents, who breeds thy ſecret ſmart?
Who ſeekes the meane, thy ſorrowes may be leſſe?

Who calls thy wits togither to their worke:
Who warnes thy will, to follow warie wit?
Who lets thee ſee in loue what ſorrowes lurke?
Who makes thee feele the force of fancies fit?

Who taught thee firſt to trie before thou truſt?
Who bids thee keepe a faithfull tried freend?
Who wils thee ſay, loue wantons he that luſt?
Who winnes the wiſh, that hath a happie end?

Care then to keepe, that faithfull friend in ſtore,
Whoſe loue commands, that thou ſhalt care no more.
