Conſpiracie gainſt the perſon of a Prince,
Is treaſon gainſt the deitie of heauen.
—Treaſon is but truſted like the Foxe,
Who nere ſo tamde, ſo cheriſht, and lockt vp,
Will haue a wilde tricke of his auncetors.
No vertue merits prayſe once toucht with blot of treaſon.
VVho fayleth one is falſe, though truſty to another▪
There is no treaſon woundeth halfe ſo deepe,
As that which doth in Princes boſome ſleepe.
VVho that reſiſteth his dread ſoueraigne Lord,
Doth damne his ſoule by Gods owne verie word,
A Chriſtian ſubiect ſhould with honour due,
Obey his ſoueraigne though he were a Iew,
VVhereby aſſured when ſubiects do rebell,
Gods wrath is kindled, threatning fire and hell.
Was neuer rebell before the world and ſince,
That could or ſhould preuaile againſt his Prince.
Reuolted ſubiects of themſelues will quaile.
