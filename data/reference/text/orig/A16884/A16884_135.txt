Prayers heart and ſides, and feet, are full of wings
(Like to th'Arcadian which Ioues arrand brings)
Her body burning, from her lips doth come
The ſmoake of Incenſe, and of ſweet Amome.
Heauens are propitious vnto fearfull prayers.
Faſting (though faint) her face with ioy ſhe cheares,
In weaknes ſtrong, and young in aged yeares.
Quicke health preſeruer, curbing Cupids fits,
Watchfull, purge humors, and refining wits.
