Folly in youth is ſinne, in age is madnes.
A greater ſigne of Folly is not ſhowne,
Then truſting others force, diſtruſt our owne:
— Wicked men repine their ſinnes to heare,
And Folly flings, if counſell touch him neare.
Faire fooles delight to be accounted wiſe.
Fooles will find fault without the cauſe diſcerning.
And argue moſt of that they haue no learning.
— There is a method, time, and place,
Which fooles obſeruing do cōmence, ere wiſe mē haue their grace.
Tis better be a foole then be a foxe,
For Folly is rewarded and reſpected,
Where ſubtiltie is hated and reiected.
— The fooliſh commmons vſe
Obey them moſt, who doth them moſt abuſe.
A witleſſe foole may euery man him geſſe,
That leaues the more, and takes him to the leſſe.
