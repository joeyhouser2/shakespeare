— At ſuch times when Lawyers walk the ſtreetes
Without long rowles of papers in their hands,
When friendly neighbour with his neighbour meetes,
Without falſe challenge to each others lands,
The Counſellour without his Clyent ſtands:
When that large Capitall lies void and waſt
Where Senatours and Iudges late were plaſt.
