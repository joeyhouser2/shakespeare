Our God himſelfe for wiſedome moſt is prayſed,
And men to God thereby are nigheſt rayſed.
Wiſedome doth warne, whilſt foe is in the gate.
To ſtay the ſtep, ere forced to retreate.
VViſedome muſt iudge twixt men apt to amend,
And mindes incurable borne to offend.
—In daunger wiſedome doth aduiſe,
In humble termes to reconcile our foes.
—Wiſedome and the ſight of heauenly things,
Shines not ſo cleere as earthly vanities.
Tis ſayd a wiſe man all miſhaps withſtands,
For though by ſtarres wee borne to miſchiefes are,
Yet prudence bailes vs quite from carefull bands.
Fore-ſight doth ſtill on all aduantage lie.
Wiſe men muſt giue place to neceſsitie.
— A wiſeman poore
Is like a ſacred Booke that's neuer read,
T'himſelfe hee liues, and to all elſe ſeemes dead:
This age thinkes better of a gilded foole,
Then of thred bare ſaint in wiſedomes ſchoole.
VViſe men let faults ore-paſſe, they cannot mend.
VVho can themſelues beware by others coſts,
May bee accounted well among the wiſe.
—Whatſoeuer Starres ſeeme to importune,
Wiſedome predominates both fate and fortune.
