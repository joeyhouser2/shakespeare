Like to a riuer that is ſtopt his courſe,
Doth violate his bankes, breakes his owne head.
Deſtroyes his bounds, and ouerruns by force
The neighbour fieldes, in regularly ſpread.
Euen ſo this ſudden ſtop of Warre doth nurſe
Home toiles within it ſelfe from others lead,
So daungerous the chaunge thereof is tried,
Ere mindes come ſoft, or otherwiſe imploide.
