How errour was maine ſaile, each waue a teare.
The Mr. loue himſelfe; deepe ſighes weare winde,
Cares rowd with vowes, the ſhip vnmerry minde.
Falſe hope as firme oft turn'd the boate about,

Diſpaire the cable, twiſted all with doubt,
