As then no winde at all there blew,
No ſwelling clowde accloyd the ayre,
The skye like graſſe of watched hue
Reflected Phaebus golden haire:
The garniſht trees no pendant ſtird,
Nor voyce was heard of any bird.
The King of windes calls home his poſts againe,
And Amphitrite ſmooth's her watry plaine,
The ayre his clowdes hath chang'd to chriſtall cleere,
And now the lamps of light from heauen appeare.
