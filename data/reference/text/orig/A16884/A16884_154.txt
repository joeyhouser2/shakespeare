The bodies life with meates and aire is fed,
Therefore the ſoule doth vſe the taſting power,
In vaines which through the tong & pallat ſpred.
Diſtinguiſh euery relliſh ſweet and ſower.
This is the bodies nurſe: but ſince mans wit
Found the Art of cookery to delight his ſence,
More bodies are conſumde and kild with it,
Then with the ſword, famine, or peſtilence.
—That fourth band which cruell battery bent
Againſt the fourth bulwarke, that is the taſte:
Was as the reſt, a griſly rabblement,
Some mouth like greedy Eſtriges, ſome fac'ſt
Like loathly Toades, ſome faſhioned in the waſte
Like ſwine, for ſo deſeru'd his luxurie,
Surfet, miſdiet, and vnthriftie warke,
Vaine feaſts, and idle ſuperfluitie,
All thoſe this ſences fort aſſaile inceſſantly.
