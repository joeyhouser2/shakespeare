Free is the Heart, the temple of the minde,
The ſanctuarie ſacred from aboue,
Where nature keepes the keys that looſe and binde,
No mortall hand force, open can that doore,
So cloſe ſhut vp, and lock to all mankinde.
The bodies wound, by medicines may be eaſed,
But griefes of heart, by ſalues are not appeaſed.
By thought of heart, the ſpeech of tongue is carried.
