Night was farre ſpent, and now in Ocean deepe,
Orion flying faſt from hiſsing ſnake,
His flaming head did haſten for to ſteepe.
By this th'eternall lamps wherewith high Ioue,
Doth light the lower world, were halfe yſpent,
And the moyſt daughters of huge Atlas ſtroue
Into th'ocean deep to driue their wearie droue.
— The gentle humorous night,
Implyes her middle courſe, and the ſharpe eaſt,
Breathes on my ſpirit with his fierie ſteedes.
The ſilent night that long had ſoiourned,
Now gan to caſt her ſable mantle off,
And now the ſleepie waine-man ſoftly droue
His ſlow-pac't teeme that long had trauailed.
