Her hand-maides in Amazon-like attire,
Went chaſte and modeſt like Dianaes traine,
One by her gazing lookes ſeemes to aſpire
Beyond the Moone, and in a high diſdaine,
To deeme the world and worldly treaſures vaine.
She hight Aſtrologie, on whoſe bright lawne,
Spheres Aſtrolabes and skilfull globes are drawn.
