— O Souldiers enuie neere ally to Kings
Maieſticke humour, carefull iealous thought:
Thou, which awak'ſt vs from ignoble things,
A paſſion neareſt to a godhead brought.
Onely indefinite: to whom none brings
Limit or bound, thou greater then our thought,
Who holds thee, holds a power to make him able,
Who looſes then, becomes moſt miſerable.
None is ſo poore of ſence and eine,
To whom a ſouldier doth not ſhine.
No elegancie can bewtifie
A ſhameleſſe lumpe of gluttonie:
His heart ſweete Cupids tents reiects,
That onely meate and drinke affects.
O Flora all mens intellects,
Know ſouldiers power ſuch reſpects,
Meere helpes for need his minde ſufficeth,
Dull ſleepe and ſurfets he deſpiſeth:
Loues trumpe his temples exerciſeth,
Courage and loue his life compriſeth.
