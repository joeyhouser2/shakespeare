A wiſe man liuing like a drone, an old man not deuout,
Youth diſobedient, rich men that are charitie without:
A ſhameles womā, vicious Lords, a poore man proudly ſtout.
Cōtentious Chriſtiās, Paſtors, that their functiōs do neglect,
A wicked King, no diſcipline, no lawes men to direct,
Are twelue the fouleſt faults that moſt all common-wealths infect.
