Looke how a bright ſtarre ſhooteth from the skie,
So glides he in the night from Venus eye,
Which after him ſhe darts as on a ſhore,
Gazing vpon a late embarqued frend,
Till the wild waues will haue them ſee no more,
Whoſe ridges with the meeting cloudes contend.
So did the mercileſſe and pitchy night
Fold in the obiect that did feed her ſight.
