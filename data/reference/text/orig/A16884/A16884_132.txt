— Such is th'ffect of two much ſtore,
It makes them loathe that which they lou'd before.
The ſtately Eagle on his pitch doth ſtand
And from the maine the fearefull foule doth ſuit,
Yet ſcornes to touch them lying on the land,
When he hath felt the ſweete of his delight,
But leaues the ſame a pray to euery Kite,
With much we ſurfet, Plentie makes vs poore,
The wretched Indian ſcornes the golden Oare.
