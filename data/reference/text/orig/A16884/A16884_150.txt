Although things ſenſible be numberleſſe,
But only fiue the Sences organs bee:
And in thoſe fiue all things their formes expreſſe,
Which we can touch, taſte, feele, or heare or ſee.
Mans eye makes what is ſeene to ſeeme ſo faire,
Mans eare makes what is heard to ſound ſo ſweete:
His touch by ſoftneſſe euery ſence is meete
For his owne obiect.
