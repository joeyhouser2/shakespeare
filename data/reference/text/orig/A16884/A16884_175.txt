Good vowes are neuer broken with good deedes,
For then good deedes were bad: vowes are but ſeeds,
And good deedes fruits.
Wee know not how to vow, till loue vnblind vs,
And vowes made ignorantly neuer binde vs.
Our vowes muſt bee perform'd to God and King.
— A promiſe made for feare is voyde.
— A man ſuch promiſe muſt forſake,
As at the firſt vnlawfull was to make.
