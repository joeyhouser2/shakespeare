Heauens Criſtall windowes with one hand God opes
Where on the world a thouſand ſeas hee droppes,
With th'other hand hee gripes and wringeth forth,
The ſpungie globe of the execrable earth,
So ſtraightly preſt that it doth ſtrait reſtore,
All liquid flouds that it had drunke before,
In euerie rocke new riuers doe beginne,
And to his aide the ſnowes came tumbling in.
The Pines and Cedars haue but bowes to ſhew,
The ſhoares do ſhrinke, the ſwelling waters grow.
