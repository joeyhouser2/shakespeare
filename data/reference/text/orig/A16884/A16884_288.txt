The more he dranke, the more he did deſire,
Like to the Ocean ſea, though it receaues
All Nilus flouds, yet all freſh water craues
From Eaſt to Weſt, yet growes he not a graine,
But ſtill is ready for as much againe.
* The ſtaues like yce in ſhiuers ſmall did flie,
The ſplints like byrds did mount vnto the skie.
