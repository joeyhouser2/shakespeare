Thoughts are the ſlaues of life, and life times foole,
And time that takes ſuruey of all the world
Muſt haue a ſtop.
Thoughts are but dreames, till their effects be tried.
Who ſo thinkes many things, brings few to a fortunate ending.
The feeble eyes of our aſpring thoughts,
Behold things preſent, and record things paſt,
But things to come exceede our humane reach.
Vnfained thoughts do ſeldome dreame on euil.
Birdes neuer limde no ſecret buſhes feare.
If all mens thoughts were written in their face,
Some one that now the reſt doth ouercrow,
Some others ebbe that wants his ſoueraignes grace,
VVhen as the Prince their inwarde thoughts ſhould know
The meaner then ſhould take the better place,
The greateſt man might ſtoope and ſit below.
