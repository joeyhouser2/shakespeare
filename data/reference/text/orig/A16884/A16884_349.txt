Faire Danubie is praiſde for being wide,
Nilus commended for the ſeuen-fold head:
Euphrates for the ſwiftneſſe of the tide,
And for the garden whence his courſe is led,
The bankes of Rhine with Vines are ouerſpred.
Take Loyre and Po, yet all may not compare
With Engliſh Thameſis for buildings rare.
