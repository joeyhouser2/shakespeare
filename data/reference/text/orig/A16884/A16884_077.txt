Oft times the greateſt haſte the worſer ſpeeds.
As buſie braines muſt beat on tickle toyes,
As raſh inuention breeds a raw deuice:
So ſuddein falles do hinder haſtie ioyes,
And as ſwift baits do fleeteſt fiſh intice,
So haſte makes waſte, and therefore now I ſay,
No haſte but good, where wiſedome beares the ſway.
The ſwifteſt bitch brings forth the blindeſt whelpes,
The hotteſt feuers coldeſt crampes enſue.
The nakedſt need, hath ouer-lateſt helpes.
Haſtie reſpect, repents when tis too late.
Raſhneſſe ſees all, but nothing can preuent.
Fore-iudging, puts out one of wiſedomes eies.
—If by raſhneſſe valour haue got honour,
We blame the raſhneſſe, but reward the valour.
O raſh falſe heat wrapt in repentance cold,
Thy haſte ſprings ſtill blood, and nere growes old.
