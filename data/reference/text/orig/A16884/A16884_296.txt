An heard of bulls, whom kindly rage doth ſting,
Do for the milky mothers want complaine,
And fill the fields with troublous bellowing,
The neighbor woods around with hollow murmuring.
As when the ſhapeleſſe huge Leuiaethan
Hath thruſt himſelfe vpon the ſandie ſhore,
Where (monſterlike) affrighting euery man,
He belloweth out a fearefull deadly rore.
Euen ſuch a Clamour through the aire doth thunder,
The dolefull preſage of ſome fearefull wonder.
