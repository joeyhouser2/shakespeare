Moſt miſerable creature vnder skie
Man, without vnderſtanding doth appeare,
For all this worlds affliction he thereby,
And fortvnes freates is wiſely taught to beare:
Of wretched life the onely ioy ſhee is,
And th'onely comfort in calamitie,
She armes the breaſt with conſtant patience,
Againſt the bitter throwes of dolours darts,
She ſolaceth with rules of ſapience,
The gentle mindes in midſt of worldly ſmarts,
When hee is ſadde, ſhe ſeekes to make him merie,
And doth refreſh his ſpirits when they bee wearie,
