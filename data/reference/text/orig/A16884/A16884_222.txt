A ſillie man in ſimple weede forworne,
And ſoyld with duſt of the long dryed way,
His ſandales were with toylſome trauell torne,
And face all tand with ſcorching ſunnie ray,
As hee had trauaild many a ſummers day,
Through boyling ſands of Affrica and Inde,
And in his hand a Iacobs ſtaffe to ſtay
His wearie limbs vpon, and eke behind,
His ſcrip did hang, in which his needmets he did bind
