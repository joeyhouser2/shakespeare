— The midnights waking ſtarre,
Sad Caſsiopeia with a heauie cheere
Puſht forth her forehead to make knowne from farre,
What time the deadly dole of earth drewe neere.
With falling miſts the darkeſome night extended
Her ſable wings, and gently ouer-ſpread
Heauens gloomie vaile, whence Phoebus lampe was fled,
Dead time of reſt to euerie mortall wight,
To cheerefull mindes that bringeth wanton ſleepe,
With many a phantaſie and deluding toy,
And penſiue heart it doth delaie and keepe
From tedious companie, that would annoy,
Dull Saturniſts that haue abiurdall ioy.
