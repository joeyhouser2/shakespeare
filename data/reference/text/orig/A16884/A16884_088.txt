Vnthankfulneſſe is that great ſin,
Which made the diuel and his angels fall:
Loſt him and them the ioyes that they were in,
And now in hell detaines them bound and thrall.
Thou hatefull monſter baſe Ingratitude,
Soules mortall poyſon, deadly killing wound:
Deceitfull ſerpent ſeeking to delude,
Blacke loathſome ditch, where all deſert is drown'd:
Vile peſtilence, which all things doeſt confound.
At firſt created to no other end,
But to greeue thoſe, whom nothing could offend.
Ingratefull who is call'd, the worſt of ill is ſpoken.
Tis true that ſlaue whom Pompey did promote,
Was he, that firſt aſſaid to cut his throte.
