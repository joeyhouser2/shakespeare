The next, fayre ſmiling with a pleaſing cheeke,
Had power to rauiſh and inchaunt mens eares,
Hight Rhetorick, whoſe ſhadowed vaile ſhowen cleere
With ſiluer tongues, and ouer it ſhe weares,
A wimpled ſcarfe, bedewd with hearers teares,
Whoſe captiue hearts ſhe ſhould detaine long while,
With pleaſance of her vnaffected ſtile.
