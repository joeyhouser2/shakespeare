O male-content ſeducing ghueſt,
Contriuer of our greateſt woes:
Which borne of winde and fed with ſhowes,
Doſt nurſe thy ſelfe in thine vnreſt,
Iudging vngotten things the beſt,
Or what thou in conceit deſigneſt.
Thou all things in the world doſt deeme,
Not as they are, but as they ſeeme,
Thou ſoule of pleaſure, houres onely ſubſtance,
Great arbitrator, vmpire of the earth,
Whom fleſhly Epicures call vertues eſſence
Thou mouing Orator, whoſe powerfull breath
Swaies all mens iudgements, Great Opinion,
Opinion is as various as light chaunge,
Now ſpeaking courtlike friendly, ſtraight as ſtrange.
Shee's any humours perfect paraſite,
Diſpleaſd with her, and pleaſd with her delight.
Shee is the Eccho of inconſtancie,
Soothing her no with nay, her I with yea.
This ſyren or Opinion, wind-borne lame,
Seeking to eaſe vs, brings vs to vnreſt:
For it adiudgeth nothing it doth ſee,
By what it is, but what it ſeemes bee.
We muſt in matters morall, quite reiect
Vulgar Opinion, euer led amiſſe:
And let autenticke reaſon be our guide,
The wife of truth, and wiſedomes gouerneſſe.
