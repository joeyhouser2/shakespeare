He hath his other weapons ſtrange among
A trunke of iron hollow made within,
And there he puts powder and pellets in,
All cloſed ſaue a little hole behind,
Whereat no ſooner taken is the flame,
The bullet flies with ſuch a furious wind,
As though from clouds a bolt of thunder came:
And what-ſo-euer in the way it finde,
It burnes it, breakes it, teares it, ſpoiles the ſame;
No doubt ſome fiend of hell or deuilliſh wight
Deuiſed it, to doe mankind a ſpight.
