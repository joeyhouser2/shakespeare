The meaneſt fault is hie offence, vrg'd of a mighty foe.
To ſhadow ſinne Might can the more pretend.
— Might is euer abſolute alone,
When of two powers ther's true coniunctione.
Power conſtrain'd is but a glorious ſlaue.
— Slight, force, are mightie things,
From which, much, if not moſt, earths glory ſprings:
If vertues ſelfe were clad in humaine ſhape,
Vertue without theſe, might go beg and ſcrape.
Vnited powers, makes each the ſtronger proue.
— Honey words make fooliſhneſſe,
And power the greateſt wit with error blinds.
All as the higheſt trees do ſheeld the ſhrubs,
From poſting Phlegons warmth, and warming fier,
So mightie men obſcure each others fame,
And make the beſt deſeruer fortunes game.
— Excellencie neuer beares this minde,
By no inferiour skill to be definde.
Where power decreed hath to find th'offence,
The cauſe is better ſtill, then the defence.
