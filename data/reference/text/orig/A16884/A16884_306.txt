A ſtately Pallace built of ſquared bricke,
Which cunningly was without morter laid,
Whoſe walles were high, but nothing ſtrong nor thick,
And golden foyle all ouer them diſplaid.
That pureſt skie with brightneſſe they diſmaid,
High lifted vp were many lofty towres,
And goodly galleries farre ouerlaid:
Full of fayre windowes and delightfull bowres,
And on the top a dyall told the timely howres.
The ſoueraigne Caſtels of the rocky yle,
Wherein Penelope the Princes lay:
Shone with a thouſand lampes, which did exile
The dimme darke ſhades, and turnd the night to day.
Not Ioues blew tent what time the ſunny ray
Behind the bulwarke of the earth retires,
Is ſeene to ſparkle with more ſprinkling fires.
