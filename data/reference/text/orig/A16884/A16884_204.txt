Next theſe, whoſe outward lookes I knew aright,
And had ſome portion of their endleſſe treaſure,
Fayre Algebra with fingers richly dight,
Sweete Muſicke founder of delightſome pleaſure,
Earth-ſcanning nymph, directreſſe of all meaſure.
Theſe humbly did her ſoueraigne highnes greet,
And meekely layd their garlands at her feete.
From euerie one ſhee pluckt a ſpeciall flower,
And layd each flower vpon a ſeuerall part,
Then from her one a ſtemme of wondrous power,
Whoſe leaues were beames, whoſe ſtalke a fiery dart.
And that ſhe layd vpon my trembling heart,
Theſe were the buds of art, this plant of bliſſe,
This gaue them life, they yeelded grace to this.
