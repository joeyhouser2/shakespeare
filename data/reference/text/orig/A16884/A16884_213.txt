Sometime they proffer, then they pauſe a while,
Sometime ſtrike out, like maſters of the play,
Now ſtand vpright, now ſtoope, another while,
Now open lie, now couer all they may.
Now ward then with a ſlippe the blow beguilde,
Now forward ſtep, now backe a little way,
Now round about, and where the tone giues place,
There ſtill the other preſſeth in his place.
