O pouertie, chiefe of the heauenly broode.
—Such is the world, this croſ-blis world of ours,
That vertue hardly hides her ſelf in poore & deſart bowers,
And ſuch be beſt as ſeeme not beſt, content exceeds a crowne
—Powerfull need (arts auncient dame, and keeper)
The early watch clocke of the ſlothfull ſleeper.
—Lacke is thrall and ſlaue to euery thing.
Need is miſtreſſe of all exerciſe.
A ſchollers want exceeds a clownes content.
No danger but in hie eſtate, none erre in meane degree.
—Where imperious need doth tyrannize,
The holy heate through worldly cares doth pawſe
Its ſoild with earthly thoughts and downward drawes.
Hence come thoſe dull conceits among the wiſe,
Which coy eard readers cenſure to proceed
From ignorance, whereas they grow by need.
The citizens like ponned pikes, the leſſer feeds the great,
The rich for meat ſeek ſtomachs, and the pore for ſtomach meat.
Be as thou art, not as thou wouldſt, it will be as it is,
Learne then to lack, and learn to liue, for croſſes neuer miſſe.
