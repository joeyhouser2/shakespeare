Why double fac'd? I marke each fortunes rare:

Why wings? I teach aboue the ſtarres to flie:
Why treade you death? I onely cannot die.
The path that leades to Vertues Court is narrow,
Thornie, and vp a hill, a bitter iourney:
But being gone through, you find al heauenly ſweets,
Th'entrance is all flintie, but at th'end
Two Towres of pearles and criſtall you aſcend.
Vertue is fayreſt in a poore art aye.
Vertue abhorres too weare a borrowed face.

When vertue riſeth, baſe affections fall.

And learnes his ſtoppe by raine in riders hand,
Where mountaine colt that is not ſadled yet,
Runnes headlong on amidſt the fallowed land,
Whoſe fierce reſiſt ſcarce bendes with any band.
So men reclaim'd by vertue tread aright,
Where ledde by follies, miſchiefes on them light.
Vertue doth •urb affection, and for conſcience flieth ſin,
To leaue for imperfection feare or ſhame no praiſe doth winne.
Vertue it ſelfe turnes vice, being miſapplyed,
And vice ſometime by action dignified.
Vertue in greateſt daunger is moſt ſhowne,
And though oppreſt, yet nere is ouerthrowne.
In vertue it is ſaid, that men themſelues ſuruie.
Honour indeede, and all things yeeld to death,
(Vertue excepted) which alone ſuruiues,
And liuing toyleth in an earthlie gaile,
At laſt to be extol'd in heauens high ioyes.
All things decay, yet vertue ſhall not die,
This onely giues vs immortallitie.
Whence is it that the flower of the field doth fade,
And lyeth buried long in winters bale,
Yet ſoon as ſpring his mantle doth diſplay,
It flowreth freſh, as it ſhould neuer faile,
But thing on earth that is of moſt auaile.
As vertues and beauties bud,
Releeuen not for any good,
The branch once dead, the bud needes eke muſt quaile▪
All that wee had, or mortall men can haue,
Seemes onely hut a ſhadow from the graue,
Vertue alone liues ſtill.
Vertue is more amiable and more ſweete,
When vertue and true maieſtie doe meete.

Muſt not heere thinke to liue, for life is wretchednes.
Vertue makes honour, as the ſoule doth ſence,
And merit, farre exceedes inheritance.

But better are two vertues ioynd in one.

And hauing this, this thou for euer haſte.
Ioy grauen in ſence, like ſnow in water waſts,
