Seuen of them came together in a knot,
VVith womens faces, wanne, with deadly cold,
So hunger-ſtaru'd, as death it ſelfe might not
Be at firſt ſight more hidious to behold:
Their wings were great, but foule black wings god wot,
Theyr tallents ſharp to gripe, and ſtrong to hold,
A large foule panch, a filthy tayle and long,
From whence there came a mighty odour ſtrong.
