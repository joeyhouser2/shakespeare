—Scaliger our ages wonder,
The learned's ſunne, who eloquently can
Speake Hebrew, Greeke, French, Latine, Nubian,
Dutch, Tuſcan, Spaniſh, Engliſh, Arabicke.
The Sirian, Perſian, and the Caldaike.
O rich quicke ſpirit; O wits Chamelion,
Which any authors colour can put on.
Great Iulius fame, and Siluius worthy brother,
Th'immortall grace of Gaſcony, their mother,
Wing-footed Hermes, purſeuant of Ioue.
