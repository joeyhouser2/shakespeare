O had Felicitie feeling of woe?
Or could on meane but moderately feede?
Or would looke downe the way that he muſt goe?
Or could abſtaine from what diſeaſes breede?
To ſtop the wound before to death it bleede?
Warre ſhould not fill Kings pallaces with mone,
Nor perill come, when tis leaſt thought vpon.
