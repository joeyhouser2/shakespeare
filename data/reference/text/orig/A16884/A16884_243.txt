Firſt came great Neptune with his three-forkt mace,
That rules the ſeas, and makes them riſe or fall;
His dewey locks did drop with brine a pace
Vnder his diademe imperiall,
And by his ſide his Queene with Coronall,
Fayre Amphitrite, moſt diuinely fayre,
vvhoſe Iuory ſhoulders were couered all
As with a robe, with her owne ſiluer hayre,
And deckt with pearles, which the Indian ſeas for her prepare.
