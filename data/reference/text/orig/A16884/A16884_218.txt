About his necke a carknet rich hee ware
Of precious ſtones all ſette in gold well tried,
His armes that carſt all warlike weapons bare,
In golden bracelets wantonly were tied,
Into his eares two rings conuayed are,
Of golden wire, at which on eirher ſide,
Two Indian pearles, in making, like two peares
Of paſsing price, were pendant at his eares,
His locks bedewd with waters of ſweete ſauour,
Stood curled round in order on his head,
He had ſuch wanton womaniſh behauiour,
As though in Ʋalence he had long beene bred,
So changd in ſpeech, in manners, and in fauour,
So from himſelfe beyond all reaſon ledde,
By theſe inchauntments of this amorous dame,
He was himſelfe in nothing but in name.
