—The bird of fame
That ſtill renewes it ſelfe and neuer dies,
And onely one in all the world there flies.
* Of all chaſt birds the Phaenix doth excell,
Of all ſtrong beaſts the Lyon beares the bell:
Of all ſweete flowers, the Roſe doth ſweeteſt ſmell.
Of all pure mettalls gold is onely pureſt,
Of all the trees the Pine hath higheſt creſt.
Of all proud birds the Eagle pleaſeth Ioue,
Of pretie fowles kind Venus likes the Doue,
Of trees Minerua doth the Oliue moue.
* Who holdeth league with Neptune and the winde?
The Phaenix gazeth on the ſunnes bright beames,
The Echinaeus ſwims againſt the ſtreames.
