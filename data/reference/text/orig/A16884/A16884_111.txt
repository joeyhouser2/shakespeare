This Lidger booke lies in rhe braine behinde,
Like Ianus eye which in his poll was ſet:
The lay mans table, Storehouſe of the minde,
Which doth remember much, and much forget.
Here ſences apprehenſions end doth take,
As when a ſtone is into water caſt:
One circle, doth an other circle make,
Till the laſt circle touch the banke at laſt.
Remēbrance is the life of grief, his graue forgetfulnes.
Remembrance freſh, makes weakened ſorrows ſtrong.
