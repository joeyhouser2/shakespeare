Sweete numbred Homer.
Heartſtealing Homer, marrow of the Muſes,
Chiefe grace of Greece, beſt pearle of Poetrie,
Drowner of ſoules, with arts orewhelming ſluces,
Embelliſhed with Phoebes lunarie,
Deckt with the graces rich imbroderie.
Sweete honey-ſuckle, whence all Poets ſprights,
Sucke the ſweete honey of diuine delights.
