This falſe painted deitie called Laude,
Which makes vs thirſt for vaine eternitie:
Twixt our deſires and hope, a cunning baud
Vſhers the ſoule vnto extremitie:
And helpt by ſlye inſinuating fraud,
Couers her deeds in ſcrowles of pietie.
The hope of praiſe makes men no trauell ſhunne,
To ſay an other day this haue we donne.
Who rightly climes the top of endleſſe praiſe,
Regards not what the wiſe diſcourſer ſaies.
—From praiſe takes enuie cauſe.
The chiefeſt praiſe is to imbrace the man
In wealth and woe, with whom our loue began.
The greateſt praiſe, in greateſt perils wonne.
The looſer wantons ſild are praiſde of many,
Vice oft findes friends but vertue ſildome any.
In Athence where Themiſtocles remaind,
Though much he conquered by his regiments,
Yet Solon was more praiſed for his intents.
Praiſe not the bewty of thy wife, though ſhe of fame be ſpred,
For Gyges moued ſo, did graft on Caudales his hed.
