A trump more ſhrill then Tritons on the Sea,
The ſaid Renowne precurſour of the traine,
Did ſound (for who rings louder then Renowne:)
He mounted was vpon a flying horſe,
And cloath'd in Faulcons feathers to the ground,
By his Eſcochion iuſtly might you geſſe,
He was the Herauld of Eternity,
And Purſeuant at Armes to mighte Ioue.
