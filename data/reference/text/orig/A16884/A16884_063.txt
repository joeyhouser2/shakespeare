Tis wiſedome to giue much, a Gift preuailes,
When deepe perſwading Oratorie failes.
A giuing hand though foule, ſhall haue faire praiſe.
— The greateſt Gifts whereof we boaſt,
Are thoſe which do attempt and tire vs moſt.
— Onely wiſedome graue, and iudgements cleere,
Gifts giu'n from heauen, that are not common heere.
Goods Gifts are often giuen to men paſt good.
Good Gifts abus'd, to mans confuſion turne.
Testators and Executors ſo giue and ſo receaue,
As doubtful whethers ioy or griefe, is more to take or leaue
For as do hogs their troghs to hoūds, ſo theſe giue & get place
Death not the Dier giues bequeſts, and therfore but graue grace.
To loiter well deſerued Gifts, is not to giue but ſell,
When to requite ingratitude, were to do euill well.
