Noah lookes vp, and in the ayre he viewes
A ſemicircle of an hundred hewes;
vvhich bright aſcending toward th'aetheriall thrones,
Hath a line drawne betweene two Horizons
For iuſt Diameter: an euen bent bow
Contriu'd of three: whereof the one doth ſhow
To be all painted of a golden hew;
The ſecond greene, the third an orient blew:
Yet ſo, that in this pure blew-golden greene,
Still (ô pall-like) ſome changeable is ſeene;
A bow bright ſhining in th'archers hand,
Whoſe ſubtile ſtring ſeemes leuell with the land,
Halfe parting heauen, and ouer vs it bends,
vvithin two ſeas wetting his horned ends;
A temporall beautie of the lampfull skyes,
vvhere powerfull Nature ſhewes her freſh-red dies.
And if you onely blew and red perceaue,
The ſame as ſignes of ſea and fire conceaue,
Of both the flowing and the flaming doome,
The iudgement past, and iudgement yet to come.
