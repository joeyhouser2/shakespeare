— Deſire, whom not the firmament,
Nor aire, nor earth, nor Ocean can content,
Whoſe lookes are hookes, whoſe bellies bottomleſſe,
Whoſe hands are gripes to ſcrape with greedines,
Vnder whoſe command,
She brings to field a rough vnruly band,
Firſt, ſecret burning, mightie ſwoln ambition,
Whom Epicurus many worlds ſuffice not,
Whoſe furious thirſt of proud aſpiring dies not,
Whoſe hands tranſported with phantaſticke paſſion,
Beare painted ſteeples in imaginaton.
Amongſt the moſt, the worſt, we beſt can chuſe,
Tis eaſie to deſire, but hard to vſe.
Deſire hath philters, which deſire procure.
If blinde deſire thy heart hath once embraced,
Inthrall'd it is, and honour ſo defaced.
Deſire with ſmall encouraging growes bolde.
What can be ſaid that Louers cannot ſay?
Deſire can make a Doctor in a day.
Things much retain'd, do make vs much deſire them,
And bewties ſeldome ſeene, makes vs admire them.
