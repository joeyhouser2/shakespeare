With victorie reuenge doth euer ceaſe,
Hee liueth long that liues victorious.
The victor can no honour iuſtly claime,
To looſe the men who ſhould aduaunce the ſame.
—That fiſher is not fine,
Who for a frogge will looſe a golden line:
The holy head-band ſeemes not to attyre
The head of him, who in his furious ire,
Preferres the paine of thoſe that haue him teend,
Before the health and ſafetie of one friend.
Vaine is the vaunt and victorie vniuſt,
That more to mightie hands then rightful cauſe doth truſt.
Loſſe is no ſhame, nor to bee leſſe then foe,
But to be leſſer then himſelfe, doth marre
Both looſers lotte, and victors prayſe alſo,
Ʋaine others ouerthrowes, who ſelf doth ouerthrow.
