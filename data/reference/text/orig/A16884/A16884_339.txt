Great Nilus land, where raine doth neuer fall.
There quakes the plant, which in Pudefetan
Is call'd the ſhamefac't, for aſham'd of man,
If toward it one do approach too much,
It ſhrinkes the boughes, to ſhun our hatefull touch.
As if it had a ſoule, a ſenſe, and ſight,
Subiect to ſhame, feare, ſorow and deſpight.
