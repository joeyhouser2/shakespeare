On the one ſide doubt, on the other ſate Delaie,
Behind the gate, that none her might eſpie;
Whoſe manner was, all paſſengers to ſtaie,
And entertaine with her occaſions ſlie.
Through which, ſome loſt great hope vnheedilie,
Which neuer they recouer might againe:
And others quite excluded forth did lie.
Long languiſhing there, in vnpittied paine,
And ſeeking often entrance afterward in vaine.
—Daunger growes by lingring till the laſt,
And phiſicke hath no helpe when life is paſt.
—Oft things done, perhaps, do leſſe annoy
Then may the doing, handeled with delay.
Delaie in cloſe awaite
Caught hold on me, and thought my ſteps to ſtay,
Faining ſtil, many a fond excuſe, to prate:
And time, to ſteale the treaſure of mans day,
Whoſe ſmalleſt minute loſt, no riches render may.
—Times delay new hope of helpe ſtill breeds.
—Fearfull tormenting
Is leaden ſeruitor, to dull delay.
He that will ſtop the brooke muſt then begin
When ſommers heat hath dried vp the ſpring:
And when his pittering ſtreames are low and thin▪
For let the winter aid vnto them bring,
He growes to be of watry flouds the king:
And though you damme him vp with loftie rankes,
Yet will he quickly ouerflow his bankes.
Ill newes deferring, is a plague as great as an ill newes.
Delay in loue breeds doubts, but ſharpe deniall death·
— Intermiſſion ſuffers men diſpute,
What dangers are, and caſt with further care,
Colde doubt cauells with honour, ſcorneth fame,
And in the end feare waighes downe faith with ſhame▪
Where hearts be knit, what helpes if not in ioy?
Delay breeds doubts, no cunning to be ioy.
