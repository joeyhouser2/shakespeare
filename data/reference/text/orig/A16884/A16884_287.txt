Had ſtockt the whole world with a ſaint deſcent.
But ſuffering ſinne in Eden him inuade,
His ſonnes the ſoones of ſinne and wrath he made.

Of licour, ſuch as firſt was in them plaſte:

Some tameneſſe from their daunted youth to hold.
