Faſt by old age pale Maladie was plac't,
Sore ſicke in bed her colour all forgone,
Bereft of ſtomacke, ſauour, and of taſte,
Ne could ſhe brook no meate but broaths alone.
Abhorring her, her ſickneſſe paſt recure,
Deteſting phiſicke, and all phiſicks cure.
Sickneſſe the herauld of armes, hearts, and all.
Th'humorous ſicke, remouing, find no eaſe,
When chaunged chambers helpe not the diſeaſe.
— O ſickneſſe thou art oft betide,
When death hath many woes to come beſide.
