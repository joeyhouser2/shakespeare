He that will thriue, muſt thinke no courſes vile.
No hurt but good (who meanes to multiplie)
Bought wit is deare, and dreſt with ſower ſauce,
Repentance comes too late, and then ſay I,
Who ſpares the firſt, and keepes the laſt vnſpent▪
Shall find that ſparing yeelds a goodly rent.
Let firſt thine owne hand hold faſt all that comes,
But let the other learne his letting flie:
