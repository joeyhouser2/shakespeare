Dumbe Silence, ſworne attendant on black night,
Thou that haſt power to cloſe vp murmures iawe:
To ſtop the barking of the watchfull hound,
And charme the gagling of thoſe waking fowle,
That ſau'd Ioues Capitoll, milde Queene of reſt.
Soft Silence, and ſubmiſſe obedience,
Both linkt together neuer do depart:
Both gifts of God, nor gotten but from thence,
Both girlonds of his ſaints, againſt their foes offence.
—Silence wiſedomes mother.
Silence doth ſeem the maske of baſe oppreſſion.
