Like as the perfect Pylot feares to runne
Vpon the rocks, with ſingling ſheet doth ſhunne
Cydnaees ſtraits ot Syrtes ſinking ſands,
Or cruell Capharois with ſtormy ſtrands.
So wiſely ſhe diſhaunted the reſort
Of ſuch as were ſuſpect of light report.
Well knowing that the quaintance with the ill
Corrupts the good, and though they euer ſtill
* Looke how the peacocke ruffes his flanting taile,
And ſtrutts vnder his mooned canapie:
And how he quiuers with his mooned ſaile,
Yet when his lead pale legs he haps to ſee,
With ſhame abates his painted iollitie.
The King as proud as peacocke in his loue,
Yet droupes again when words nor tears will moue.
