—Ah that language ſweete
Sure bond of Cities friendſhips maſticke ſweete,
Strong curbe of anger yerſt vnited, now
In thouſand drie brookes ſtraies, I wot not how:
That rare rich gold, that charme griefe, fancie mouer•
That calm-rage, harts theefe, quel-pride coniure-louer,
That pureſt coine then currant in each coaſt
Now mingled, hath ſound, waight, and colour loſt.
Tis counterfeit, and ouer euery ſhoare,
The confuſd fall of Babell yet doth roare.
— Then all ſpake the ſpeech
Of God himſelfe, th'old ſacred Idiome rich,
Right perfect language, wher's no point nor ſigne.
But hides ſome rare deepe miſterie behind.
