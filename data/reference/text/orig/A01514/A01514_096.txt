But hold my tale to Rugge and all the reſt
Of good Grayes Inne, where honeſt Yeluerton,
And I Per ſe ſometimes yféere did reſt,
When amitie firſt in our breſts begonne,
Which ſhall endure as long as any Sunne
May ſhine on earth, or water ſwimme in Seas,
Let not my verſe your lawlike minds diſpleaſe,
