A Lady once did aſke of me,
This preatie thing in priuitie:
Good ſir (quod ſhe) faine would I craue,
One thing which you your ſelfe not haue:
Nor neuer had yet in times paſt,
Nor neuer ſhall while life doth laſt.
And if you ſéeke to find it out,
You looſe your labour out of doubt:
Yet if you loue me as you ſay,
Then giue it me, for ſure you may.
