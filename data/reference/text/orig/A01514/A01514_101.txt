But to conclude, I meane no more but thus,
In all eſtates ſome one may treade awrye,
And he that liſt my verſes to diſcuſſe,
Shall ſée I ment no more, but modeſtly
To warne the wiſe, that they ſuch faults do flie
As put downe peace by couine or debate,
Since warre and ſtrife bryng wo to euery ſtate.
