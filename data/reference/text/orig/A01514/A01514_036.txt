HOwe long ſhe lookt, that lookt at me of late,
As who would ſay, hir lookes were all for loue:
When God he knowes they came from deadly hate,
To pinch me yit with pangs which I muſt proue.
But ſince my lokes hir liking maye not moue,
Looke where ſhe likes, for lo this looke was caſt,
Not for my loue, but euen to ſee my laſt.
