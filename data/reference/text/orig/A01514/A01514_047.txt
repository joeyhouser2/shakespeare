IF yelding feare, or cancred villanie,
In Caeſars haughtie heart had tane the charge,
The walles of Rome had not bene rearde ſo hye,
Nor yet the mightye Empire left ſo large.
If Menelaus could haue ruld his wyll,
With fowle reproche to looſe his faire delight,
Then had the ſtately towres of Troy ſtoode ſtyll,
And Greekes with grudge had dronke their owne deſpight.
If dread of drenching waues or feare of fire,
Had ſtayde the wandring Prince amydde his race,
Aſcanius then, the fruite of his deſire,
In Lauine Lande had not poſſeſſed place.
But true it is, where lottes doe lyght by chaunce,
There Fortune helpes the boldeſt to aduaunce.
