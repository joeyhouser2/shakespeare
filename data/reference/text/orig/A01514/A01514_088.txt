Your ſkilfull minde (O Quéene without compare)
Can ſoone conceyue that cauſe conſtraynes me ſo,
Since wicked warres haue bredde ſuch cruell care,
In Flaunders, Fraunce, in Spaine and many mo,
Which reape thereby none other worth but wo:
Whiles you (meane while) enioy the fruites of peace,
Still prayſing God, whoſe bounties neuer ceaſe.
