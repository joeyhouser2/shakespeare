O noble Quéene, whoſe high foreſight prouides,
That waſt of warre, your realmes doth not deſtroye,
But pleaſaunt peace, and quiet concord glydes,
In euery coaſt, to driue out darke anoye,
O vertuous dame, I ſay Pardonez moy,
That I preſume in worthleſſe verſe to warne,
Thambitious Prince, his dueties to deſcerne.
