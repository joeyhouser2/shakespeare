THe Skies gan ſcowle, orecaſt with miſty clowdes,
When (as I rode alone by London waye,
Cloakeleſſe, vnclad) thus did I ſing and ſay:
Behold quoth I, bright Titan how he ſhroudes
His head abacke, and yelds the raine his reach,
Till in his wrath, Dan Ioue haue ſouſt the ſoile,
And waſht me wretch which in his trauaile toile.
But holla (here) doth rudeneſſe me appeach,
Since Ioue is Lord and king of mighty power,
Which can commaund the Sunne to ſhewe his face,
And (when him lyſt) to giue the raine his place.
Why doe not I my wery muſes frame,
(Although I bée well ſouſed in this ſhowre,)
To write ſome verſe in honour of his name?
