THe thriſtles thred which pampred beauty ſpinnes,
In thraldom binds the fooliſh gazing eyes:
As cruell Spiders with their crafty ginnes,
In worthleſſe webbes doe ſnare the ſimple Flies.
The garments gay, the glittring golden gite,
The tyſing talk which flowes from Pallas pooles:
The painted pale, the (too much) red made white,
Are ſmiling baytes to fiſhe for louing fooles.
But lo, when eld in toothleſſe mouth appeares,
And hoary heares in ſtéede of beauties blaze:
Than had I wiſt, doth teach repenting yeares,
The tickle track of craftie Cupides maze.
Twixt faire and foule therfore, twixt great and ſmall,
A louely nuthrowne face is beſt of all.
