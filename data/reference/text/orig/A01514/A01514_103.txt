To ſcourge the cryme of vvicked Laius,
And vvrecke the foule Inceſt of Oedipus,
The angry Gods ſtyrred vp theyr ſonnes, by ſtrife
VVith blades embrevved to reaue eache others life:
The vvife, the mother, and the concubyne,
(VVhoſe fearefull hart foredrad theyr fatall fine,)
Hir ſonnes thus dead, diſdayneth longer lyfe,
And ſlayes hirſelf vvith ſelfſame bloudy knyfe:
The daughter ſhe, ſurpriſde vvith childiſh dreade
(That durſt not dye) a lothſome lyfe doth leade,
Yet rather choſe to guide hir baniſht ſire,
Than cruell Creon ſhould haue his deſire.
Creon is King, the type of Tyranny,
And Oedipus, myrrour of miſery.
