For well wot you. our maſter Chriſt himſelfe,
Which had but twelue Apoſtles in his trayne,
Had Iudas yet, which ſolde for worldly pelfe
Our Sauiour: this text is true and playne:
And where ſo many Lawyers do remayne,
There may be ſome although that you be none,
Which bréede debate and loue to caſt a bone.
