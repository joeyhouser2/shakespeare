Of VVilton Grey (to whome theſe rimes I wrote)
With all the Barons hold of Engliſh ſoyle,
I humbly craue that it may be forgotte,
Although my Muze haue ſéemde to kéepe a coyle
With mighty men which put the weake to foyle:
I ment not you ſince, by your déedes appeares,
You rule with right, like wiſe and worthy peares.
