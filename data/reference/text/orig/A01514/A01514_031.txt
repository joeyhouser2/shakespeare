I Caſt mine eye and ſawe ten eyes at once,
All ſéemelye ſet vppon one louely face:
Twoo gaz'd, twoo glanc'd, twoo watched for the nonce,
Twoo winked wiles, twoo fround with froward grace.
Thus euerye eye was pitched in his place.
And euerye eye which wrought eche others wo,
Saide to it ſelfe, alas why lookt I ſo?
And euerye eye for ielouſie did pine,
And ſigh'd and ſayde, I would that eye were mine.
