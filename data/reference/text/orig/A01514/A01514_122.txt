NOt ſtately Troye though Priam yet did liue,
Could now compare Founteine bel'eaü to paſſe:
Nor Syrian towers, whoſe loftie ſteppes did ſtriue,
To climbe the throne where angry Saturne was.
For outward ſhew the ports are of ſuch price,
As ſkorne the coſt which Ceſar ſpilt in Roome:
Such works within as ſtayne the rare deuiſe,
Which whilome he Apelles wrought on toome.
Swift Tiber floud which fed the Romayne pooles,
Puddle to this where Chriſtall melts in ſtreames,
The pleaſaunt place where Muſes kept their ſchooles,
(Not parcht with Phoebe, nor baniſht from his beames)
Yeeld to thoſe Dames, nor ſight, nor fruite, nor ſmell,
Which may be thought theſe gardens to excell.
