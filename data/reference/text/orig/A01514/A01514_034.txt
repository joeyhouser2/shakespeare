A Lymone (but no Lemmane) Syr you found,
For Lemmans beare their name to broade before:
The which ſince it hath giuen you ſuch a wound,
That you ſéeme now offended very ſore:
Content your ſelfe you ſhall find (there) no more.
But take your Lemmans henceforth where you luſt,
For I wyll ſhewe my letters where I truſt.
