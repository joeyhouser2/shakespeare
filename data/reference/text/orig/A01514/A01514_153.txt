FAire Berſabe the bright once bathing in a Well,
With deawe bedimmd King Dauids eies that ruled Iſraell.
And Salomon himſelfe, the ſource of ſapience,
Against the force of ſuch aſſaultes could make but ſmall defence:
To it the ſtoutest yeeld, and ſtrongest feele like wo.
Bold Hercules and Sampſon both, did proue it to be ſo.
What wonder ſeemeth then? when ſtarres ſtand thicke in skies,
If ſuch a blaſing ſtarre haue power to dim my dazled eyes?
