IF thou ſitte here to viewe this pleaſant garden place,
Think thus: at laſt will come a froſt, & all theſe floures deface:
But if thou ſitte at eaſe to reſt thy wearie bones,
Remember death brings finall reſt to all oure gréeuous grones.
So whether for delight, or here thou ſitte for eaſe,
Thinke ſtill vpon the latter day, ſo ſhalt thou God beſt pleaſe.
