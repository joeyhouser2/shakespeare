All were to little for the merchauntes hande,
And yet my brauerye bigger than his booke:
But when this hotte accompte was coldly ſcande,
I thought highe time about me for to looke:
With heauie cheare I caſte my head abacke,
To ſée the fountaine of my furious race.
Comparde my loſſe, my liuing, and my lacke,
In equall balance with my iolye grace.
And ſawe expences grating on the grounde
Like lumpes of lead to preſſe my purſſe full ofte,
When light rewarde and recompence were founde,
Fléeting like feathers in the winde alofte:
Theſe thus comparde, I left the Courte at large,
For why? the gaines doth ſéeldome quitte the charge.
