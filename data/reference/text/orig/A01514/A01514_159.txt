A Cloud of care hath coured all my coſte,
And ſtormes of ſtrife doo threaten to appeare:
The waues of woo, which I mistrusted moste,
Haue broke the bankes wherein my life lay cleere:
Chippes of ill chaunce, are fallen amyd my choyce,
To marre the mynd, that ment for to reioyce.

Before J ſought, J founde the hauen of hap,
VVherin (once found) I ſought to shrowd my ship,
But lowring loue hath lifte me from hir lap,
And crabbed lot beginnes to hang the lip:
The proppes of darke miſtrust do fall ſo thick,
They pearce my coate, and touch my skin at quick.

VVhat may be ſaide, where truth cannot preuaile?
VVhat plea maie ſerue, where will it ſelfe is iudge?
VVhat reaſon rules, where right and reaſon faile?
Remedileſſe then muſt the guiltleſſe trudge:
And ſeeke out care, to be the caruing knife,
To cut the thred that lingreth ſuch a life.
