If you (my liege) vouchſafe in gratious wiſe,
To pardon that which paſſeth from my Muſe,
Then care I not what other kings deuiſe,
In warres defenſe: nor though they me accuſe,
And ſay that I their bloudie déedes abuſe:
Your onely grace my ſoueraigne Lady be,
Let other Kings thinke what they liſt of me.
