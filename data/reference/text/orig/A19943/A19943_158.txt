OFt did I heare, our Eyes the paſſage were,
By which Loue entred to auaile our hearts;
Therefore I guarded them, and voyd of feare
Neglected the defence of other parts.
Loue knowing this, the vſuall way forſooke;
And ſeeking, found a by-way by mine Eare:
At which hee entring, my Hart pris'ner tooke,
And vnto thee ſweet Philomel did beare.
Yet let my hart, thy hart to pittie moue,
Whoſe paine is great, although ſmal fault appeare
Firſt it lies bound in fettering chaines of Loue,
Then each day it is rackt with hope and feare.
And with loues flames t'is euermore conſumed,
Only, becauſe to loue thee it preſumed.
