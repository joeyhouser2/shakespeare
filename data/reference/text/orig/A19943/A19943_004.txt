IOYNE Mates in mirth to me,
Graunt pleaſure to our meeting:
Let Pan our good God ſee,
How gratefull is our greeting.
Ioyne hearts and hands, ſo let it be,
Make but one Minde in Bodies three.

Ye Hymnes, and ſinging skill
Of God Apolloes giuing,
Be preſt our reedes to fill,
With ſound of muſicke liuing.
Ioyne hearts and hands, &c.

Sweete Orpheus Harpe, whoſe ſound
The ſtedfaſt mountaynes moued,
Let heere thy skill abound,
To ioyne ſweete friends beloued.
Ioyne hearts and hands, &c.

Now ioyned be our hands,
Let them be ne'r a ſunder,

By metamorphoz'd wonder.

As one for euer ioyned bee.
