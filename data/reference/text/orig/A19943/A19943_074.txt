REaſon and Loue lately at ſtrife, contended,
Whoſe Right it was to haue my minds protectiō,
Reaſon on his ſide, Natures wil pretended,
Loues Title was, my Miſtreſſe rare perfection.
Of power to ende this ſtrife, each makes election,
Reaſons pretence diſcourſiue thoughts defended;
But loue ſoon broght thoſe thoghts into ſubiectiō
By Beauties troopes, which on my ſaint depended.
Yet, ſince to rule the minde was Reaſons dutie,
On this Condition it by loue was rendred,
That endles Praiſe by Reaſon ſhould be tendred,
As a due Tribute to her conquering Beautie.
Reaſon was pleaſde withall, and to loues Royalty,
He pledg'de my Hart, as Hoſtage for his Loyalty.
