AS ſoone may water wipe me drie,
And fire my heate allay,
As you with fauour of your eye,
Make hotte deſire decay:
The more I haue,
The more I craue;
The more I craue, the more deſire,
As piles of wood encreaſe the fire.

The ſenceleſſe ſtone that from on hie
Deſcends to Earth below,
With greater haſte it ſelfe doth plie,
The leſſe it hath to goe:
So feeles deſire
Encreaſe of fire,
That ſtill with greater force doth burne,
Till all into it ſelfe it turne.

The greater fauour you beſtow,
The ſweeter my delight;
And by delight Deſire doth grow,
And growing gathers might.
The leſſe remaines,
The more my paines,
To ſee my ſelfe ſo neere the brinke,
And yet my fill I cannot drinke.
