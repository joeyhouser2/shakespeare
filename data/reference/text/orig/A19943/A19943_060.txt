Shut not (ſweet Breaſt) to ſee me all of fire.
Flie not (deere Hart) to find me al of ſnowe.
Thy ſnow inflames theſe flames of my deſire.
And I deſire, Deſiers ſweet flames to know.
Thy Snow n'ill hurt me.
Nor thy Fire will harme me.
This cold will coole me.
And this heate wil warme me.
Take this chaſte fire to that pure virgin ſnow▪
Being now thus warm'd, Ile n'ere ſeek other fire▪
Thou giu'ſt more blis thā mortal harts may know
More bliſſe I take than Angells can deſire.
Let one griefe harme vs;
And let one ioy fill vs:
Let one loue warme vs;
And let one death kill vs.
