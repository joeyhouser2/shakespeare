WIſdome warns me to ſhun that once I ſought for
And in time to retire my haſty footſteps:
Wiſdome ſent from aboue, not earthly wiſdome,
No ſuch thoughts can ariſe from earthly wiſdome.
Long, too long haue I ſlept in eaſe vneaſie,
On falce worldly releefe my truſt repoſing;
Health and wealth in a bote, no ſterne nor ankor,
(Bold and blinde that I was) to Sea be taking:
Scarce from ſhore had I lancht, when all about mee,
Waues like hilles did ariſe, till help from heauen,
Brought my Ship to the Porte of late repentance.

O nauis, referent in mare te noui
Fluctus.—
