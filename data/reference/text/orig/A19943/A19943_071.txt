FAyre is thy face, and great thy wits perfection,
So fayre alas, ſo hard to bee expreſt,
That if my tyred pen ſhould neue• reſt,
It ſhould not blaze thy worth, but my affection.
Yet let me ſay, the Muſes make election
Of your pure minde, there to erect their neaſt,
And that your face is ſuch a flint-hard breaſt,
By force thereof, without force feeles ſubiection.
Witnes mine Eare, rauiſht when you it heares,
Witneſſe mine Eyes rauiſht when you they ſee,
Beauty and Vertue, witneſſe Eyes and Eares,
In you (ſweet Saint) haue equall ſoueraingntie.
But if, nor Eyes, nor Eares, can prooue it true,
Witneſſe my Hart, their's none that equalls you.
