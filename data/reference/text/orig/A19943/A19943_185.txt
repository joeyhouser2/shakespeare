WEre I as baſe as is the lowly playne,
And you (my Loue,) as high as heau'n aboue,
Yet ſhould the thoughts of me your humble ſwaine,
Aſcend to Heauen, in honour of my Loue.
Were I as hight as Heau'n aboue the playne,
And you (my Loue) as humble and as low
As are the deepeſt bottoms of the Mayne,
Wherso'ere you were, with you my Loue ſhould go
Were you the Earth (deere Loue) and I the skies,
My loue ſhould ſhine on you like to the Sun,
And looke vpon you with ten thouſand Eyes,
Till heau'n wax't blind, and til the world were dun
Whereſo'ere I am, below, or els aboue you,
Whereſo'ere you are, my hart ſhal truly loue you
