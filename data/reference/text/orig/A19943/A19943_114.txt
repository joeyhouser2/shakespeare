That himſelfe too much eſteemeth.

•oy diſdaine hath kindnes place,

True Deſire is counted baſe,
Hope with hope is hardly fed:
Loue is thought a fury needleſſe,
Hee that hath it, ſhal die ſpeedleſſe.

Then mine eies, why gaze you ſo?
Beautie ſcornes the Teares you ſhed;
Death you ſeeke to end my woe,
O that you of death were ſped!
But with Loue hath death conſpired,
To kill none whom Loue haue fired.
