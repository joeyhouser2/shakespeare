LIke a Sea-toſſed Barke with tackling ſpent,
And Starres obſcur'd his watry iornies guide
By lowd tempeſtuous windes and raging tide,
From waue to waue with dreadfull fury ſent,
Fares my poore Hart; my Hart-ſtrings being rent,
And quite diſabled your fierce wrath to bide,
Since your faire eies my Stars thēſelues do hide
Clouding their light in frownes and diſcontent.
For from your frowns do ſpring my ſighes & teares
Teares flow like ſeas, & ſighes like winds do bloe
Whoſe ioyned rage moſt violently beares
My Tempeſt-beaten hart from woe to woe.
And if your Eyes ſhine not that I may ſhun it,
On Rocke, deſpaire, my ſighes, and teares wil run it
