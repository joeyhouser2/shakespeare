ANd would you ſee my Miſtres face?
It is a flowry garden-place:
Where knots of beauty haue ſuch grace,
That al is worke, and no where ſpace.

It is a ſweet delicious Morne,
Where day is breeding, neuer borne:
It is a Meadow yet vnſhorne,
Which thouſand flowers do adorne.

It is the Heauens bright reflexe,
Weake eyes to dazle and to vexe:
It is th'Idaea of her ſex,
Enuie of whom doth world perplex.

It is a face of death that ſmiles,
Pleaſing, though it kill the whiles:
Where death and loue in pretty wiles,
Each other mutually beguiles.

It is fayre Beauties freſheſt youth,
It is the fain'd Eliziums truth:
The ſpring that wintred Harts renu'th,
And this is that my Soule purſu'th.
