BEſt pleas'd ſhee is, when Loue is moſt expreſt,
And ſomtime ſaies that loue ſhold be requite•
Yet is ſhe grieu'd my loue ſhould now be righte•
When that my faith hath prou'd what I proteſt.
Am I belou'd whoſe hart is thus oppreſt?
Or deere to her, and not in her delighted:
I liue to ſee the Sun, yet ſtill benighted,
By her deſpayre is blam'de, and hope ſuppreſt.
Shee ſtill denies, yet ſtill her hart conſenteth,
Shee grants mee all, but that which I deſire;
Shee fuell ſends, but bids mee leaue the fire,
Shee lets me die, and yet my death lamenteth.
O fooliſh Loue, by reaſon of thy blindnes,
I die for want of Loue, yet kild with kindnes.
