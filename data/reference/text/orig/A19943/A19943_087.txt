SOme men, they ſay, are Poets borne by kinde,
And ſuck that ſcience from their mothers breſt:
An eaſie Arte that comes with ſo great reſt,
And happy men to ſo good hap aſſignde.
In ſome, deſire of praiſe enflames the minde,
To clime with paine Parnaſſus double creſt:
Some, hope of rich Rewardes hath ſo poſſeſt,
That Gold, in Caſtall Sands they ſeeke to finde.
Me, neither Nature hath a Poet made,
Nor loue of Glory mou'de to learne the trade,
Nor thirſt of Golde perſwaded for to write;
For Natures graces are too fine for mee,
Praiſe like the Peacockes pride her ſelfe to ſee,
Deſire of Gaine the baſeſt mindes delight.
