MIne eie with all the deadly ſinnes is fraught,
1. Firſt prowd, ſith it preſum'd to looke ſo hie:
a watchman being made, ſtoode gazing by,
2. and idle, tooke no heede till I was caught:
And enuious, beares enuy that by thought
ſhould in his abſence be to her ſo nie:
to kill my heart, mine eye let in her eie,
4. and ſo conſent gaue to a murther wrought:
5. And couetous, it neuer would remoue
from her faire haire, gold ſo doth pleaſe his ſight
6. vnchaſte, a bawde betweene my heart and loue
7. a glutton eye, with teares drunke euery night.
Theſe ſinnes procured haue a Goddeſſe ire:
Wherefore my heart is damnd in Loues ſweete fire
