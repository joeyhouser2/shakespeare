YE walles that ſhut me vp from ſight of men,
Incloſde wherein, aliue I buried lie.
And thou, ſometime my bed, but now my den,
Where, ſmothred vp the light of Sunne, I flie.
O ſhut your ſelues, ech chinke and creuis ſtraine,
That none but you may heare me thus complain.

My hollow cries that beate thy ſtony ſide,
Vouchſafe to beate, but beate them backe againe,
That when my griefe hath ſpeech to me denide,
Mine eares may heare the witnes of my paine.
As for my Teares, whoſe ſtreames muſt euer laſt
My ſilent cowch ſhall drinke them vp as faſt.
