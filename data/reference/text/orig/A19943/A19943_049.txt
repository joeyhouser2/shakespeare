O Hand of all handes liuing,
The ſofteſt, moiſteſt, whiteſt,
•ore skild then Phoebus on a Lute in running;
•ore then Minerua, with a Needle cunning;
Then Mercury more wily,
In ſtealing Harts moſt ſliely.
Since thou, deere Hand, in theft ſo much delighteſt,
Why fall'ſt thou now a giuing?
Ay mee! thy gifts are thefts, and with ſtrange Art,
In giuing me thy Gloue, thou ſtealſt my Hart.
