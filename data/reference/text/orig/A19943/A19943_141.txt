THe Bull by nature hath his hornes,
The Horſe his hooues to daunt their foes,
The light-foot Hare the hunter ſcornes,
The Lions teeth his ſtrength diſcloſe.

The Fiſh, by ſwimming, ſcapes the weele,
The Bird, by ſlight, the fowlers net.
With wiſedome Man is arm'd as ſteele,
Poore women none of theſe can get.

What haue they then? faire Beauties grace,
A two-edg'd Sworde, a truſty Shielde,
No force reſiſts a louely face,
Both fire and ſworde to Beautie yielde.
