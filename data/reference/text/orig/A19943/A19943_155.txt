•Yme, poore Soule, whom bound in ſinful chains
This wretched body keepes againſt my will!
Aye mee poore Body, whom for all my paines,
This froward ſoule cauſleſſe condemneth ſtil.
Cauſles? whenas thou ſtriu'ſt to ſin each day?
Cauſles: whenas I ſtriue thee to obay.
Thou art the meanes, by which I fall to ſin,
Thou art the cauſe that ſet'ſt this means awork
No part of thee that hath not faultie bin:
I ſhew the poyſon that in thee doth lurke.
I ſhall be pure when ſo I part from thee:
So were I now, but that thou ſtaineſt mee.
