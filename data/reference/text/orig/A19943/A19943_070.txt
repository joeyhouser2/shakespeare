BVt if my Lines may not be held excuſed,
Nor yet my Loue finde fauour in your Eyes,
But that your Eyes as Iudges ſhall be vſed,
Euen of the fault which frō themſelues doth riſe,
Yet this my humble ſuite do not deſpiſe,
Let mee bee iudged as I ſtand accuſed,
If but my fault my doome doe equaliſe,
What er'e it bee, it ſhal not be refuſed.
And ſince my Loue already is expreſſed,
And that I cannot ſtand vpon deniall,
I freely put my ſelfe vpon my triall,
Let Iuſtice doome mee as I haue confeſſed.
For in my Doome if Iuſtice bee regarded,
My Loue with Loue againe ſhall bee rewarded.
