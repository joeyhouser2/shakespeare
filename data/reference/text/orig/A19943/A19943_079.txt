I Haue entreated, and I haue complained,
I haue diſprays'd, and prayſe I like wiſe gaue,
All meanes to win her Grace I tryed haue,
And ſtill I loue, and ſtill I am diſdained.
So long I haue my Tongue and Pen conſtrained,
To praiſe, diſpraiſe, complaine, and pitty craue,
That now, nor Tongue, nor Pen, to me her ſlaue
Remaines, whereby her Grace may be obtained.
Yet you (my Sighs) may purchace mee releefe,
And yee (my Teares) her rocky hart may moue;
Therefore my ſighes ſigh in her eares my greefe,
And in her Hart my Teares imprint my Loue.
But ceaſe vaine ſighes, ceaſe ceaſe yee fruitles teares,
Teares cannot pierce her Hart, nor ſighes hir Eares.
