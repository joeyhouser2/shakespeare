WHen will the fountaine of my Teares be drie?
When will my ſighes be ſpent?
When will Deſire agree to let me die?
When will thy hart relent?
It is not for my life I pleade,
Since death the way to reſt doth leade,
But ſtay for thy conſent,
Leſt thou be diſcontent.

For if my ſelfe without thy leaue I kill,
My Ghoſt will neuer reſt:
So hath it ſworne to worke thine only will,
And holds that euer beſt.
For ſince it only liues by thee,
Good reaſon thou the ruler bee:
Then giue me leaue to die,
And ſhew thy powre thereby.
