OF Atreus Sonnes faine would I write,
And faine of Cadmus would I ſing:
My Lute is ſet on Loues delight,
And onely Loue ſounds eu'ry ſtring.

Of late my Lute I alt'red quite,
Both frets and ſtrings for tunes aboue,
I ſung of fierce Alcides might,
My Lute would ſound no tune but Loue,
Wherefore yee worthles all farewell,
No tune but Loue my Lute can tell.
