BReake heauy hart, and rid mee of this paine,
This paine that ſtill encreaſeth day by day:
By day with ſighes I ſpend my ſelfe in vaine,
In vayne by night with teares I waſte away:
Away I waſte with teares by night in vaine,
Teares, ſighs, by night, by day encreaſe this paine.

Mine Eyes no Eies, but fountaines of my teares,
My teares no teares, but floods to moyſt my hart:
My hart no hart, but harbour of my feares,
My feares no feares, but feelings of my ſmart:
My ſmart, my feares, my hart, my teares, mine eies
Are blind, dryde, ſpent, paſt, waſted with my cries.

And yet mine Eyes, thogh blind, ſee cauſe of greefe:
And yet my teares, thogh dride, run down amaine:
And yet my hart, though ſpent, attends releefe,
And yet my feares, though paſt, encreaſe my paine:
And yet I liue, and liuing, feele more ſmart,
And ſmarting, cry in vaine, breake heauy hart.
