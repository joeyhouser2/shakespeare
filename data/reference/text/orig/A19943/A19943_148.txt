Begin, and halfe is done, yet halfe vndone remaines,
Begin that half, & al is done, & thou art eaſd of pains
The ſecond halfe is all, when halfe thereof is dun,
The other halfe is al again, new work muſt be begun
Thus he that ſtil begins, doth nothing but by halues,
And things half done, as good vndone, half oxen are but calues.
