Of all my old Delights yet one was left,
Painting alone to eaſe my minde remain'd;
By which, whenas I look't to be bereft
Of theſe heart-vexing woes that ſtill me ſtrain'd,
From forth mine eies the blood for colours came,
And teares withall to temper ſo the ſame.

Adieu my foode that wontſt my taſte to pleaſe,

Painting, Adieu, that oft refreſht my ſight,
Since neither taſte, nor eares, nor ſight, nor mind,
