PAle Death himſelfe did Loue my Philomel,
When hee her Vertues and rare beutie ſaw:
Therefore hee ſickneſſe ſent, which ſhould expell,
His Riuall life, and my Decre to him draw.
But her bright beauty dazeled ſo his Eyes,
That his dart life did miſſe, though her it hitt:
Yet not therewith content, new meanes hee tries,
To bring her vnto Death, and make life flitt.
But Nature ſoone perceiuing, that hee meant
To ſpoyle her only Phoenix, her chiefe pride:
Aſſembled all her force, and did preuent
The greateſt miſchiefe that could her betide.
So both our liues and loues Nature defended,
For had ſhee dide, my loue and life had ended.
