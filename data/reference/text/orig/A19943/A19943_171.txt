WHen to her Lute Corinna ſings,
Her voyce reuiues the leaden ſtrings,
And doth in higheſt notes appeere,
As any challeng'd Eccho cleere.
But when ſhee doth of mourning ſpeake,
Eu'n with her ſighes the ſtrings do breake.

And as her Lute doth liue or die,
Led by her paſſions, ſo muſt I:
For when of pleaſure ſhee doth ſing,
My thoughts enioy a ſodaine ſpring:
But if ſhe doe of ſorrow ſpeake,
Eu'n from my heart the ſtrings doe breake.
