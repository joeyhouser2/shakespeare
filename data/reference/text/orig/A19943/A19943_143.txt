NAture in her worke doth giue,
To each thing that by her doth liue:
A proper gift whereby ſhee may,
Preuent in time her owne decay.
The Bull a horne, the horſe a hoofe,
The light-foote hare to run aloofe:
The Lyons ſtrength who may reſiſt,
The birds aloft, flie where they liſt.
The fiſh ſwimmes ſafe in waters deepe,
The ſilly worme at leaſt can creepe:
What is to come, men can forecaſt,
And learne more witt, by that is paſt:
The womans gift what might it bee,
The ſame for which the Ladies three,
Pallas, Iuno, Venus ſtraue,
When each deſired it to haue.
