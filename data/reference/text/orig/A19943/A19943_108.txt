VNhappy Eies, the cauſers of my paine,
That to my foe betray'd my ſtrongeſt hold,
Wherein, he like a Tyrant now doth raigne,
And boaſts of winning that which treaſon ſolde.
Too late you call for help of me in vaine,
Whom Loue hath bound in chaines of maſſie gold;
The teares you ſhed increaſe my hote deſire,
As water on the Smithie kindles fire.
The ſighs that from my Heart aſcend,
Like winde diſperſe the flame throughout my breſt,
No part is left to harbour quiet reſt,
I burne in fire and do not ſpend;
Like him, whoſe growing maw,
The vulture ſtill doth gnaw.
