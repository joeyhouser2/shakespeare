OUr Ioy is ioynd with greuous groens,
Our triumphe tornd to tears:
The brantch whoes blosoms gladnes broght,
a bitter berry bears.
In howse and street whear mirth was hard,
is moen and moorning noies:
The sommer day is dimd with clowds,
eclypsed aer our ioyes.
The loedstar leaus our wished cowrs,
and clims the heauens hie:
Our sofrant will no longer lord,
in walls of Brislow lye.
No maruell sins our barrain soyl,
and ground of groes deuyce:
Haeth yelded no thing that might pleas,
a prince of so great price.
Our deuties aer not half dischargd,
no thoghe we kist the grownd:
And prostraet fall full flat on face,
whear her foet steps aer fownd.
