# Edited texts (19th-century editions)

These texts come from public-domain modern editions, used as stopgaps where no early print or digitized manuscript was available. They are **not** original-spelling witnesses.

- **Spelling is modernized by the editor.** Use these only after every text has been put through the same normalization layer. Leave them out of spelling-habit features and out of character n-grams computed on original spelling.
- **Punctuation and capitalization are editorial.**

Not found in Hannah 1870: May No. 12 ("Winged with desire") and No. 13 ("Whereas the heart at tennis plays"). Grosart's *Miscellanies of the Fuller Worthies' Library* (1872) prints the Oxford poems, but the only online copies found are Internet Archive's lending-only 1970 AMS reprint and HathiTrust, which blocks automated access. Those two poems still need a person to read Grosart in a browser or library, or a look at the manuscript (Bodleian MS Rawl. poet. 85).
