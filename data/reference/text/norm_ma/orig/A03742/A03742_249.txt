Thence comes that oft in deep despair to rise to better state.
On heaven and heavenly lamps he layeth the fault of all his fate.
On God and Gods decreed dome cryeth out with cursing breath,
Each thing that gave and saves him life he damneth of his death.
The womb him bare, the breasts he sucked, each star that with their might.
