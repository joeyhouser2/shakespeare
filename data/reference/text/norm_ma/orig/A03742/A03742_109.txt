Sufficed not (madame) that you did tear,
My woful hart, but thus also to rent:
The weeping paper that to you I sent.
Whereof each letter was written with a tear.
Could not my present paines• alas suffise.
Your greedy hart? and that my hart doth feel,
Torments that prick more sharper then the stele,
But new and new must to my lot arise.
Use then my death. So shall your cruelty:
Spite of your spite rid me from all my smart,
And I no more such torments of the hart:
Feel as I do. This shall you gain thereby.
