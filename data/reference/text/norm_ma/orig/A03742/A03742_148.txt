The blazing brands, that never do consume:
The roring rout, in Plutoes den that dwell,
The fiery breath, that from those ympes doth fume:
The dropsy drieth, that Tantale in the flood
Endureth ay, all hopeless of relief:
He honger steruen, where fruit is ready food
So wretchedly his soul doth suffer grief:
The liver gnawn of gylefull Promethus.
Which Uultures fell with strained talant tire:
The labour lost of wearied Sisiphus:
These hellish hounds, with pains of quenchless fire,
Can not so sore the silly souls torment,
As her untruth my hart hath all to rent.
