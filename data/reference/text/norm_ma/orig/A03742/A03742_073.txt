What man hath heard such cruelty before?
That, when my plaint remembered her my woe,
That caused it: she cruel more and more,
Wished each stitch, as she did sit and sow,
Had pricked my hart, for to encrease my sore,
And, as I think, she thought it had been so.
For as she thought, this is his hart in deed:
She pricked hard: and made her self to bled.
