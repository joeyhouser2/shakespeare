I Find no peace, and all my war is done:
I feare• and hope: I burn, and frese like yse:
I fly aloft, yet can I not arise:
And nought I have, and all the world I season.
That locks not loseth, holdeth me in prison,
And holds me not, yet can I scape no wise:
Nor lettes me live, nor die, at my devise,
And yet of death it giveth me occasion.
Without eye I se, without tong I plain:
I wish to perish, yet I ask for health:
I love another, and I hate my self.
I fede me in sorrow, and laugh in all my pain.
Lo, thus displeaseth me both death and life.
And my delight is causer of this strife.
