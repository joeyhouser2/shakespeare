Slond who so list upon the slipper wheel,
Of hye astate and let me here rejoice.
And use my life in quietness each dele,
Unknown in court that hath the wanton toys,
In hidden place my time shall slowly pass
And when my years be past withouten noise
Let me die old after the common trace
For gripes of death doth he to hardly pass
That known is to all: but to him self alas,
He dyeth unknown, dased with dreadful face.
