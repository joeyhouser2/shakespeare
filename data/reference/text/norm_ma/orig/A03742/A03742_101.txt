Like unto these unmeasurable mountains,
So is my painful life, the burden of yre.
For hye be they, and hye is my desire.
And I of tears, and they be full of fountains.
Under craggy rocks they have barren plains,
Hard thoughts in me my woeful mind doth tire,
Small frute and many leaves their tops do attire,
With small effect great trust in me remains.
The boistous windes oft their hie boughs do blast:
Hote sighs in me continually be shed.
wild beasts in them, fierce love in me is fed.
Unmoveable am I: and they stedfast.
Of singing birds they have the tune and note:
And I always plaints passing through my throte.
