Love, that liveth, and reigneth in my thought,
That built his seat within my captive brest,
Clad in the arms, wherein with me he fought,
Oft in my face he doth his banner rest.
She. that me taught to love, and suffer pain,
My doubtful hope, and eke my hot desire,
with shamefast cloke to shadow and restrain,
Her smiling grace converteth straight to yre.
And coward love then to the hart apace
Taketh his flight, whereas he lurks and plains
His purpose lost, and dare not shew his face.
For my lords gilt thus faultless bide I pains,
Yet from my lord shall not my foot remove.
Sweet is his death, that takes his end by love.
