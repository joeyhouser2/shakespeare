With hart as true as any lover might.
Yet her betide in loving this unright
That as in hart with love surprised thus
She on a day to see this Cephalus
Where he was wont to shroud him in the shade
When of his hunting he an end had made.
Within the woods with dreadful lote forth stalketh
So bussly love in her hed it walketh.
That she to seen him may her not restrain.
This Cephalus that heard one shake the leaves
Uprist all egre thrusting after pray,
With dart in hand him list no further dame,
To see his love but slew her in the greaves,
That ment to him but perfect love alway.
So curious bene alas the rites all,
Of mighty love that unnethes may I think,
In his high service how to look or wink.
Thus I complain that wretchedest am of all,
To you my love, and sovereign lady dere,
That may my hart with death or life steer
As ye best list. That ye vouchsafe in all
Mine humble service. And if me misfall,
By negligence, or else for lack of wit.
