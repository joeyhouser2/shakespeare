The answer that ye made to me my dere,
When I did sue for my poor hearts redress:
Hath so appalde my countenance and my cheer:
That in this case, I am all comfortless:
Sins I of blame no cause can well express.
I have no wrong, where I can claim no right.
Nought tane me fro, where I have nothing had,
Yet of my woe, I can not so be quite.
Namely, sins that another may be glad
With that, that thus in sorrow makes me sad.
Yet none can claim (I say) by former graunt,
That knoweth not of any graunt at all.
And by desert, I dare well make avaunt,
Of faithful will, there is no where that shall
Bear you more truth, more ready at your call.
Now good then, call again that bitter word:
That touched your friend so nere with pangs of pain:
And say my dere that it was said in bord.
Late, or to soon, let it not rule the gain,
Wherewith free will doth true desert retain.
