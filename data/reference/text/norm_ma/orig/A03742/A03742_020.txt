Geve place ye lovers, here before
That spent your bostes and brags in vain,
My Ladies beauty passeth more
The best of yours, I dare well sayen,
Then doth the son, the candle light,
Or brightest day, the darkest night,
And thereto hath a troth as just,
As had Penelope the fair,
For what she saith, ye may it trust.
As it by writing sealed were,
And virtues hath she many moe,
Than I with pen have skill to show.
I could rehearse, if that I wold
The whole effect of natures plaint,
when she had lost the perfect mould,
The like to whom she could not paint:
with wringing hands how she did cry,
And what she said, I know it, I.
I know, she swore with raging mind.
Her kingdom only set apart,
There was no loss, by law of kind,
That could have gone so nere her hart.
And this was chiefly all her pain:
She could not make the like again.
Sith nature thus gave her the praise
To be che chiefest work she wrought:
In faith me think some better ways
On your behalf might well be sought,
Then to compare (as ye have done)
To match the candle with the sun.
