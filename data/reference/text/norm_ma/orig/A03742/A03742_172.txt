Nature that taught my silly dog god wat:
Even for my sake to like where I do love,
Inforced him whereas my lady sat
With humble sute before her falling flat.
As in his sort he might her pray and move
To rue upon his lord and not forget
The stedfast faith he beareth her and love,
Kissing her hand whom she could not remove.
A way that would for frowning nor for threte
As though he would have said in my behove.
Pity my lord your slave that doth remain,
Lest by his death, you guiltless slay us twaine.
