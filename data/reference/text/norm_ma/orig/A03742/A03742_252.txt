NO joy have I, but live in heaviness,
My dame of price bereft by fortunes cruelness,
My hap is turned to unhappiness,
Unhappy I am unless I find relesse.
My pastime past, my youthlike years are gone,
My mouths of mirth, my glistering days of gladsomnesse:
My times of triumph turned into mone.
Unhappy I am unless I find relesse.
My wonted wind to chaunt my cheerful chance,
Doth sigh that song sometime the ballads of my less:
My sobs, my sore and sorrow do advance.
Unhappy I am unless I find relesse.
I mourn my mirth for grief that it is gone,
I mourn my mirth whereof my musing mindefulnesse:
Is ground of greater grief that grows theron,
Unhappy I am unless I find relesse.
No joy have I: for fortune frowardly:
Hath bent her browes hath put her hand to cruelness:
Hath rest my dame, constrained me to cry,
Unhappy I am unless I find relesse.
