The piller perished is whereto I lent,
The strongest stay of mine unquiet mind:
The like of it no man again can find:
From East to West still seeking though he went.
To mine unhap for happen away hath rent,
Of all my joy the very bark and rind:
And I (alas) by chance am thus assigned,
Daily to moorne till death do it relent.
But sins that thus it is by destiny,
What can I more but have a woeful hart,
My pen, in plaint, my voice in careful cry:
My mind in woe, my body full of smart,
And I my self, my self always to hate,
Till dreadful death do ease my doleful state.
