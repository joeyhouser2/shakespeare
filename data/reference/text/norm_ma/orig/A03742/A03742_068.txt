From these hie hills as when a spring doth fall,
It trilleth down with still and suttle course,
Of this and that it gathers ay and shall,
Till it have just down flowed to stream and force:
Then at the foot it rageth over all.
So fareth love, when he hath •ane a source.
Rage is his rain. Resistance vaileth none.
The first eschew is remedy alone.
