A valiant corpse, where force, and beauty met:
Happy, alas, to happy, but for foes:
Lived, and ran the race, that nature set:
Of manhoods shape, where she the mold did lose.
But to the heavens that simple soul is fled:
Which left with such, as covet Christ to know,
Witness of faith, that never shall be ded:
Sent for our health, but not received so.
Thus, for our guilt, this jewel have we lost:
The earth his bones, the heavens possess his gost.
