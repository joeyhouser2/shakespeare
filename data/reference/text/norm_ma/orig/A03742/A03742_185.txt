AS Cypress tree that rent is by the root,
As branch or slip bereft fron whence it grows
As well sowen seed for drought that can not sprout
As gaping ground that raineles can not close
As moules that want the earth to do them bote
As fish on land to whom no water flows,
As Thameleon that lacks the air so sote,
As flowers do fade when Phebus rarest shows.
As Salamandra repulsed from the fire:
So wanting my wish I die for my desire
