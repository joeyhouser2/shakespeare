Holding my peace alas how loud I cry,
Pressed with hope and dread even both at ones,
Strained with death, and yet I cannot die.
Burning in flame, quaking for cold that groans,
Unto my hope withouten wings I fly.
Pressed with dispayre, that breaketh all my bones.
Walking as if I were, and yet am not.
Feigning with mirth, most inwardly with mones.
Hard by my help, unto my health not nye.
Mids of the calm my ship on rock it rones.
I serve unbound, fast fettered yet I lie.
In stead of milk that fede on marble stones,
My most will is that I do espy:
That works my joys and sorrows both at ones.
In contrairs standeth all my loss and gain:
And lo the guiltless causeth all my pain.
