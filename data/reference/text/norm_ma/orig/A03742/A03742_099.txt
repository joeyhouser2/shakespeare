Love, Fortune, and my mind which do remember
Eke that is now, and that that once hath bene:
Torment my hart so sore that very often
I hate and envy them beyond all measure.
Love sleeth my hart while Fortune his deprivee
Of all my comfort: the foolish mind than:
Burneth and plainth: as one that very sildam.
Liveth in rest. So still in displeasure
My pleasant days they fleet away and pass.
And daily doth mine yll change to the worse.
while more then half is run now of my course.
Alas not of stele, but of brittle glass,
I se that from my hand falleth my trust:
And all my thoughts are dashed into dust.
