Full fair and white she is, and White by name:
Whose white doth strive, the lilli•s white to stain:
Who may contemn the blast of black defame:
who in dark night, can bring day bright again.
The ruddy rose inpreaseth with clear heew,
In lips and cheeks, right orient to behold:
That the nearer gaser may that beauty reew,
And feel dispar•t in limbs the chilling cold:
For White, all white his bloodless face will be:
The ashy pale so alter will his cheare.
But I that do possess in full degree
The hearty love of this my hart so deare:
So oft to me as she presents her face,
For joy do feel my hart spring from his place.
