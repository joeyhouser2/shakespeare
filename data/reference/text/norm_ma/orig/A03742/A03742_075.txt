What vaileth troth? or by it, to take pain
To strive by steadfastness, for to attain
How to be just: and flee from doubleness?
Since all alike, where ruleth carftinesse,
Rewarded is both crafty false, and plain.
Soonest he speeds, that most can lie and fain.
True meaning hart is had in hi• disdain.
Against deceit, and cloaked doubleness,
What vaileth troth, or parfit steadfastness.
Deceived is he, by false and crafty train,
That means no gile, and faithful doth remain
Within the trapped, without help or redress.
But for to love (lo) such a stern mistress,
Where cruelty dwells, alas it were in vain.
