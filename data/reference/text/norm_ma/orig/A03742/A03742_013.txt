I Never saw my Lady laye apart
Her cornet black, in cold nor yet in heat,
Sith first she knew my grief was growen so great,
Which other fancies driveth from my hart.
That to my self I do the thought reserve,
The which unwares did wound my woful brest,
For on her face mine eyes mought never rest,
Sins that she knew I did her love and serve,
Her golden tress is clad alway with black,
Her smiling lokes to hide thus evermore,
And that restrains which I desire so sore.
So doth this corner govern my alack:
In somer, sun: in winters breath, of frost:
Whereby the light of her fair lokes I lost.
