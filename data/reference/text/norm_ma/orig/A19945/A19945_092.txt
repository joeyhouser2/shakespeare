CAESAR, thou hast overcome to thy great fame
Proud Germane, valiant Gauls, and Brittons rude,
Romes liberty (but to thine eternal shame)
And her great Champion thou hast eke subdu'de.
Yet neither shall thy triumphs with my name
Be graced, nor sword be with my blood imbrued.
Though all the conquered earth do now serve thee,
Cato will die unconquered, and free.
