Best pleased she is, when love is most exprest,
And sometime says that love should be requited,
Yet is she grieved my love should now be righted,
When that my faith hath proved what I protest.

Am I beloved whose heart is thus opprest,
Or deer to her, and not in her delighted?
I live to see the Sun, yet still benighted,
By her despair is blam'de, and hope supprest,

She still denies, yet still her heart consenteth:
She grants me all, but that which I desire,
She fuel sends, but bids me leave the fire,
She lets me die, and yet my death lamenteth.
O foolish love, by reason of thy blindness.
I die for want of love, yet killed with kindness.
