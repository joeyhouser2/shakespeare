O most unhappy Dido,
Unhappy Wife, and more unhappy Widow!
Unhappy in thy Mate,
And in thy Lover more unfortunate,
By treason th'one was reft thee,
By treason th'other left thee.
That left thee means to fly with,
This left thee means to die with.
The former being dead,
From Brothers sword thou fliest:
The latter being fled,
On Lovers sword thou diest.
