MILO dominion est, peregre Milone profecto,
Arua vacant, uxor non minus inde parit.
Cur sit ager sterilis, cur uxor lact tet, edam,
Quo fodiatur ager non habet, uxor habet.

MILO lives long in France, and while he's there,
his ground bears nought, his wife doth children bear
Why should th'one barren, th'other fertile be?
His ground lacks plowing up,, so doth not she.
