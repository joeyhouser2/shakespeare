This sword is mine, or will Laertes Son
Win this as he Achilles armour won?
This sword which you O Greeks oft bathed have known
In Troian blood, ile now bath in mine own.
This fearless breast which all mine enemies fierce
Have left unpierced, now I my self will pierce.
So men shall say, Aiax to none did yield
But t'Aiax self, and Aiax, Aiax killed.
