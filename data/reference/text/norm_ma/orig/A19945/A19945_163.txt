O Hand of all hands living,
The softest, moistest, whitest:
More skilled then Phoebu•… on a Lute in running:
More then Minerua, with a Needle cunning:
Then Mercury more wily,
Instealing Hearts most slily.

Since thou deer hand, in theft so much delightest,
Why fallest thou now a giving?
Ay me! thy gifts are thefts, and with strange Art,
In giving me thy Glove, thou stealest my Hart.
