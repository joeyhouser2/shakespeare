Foure teeth of late you had, both black and shaking,
Which durst not chew your meat for fear of aching,
But since your cough (without a Barbers aid)
Hath blown them out, you need not be afraid.
On either side to chew hard crusts, for sure
Now from the Too•…h-ach you live most secure.
