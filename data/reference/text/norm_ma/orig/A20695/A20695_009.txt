As I sing, sweet flowers Ile strow, from the fruitful valleys brought:
Praising him by whom they grow, him that heaven and earth hath wrought, him that all things framed of nought,
Him that all for man did make,
But made man for his own sake.

Music all thy sweetness lend, while of his high power I speak,
On whom all powers else depend, but my brest is now too weak, trumpets shrill the air should break,
All in vain my sounds I raise,
Boundless power asks boundless praise,
