Who goes to gaze of every gallant girl,
And casts his eyes at every glancing gloze:
Whose masking mind, with every motion moved,
In fine shall find, his fancy fraught with woes.

For pleasure spent, is but a wishing vain,
By crooked chance, depriude of brave delight:
Cut of by care, a heap of hurtful harms,
Our gaze ungained, which whilom pleased our sight.

Our bants do vade, our pleasures pass away,
Our sugerest sweets, reaps sorrowing sobs in fine:
Our braggest boast, of beauties bravest blaze,
To forowed browes, doth at the length resign.

Our foolish fancy filde, with filthy vice,
Pursues his hurt, unto anothers harms:
A hovering hart, with every gloze enticed,
gains loathsome-love, whence nought but sorrow swarms.

Leave then to gaze, of every glancing gloze,
Contemn the sleights, of beauties sugerest bate:
Whose outward sheath, with colours brave imbost,
Shuns cruel craft, and envious hurtful hate.
