TO darksome cave, where crawling worms remain.
Thou worldly wretch, resign thy boasting breath:
Yield up thy pomp, thy corpse must pass again,
From whence it came, compelled by dreadful death.
Oh sickness sore, thy pains do pearce my hart,
Thou messenger of death, whose goring gripes me greve:
Permit a while, me loth yet to depart
From friends and goods, which I behind must leave.
Ah silly soul, entis'de with worldly vain,
As well as thou, thy friends must yield to death:
Though after thee, a while they do remain,
They shall not still, continue on the earth.
What must I then need, shrine in gastly grave?
And leave what long, I got with tedious toil:
Prolong me yet, and let me licence have,
Till elder years, to put your Brutes to foil.
O foolish man, allured by lewd delight,
Thy labors lost, these goods they are not thine:
But as (thou hadst) so others have like right,
(Of them) when thou, shalt up thy breath resign.
Then farewell world, the Nurse of wicked vice,
Adue vile dross which moves mens minds to ill▪
Farewell delights, which did my youth entice,
To serve as slave, unto unsatiate will.
