The careless count, which doth the same imbrace:
The doubtful hope, to reap my due dezart,
The pensive path, that guides my restless race:
Are at such war, within my wounded brest,
As doth bereave, my joy and eke my rest.

My greedy will, which seeks the golden gain,
My luckless lot, doth always take in worth:
My matched mind, that dreads my suits in vain,
My pittious plaint, doth help for to set forth:
So that betwixt, two waves of raging Seas,
I drive my days, in troubles and disease·

My woeful eyes, do take their cheefe delight,
To feed their fill, upon their pleasant maze:
My hidden harms, that grow in me by sight,
With pining pangs, do drive me from the ga•e:
And to my hap, I reap none other hire,
But burn my self, and I to blow the fire.
