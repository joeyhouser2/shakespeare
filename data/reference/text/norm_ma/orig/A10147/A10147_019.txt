When shall relief release my woe?
When shall desert, disdain digest?
When shall my hap, hap to me so?
That my poor hart may come too rest.
When shall it so? when shall it so?
When shall long love be looked upon?
When shall tried truth be homeliest?
When shall hope have that hope hangeth on?
That my poor hart may come to rest.
When shall it so? etc.
When shall I see she seethe right?
When shall I hear she heareth me best?
When shall I feel, she feeleth delight?
That my poor harte may come to rest.
When shall it so. etc.
When stint all storms that thus agreeve?
When stint all stayes that wrong hath wrest?
When stint all strifes right to releeve?
That my poor hart may come to rest.
When shall it so? etc.
When right shall see right time to boast?
When right shall aright unright oppress?
When right shall reign and rule the roast?
Then my poor harte shall come to rest.
Then shall it so. etc.
When shall I watch the time to see?
Now shall I wish the time possest,
Now shall I think each day years three
That my poor harte may come to rest.
When shall it so? etc.
