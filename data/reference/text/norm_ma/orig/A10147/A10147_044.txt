O Cruel hart with falsehood infect, of force I must complain,
Whose poison hid, I may detect, as cause doth me constrain:
Thy name I shrine within my brest, thy deeds though I do tell,
No mind of malice I protest, thy self doth know it well.
If thy deserts then bids me write, I cannot well revoke it,
I shall not spare to shew thy spite, I will no longer cloak it:
As Troylus truth shall be my shield, to keep my pen from blame,
So Cressids craft shall keep the feeld, for to resound thy shame.
Ulisses wife shall mate the sore, whose wishly troth doth shine,
Well Fair and False, I can no more, thou art of Helens line:
And daughter to Diana eke, with pale and deadly cheare,
Whose often change I may well like, two moonthes within the year.
