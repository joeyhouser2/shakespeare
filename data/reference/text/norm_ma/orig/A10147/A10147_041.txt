Behold you Dames the reign in fames, whose looks mens hearts do lead
And triumph in the spoil of those, upon whose breasts you trede.
A myrt or make of M, whose molde, Dame Nature in disdain,
To please her self, and spight her foes, in beauty raised to reign:
Whose sunny beams and starry eyes, presents a heavenlyke face,
And shows the world a wonderous work, such are her gifts of grace
In forehead, feature beareth, brunt in face doth favor guide,
In looks is life, in shape is shame, in cheeks doth colour hide:
In body seemelynesse doth shew, in words doth wisdom shade,
All parts of her doth praise deserve, in temperance is her trade.
In humble port is honor plaste, in face is maidens smiles
Her life is graft with Golden gifts, her deeds devoid of gyles.
And as the Star to Mariners, is guide unto the Port,
So is this M, a heavenly joy, to Lovers that resort:
Who run and room with inward wounds, and folded arms across,
And hide their harms with cloaks of care, and feed their hope with loss.
Her looks doth lift above the skies, her frowns to Hell doth throw
All sues to her, she seeks on none, that daily proof doth show:
Wherefore her saying late set forth, she burnt and could not flee,
Though ment in praise, yet far amiss, I take it written be.
She is none such as if she would, that any would disdain:
But for the smarts of others griefs, of pity she did plain,
As one most lothe of any life, for love of her be lost,
Or that with blood or cruel deeds, men write her beauties boast:
For mercy is in M, her brest, and modest is her life,
A courtuous maid, and like to prove, a constant worthy wife.
