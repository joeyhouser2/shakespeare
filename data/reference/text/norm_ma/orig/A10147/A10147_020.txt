Like as the Hauke is led by lure, to draw from tree to tree,
So is my hart through force of love, where ever my body be
The Hauke to pray doth double wing, her flight is fled in vain
I make my flight in waste of wind, my hope receiveth no gain.
Hawks that be high it hurts to light, two flightes without reward
My flight is two, and three again, alas Mistress regard:
The Hauke brought low, is soon made high, by feeding on warm food
Your mouths breath sets me aloft, there is nothing so good.
Good Lady then strain forth the strings, whose tune may me revive
And with strange tongue do not prolong, my joys thus to deprive.
Within your brest my hart is hid, your will and it is one,
Regard my smart, the cure is yours, and loss, when I am gone.
Thus all your own, I recommend me wholly to your grace,
As seemeth you best for to reward, my plight and woeful case.
Which plight if you do counterpaise, with joys, as doth belong,
My hart for joy would tune accord, to sing some pleasant song.
