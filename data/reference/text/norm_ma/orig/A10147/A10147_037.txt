His time he spent in Uertues lore, as seemed his state full well,
By serious study what he could, he sought for to excel.
But what of all this same? the fates no wight in time will spare,
When gastly death hath pierced in earth, then must our bodies wear
In age aswell in youths, in youths aswell in age,
No certain time we have to bide, when death with us will wage.
No thing can still abide, but comes to nought in end,
The craggy Rocks the sturdiest oaks: stark rotten once is rend.
And so hath Fletcher, now to death paid his due,
What he is now we must be all, his Funeral then vew.
