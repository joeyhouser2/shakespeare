A doubtful, dying, doleful, Dame,
Not fearing death, nor forcing life:
Nor caring ought for flitting fame,
Emongst such sturdy storms of strife:
Here doth she mourn and write her will,
Upon her liked Lovers end:
Graunt (Muses nyne) your sacred skill,
Help to assist your mournful freend:
Emboldened with your Nimphish aid,
She will not cease, but seek to sing:
And eke employ her willing head,
Her Gruffithes praise, with ruth to ring.
