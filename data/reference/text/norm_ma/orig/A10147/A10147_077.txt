Dear Lady decked with cumlynesse,
To countervail my clemency:
Be prest, I pray, in readiness,
To yield your courteous courtesy.

Let me you find Penelope,
In mind, and loyal hart:
So shall I, your Ulisses be,
Till breathing life depart.

Yield love for love, to him who likes,
To live in lynckes of loyalty:
And graunt him grace, who nothing seeks,
For his good will, but curtesy.

Let me your bondmen, favour •nde,
To gratify: my willing harte:
Whom no attempt, to please your mind,
Shall hinder me, to play my part.

Permit me not, in lingering sort,
To labour in a barrayn soil:
Ne give occasion to report,
How loitering love, reaps troubled toil.

But let me say, my hart obtained,
The gloze, which pleased my glancing eyes:
And that I have for guerdon gained,
The best that in my Lady lies.

So shall I boast of that, which best
Doth please the prime of my desire:
And glory in a gained rest,
Which through your favour I aspire.
