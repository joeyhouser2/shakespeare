I Spake when I ment not, in speeding to gain,
I sought, when I sped not, but travailed in vain:
I found where I feard not, would writh with the wind,
I lost where I loved not, nor for••d to find.
Nothing in which, truth is not trusty,
But double is such, and beauty but rusty:
I cool with the cold, I leve that I like not,
I know not the old, that rotteth and ripes not.
I savor no such, that fondly doth favor,
I care not to much, for such sorry savor:
I taste or I try, in part or in all,
I care not a fly, the loss is but small.
I labor at leisure, I prick without pain,
In using for pleasure, beats in my brain:
I spare not in byrding, to beat well the bush,
Nor leave not in striking, as long as they rush.
I try ere I trust, nought wasting but wind,
Before I find just, they know not my mind:
I jet not with Geminie, nor tarry not with Tawre
In bluttring who blears me? I leave them with Law••.
For fier who findeth, in burning to bight,
The wise man he warneth, to leap from the light:
For seeing the weed, and losing from bands,
The plowing in Sea, and sowing in Sands.
