IF only sight suffise, my hart to lose or bind,
What cause have I to move debate, whereby no peace I find?
If that my restless will, by pain doth still renew,
What force have I? but she consent, my foe for to subdue?
To yield and suffer then, I think it for the best,
And by desert as time shall serve, to purchase quiet rest.
Let jealous envy lowre with browes, and visage bent,
I know the worst, no shameless tongue, shall alter mine intent.
The Dice of Love are thrown, god speed the doubtful chance
Misdeem who list, so she at last, my service will advance.
To ask and to o•tayne, that Fortune were so swift,
Sith travail is the ready way, unto each noble gift.
And feeble is the joy, that lightly is begun,
As tender Flare can bear no stress, before that it be spun.
Wherefore with sad advice, in hope my harte shall dwell,
And all the tale that I confess, in silence will I tell
Unto her self alone, whose favour I require,
None else shall know her name for me, to constre my desire.
