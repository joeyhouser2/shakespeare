Oft and in vain my rebels thoughts have ventured,
to stop the passage of my vanquished hart,
And close the way, my friendly foe first entered,
Striving thereby to free my better part.

Whilest guarding thus the windows of my thought,
Where my harts-thief to vex me made her choice,
And thether all my forces to transport,
Another passage opens at her voice.

Her voice betrays me to her hand and eye,
My freedoms-tyrant glorying in her art:
But (ah) sweet foe, small is the victory
With three such powers to plague one silly hart.
Yet my souls sovereign, since I must resign,
Reign in my thoughts, my love and life are thine.
