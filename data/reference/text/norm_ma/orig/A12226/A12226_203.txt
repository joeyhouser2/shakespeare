Faction that ever dwells, in Court where wit excelles,
hath set defiance.
Fortune and Love have sworn, that they were never borne,
of one alliance.

Cupid which doth aspire, to be God of Desire,
Swears he gives laws;
That where his arrows hit, some joy, some sorrow it,
Fortune no cause.

Fortune swears weakest hearts (the books of Cupids Arts)
turned with her wheel,
Senseless themselves shall prove: venter hath place in Love,
ask them that feel.

This discord it be got Atheists, that honor not.
Nature thought good,
Fortune should ever dwell in Court where wits excel,
Love keep the wood.

So to the wood went I, with Love to live and die,
Fortune's forlorn:
Experience of my youth, made me think humble Truth
In deserts borne.

My Saint I keep to me, and Joane her self is she,
Joane fair and true:
She that doth only move passions of love with Love:
Fortune adieu.
