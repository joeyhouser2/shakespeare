Yet worse than worse, I say thou art a Thief. A thief?
Now God forbid: a thief, and of worst thieves a thief;
Thieves steal for need, and steal for goods, which pain recovers
But thou, rich in all joys, dost rob my goods from me,
Which cannot be restored by time nor industry:
Of foes the spoil is evil, far more of constant lovers.
