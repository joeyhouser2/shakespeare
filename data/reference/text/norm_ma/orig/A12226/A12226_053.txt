Love still a Boy, and oft a wanton is,
Schoolde only by his Mothers tender eye:
What wonder then if he his lesson miss,
When for so soft a rod deare play he try.
And yet my star, because a sugared kiss,
In sport I suck, while she a sleep doth lie:
Doth lowre, nay chide, nay threat for only this:
Sweet it was saucy love, that prest so nye.
But no scuse serves, she makes her wrath appear
In Beauties throne, see now who dares come neere
Those scarlet Judges, threatening bloody pain.
O heavenly Fool, thy most kiss worthy face
Anger invests with such a lovely grace,
That Angers self I needs must kiss again.

I Never drank of Aganippe well,
Nor never did in shade of Tempe sit:
And Muses scorn with vulgar brains to dwell,
Poor Lay-man I, for sarcred rites unfit.
Some do I hear of Poets fury tell,
But God wot, wot not what they mean by it:
And this I swear by blackest brook of hell,
I am no Pickpurse of an others wit.
How falls it than, that with so smooth an ease
My thoughts I speak? And what I speak I show
In verse; and that my verse best wits doth please,
Gesse we the cause. What is it this? fie no.
Or so? much less. How then? sure thus it is;
My Lips are sure inspired with Stellas kiss.

OF all the Kings that ever here did reign,
Edward nam fourth, as first in praise I name:
Not for his fair outside, nor well linde brain,
Although less gift, are feathers of high fame.
Nor that he could young wise, wise valiant frame
His Sires revenge, joynde with a kingdoms gain:
And gained by Mars, could yet make Mars so tame,
That balance weighed what sword did late obtain.
Nor that he made the Flower deluce so fraide,
Though strongly hedgd of bloody Lyons pawes:
That witty Lewes to him a tribuite paid;
Nor this nor that, nor any such small cause,
But only, for this worthy King durst prove,
To lose his Crown, rather then lose his Love.
