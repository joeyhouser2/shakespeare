Not at first sight, nor with a dribbing shot,
Love gave the wound, which while I breath will bleed:
But known, worth did in tract of time proceed,
Till by degrees it had full conquest got.
I saw and liked, I liked but loved not,
I loved, but did not straight what Love decreed:
At length to Loves decrees, I first agreed.
Yet with repining at so partial lot.
Now even that footstep of lost liberty
Is gone, and now like slave borne Muscovite:
I call it praise to suffer tyranny,
And now imploy the remnant of my wit
To make my self beleeve that all is well,
While with a feeling skill I paint my hell.
