IF yielding fear, or cankered villainy,
In Caesars haughty heart had tane the charge,
The walls of Rome had not bene rearde so hye,
Nor yet the mighty empire left so large.
If Menelaus could have ruled his will
With fowl reproach to lose his fair delight,
Then had the stately towers of Troy stood still,
And Greeks with grudge had drunk their own despight.
If dread of drenching waves or fear of fire,
Had stayed the wandering Prince amid his race,
Ascanius then, the frute of his desire
In Lavine land had not possessed place,
But true it is, where lots do light by chance,
There Fortune helps the boldest to advance.
