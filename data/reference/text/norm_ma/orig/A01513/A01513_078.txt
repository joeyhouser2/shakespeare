First love my concubine, whom I have kept so trim,
Even she for whom I seemed of yore, in seas of joy to swim:
To whom I dare avow, that I have served as well,
And played my part as gallantly, as he that bears the bell:
She cast me off long since, and holds me in disdain,
I cannot prank to please her now, my vaunting is but vain.
My writhled cheeks bewray, that pride of heat is past,
My staggering steps eke tell the truth, that nature fadeth fast
My quaking crooked joints, are cumbered with the cramp,
