O Polinice, now hast thou with thy blood
Bought all too deare the title to this realm,
That cruel he Eteocles thee rest,
And now also hath reft thee of thy life,
Alas, what wicked deed can wrath not do?
And out alas for me▪
Whyle thou yet livedest I had a lively hope
To have some noble wight to be my pheere,
By whom I might be crowned a royal Queen:
But now, thy hasty death hath done to die
This dying hope of mine, that hope henceforth
None other wedlock, but tormenting woe,
If so these trembling hands for coward dread
Dare not presume to end this wretched life.
Alas deare dame, let not thy raging grief
Heap one mishap upon anothers head.

But most unhappy now, that lack of sight

And still tormented in such misery,
