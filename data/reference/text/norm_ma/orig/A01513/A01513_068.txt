IF what you want, you (wanton) had at will,
A stedfast mind, a faithful loving hart:
If what you speak you would perform it still,
If from your word your deed could not revert.
If youthful years your thoughts did not so rule,
As elder days may scorn your friendship frail:
Your doubled fanfie would not thus recule,
For p•evish pride which now I must bewail.
For Cressyde fair did Troylus never love,
More deare than I esteemed your framed cheare:
Whose wavering ways (since now I do them prove)
By true report this witness with me bear:
That if your friendship be not too deare bought,
The price is great, that nothing gives for nought.
