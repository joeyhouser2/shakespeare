When stedfast friendship (bound by holy othe)
Did part perforce my presence from thy sight.
In dreams I might behold how thou wert loth
With troubled thoughts to part from thy delight.
When Popler walls enclosed thy pensi•e mind,
My painted sh•dow did thy woes revive:
Thine evening walks by Thames in open wind,
Did long to see my sailing boat arrive.
But when the dismold day did seek to part
From London walls thy longing mind for me.
The sugared kisses (sent to thy deare hart)
With secret smart in broken sleeps I see.
W•erfore in tears I drench a thousand fold,
Till these moist eyes thy beauty may behold.
