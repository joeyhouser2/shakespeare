Farewell deer love whom I have loved and shall,
Both in this world, and in the world to come,
For proof whereof my sprite is Charons thrall,
And yet my corpse attendant on thy tomb.
Farewell deer sweet, whose wanton will to please
Each taste of trouble seemed mell to me,
Farewell sweet deer, whose doubts for to appease,
I was contented thus in bale to be.
Farewell my life, farewell for and my death,
For thee I live, for thee now must I die,
Farewell from bath, whereas I feel my breath
Forsake my brest in great perplexity,
Alas how welcome were this death of mine,
If I had dyde betweene those arms of thine.
