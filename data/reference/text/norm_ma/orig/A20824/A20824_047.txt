My hart the Anuile where my thoughts do beat,
My words the hammers, fashioning my desires,
My breast the forge, including all the heat,
Love is the fuel which maintains the fire.

My sighs, the bellows which the flame increaseth,
Filling mine ears with noise and nightly groaning,
Toiling with pain, my labour never ceaseth,
In grievous passions my woes still bemoaning.

Mine eyes with tears against the fire striving,
With scorching gleed my hart to cinders turneth:
But with those drops the coals again revyving,
Still more and more unto my torment burneth.
With Sisiphus thus do I role the stone,
And turn the wheel with damned Ixion.
