Placed in the forlorn hope of all dispayre,
Against the Fort where Beauties Army lies,
Assailed with death, yet armed with gastly fear,
Lo thus my love, my life, my fortune tries.

Wounded with Arrows from thy lightning eyes,
My tongue in pain, my hearts counsels bewraying,
My rebel thought for me in Ambush lies,
To my loves foe her Chieftain still betraying.

Record my love in Ocean waves (unkind,)
Cast my deserts into the open air,
Commit my words unto the fleeting wind,
Cancel my name, and blot it with dispayre,
So shall I be, as I had never been,
Nor my disgraces to the world be seen.
