See chaste Diana, where my harmless hart,
Roused from my breast, his sure and safest layre,
Nor chaste by hound, nor forced by Hunters arte,
Yet see how right he comes unto my fair.

See how my Dear comes to thy Beauties stand,
And there stands gazing on those darting eyes,
Whilst from their rayes by Cupids skilful hand,
Into his hart the piercing Arrow flies.

See how he looks upon his bleeding wound,
Whilst thus he panteth for his latest breath,
And looking on thee, falls upon the ground,
Smiling, as though he gloried in his death.
And wallowing in his blood, some life yet laft,
His stone-cold lips doth kiss the blessed shaft.
