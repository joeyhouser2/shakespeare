Fair art thou Phillis, I so fair (sweet maid)
As nor the sun, nor I have seen more fair,
For in thy cheeks sweet roses are embayde,
And gold more pure then gold doth guild thy hair.
Sweet Bees have hived their honey on thy tongue,
And Hebe spiced her Necter with thy breath:
About thy neck do all the graces throng,
And lay such baits as might entangle death.
In such a breast what heart would not be thrall?
From such sweet arms who would not wish embraces?
At thy fair hands who wonders not at all,
Wounder it self through ignorance embases?
Yet naithelesse tho wonderous gifts you call these,
My faith is far more wonderful then all these.
