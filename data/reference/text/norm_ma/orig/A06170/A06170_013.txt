Ah trees why fall your leaves so fast?
Ah Rocks where are your robes of moss?
Ah flocks, why stand you all agast?
Trees, rocks, and flocks, what are you pensive for my loss?
The birds me thinks, tune nought but moan,
The windes breath nought but bitter plaint,
The beasts forsake their dens to groan,
Birds, windes, and beasts, what doth my loss your powers attaint?
Floods weep their springs above their bounds,
And Echo wails to see my woe,
The robe of ruth doth cloath the grounds:
Floods, Echo, gronudes, why do you all these tears bestow?
The trees, the rocks, and flocks reply,
The birds, the windes, the beasts report,
Floods, Echo, grounds, for sorrow cry,
We grieve suice Phillis nill kind Damons love consort.
