How languisheth the Primrose of loves garden?
How trill her tears th'Elixar of my senses:
Ambitious sickness, what doth thee so harden,
Oh spare and plague thou me for her offences.
Ah Roses, loves fair Roses do not languish,
Blush through the milk-white vaile that holds you covered:
If heat or cold may mitigate your anguish,
He burn, He frize, but you shall be recovered.
Good God would beauty mark now she is crazed,
How but one shower of sickness makes her tender:
Her Judgments then to mark my woes amazed,
To mercy should opinions fort surrender:
And I (oh would, I might, or would she ment it,)
Should herrie love, who now in hart lament it.
