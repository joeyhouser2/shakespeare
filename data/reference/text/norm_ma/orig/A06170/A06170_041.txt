These fierce incessant waves that stream along my face,
Which show the certain proof of my never-ceasing pains,
Fair Phillis are no tears that trickle from my brains:
For why such streams of ruth, within me find no place.
These floods that wet my cheeks, are gathered fron thy grace
And thy perfections, and from hundreth thousand flowers
Which from thy beauties spring: whereto I medly showers
Of Rose and Lillies to, the colours of thy face.
My love doth serve for fire, my hart the fornace is,
The aperries of my sighs augment the burning flame,
The Limbique is mine eye that doth distil the same:
And by how much my fire is violent and fly,
By so much doth it cause the waters mount on hie,
That shower from out mine eyes, for to asswage my miss.
