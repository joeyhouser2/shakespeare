I do compare unto thy youthly clear,
(Which always bides within thy flowering prime,)
The month of Aprill, that bedews our clime
With pleasant flowers, when as his showers appear,
Before thy face, shall fly false cruelty,
Before his face, the doaly season fleets,
Mild been his looks, thine eyes are full of sweets:
Firm is his course, firm is thy loyalty.
He paints the fields through liquid christall showers,
Thou paintest my verse with Pallas learned flowers:
With Zephirus sweet breath he fills the plains,
And thou my hart with weeping sighs doost wring,
His browes are dewd with mornings christall spring,
Thou makest my eyes with tears bemoan my pains.
