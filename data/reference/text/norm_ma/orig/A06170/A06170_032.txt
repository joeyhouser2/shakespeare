Not causeless were you christned (gentle flowers)
The one of faith, the other fancies pride,
For she who guides both faith and fancies power,
In your fair colours wrapes her Ivory side:
As one of you hath whiteness without stain,
So spotless is my love and never tainted:
And as the other shadoweth faith again,
Such is my lass, with no fond change acquainted:
And as nor tyrant son nor winter weather,
May ever change sweet Amaranthus hew:
So she tho love and fortune join together,
Will never leave to be both fair and true:
And should I leave thee then thou pretty else?
Nay first let Damon quite forget himself.
