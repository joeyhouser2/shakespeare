My frail and earthly bark, by reasons guide,
(Which holds the helm, whilst will doth weilde the sail:)
By my desires (the windes of bad betide,)
Hath sailed these wordly Seas with small avail.
Vain objects serve, for dreadful Rocks to quail,
My brittle boat from haven of life that flies
To haunt the Sea of mundane miseries:
My sowle that draws Impressions from above,
And views my course, and sees the windes aspire,
Bids reason watch to scape the shoals of love:
But lawless will enflamd with endless ire
Doth steerem poop whilest reason doth retire.
The streams increase loves waves my bark do fill,
Thus are they wracked that guide their course by will.
