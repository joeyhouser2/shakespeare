Phaebe sat,
Sweet she sat,
sweet sat Phaebe when I saw her,
White her brow
Coy her eye,
brow and eye, how much you please me?
Words I spent,
Sighs I sent,
sighs and words could never draw her,
Oh my Love,
Thou art lost,
since no sight could ever ease thee.

Phaebe sat
By a Fount,
sitting by a Fount I spied her,
Sweet her touch,
Rare her voice,
touch and voice, what may distain you?
As she sung,
I did sigh,
And by sighs whilst that I tride her,
Oh mine eyes
You did lose,
her first sight whose want did pain you.
Phoebes flocks
White as wool,
yet were Phoebes looks more whiter,
Phoebes eyes
Dovelike mild,
Dovelike eyes both mild and cruel,
Montane swears
In your Lamps,
he will die for to delight her,
Phoebe yield
Or I die,
shall true hearts be fancies fuel?
