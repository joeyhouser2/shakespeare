Burst forth my tears, assist my forward grief,
And shew what pain imperious love provokes
Kind tender Lambs, lament Loves scant relief,
And pine, since pensive care my freedom yokes,
Oh pine, to see me pine, my tender Flocks.

Sad pining care, that never may have peace,
At Beauties gate, in hope of pity knocks:
But mercy sleeps, while deep disdains encrease,
And Beauty hope in her fair bosom yokes:
Oh greeve to hear my grief, my tender Flocks.

Like to the windes my sighs have winged been,
Yet are my sighs and suits repayed with mocks:
I plead, yet she repineth at my teen,
O ruthless rigour, harder then the Rocks,
That both the Shepherd kills, and his poor Flocks.
