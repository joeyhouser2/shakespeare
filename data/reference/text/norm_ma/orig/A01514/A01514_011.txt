IF Virgill how to till the Earth, to every man doth tell,
And Galen he in Physics arte doth many men excel,
If Poets old deserven praise, by painting out aright,
The fruits of vice, as Ovid doth, and many mo that wright,
By learned skill of many things: If such exalt their name,
And for their hire, deserved praise by trump of Lady Fame:
Why should the Author of this book then leese his due desart,
Sith he so friendly here to us, hath showed his skilful arte?
The healthsome herbs and flowers sweet, fron weeds he hath divided,
The fruits of Gives in prison strong he hath right well decided.
Of wars also, and warriors to, even like a Martial knight,
He hath discoursed, and showed the lots, that thereupon do light:
Virgill is dead, and Galen gone, with Poets many more:
Yet works of theirs be still alive, and with us kept in store.
This Author lives, and Gascoigne heights, yet once to die most sure,
Alas the while that worthy wights may not always endure,
But works of his among the best, for ever more shall rest,
When he in heaven shall take a place prepared for the blest.
