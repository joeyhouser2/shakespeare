IF fortune favoured him, then may that man rejoice,
And think himself a happy man by hap of happy choice.
Who loves and is beloved of one as good, as true,
As kind as Cleopatra was, and yet more bright of hew.
Her eyes as grey as glass, her teeth as white as milk,
A ruddy lip, a dimpled chin, a skin as smooth as silk.
A wight what could you more, that may content mannes mind,
And hath supplies for every want, that any man can find.
And may him self assure, when hence his life shall pass,
She will be stong to death with snakes, as Cleopatra was.
