Long while (alas) she would not yield, but it was she that rul'de the rost,
Until by proof she did confess, if he were gone her joy was lost▪
And then she cried, oh dainty Love, I now do find it is for thee,
That I am loved and honored both, and thou hast power to conquer me▪
But when I heard her yield to Love, oh how my hart did leap for joy,
That now I had some little hope, to have an end of mine annoy.
