Truth shows her self in secret of her trust,
Wisdom her grace in honour of her Love:
Uertue her life where love is not unjust,
Love in his sweet that doth •…o sorrow prove.

Truth hath in hate to hear a feigned tale,
Wisdom doth frown where Folly is in place:
Honour is gone where Beauty is too small,
And Uertue dies where Love is in disgrace.

I leave your truth to your desired trust,
Your wisdom to the wonder of the wise:
Your highest joy to judgement of the just,
Where Uertue lives, and Honour never dies.
And he vouchsafe you that all truth preserveth,
What Truth of Love, and Love of Truth deserveth.
