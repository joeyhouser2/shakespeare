Who deals with fire may burn his fingers ends,
And water drowns the foot that goes too deep:
A lavish tongue will quickly lose his friends,
And he a fool that can no counsel keep.
Yet where desire doth egg the tongue to speak,
Somewhat must out, or else the heart will break.

To speak but truth deserve no deadly blame,
Though truth mistane sometime be petty treason:
Yet causeless death deserveth no defame,
Though ruthless rage will never yield to reason:
