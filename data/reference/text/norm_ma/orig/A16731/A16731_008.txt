Pure of the fair that never fadeth hue,
Exceeding sweet that every sweet exceedeth:
Neere to the heavens where highest glances grow'
Excellent fruit that such a fancy feedeth,
Love in the eyes, and honour in the heart,
O Princes, Angels Goddess, heavenly feature,
Perfection far above all natures arte,
Exception none, was ever such a creature.
Rich, vertuous, wise, fair, courteous, comely, kind,
Joy to the hearts of all that do behold her,
Courtly of grace, and of a princely mind,
High in the heavens, the Angels have enrolde her.
