Perfection •…eereles, Uertue without pride,
Honor and learning linked with highest Love,
Joy of the thought in true discretion tride,
Love of the life that highest honours prove.
In Angels arms with heavenly hands embraced▪
Paradise pleased, and all the world disgraced.

Seek all the world, oh seek and never find,
In earthly mould the mount of such a mind:
Divinest gifts that God on man bestoweth,
No glory such as of such glory groweth.
End of the joys that hath all grief begun,
Yet let me weep when all the world is done.
