Sweet birds that sit and sing amid the shady valleys,
And see how sweetly Phillis walks amid her garden allies▪
Go round about her bower and sing, as ye are bidden,
To her is only known his faith, that fron the world is hidden.
And she among you all that hath the sweetest voice,
Go chirp of him that never told, yet never changed his choise.
And not forget his faith, that lived for ever loved,
Yet never made his fancy known, nor ever favour mou'•….
And ever let your ground of all your grace be this,
To you, to you, to you the due of love and honour is.
On you, on you, on you▪ our music all attendeth,
For as on you our Muse begun, in you all music endeth.
