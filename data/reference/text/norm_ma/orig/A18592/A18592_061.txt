Within thy brest, my hart doth rest.
Within the cir•uit of a Christall sphere,
Thy eyes are placed, and underneath those eyes,
Brest of hard flint, ears that do scorn to hear
My days sad groanings, and night waking cries,
Hart sore sick passions, and Loves agonies,
Doth it become thy beauty? no, a stain
Rests on thy bright brow wrinkled with disdain.
