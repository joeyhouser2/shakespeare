O let me hear, from thee my deare.
O tongue thou hast blasphemed thy holy Goddess,
Let me do penance for offending thee,
Me do thou blame for my forgetfulness:
Hear my submission, thou wilt succor me:
From thy hearts closet commeth gentleness,
Thee hath the world admired for clemency,
My hart is sorry, and Ile bite my tongue,
Dear that to thee, to thee I offered wrong.
