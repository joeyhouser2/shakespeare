Oft have I gazed with astonished eye,
At monstrous issues of ill shaped birth,
When I have seen the Midwife to old earth,
Nature produce most strange deformity▪

So have I marveled to observe of late,
Hard favoured Feminines so scant of fair,
That Masks so choicely, sheltered of the air,
As if their beauties were not theirs by fate.

But who so weak of observation,
Hath not discerned long since how virtues wanted,
How parsimoniously the heavens have scanted,
Our chiefest part of adornation?

But now I cease to wonder, now I find
The cause of all our monstrous penny-shows:
Now I conceit from whence wits scarc'•je grows,
Hard favoured features, and defects of mind.
Nature long time hath stored up vertue, fairness,
Shaping the rest as foils unto this Rareness.
