Ore my heart your eyes do idolatrize.
Ore the wide world my love-lays Ile be sending,
My love-lays in my Loves praise always written,
Heart-comfortable motions still attending,
Your beauty and your vertuous zeal commending,
Eyes that no frosts-cold-rage hath ever bitten:
Do you then think that I in Loves hot fire,
Idolatrize and surphet in desire.
