My heart I send, to be your friend.
My deare souls comfort, and my hopes true solace,
Heart of my heart, and my lives secret joy,
I in conceit do thy sweet self embrace,
Send cloudy exhalations clean away
To the blind misty North, there for to stay:
Be thou my arbour and my dwelling place,
Your arms the circling folds that shall enclose me,
Friend me with this, and thou shalt never lose me.
