Renowmed Arthur and thrice-worthy Britaine:
O how a lively blood doth fill my veins,
At this proud message of the haughty Romans,
I hitherto my Lord have bene in fear,
Lest that the worthy Britaines with much ease,
And long continual, peace and quietness,
Should grow to too much slouth and cowardize,
And lose that honorable Reputation,
Of Chivalry and Martial discipline:
Wherein (right Noble King) we have bene counted,
For to surmount all Nations of the world.
For where the use of Arms is not esteemed,
But buried in Oblivions loathsome cave,
And want on dallying held in aestimate,
It cannot chuse but pale-faced Cowardize,
Must dim and clean deface all worthy Vertue▪
