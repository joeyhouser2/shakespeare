For you I die, being absent from mine eye.
For all the holy rites that Venus useth,
You I conjure to true obedience:
I offer faith, which no kind hart refuseth,
Die perjured Envy for thy late offence,
Being enamored of rich Beauties pride,
Absent, I freeze in Winters pining cold,
From thee I sit, as if thou hadst denied,
My lovesick passions twentie times retold:
Eye-dazling Mistress, with a look of pity,
Grace my sad Song, and my hearts pining Ditty.
