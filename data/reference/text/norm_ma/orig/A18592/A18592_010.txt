Wisely foreseeing what you think convenient,
Touching the proud commandment sent from Rome,
A thing at first carefully deliberated,
Is in the end most easily tolerated:
We therefore shall with easier burden brook,
The haughty message of Tiberius Lucius,
If mongst our selves in wisdom we confer,
How and which way to answer his demaund,
And surely (noble Followers) I suppose,
We have no cause to fear their foreign braves,
For that upon a most unjust request,
He seeks to have a tribute paid from Britaine,
Because forsooth in Julius Caesars time,
Through jars and discords of the ancient Brytaine,
The tribute hath been due and payable:
For when our country was at full possest,
With civil garboils and domestic brawls,
Their Caesar did arrive wihin this land,
And with this armed soldiers full of force,
Brought in subjection that unquiet Nation,
By this allegiance they unjustly crave,
Tribute and satisfaction at our hands,
For nothing that is got by violence,
May justly be possest by violence.
Sith therefore he presumeth to demaund,
A thing being most unlawful at our hands,
By the same reason let us demaund of him,
Tribute at Room mauger their Romish power,
And he that is the mightier in force,
Let him possess the honor of the tribute,
For if his allegations and demands,
Be forcible and worthy to be kept,
Because their Caesar and some Roman Princes,
Have sometimes conquered Brytania,
By the like reason I do think that Room,
Ought to pay tribute and to do us homage,
Because my Predecessors conquered it:
Bellin the noble King of Brytanie,
With his brave brother Brennus warlike aid,
Being then accounted Savoies noble Duke,
Razed the walls of Room, and set his Standard
With victory upon the City gates,
And in the middle of their Market place,
Hung up twentie of their chiefest Noblemen.
And Constantine the son of Helena,
And Maximinianus my neere Cou•ins,
W•re both inthroniz'din the Imperial seat,
And government of Romes great Empery▪
As touching Fraunce and other Ilands there,
We need not answer their outbraving termes:
For they refused to defend their own,
When we by force redeemed them from their hands.
Then counsel me thrice-worthy Brytaine Peers,
Abandoning base cowardize and fears.
