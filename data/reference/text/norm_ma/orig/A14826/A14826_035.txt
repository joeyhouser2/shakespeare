O Thou that rulest in Ramnis golden gate,
Let pity pierce thy unrelenting mind:
Unlade me of the burthen cruel fate,
(Fell envious fates too cruel and unkind)
Have heaped upon me by too froward love,
Too froward love the enemy of fortune:
Whose fierce assaults my hart (too late) did prove,
My silly hart which sorrow did importune.
Yet in thy power is my hearts redeeming,
My hearts redeeming from vile thraldomes force:
Vile thrall to one my sorrows not esteeming,
Though she be cruel yet have thou remorce.
Be thou to me no more inconstant variable,
But let thy fickle wheel rest firm and stable.
