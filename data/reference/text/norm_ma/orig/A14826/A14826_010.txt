Then from her fled my hart in sorrow wrapped.
Like unto one that shund prusuing slaughter:
All well nigh breathless told me what had happed,
How both in Court and country he had sought her.
The dreary tears of many love repenting,
Corrivals in my love whom fancy stroked:
Partners in love and partners in lamenting,
My fellow thralls whose necks as mine were yoked.
The shepherds praises and their hearts amiss,
Urged by my Mistress over weening pride:
For none that sees her but captived is,
And last he told which to my hart did glide;
How all the tears I spent were vain and forceless,
For she in hart had vowed to be remorseless.
