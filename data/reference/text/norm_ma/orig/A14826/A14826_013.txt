MY hart accused mine eyes and was offended,
Vowing the cause was in mine eyes aspiring:
Mine eyes affirmed my hart might well amend it,
If he at first had banished loves desiring.
Hart said that love did enter at the eyes,
And from the eyes descended to the hart:
Eyes said that in the hart did sparks arise,
Which kindled flame that wrought the inward smart
Hart said eyes tears might soon have quenched that
Eyes said hearts sighs at first might love exile:
So hart the eyes and eyes the hart did blame,
Whilst both did pine for both the pain did feel.
Hart sighed and bled, eyes wept and gazed too much,
Yet must I gaze because I see none such.
