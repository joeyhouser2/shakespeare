Who taught thee first to sigh A lass sweet hart? love.
Who taught thy tongue to marshal words of plaint? love.
Who fild thine eyes with tears of bitter smart? love.
Who gave thee grief and made thy joys so faint? love.
Who first did paint with coullers pale thy face? love.
Who first did break thy sleeps of quiet rest? love.
Who forced thee unto wanton love give place? love.
Who thralled thy thoughts in fancy so distrest? love.
Who made thee bide both constant firm and sure. love.
Who made thee scorn the world and love thy friend? love.
Who made thy mind with patience pains indure? love.
Who made thee settle stedfast to the end. love.
Then love thy choice though love be never gained,
Still live in love, despair not though disdained.
