When neither sighs nor sorrows were of force
I let my Mistress see my naked brest:
where view of wounded hart might work remorce,
And move her mind to pity my unrest.
With fled fast eye she gazed on my hart,
wherein she saw the picture of her beauty:
which having seen as one agast she start,
Accusing all my thoughts with breach of duty.
As if my hart had robbed her of her fair,
No, no, her fair bereand my hart of joy:
And fates disdain hath killed me with despair,
Despair the fountain of my sad annoy.
And more, alas, a cruel one I served,
Lest loved of her whose love I most deserved.
