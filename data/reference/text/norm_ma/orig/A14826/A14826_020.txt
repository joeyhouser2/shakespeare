The bank whereon I leaned my restless head,
Placed at the bottom of a myrtle tree:
I oft had watered with the tears I shed,
Sad tears did with the fallen earth agree.
Since when the flocks that graze upon the plain,
Do in their kind lament my woes though dumb:
And every one as faithful doth refrain
To eat that grass which sacred is become.
And every tree forbeareth to let fall,
