Oft have I railed against love many ways,
But pardon love I honour now thy power:
For were my Palace Greece Pyramides,
Cupid should there erect a stately bower.
And in my Palace sing his sugared songs,
And Venus Doves my self will finely feed:
And nurce her sparrows and her milk white Swans.
Yea, in my restless bosom should they breed.
And thou deare Lady sacred and divine,
Shalt have thy place within my hart assigned:
Thy picture yea thy fiery darting eien,
Ile carry painted in my grieved mind.
The chiefest coulle•s shall be scarlet blood,
Which Cupid pricketh from my woeful hart:
And tears commixt shall further forth my good,
To paint thy glories cording their desart.
I now am changed from what I wont to be,
Cupid is God, And there is none but he.
