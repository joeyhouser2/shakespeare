Tho with a shower of tears I entertained,
My wounded hart into my brest accloyed:
With thousand sundry cares and griefs unfeigned,
Unfeigned griefs and cares my hart annoyed.
Annoying sorrows at my hearts returning,
Assailed my thoughts with never ceasing horror:
That even my hart hart like to Aetna burning,
Did often times conspire for to abhor her.
But envious love still bent to eke my mourning,
A grievous penance for my fault inflicted:
That eyes should weep and hart be ever groaning;
So love to work my sorrows was addicted.
But earths sole wonder whose eyes my sense appalled,
The fault was loves, then pardon me, for love is frantic called.
