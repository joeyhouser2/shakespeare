Long have I swome against the wished wave,
But now constrained by a lothsome life:
I greedily do seek the greedy grave,
To make an end of all these storms and strife.
Sweet death give end to my tormenting woes,
And let my passions penetrate thy brest:
Suffer my hart which doth such griefs in close
By timely fates inioie eternal rest.
Let me not dwell in dole sith thou maist ease me,
Let me not languish in such endless durance:
One happy stroke of thy sad hand will please me,
Please me good death it is thy procurance.
To end my hearts grief (heart she did abhor thee)
O hast thee gentle death I linger for thee.
