Diana and her nimp•s in sylvan brook,
Did wash themselves in secret far apart:
Bu• bold Acteon dard on them to look,
For which fair Phoebe turned him to a Hart.
His hounds unweeting of his sudden change,
Did hale and pull him down with open cry:
He then repenting that he so did range,
would speak but could not, so did sigh and die.
But my Diana fairer and more cruel,
Bereft me of my hart and in disdain:
Hath turned it out to feed on fancies fuel,
And live in bondage and eternal pain.
So hartles do I live yet cannot die,
Desire the dog, doth chase it to and fro:
Unto her brest for succour it doth fly,
If she debar it whither shall it go.
Now lives my hart in danger to be slain,
Unless her hart my hart will entertain.
