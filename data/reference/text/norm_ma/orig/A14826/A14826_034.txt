Imperious love who in the prime of youth,
I light esteemed as an idle toy:
Though late thy fiery dart hath caused my ruth,
And turned sweet happiness to dark annoy.
Why hast thou pleasure in my hearts deep groaning,
And dost not rew and pity my vexations?
Why hast thou joy at my laments and moaning,
And art not moved at my imprecations?
Why hast thou stroke my hart with swift desire,
And pierced my Ladies eyes with fell disdain?
Why hath fond fancy set my thoughts on fire,
And pent my hart in prison of sad pain?
Why am I drownd in dolors never ceasing,
My joys still fading, and my woes increasing.
