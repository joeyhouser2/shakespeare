MY watery eyes let fall no trickling tears,
But floods that over flow abundantly:
Whose spring and fountain first in forced by fears,
Doth drown my hart in waves of misery.
My voice is like unto the raging wind,
Which roareth still and never is at rest:
The divers thoughts that tumble in my mind,
Are restless like the wheel that wherles alway.
The smoky sighs that boil out of my brest,
Are far unlike to those which others use:
For Lovers sighs sometimes do take their rest,
And lends their minds a little space to muse.
But mine are like unto the surging seas,
Whom tempest calm nor quiet can appease.
