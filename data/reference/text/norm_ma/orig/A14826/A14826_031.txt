O Would my love although too late lament me,
And pity take of tears from eyes distilling:
To bear these sorrows well I could content me,
And ten times more to suffer would be willing.
If she would dame to grace me with her favour,
The thought thereof sustained grief should banish:
And in beholding of her rare behaviour,
A smiyle of her should force despair to vanish:
But she is bent to tiranze upon me,
Despair persuades there is no hope to have her:
My hart doth whisper I am woe begone me,
Then cease thy vain plaints and desist to crave her.
Here end my sorrows here my salt tears stint I,
For shes obdurate, stern, remorseless flinty
