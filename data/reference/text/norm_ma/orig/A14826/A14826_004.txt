She smiled to see her son in such a rage,
I laught to think how I had Love prevented:
He frowned and vowed nought should his ire asswage,
Till I had stooped to Love, and love repented.
The more he raged the greater grew our laughter,
The more we laught the fiercer was his ire:
And in his anger sware my poor hearts slaughter,
Which in my breast beauty should set on fire.
Fair Venus seeing her deer son in chollar,
Fearing mishap by his too hasty anger:
Persuaded him that she would work my dollor,
And by her means procure my endless langor.
So Love and loves Queen (Love having consented,
Agreed that I by Love should be tormented.
