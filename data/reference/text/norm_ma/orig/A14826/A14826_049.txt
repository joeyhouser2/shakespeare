Were words dissolved to sighs, sighs into tears,
••d every tear to torments of the mind:
The minds distress into those deadly fears,
That find more death than death it self can find.
Were all the woes of all the world in one,
Sorrow and death set down in all their pride:
Yet were they insufficient to bemoan,
The restless horrors that my hart doth hide.
where black despair doth feed on every thought,
And deep despair is cause of endless grief:
where every sense with sorrows overwrought,
Lives but in death despairing of relief.
whilst thus my hart with loves plague torn asunder,
May of the world be cald the woeful wonder.
