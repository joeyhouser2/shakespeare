Go Idle lines unpolished rude and base,
Unworthy words to blason beauties glory:
(Beauty that hath my restless hart in chase,
Beauty the subject of my rueful story.)
I warn thee shun the bower of her abiding,
Be not so bold ne hardy as to use to her:
Least she inraged with thee fall a chiding,
And so her anger prove thy woes renewer.
Yet if she deign to rew thy dreadful smart,
And reading laugh, and laughing so mislike thee:
Bid her desist, and look within my hart,
Where she may see how ruthless she did strike me.
If she be pleased though she reward thee not,
What others say of me regard it not.
