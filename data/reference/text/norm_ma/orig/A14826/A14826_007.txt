Then on the sudden fast away he fled,
He fled apace as from pursuing foe:
Ne ever looked he back, ne turned his head,
Until he came whereas he wrought my woe.
Tho casting from his back his bended bow.
He quickly clad himself in strange disguise:
In strange disguise that no man might him know,
So couched himself within my Ladies eyes.
But in her eyes such glorious bearnes did shine,
That wellnigh burnt loves party coloured wings,
Whilst I stood gazing on her sun-bright eien,
The wanton boy she in my bosom flings.
He built his pleasant bower in my brest,
So I in love, and love in me doth rest.
