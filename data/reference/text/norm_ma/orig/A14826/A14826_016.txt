A Ye me that love wants power to pierce the hart,
Of my hearts object beauties rarest wonder:
What is become of that heart-thrilling dart,
Whose power brought the heavenly powers under.
Ah gentle love if empty be thy quiver.
Un mask thy self and look within my brest:
Where thou shalt find the dart that made me shiver,
But can I live and see my love distrest.
Ah no that shaft was cause of sorrow endless,
And pain perpetual should my Lady prove:
If hart were pierced, the deare love be not friendless,
Although I never found a friend of love,
If not without her hart, her love be gained,
Let me live still forlorn and die disdained.
