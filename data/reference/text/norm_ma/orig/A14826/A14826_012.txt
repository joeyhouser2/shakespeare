MY hart imposed this penance on mine eyes,
(Eyes the first causers of my harss lamenting:
That they should weep till love and fancy dies,
Fond love the last cause of my hearts repenting.
Mine eyes upon my hart inflict this pain,
(Bold hart that dard to harbour thoughts of love)
That it should love and purchase fell disdain,
A grievous penance which my hart doth prove.
Mine eyes did weep as hart had them imposed,
My hart did pine as eyes had it constrained:
Eyes in their tears my paled face disclosed,
Hart in his sighs did show it was disdained.
So th'one did weep th'other sighed, both grieved,
For both must live and love, both unrelieved.
