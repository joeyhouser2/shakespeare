Now Love triumphed having got the day,
Proudly insulting, tyrannizing still:
As Hawke that ceazeth on the yielding pray,
So am I made the scorn of Victors will.
Now eyes with tears, now hart with sorrow fraught,
Hart sorrows at my watery tears lamenting:
Eyes shed salt tears to see hearts pining thought,
And both that then love scorned are now repenting.
But all in vain too late I plead repentance,
For tears in eyes and sighs in hart must weeld me:
The feathered boy hath doomed my fatal sentence,
That I to tyrannizing Love must yield me.
And bow my neck erst subject to no yoke,
To Loves false lure (such force hath beauties stroke.
