Tho taking in her lap the God of love,
She lightly mounted through the Christall air:
And in her Coach ydrawne with silver Doves,
To Vulcans smoky Forge she did repair.
Where having won the Ciclops to her will,
Loves quiver fraught with arrows of the best:
His bended bow in hand all armed to kill,
He vowed revenge and threatened my unrest.
And to be sure that he would deadly strike me,
His blindfold eyes he did a while uncover:
Choosing an arrow that should much mislike me,
He bade wound him that scorns to be a Lover.
But when he saw his bootless arrow shiver,
He brake his bow, and cast away his quiver.
