Where may I now my careful corpse convey,
From company the worker of my woe:
How may I wink or hide mine eyes always,
Which gase on that whereof my grief doth grow,
How shall I seem my sighs for to suppress,
Which help the hart which else would swelt in sunder,
Which hurts the help that makes my torment less:
Which helps and hurts, O woeful weary wonder,
How now, but thus in solitary wise:
To step aside and make hie way to moan,
To make two fountains of my dazzled eyes,
To sigh my fill till breath and all begone.
To die in sorrow and in woe repent me,
That love at last would though too late lament me.
