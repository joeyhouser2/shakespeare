Here end my sorrow no here my sorrow springeth,
Here end my woe no here begins my wailing:
Here cease my grief no here my grief deep wringeth
Sorrow woe grief nor ought else is availing.
Here cease my tears no here begins eyes weeping,
Here end my plaints no here begins my pining:
Here hart be free no sighs in hart still keeping,
Tears plaints and sighs all cause of joys declining.
Here end my love no here doth love inspire me,
Here end my hope no here doth hope fair flourish:
Here end my life no let not death desire me,
Love hope and life and all with me must perish.
For sorrow woe grief tears and plaints oft plained,
Sighs love hope life and I must die disdained.
