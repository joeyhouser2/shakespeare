Hopeless and helpeles too, poor love amated,
To see himself affronted with disdain:
And all his skill and power spent in vain,
At me the only object that he hated.
Now Cytherea from Olimpus mount,
Descending from the sphere with her deer son:
With Dovelike wings to Alcidalyon,
Love on her knee, she by the Christall fount;
Advised the boy what scandal it would be,
If Fame should to the open world discover
How I survived and scorned Loves sacred power.
Then Cupid lightly leaping from her knee,
Unto his mother vowed my discontenting:
Unhappy vow the ground of my lamenting.
