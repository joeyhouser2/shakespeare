Come shadow of my end: and shape of rest,
Allied to death, child to this black fast night,
Come thou and charm these rebels in my brest,
Whose waking fancies doth my mind affright.
O come sweet sleep, come or I did for ever,
Come ere my last sleep, coms or come never.
