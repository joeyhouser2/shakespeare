Hast hapless sighs and let your burning breath
Dissolve the Ice of her indurate harte,
Whose frozen rigor like forgetful death,
Feels never any touch of my desert:
Yet sighs and tears to her I sacryfise,
Both from a spotless hart and patient eyes.
