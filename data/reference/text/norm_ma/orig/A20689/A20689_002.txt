But what can stay my thoughts they may not start,
Or put my tongue in durance for to die?
When as these eyes the keys of mouth and harte
Open the lock where all my love doth lie;
Ile seal them up within their lids for ever,
So thoughts and words and looks shall die together,

How shall I then gaze on my mistress eyes?
My thoughts must have some vent else hart will break,
My tongue would rust as in my mouth it lies
If eyes and thoughts were free and that not speak.
Speake then and tell the passions of desire
Which turns mine eyes to floods, my thoughts to fire
