Shall rigor reign where youth hath ron, shall fancy now forsake.
Shall fortune lose that favour won, shall not your anger flake:
Shall hateful harte be had in you, that friendly did pretend,
Shall slipper thoughts and faith untrue, that harte of yours defend

Shall nature shewe your beauty fair, that gentle seems to be,
Shall frowardness, your fancies ayer, be of more force then she:
Shall now disdain the drag of death, direct and lead the way,
Shall all the imps upon the yearth, rejoice at my decay.

Shall this the service of my youth, have such reward at last,
Shall I receive rigor for ruth, and be from favour cast:
Shall I therefore berent my harte, with wights that wish to die,
Or shall I bathe my self with tears, to feed your fickle eye.

No no I shall in pain lie still, with Turtle dove most true,
And vow my self to wit and will, their counsels, to ensue:
Good Ladies all that lovers be, your help hereto purtende,
Give place to wit, let reason seem, your enemy to defend.

Lest that you think as I have thought, your self to strive in vain,
And so to be in thraldom brought, with me to suffer pain.
