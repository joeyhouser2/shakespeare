When first mine eyes did vew, and mark thy beauty fair for to behold,
And when mine ears, gan first to hark, the pleasant words that thou me told
I would as then I had been free, fron ears to hear, and eyes to se

And when my hands did handle oft, that might thee keep in memory,
And when my feet had gone so soft, to find and have thy company,
I would each hand a foot had been, and eke each foot, a hand so seen.

And when in mind I did consent, to follow thus my fancies will,
And when my harte did first relent, to taste such bait my self to spill,
I would my harte had been as thine, or else thy harte as soft as mine.

Then should not I such cause have found, to wish this monstruous sight to se,
Ne thou alas that madest the wound, should not deny me remedy,
Then should one will in both remain, to graunt one hart which now is twaine.
