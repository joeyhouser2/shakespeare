THE wrong is great, the pain above my power,
That yields such care in doubtful dens to drown:
Such happen is hard, where fortune doth so lower,
As friendly look, is turned to froward frown.
Is this the trust that faithful treendes can find?
With those that yet have promise broke?
By deeds in dout, as though no words can bind,
A vowed freend to hold him to his yoke.

O faithless freend? what can assure your mind,
That doubts so soon, before you have cause why?
To what hard happen? doth Fortune here me bind,
When words nor deeds can no way satisfy.
What can I write? that hath not oft been said?
What have I said? that other hath not affirmed?
What is approved? that ought to be assayed?
Or what is vowed? that shall not be performed?

Cast of mistrust, in haste no credit give,
To this or that, that breedeth friends unrest:
No doubt at all, but trust me if I live,
My deeds shall prove, that all is for the best.
And this beleeve, the Sea shall cease to flow,
The Sun to shine within the settled sky:
All things on earth, shall leave to spring and grow,
Yea every foul shall want, his wings to fly.

Ear I in thought, shall seem once to retire,
If you my freend remain, as I desire:
Now lose no time, but use that whyle you may,
Forget not this, a dog shall have a day.
