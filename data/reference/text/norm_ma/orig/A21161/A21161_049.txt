I Would to God I were Acteon, that Diana did disguise,
To walk the Forest up and down, whereas my lady lies:
An Harte of here and hew, I wish that I were so,
So that my Lady knew me, only and no mo.
The shalyng Nuts and Mast, that falleth from the tree,
Should well suffice for my repast, might I my lady see:
It should not greve me, there in frost, to lie upon the ground,
Delite should easily quite the cost, what evil so that I found.
Sometime that I might say, when I saw her alone,
Behold, see yonder slave alday, that walketh the woods alone,
