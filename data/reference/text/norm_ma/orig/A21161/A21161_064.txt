I Would it were not as I think, I would it were not so,
I am not blind although I wink, I feel what winds do blow:
I know where craft, with smiling cheare, creps into bloody brest,
I hear how feigned speech, speaks fair, where hatred is possest.
I se the Serpent lie and Jurck, under the green allow,
I see him watch a time, to work, his poison to bestow.

In friendly looks such fraud is found, as faith for fear is fleade,
And friendship hath received such wound, as he is almost dead,
And hateful harte with malice great, so boils in cankered mind:
That flatteries flearyng in my face, had almost made me blind,
But now I see all is not gold, that glittereth in the eye,
Nor yet such friends as they profess, as now by proof I find.

Though secret spight by craft, hath made a coat of Panters skin,
And thinks to find me in the shade, by sleight to wrapp me in,
Yet God be praised my eye is clear, and can behold the Son:
When falsehood dares not once appear, to end that he begun,
Thus time shall try the thing amiss, which God soon shortly send.
And turn the hat that feigned is, to be a faithful friend.
