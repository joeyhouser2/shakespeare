Praised be Dianas fair and harmless light,
Praised be the dewes, wherewith she moists the ground;
Praised be her beams, the glory of the night,
Praised be her pour, by which all powers abound.

Praised be her Nimphs, with whom she decks the woods,
Praised be her knights, in whom true honor lives,
Praised be that force, by which she moves the floods,
Let that Diana shine, which all these gives.

In heaven Queen she is among the spheres,
In ay she Mistress like makes all things pure,
Eternity in her oft change she bears,
She beauty is, by her the fair endure.

Time wears her not, she doth his chariot guide,
Mortality below her orb is plaste,
By her the vertue of the stars down slide,
In her is virtues perfect image cast.

A knowledge pure it is her worth to kno,
With Circes let them dwell that think not so.
