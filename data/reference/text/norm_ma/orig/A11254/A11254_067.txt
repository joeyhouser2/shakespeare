OF thick and thin, light, heavy, dark and clear,
White, black, and blew, red, green, and purple die:
Gold, Silver, Brass, Lead, Iron, Tin, and Copper,
Moist air, hot fire, cold water, earth full dry:
Blood, Choler, Flegme, and Melancholy by,
A mixed mass, a Chaos all confused,
Such was the world, till God division used.

In framing heaven and earth, God did divide,
The first days light, and darkth, to night and day.
The second, he a firmament applied,
Third, fruitful earth appeared, Seas took their way,
Fourth, Sun and Moon, with Stars in skies he fixed,
Fift, Fish and Foul, the Sea and land possest,
And God made Man, like to himself, the sixt:
The seaventh day, when all things he had blest:
He hallowed that, and therein took his rest.
