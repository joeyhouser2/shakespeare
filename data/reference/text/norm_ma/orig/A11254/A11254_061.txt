Who plucks thee down fron hie desire poor hart?
Who comforts thee in depth of thy distress?
Amid contents, who breeds thy secret smart?
Who seeks the mean, thy sorrows may be less?

Who calls thy wits together to their work:
Who warnes thy will, to follow wary wit?
Who lets thee see in love what sorrows lurk?
Who makes thee feel the force of fancies fit?

Who taught thee first to try before thou trust?
Who bids thee keep a faithful tried freend?
Who wills thee say, love wantons he that lust?
Who wins the wish, that hath a happy end?

Care then to keep, that faithful friend in store,
Whose love commands, that thou shalt care no more.
