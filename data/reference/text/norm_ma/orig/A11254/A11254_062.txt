Those eyes that holds the hand of every hart,
Those hands that holds the hart of every eye,
That wit that goes beyond all natures Art,
That sence too deep, for wisdom to descry,
That eye, that hand, that wit, that heavenly sence,
All these doth show my Mistress Excellence.

Oh eyes that perce into the purest hart,
Oh hands that hold, the highest hearts in thrall,
Oh wit that ways the depth of all desart,
Oh sence that shows, the secret sweet of all,
The heaven of heavens, with heavenly powers preserve thee,
Love but thy self, and give me leave to serve thee.

To serve, to live, to look upon those eyes,
To look, to live, to kiss that heavenly hand,

To sound that wit, that doth amaze the wise,
To know that sence, no sence can understand,
To understand that all the world may know,
Such wit, such sence, eyes, hands, there are no moe.
