Eskaept from waltering waves,
from sword and fier, and enemies sleight:
From storms and sturdy flaws,
from roeryng shot and fearful fight.
I come to quiet land,
where noble Prince doth pastimes vew:
And bring a boek in hand,
of all the shoes, and matter trew.
That must by practies pass,
before your highness as it false:
And surely sent I was,
by those that keeps your warlike wauls.
To crave your curteys aid,
in their defence that peace desires:
Whoes staet is maed afraid,
by falls dissenshons kindled fiers.
As your poer people have,
throw peace possest great gain and good:
So still such peace they crave,
as may avoid the loss of blood.
As heer I cam a main,
so have I promesd if I may:
For to retorn again,
throw salt sea foem the saem self way.
