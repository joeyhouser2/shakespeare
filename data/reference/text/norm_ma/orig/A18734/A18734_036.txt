Our Joy is joined with grievous groens,
Our triumph turned to tears:
The brantch whoes blosoms gladness brought,
a bitter berry bears.
In house and street where mirth was hard,
is moen and morning noies:
The sommer day is dimmed with clouds,
eclipsed aer our joys.
The loedstar leaves our wished cowrs,
and clims the heavens hie:
Our sofrant will no longer lord,
in walls of Brislow lie.
No marvel sins our barrain soil,
and ground of groes devyce:
Haeth yielded no thing that might pleasant,
a prince of so great price.
Our duties aer not half discharged,
no though we kist the grownd:
And prostraet fall full flat on face,
where her foet steps aer found.
