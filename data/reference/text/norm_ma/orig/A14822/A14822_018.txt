Mine eyes die first, which last enjoyed life,
Not hurt by bleared eyes, but hurt with light
Of such a blazing star as kindleth strife
Within my brest as well by day as night:
And yet no poisoned Cockatrice lurked there,
Her vertuous beams dissuade such foolish fear.
Besides, I live as yet; though blinded now
Like him, that saw Mineruaes naked side,
And lost his sight (poor soul) not knowing how;
Or like to him, whom evil chance betide,
