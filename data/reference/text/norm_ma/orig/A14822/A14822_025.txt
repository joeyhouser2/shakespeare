IF Poets have done well in times long past,
To glose on trifling toys of little price:
Why should not I presume to fame as fast,
Espying forth a ground of good devise?
A Sacred Nymph is ground whereon ile write,
The fairest Nymph that ever yet saw light.
And since her song hath fild mine ears with joy,
Her virtues pleased my mind, her face mine eye,
I dare affirm what some will think a toy,
She Phoenix is, though not of Arabie;
And yet the plumes about her neck are bright,
And Sol him self in her hath chief• delight.
You that will know why Sol affords her love,
Seek but the cause why Peakocks draw the place,
Where Juno sits; why Venus likes the Dove;
Or why the Owl befits Mineruaes grace;
Then if you grudge, that she to Sol belong,
Mark but her face, and hear her skill in song.
