Morte! che vuoi? te bramo: Eccomi appresso;
Prendemi: a che? che manchi el mio dolore;
Non posso: ohime, non puoi? non per adesso;
Perch? pero che in te non regnail core. etc.
Amor, amor: chi è quel che chiama tanto?
Un tuo seruo fidel; non ti conosco; etc.
Come gentle Death; who calls? one thats opprest:
What is thy will? that thou abridge my woe,
By cutting of my life; cease thy request,
I cannot kill thee yet: alas, why so?
Thou wantest thy Hart. Who stoale the same away?
Love, whom thou servest, intreat him if thou may.
Come, come, come Love: who calleth me so oft?
Thy Uassall true, whom thou should'st know by right.
What makes thy cry so faint? my voice is soft,
And almost spent by wailing day and night.
Why then, whats thy request? that thou restore
To me my Hart, and steal the same no more.
And thou, O Death, when I possess my Hart,
Dispatch me then at once: why so?
By promise thou art bound to end my smart.
Why, if thy Hart return, then whats thy woe?
That brought from cold, It never will desire
To rest with me, which ani more hote then fire.
