Thy book beginning sweet and ending sowre,
Dear friend, betrays thy false success in love,
Where smiling first, thy Mistress falls to lowre,
When thou did'st hope her courtesy to prove;
And finding thy expected luck to fail,
Thou false from praise, and dost begin to rail.
To use great terms in praise of thy devise,
I think were vain: therefore I leave them out;
Content thee, that the Censure of the wise
Hath put that needeles question out of doubt:
Yet how I weigh the work that thou hast wrought,
My judgement I refer unto thy thought.
