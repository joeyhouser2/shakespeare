Hark you that list to hear what saint I serve:
Her yellow locks exceed the beaten gold;
Her sparkling eyes in heaven a place deserve;
Her forehead high and fair of comely mould;
Her words are music all of silver sound;
Her wit so sharp as like can searse be found:
Each eybrowe hangs like Iris in the skies;
•Her Eagles nose is straight of stately frame;
On either cheek a Rose and Lillie lies;
Her breath is sweet perfume, or holly flame;
Her lips more red then any Coral stone;
Her neck more white, then aged Swans the mone;
Her brest transparent is, like Christall rock;
Her singers long, fit for Apolloes Lute;
Her slipper such as Momus dare not mock;
Her virtues all so great as make me mute:
What other parts she hath I need not say,
Whose face alone is cause of my decay.
