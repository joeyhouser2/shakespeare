MY little book go hye thee hence away,
Whose price (God know's) will countervail no part
Of pains I took, to make thee what thou arte:
And yet I joy thy birth. But hence I say,
Thy brothers are half hurt by thy delay;
For thou thy self arte like the deadly dart,
Which bred thy birth from out my wounded hart.
But still observe this rule where ere thou stay,
In all thou mayest tender thy fathers fame,
" Bad is the Bird, that fileth his own nest.
If thou be much misliked, They are to blame,
Say thou, that deeds well done to evil wrest:
Or else confess, A Toy to be thy name;
" This trifling world A Toy beseemeth best.
