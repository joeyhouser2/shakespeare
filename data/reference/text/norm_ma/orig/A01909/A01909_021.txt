The little Fish, that in the streme doth fleet
With brode forth stretched Fyns for his disport
When as he spies, the Fishes bait so sweet.
In haste he hies, fearing to come to short.
But all to soon (alas) his greedy mind,
By rash attempt, doth bring him to his bane,
for where he thought a great relyefe to find,
By hidden hook, the simple fole is tane.
So fareth man, that wanders here and there,
Thinking no hurt to happen him thereby,
He ronnes amain, to gase on Beauties cheare,
Takes all for gold that glisters in the eye,
And never leaves to feade by looking long,
On Beauties Bait, where Bondage lies enwrapped,
Bondage that makes him sing an other song,
And makes him curse the bait that him entrapte.
Nevell to the, that lovest their wanton looks,
Feade on the bait, but pet beware the Hooks.
