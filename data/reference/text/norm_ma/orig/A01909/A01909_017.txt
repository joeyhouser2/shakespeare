Old Socrates, whose wisdom did excel,
And past the reach, of wisest in his time,
Surmounted all, that on the earth did dwell,
That Craggy Hyls, of vertue hye did clyme,
That Socrates, my Cobham did allow,
Each man in youth, him self in Glass to vew,
And wild them oft, to use the same, but how?
Not to delight, in form of fading hew.
Nor to be proud thereof, as many be,
But for to strive, by beauty of the mind,
For to adorn, the beauty he doth se.
If warlike form, Dame Nature him assygnde,
By vertuous life, than countenance for to get,
That shall deface, the fairest of them all,
Such Beauty as no age nor years will fret:
That flies with fame, when fickle form doth fail,
Thus much I say, that here to the present,
My words a Glass for the to look upon.
To the whom God, in tender years hath lent,
A towardenes, that may be mused upon,
Such towardenes, as in more graver years,
Doth sure a hope, of greater things pretend,
Thy noble mind, that to thy friends appear,
Doth show the blood, whereof thou doste descend,
The gentleness, thou usest unto all such,
As smally have deserved good will of the,
Doth show the grace, thou hast that sure is much,
As ever yet, in any I did se,
Thy wit as ripe, as Nature well can gyve,
Declares a greater hope than all the rest,
That shall remain, to the whilst thou doste live,
In desperate yls, a Medicine ever prest.
The good behavyour, of thy self in place
Wheresoever that thou chancest for to light,
So much both beauty, mind and wit doth grace
As well can be required of any wight.
What resteth now? but only God to praise,
Of whom thou hast received these Gifts of thyns,
So shalt thou long, live hear with happy days,
And after Death, the starry Skies shalt clyme,
Let naughty men, say what they list to the,
Trade thou thy self, in serving him above,
No sweter service can devised be,
Whom if thou fearest, and faithfully doste love,
Be sure no thing, on earth shall the annoy,
Be sure he will, the from each harm defend,
Be sure thou shalt, long time thy life enjoy,
And after many years to have a blessed end.
