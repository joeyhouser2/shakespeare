Farewell thou fertyll soil, that Brutus fyrst out found,
When he pooresoule, was driven clean fron out his Country ground.
That Northward layst thy lusty sides amid the raging Seas.
Whose wealthy Land doth foster upp, thy people all in ease,
While others scrape and cark abroad, their simple food to get,
And selye Souls take all for good, that commeth to the Net.
Which they with painful pains dopych, in barrain burning Realms:
While we have all with out restraint among thy wealthy streams.
O blest of God thou Pleasant Ile, where wealth her self doth dwell:
Wherein my tender years I past, I bid thee now farewell.
For Fancy dryves me forth abroad, and bids me take delight,
In levyng thee and ranging far, to se some stranger sight.
And say I was not framed hear, to live at home with eas:
But passing forth for knowledge sake to cut the foaming seas.
