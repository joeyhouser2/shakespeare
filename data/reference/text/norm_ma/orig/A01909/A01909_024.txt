AS ofte as I remembre with my self,
The Fancies fond, that flame by foolish Love,
And mark the Furyes fell, the blinded elfe
And Venus she that raynes so sore above,
As ofte as I do se the woeful state,
Of Lovers all, and eake their misery,
The ones desiring mind the others hate,
Troth with the one, with the other Treachery,
So ofte say I, that blessed is the wight,
Yea Nevell blest, and double blest again,
That can by reason rule his mind a right,
And take such foolish fading toys for vain.
