The plunged mind in floods of griefs
The Senses drowned quyght,
The Hart opprest. The flesh consumed
The changed state outright.
The Body dried by broiling blase,
Of prevy scorching Flame.
The doulfull Face. The countnaunce sad
The drooping Courage tame.
The Scalding sighs. The grievous groones
The burning rage of fire
The ernest sute, The fruitless Toil.
The deep and hot Desire,
The Brains quite bruised and crushed with Cares.
The ever during sore.
The very pains of Hell it self,
with thousande mischyefes more,
Which wounded Hearts enflamd with Love
with Grief do overflow,
And works their endless plage and spight
Till Death from thence do grow.
All these conclude him blest (my Googe)
and trible blest again,
That taught by tract of Time can take
Such fading Toys for vain.
