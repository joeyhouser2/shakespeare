The rushing Rivers that do run
The valleys sweet adjourned new
That leans their sides against the Sun
with Flours fresh of sundry hew,
Both Ash and Elm, and Oke so hye,
Do all lament my woeful cry.

while winter blak, with hydious storms
Doth spoil the ground of Summers green,
while springtime sweet the leaf returns
That late on tree could not be seen,
while sommer burns while harvest rains
Still still do rage my restless pains.

No end I find in all my smart,
But endless torment I sustain
Since fyrst alas, my woeful Hart
By sight of the was forced to plain,
Since that I lost my Liberty,
Since that thou madste a Slave of me

My Hart that once abroad was free
Thy Beauty hath in durance brought
Ons reason ruled and guided me,
And now is wit consumed with thought
Ons I rejoiced above the Sky,
And now for the I alas I die.

Ons I rejoiced in Company,
And now my chief and whole delight
Is from my friends away to fly
And keep alone my wearied spryght
Thy face divine and my desire,
Fron flesh hath me transformed to fire.

O Nature thou that fyrst did frame,
My Ladies hear of purest Gold
Her face of Crystal to the same.
Her lips of precious Rubies molde
Her neck of Alablaster white
Surmounting far each other Wight

Why dydst thou not that time devise
Why dydst thou not foresee before?
The mischyefe that thereof doth rise,
And grief on grief doth heap with stor
To make her Hart of War alone,
And not of Flint and Marble Stone.

O Lady show thy favour yet,
Let not thy Servant die for the
Where Rigour ruled, let Mercy syt
Let Pity Conquer Cruelty,
Let not Disdain, a Fiend of Hell,
Posses the place, where Grace should dwell.
