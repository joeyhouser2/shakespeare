Good aged Bale: that with thy hoary hears
Doste yet persyste, to turn the painful Book,
O happy man, that hast obtaynde such years,
And leavst not yet, on Papers pale to look,
Gyve over now to beat thy wearied brains,
And rest thy Pen that long hath laboured sore
For aged men unfyt sure is such pain,
And the beseems to labour now no more,
But thou I think DONE Platoes part will play
With Book in hand, to have thy dying day.
