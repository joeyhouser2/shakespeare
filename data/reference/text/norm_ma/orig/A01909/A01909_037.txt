NO vainer thing there can be found amid this vale of strife,
As Ancient men report have made then trust uncertain life.
This trwe we daily find, by proofs of many years,
As answered well, unto thy vertuous fame,
The gentleness that at thy hands I found
In staungers house, all unacquainted I,
Good S. hath my Hart to the so bound,
That from the can it not be forced to fly,
In pledge whereof, my service here I gyve
If thou so wylte, to serve the whylst I live.
