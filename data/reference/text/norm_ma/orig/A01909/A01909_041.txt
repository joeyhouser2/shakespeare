O Raging Seas, and mighty Neptunes rayn• ▪
In monstrous Hills, that throwest thy self so hye,
That with thy sludes, doest beat the shores of Spayne:
And break the Clyves, that dare thy force envy.
Cease now thy rage, and laye thine Ire a side,
And thou that hast, the governance of all,
O mighty God, graunt Wether Wind and Tide,
Till in my Country Coast, our Anker fall.
