(O cruel hart) doste thou no more esteame the Love of me,
Regardest thou not, the health of him? that the, above the rest
Of Creatures all, and next to God, hath dearest in his brest.
Is pity placed from the so far is gentleness exylde?
Hast thou been fostered in the Caves, of Wolves or Lyons wild?
Hast thou been so? why then no force, the less I marvel I,
Such as the Dam, such is the yong experience trewe doth try.
Sith thou art of so fierce a mind, why did not God then place
In the, with such a Tigers Harte, a fowl yll faverde face?
Sure for no other end but that, he likes no Lovers trade,
And the therefore a raging Fende, an Angels face hathe made.
Such one as thou, was Go•gon ones as ancient Poets tell,
Who with her Beauty maz•d men, and now doth raygne in H•ll.
But mercy yet, of the I crave, if ought in the remain,
And let me not so long the force, of flaming fire sustain,
Let pity joynde with beauty be, so shall I not disdain.
My blood, my hart, my life to spend with toil, with strife, and pain,
To do the good, my breath to lose, if need shall so require,
But for my service and my pains thou gyvest me hate for hire.
Well now take this for end of all, I love and thou doste hate,
Thou livest in pleasures happily. and I in wretched stay.
Pains can not last for evermore, but time and end will try,
And time shall tell me in my age, How youth led me awry.
Thy face that me tormented so, in time shall sure decay,
And all that I do like or love, shall vanish quite away,
Thy face in time shall wrynckled be, at which I shall be glad,
To see thy form transformed thus, that made me once so sad,
Than shall I blame my folly moch and thank the mightiest king
That hath me saved till such a day, to se so fond a thing.
And till that time I will keep close my flames and let them blase,
All secretly within my brest, no man on me shall gase.
I will not trespass sinfully, for God shall geve me grace
To se the time wherein I shall neglecte thy foolish face,
And till that time adieu to thee, God keep thee far from me,
And send thee in that place to dwell, that I shall never see.
