This Mirror left of this thy Bird I find,
Hath not such force, to enter in the Hert,
To root away, Unthankfulness of mind,
As others have, the Virtues to pervert, (so prone we are to Vice:)
The Tench by kind, hath Salve for every Sore,
And heals the maimed Pike in his distress,
The Churlish Pike foe Gentleness therefore,
In his reward, doth Cruelty express.
His murdering mind, his filthy spotted faith,
When hungre pricks to fill his greedy Jaws,
He gripes his poor Chirurgeon unto death.
Who late to him of life was only cause.
Thy Merlians have few Ayryes in our ground
But Pikes have Spawns good stoore in every Pound
