Gyve Money me, take Friendship who so list,
For Friends are gone come once Aduersytie,
When Money yet remaineth safe in Chest,
That quickly can the bring from misery,
Fair face show friends, when riches do habounde,
Come time of proof, farewell they must away,
Beleve me well, they are not to be found.
If God but send the once a lowering day.
Gold never starts aside, but in distress,
Finds ways enough, to ease thine heaviness.
