Not from the hye Cytherion Hill nor from that Ladies throne
Fron whens flies forth the winged boy that makes some sore to grone.
But nearer hence this token coms, from out the Dungeon deep,
Where never Plutto yet did raygne nor Proserpyne did sleep.
Whereas thy faithful Servant lives. whom duty moves aright,
To wail that he so long doth lack, his own deare Masters sight.
