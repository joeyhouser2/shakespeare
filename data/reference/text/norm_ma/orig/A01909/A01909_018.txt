Devyne Camenes that with your sacred food,
Have fed and fosterde up from tender years,
A happy man, that in your favour stood
Edwards in Court that can not find his fears
Your names be blest, that in this present age
So fine a head, by Arte have framed out
Whom some hereafter helped by Poets rage,
Perchance may match, but none shall pass (no doubt)
O Plautus if thou wert alive again,
That Comedies so finely dydste endite.
Or Terence thou that with thy pleasant brain,
The hearers mind on stage dydst much delight.
What wold you say sirs if you should behold,
As I have done the doings of this man?
No word at all, to swear I durst be bold,
But burn with tears, that which with mirth began,
I mean your books, by which you gate your name,
To be forgot, you wolde commit to flame.
Alas I wolde Edwards more tell thy praise,
But at thy name my muse amazed stayes.
