Wan Mars had moved mortal hate and forced sumysh heat
And hye Bellona had decreed, to syt with Sword in Seat,
The Scottes untrue with fighting hand, their promise to deny,
Assembled fast, and England thought, the troth with them to try.
Chose Musclebroughe their fighting place amid those barren fields
Their breche of faith, there not to try with troth, but trotheles Shyeldes
In battle brave, and Army strong Encamped sore they laye,
Ten Scottes to one (a dreadful thing a doleful fighting day.)
That English men were all aghast, with quaking staves in hand.
To se their enemies lie so near, and death with them to stand.
No other remedy there was, but fight it out or fly.
And who should fyrst the Onset gyve, was sure therin to die.
Thus all dismayed, and wrapt in fear with doubtful mind they stand,
If best it be, with flight of foot, to strive or fight of hand.
Till at the length, a Captain stout. with hawtye mind gan speak.
O Towards all, and maydly men of Courage faint and weak,
Unworthy come of Brutus race, is this your manhood gone,
And is there none you Dastards all. that dare them set upon.
Then Shelley all inflamed with heat with heat of valiant mind,
No Towards we, nor maydly men, ne yet of Dastards kind,
I wold you wist did ever come, but dare be bold to try,
Our manhood hear, though nought appear but deth to all mens eye
And with these words (O noble hart) no longer there he stayed,
But forth before them all he sprang as one no whyt dismayed
With charged staff on foaming horse his Spurs with heals he strikes,
And forward ronnes with swyftye race, among the mortal Pikes
And in this race with famous end, to do his Country good,
Gave Onset fyrst upon his Foes, and lost his vital blood.
