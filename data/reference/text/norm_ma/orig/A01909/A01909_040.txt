O Fond affection, wounder of my Hart,
When wylt thou Cease, to breed my restless pain,
When comes the end, of this my Cruel smart:
When shall my force, beat back thy force again.
When shall I say, this restless rage of mine:
By Reason ruled, is banished quite a way,
And I escaped, these cruel bonds of thine:
O flaming seend, that seakest my decay.
Safe thinking I, •haribdis Rage to fly,
On Scyll a Rock, in Bonyvall I die.
