What length of verse can serve brave Mopsas good to show?
Whose virtues strange, and beauties such, as no ma then may know
Thus shrewdly burdened then, how can my Muse escape?
The gods must help, and precious things must serve to shew her shape.
Like great god Saturn fair, and like fair Venus chaste:
As smooth as Pan, as Juno mild, like goddess Iris fast.
With Cupid she foresees, and goes god Vulcans pace:
And for a taste of all these gifts, she steals god Momus grace.
Her forhead jacinth like, her cheeks of opal hue,
Her twinkling eyes bedecked with pearl, her lips as Saphir blew:
Her hair like Craple-stone; her mouth O heavenly wide;
Her skin like burnished gold, her hands like silver ure untried.
As for her parts unknown, which hidden sure are best:
happy be they which well beleeve, and never seek the rest.
