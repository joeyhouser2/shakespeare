AS rage doth kindle still the fire of wrath,
Patience to quench i•, store of water hath.
As fire impairs not gold, but makes it bright,
So greatest wrongs by patience are made light.
As physic doth repair decayed health,
So patience brings true blessings to the soul.
As water quencheth the extremest fire,
So patience qualifies the mightiest wrongs.
As Diamonds in the dark are best discerned,
So patience is in trouble best approved.
As angry spleens are hasty in revenge,
So discreet souls brook all with patience.
