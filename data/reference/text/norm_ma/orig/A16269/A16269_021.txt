AS greatest calms oft turn to thunderclaps,
So sweetest hopes do change to sourest haps.
As in mean places may much wealth be hid,
So little hopes may mighty things expect.
As sadness is the hearts chief punishment,
So hope is highest help in deep distress.
As one part of the body toils for all,
So hope strives to accomplish all desires.
As every mettle is of Sulphur made,
So every pleasure doth from hope proceed.
As honest pastimes can no way offend,
So good mens hopes must needs have happy end.
