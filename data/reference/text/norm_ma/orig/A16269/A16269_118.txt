AS courage addeth wings to brave desire,
So bloody shows doth quench incensedire.
As it is valour to be conqueror,
So wisdom maketh use of victory.
As courage keeps the mind from base assaults,
So cowardise infects it with all faults.
As courage is esteemed a wise mans coat,
So cowardise is follies cognisance.
As Faulconers do in Falcons most delight,
So mighty men reioyceth in their might.
