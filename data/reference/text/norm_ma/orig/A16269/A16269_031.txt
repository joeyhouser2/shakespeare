AS no content is like the sweets of love,
So no despair can match with jealousy.
Love, as it is divine with loyalty,
So is it hellish, wrapt in jealousy.
As from small brooks great rivers do arise,
So huge distemper springs from jealousy.
As Crows do deem their brood the fairest birds,
So jealous men their own choise most commend.
As ships in tempests by the winds are tost,
So fond conceits do hurry jealous heads.
As kindness doth delight in company,
So is it poison to mad jealousy.
