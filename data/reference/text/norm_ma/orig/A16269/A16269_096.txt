Scevola in the greatness of his mind,
Entered Porsennaes Tent to murder him.
Queen Tomiris to shew her dauntless mind,
With Cyrus blood, revenged her deare sons death.
Zenobia told Aurelian in the field,
He was not able to subdue her mind.
Lucius Dentatus, never matched for mind,
Came eighteene times a conqueror from field.
Cicero saith, the goodness of the m•nd,
Is most discerned in pardoning injuries.
Socrates said, His quietness in mind
Was cause he never sickened till his death.
