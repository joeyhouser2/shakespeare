AS shadows on our bodies do attend,
So praise doth wait on vertue to the end.
As praising make the Peacock spread her tail,
So men commended do express themselves.
As fools in folly are not to be soothed,
So wicked actions are not to be praised.
As Cocks by crowing shew their victory,
So mens own praises blab their obloquy.
As niggards are discerned by their gifts,
So mens commendings do express their love.
As greatest praises fatten not thy fields,
So much commending pleaseth not thy friend.
