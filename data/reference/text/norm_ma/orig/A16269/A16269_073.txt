As Bavens by their bans are easily known,
So envies looks do most disclose her self.
As greatest floods have always quickest ebbs,
So envies heat is commonly soon cold.
As rigour blasteth fancies fairest blooms,
So envy doth the no lest man disgrace.
As no disease with inward grief compares,
So nothing more than envy wounds the mind.
As fire not maintained is quickly out,
So envy not supplied, dies of it self.
As in the Cedar, worms do never breed,
So in the wise, envy can have no power.
