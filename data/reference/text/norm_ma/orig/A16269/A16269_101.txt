AS Rats and Mice do feed upon our meat,
So slanderers feed on flesh of other men.
As divers meats do hurt digestion,
So changeable reports begetteth slaunder.
As Princes arms reach very far in length,
So slaunder stretcheth unto following times.
As deep incisions are for festered sores,
So mighty means must cure up slanders wounds.
As vultures prey upon dead carrion,
So slanderers feed upon mens living names.
As Somners live by peoples daily sinnes,
So slanders live by killing mens good fame.
