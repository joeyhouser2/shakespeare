AS hottest love hath soonest cold disdain,
So greatest pleasures have the greatest pain.
As good and ill each other do pu••ue,
So hateful estimation scorn succeeds.
As Adders keep their venom in their tails,
So scoffers poison lurketh in their tongues.
As fairest beauty may deserve some blame,
So wittiest scoffs prove but ridiculous.
As some things sweet in taste, are sowre going down,
So scoffs that like the ear, dislike the mind.
As fair demeanour most commends a man,
So scorns and scoffs as much dishonour him.
