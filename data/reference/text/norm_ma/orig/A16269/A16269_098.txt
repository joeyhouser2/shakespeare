Zaleucus to the Locrians made a law,
To lose their eyes that sinned in foul desires.
Appius was banished the city Rome,
For leud affection to Virginia.
Mark Anthonie disgraced his former fame,
By not restraining his affections.
King Alexander hated to the death
In men or women lose and leud desires.
Fancy (saith Aristotle) often makes
A frenzy in their souls are led thereby.
Desire (saith Socrates) no limits holds,
And therefore hardly can be mastered.
