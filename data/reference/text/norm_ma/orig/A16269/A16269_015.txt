AS the best steeled glass bewrays the face,
So truth best shineth in an honest soul.
As purest Emeralds without foils shine best,
So truth most pleaseth in her plainest clothes.
As wormwood fitteth not a lickerish taste,
So truth doth never please a liars tongue.
As clouds oft threaten rain, and yet shed none,
So storms oft menace truth, yet hurt her not.
As darkness is an enemy to light,
So falsehood is continual foe to truth.
As mean attire impairs not beauties face,
So poorest rags to truth give no disgrace.
