AS persecution is the bodies death:
So foul despair quite kills the conscience.
As the bright Sun doth lighten all the world,
So a clear conscience shineth in the soul.
As beauty is a thing glads mortal sight,
So unstained conscience doth high heaven delight.
As wine cheers up the heart when it is sad,
So peace of conscience makes it much more glad.
As brazen walls defend a city best,
So conscience taintless, is at peace and rest.
As gold is best, when through the fire 'tis tride,
So conscience is by troubles purified.
