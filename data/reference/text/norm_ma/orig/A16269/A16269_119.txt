The Roman• Sergius, losing his right hand,
Slew with his left hand, foure in single fight.
Scaevola entered king Porsennaes Tent,
Either to kill him, or be slain by him.
Agis dissuaded from the fight, replied:
No man won shame, that with true courage dyde.
Stout All•ibiades cheered up his followers,
By his courageous lead•ng them to field.
Courage, saith Seneca, is of such power,
As it can conquer any misery.
Plato saith, Courage elevates the mind,
To all things that are laudable and just.
