AS fire supprest, is much more forcible,
So griefs concealed, urge greater passions.
As streams restrained, break through or overflow,
So sorrow smothered, grows to greater woe.
As tenderest wood is most annoyed of worms,
So feeblest minds doth sorrow most afflict.
As clouds do rob us of fair heavens beauty,
So care bereaves us of our speeches liberty.
As the sweet rose doth grow among the briars,
So oft in sorrows some content is found.
As discreet Pilots do for storms prepare,
So in our joy let us provide for care.
