AS rain makes every ground bring forth encrease.
So faith of every soul doth shew the fruits.
As honours fire doth kindle high desires,
So zealous faith lifts up the lowest soul.
As night doth best the diamonds glory show,
So sharp affliction best makes faith to grow.
As wisdom is the only way to weal,
So true discretion best directeth zeal.
As love and hate cannot agree in one,
So without zeal, faith thinks her self alone.
