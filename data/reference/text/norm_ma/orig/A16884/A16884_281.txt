Look as great Cinthia in her silver Carre
Rides in her progress round about her sphere,
Whose tendance is the fair eye-dazeling stars
Trooping about her Chariot, that with clear
And glorious shows makes every eye delight
To gaze upon the beauty of the night,
Clad and attended with the worlds delight,
So is the Queen in majesty brought forth.
Like trident-maced Neptune in his pride,
Mounted upon a Dolphin in a storm,
Upon the tossing billows forth doth ride,
About whose train a thousand Tritons swarm:
When Phaebus seems to set the waves on fire,
To shew his glory, and the Gods desire:
Or like unto the fiery-faced sun,
Upon his wagon prancing in the West,
Whose blushing cheeks with flames seem overrun
Whilst sweating thus he gallops to his rest:
Such was the glory wherein now I stood,
Which makes the Barons sweat their dearest blood.
As stately Thames inricht with many a flood
And goodly rivers that have made their graves
And buried both their names and all their good
within his greatness to augment his waves,
Glides on with pomp of waters unwithstood
Unto the Ocean, which his tribute craves,
And lays up all his wealth within that pour,
which in it self all greatness doth devour:
So flocked the mighty with their following train
Unto the all-receaving Bullenbrooke.
Then thou on thine imperial Chariot set
Crowned with a rich imperled Coronet,
Whilst the Parisian dames as thy train past
Their precious incense in aboundance cast:
As Cynthia from the wave-embateled shrowds
Opening the west, comes streaming through the clouds,
With shining troops of silver-tresled stars
Attending on her as her Torchbearers,
And all the lesser lights about her throne,
With admiration stand as lookers on,
Whilst she alone in height of all her pride
The Queen of light along her sphere doth glide.
