— O Soldiers envy neere ally to Kings
Majestic humour, careful jealous thought:
Thou, which awakest us from ignoble things,
A passion nearest to a godhead brought.
Only indefinite: to whom none brings
Limit or bound, thou greater then our thought,
Who holds thee, holds a power to make him able,
Who looses then, becomes most miserable.
None is so poor of sence and eine,
To whom a soldier doth not shine.
No elegancy can beautify
A shameless lump of gluttony:
His heart sweet Cupids tents rejects,
That only meat and drink affects.
O Flora all mens intellects,
Know soldiers power such respects,
Mere helps for need his mind sufficeth,
Dull sleep and surfeits he despiseth:
Loves trump his temples exerciseth,
Courage and love his life compriseth.
