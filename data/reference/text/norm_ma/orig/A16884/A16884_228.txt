The silver Moon, dread sovereign of the deep
That with the floods fills up her horned head,
And by her wain, the waining ebbs doth keep.
—With a brase of silver hinds,
〈◊〉 Ivory Chariot swifter then the windes,
〈◊〉 great Hyperions horned daughter drawn,
•nchauntresse like, decked in disparent Lawn.
Circled with charms and incantations,
That ride huge spirits and ouragious passions;
Music and mood she loves, but love she hates,
As curious Ladies do their public cates.
Natures bright eyesight, and the nights fair soul,
That with thy triple forhead doost control
Earth, seas, and hell, and art in dignity
The greatest and swiftest Planet in the sky.
