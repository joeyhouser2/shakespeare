Sweet numbered Homer.
Heartstealing Homer, marrow of the Muses,
Chief grace of Greece, best pearl of Poetry,
Drowner of souls, with arts overwhelming sluices,
Embellished with Phoebes lunary,
Decked with the graces rich imbroderie.
Sweet honeysuckle, whence all Poets sprights,
Suck the sweet honey of divine delights.
