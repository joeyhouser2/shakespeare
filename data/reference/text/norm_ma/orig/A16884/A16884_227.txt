The first with cloths tucked up as Nimphs in woods do range,
Tucked up even to the knees, with bows and arrows prest
Her right arm naked was, discovered was her brest:
But heavy was her pace, and such a megre cheer,
As little hunting mind (God knows) did there appear.
— Now great Phoebe in her triumph came,
With all the titles of her glorious name,
Diana, Delia, Luna, Cynthia,
Virago, Hecate, and Elythia,
Prothyria, Dictinna, Proserpine,
Latona, and Lucina most divine.
