Repentance makes two rivers of her eyes,
Her humble face dares scant behold the skies:
Her broken breast is beaten blew and black,
Her tender fleshis rent with rugged sack,
With sorrows snows her hoary waxen head,
With ashes pale, and dust is overspread.
Repentance, hope, and soft humility,
Do flank the wings of faiths triumphant car,
Repentance,
A salve, a comfort, and a cordial,
He that hath her, the keys of heaven hath,
This is the guide, this is the port, the path.
O happy they that keep within their measure,
To turn their course in time, and sound retreat,
Before that wit which late Repentance tought,
Were better never had then so deare bought.
Sinnes have their salves, repentance can do much.
—To be penitent for faults, with it a paron bears.
Then hope we health when sin is left repentantly in hart,
Add then new life, and we to God, God doth to us convert.
Yet stay thy feet in murders ugly gate,
Ill comes to soon, repentance oft too late.
Their lives no man so settled in content,
That hath not daily whereof to repent.
We see what's good, and thereto we consent,
But yet we chuse the worse and soon repent.
