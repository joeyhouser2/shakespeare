—Cruel thirst came out of Cyren land,
Where she was fostered on the burning sand,
With hote intracted tongue, and sunken eine,
With stomach worn, and wrinkled visage keen
With light and meagre, corse, and pailed veins,
In steed of blood, that brimstone hot retains,
Her poisoned mouth blew through that holy town,
Such hellish air, that stiffeled up and down.
