Vulcan begot me, Minerua me taught,
Nature my mother, Craft nourished me year by year,
Three bodies are my food, my strength is naught,
Anger, Wrath, Wast, and Noise my children deer,
Gesse friend what I am, and how I am wrought:
Monster of sea, or land, or of elsewhere
Know and use me, and I may thee defend,
And I be thy enemy I may thy life end.
