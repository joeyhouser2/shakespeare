Much like as when the beaten mariner
That long hath wandered in the Ocean wide,
Oft sowest in swelling Tethis saltish tear,
And Long time having tamde his tawny hide,
With blustering breath of heaven that none can bide,
And scorching flames of fierce Orions hound,
Soon as the port from far he hath espied,
His cheerful whistle merrily doth sound,
And Nereus crowns with cups, his mates him pledge around,
Such Joy made Una when her knight she found.
Look how a troupe of winter prisoned dames,
Pent in the inclosure of the walled towns,
Welcomes the spring usher to summers flames,
Making their pas•imes on the flowery downs,
Whose beauteous Arras wrought in natures frames,
Through eyes admire, the heart with wonder crowns.
So these wood-walled citizens at sea,
Welcome be both spring and sommer in a day.
Like as a ship with dreadful storm long tost,
Having spent all her masts and her ground hold,
Now far from harbour, like to be lost,
At last some fisher bark doth near betold,
That giveth comfort to her courage cold:
Such was the state of this most knight.
