Injustice never yet took lasting root.
Nor held that long, Impiety did win.
So foul a thing, ô thou Injustice art,
That tormentest both the doer and distrest:
For when a man hath done a wicked part,
O how he strives to excuse, to make the best:
To shift the fault t'unburden his charged hart.
And glad to find the least surmise of rest.
And if he could make his, seem others sin,
O what repose, what ease he finds therein.
Injustice never scapes unpunished still,
Though men revenge not, yet the heavens will.
