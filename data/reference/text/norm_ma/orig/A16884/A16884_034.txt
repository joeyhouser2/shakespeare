Round headed Custom th'apoplexie is,
Of bedrid nature, and lives led amiss,
And takes away all feeling of offence.
Custom abused brings vertue in disdain.
Nature with Custom joined, never fails
But by her self, and her self prevails.
Whereas to nature, forward to retain,
Lewd objects are annexed, and Customs vain,
The wounds grow desperate, and death doth •nd,
Before good counsel can the fault amend.
Custom the worlds judgement doth blind so far,
That vertue is oft arraigned at vices bar.
