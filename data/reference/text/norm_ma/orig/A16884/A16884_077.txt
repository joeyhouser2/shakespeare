Oft times the greatest haste the worser speeds.
As busy brains must beat on tickle toys,
As rash invention breeds a raw device:
So sudden falls do hinder hasty joys,
And as swift baits do fleetest fish intice,
So haste makes waste, and therefore now I say,
No haste but good, where wisdom bears the sway.
The swiftest bitch brings forth the blindest whelps,
The hottest fevers coldest cramps ensue.
The nakedst need, hath over-latest helps.
Hasty respect, repents when tis too late.
Rashness sees all, but nothing can prevent.
Forejudging, puts out one of wisdoms eyes.
—If by rashness valour have got honour,
We blame the rashness, but reward the valour.
O rash false heat wrapt in repentance cold,
Thy haste springs still blood, and nere grows old.
