Heavens Crystal windows with one hand God opens
Where on the world a thousand seas he drops,
With th'other hand he gripes and wringeth forth,
The spongy globe of the execrable earth,
So straightly prest that it doth strait restore,
All liquid floods that it had drunk before,
In every rock new rivers do begin,
And to his aid the snows came tumbling in.
The Pines and Cedars have but bows to shew,
The shores do shrink, the swelling waters grow.
