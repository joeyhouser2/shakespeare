Thou seest no wheat Helleborus can bring,
Nor barley from the madding Morrell spring,
Nor bleating lambs brave lyons do not breed,
That leaprous parents raise a leaprous feed.
Even so our grandsire living innocent
Had stocked the whole world with a saint descent.
But suffering sin in Eden him invade,
His sons the soon of sin and wrath he made.
As done the pots that long retains the taste
Of licour, such as first was in them plaste:
Or like the tree that bends his elder branch
That way where first the stroke had made his launch•
So see we wolves and bears and hearts full old,
Some tameness from their daunted youth to hold.
—Loves fiery dart
Could nere unfreeze the frost of her chaste hart:
But as the diamond bides the hammer strong,
So she resisted all her suitors long.
