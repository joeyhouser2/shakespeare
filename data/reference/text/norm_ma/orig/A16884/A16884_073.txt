Thou Pain, the only ghuest of loathed constraint,
The child of curse, mans weakness foster child,
Brother to woe, and father of complaint,
Thou Pain, thou loathed pain from heaven exiled.
The scourge of life, and deaths extreme disgrace,
The smoke of hell, that monster's called pain.
The thing that grievous were to do or bear
Them to renew, I wot breeds no delight.
True grief is fond, and testy as a child,
Who wayward once, his mood with nought agrees,
Old woes not infant sorrows bear them mild,
Continuance tames the one, the other wild,
Like an unpractised swimmer plunging still
With too much labour drowns for want of skill.
Pain pays the income of each precious thing.
