Noah looks up, and in the air he views
A semicircle of an hundred hues;
which bright ascending toward th'aetheriall thrones,
Hath a line drawn betweene two Horizons
For just Diameter: an even bent bow
Contrived of three: whereof the one doth show
To be all painted of a golden hew;
The second green, the third an orient blew:
Yet so, that in this pure blew-golden green,
Still (ô pall-like) some changeable is seen;
A bow bright shining in th'archers hand,
Whose subtile string seems level with the land,
Half parting heaven, and over us it bends,
within two seas wetting his horned ends;
A temporal beauty of the lampfull skies,
where powerful Nature shows her fresh-red dies.
And if you only blew and red perceive,
The same as signs of sea and fire conceive,
Of both the flowing and the flaming doom,
The judgement past, and judgement yet to come.
