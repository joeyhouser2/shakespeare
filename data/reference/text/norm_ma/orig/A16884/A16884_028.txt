We must affect our Country as our parents,
And if at any time we alienate
Our love or industry from doing it honor,
It must respect effects and touch the soul,
Matter of conscience and religion,
And not desire of rule or benefit.
Necessity enforceth every wight,
To love his native seat with all his might.
A happy quarrel is it and a good,
For countries cause to spend our dearest blood.
That public weal must needs to ruin go,
Where private profit is preferred so.
Home though it homely be, yet is sweet,
And native soil is best.
If so the temperature of Commonweal
Be guided by the course of heavenly powers,
Such as in deep affairs will justly deal,
Must have an eye to those eternal bowers,
And by their view direct this state of ours.
Then how can he a perfect states man prove,
That knows not how celestial bodies move?
—The love
That men their country and their birthright bear,
Exceeds all love, and dearer is by far:
Our countries love, then friends or children are.
