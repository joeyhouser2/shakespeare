Fast by old age pale Malady was placed,
Sore sick in bed her colour all forgone,
Bereft of stomach, savour, and of taste,
Ne could she brook no meat but broths alone.
Abhorring her, her sickness past recure,
Detesting physic, and all physics cure.
Sickness the herald of arms, hearts, and all.
Th'humorous sick, removing, find no ease,
When changed chambers help not the disease.
— O sickness thou art oft betide,
When death hath many woes to come beside.
