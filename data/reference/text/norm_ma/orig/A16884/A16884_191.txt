The joyous day gan early to appear,
And fair Aurora fro her dewy bed
Of aged Tithon gan her self to rear
With rosy cheeks, for shame as blushing red.
Now when the rosy-fingered morning fair,
Weary of aged Tithons saffron bed,
Had spread her purple robe through dewy air,
And the high hills Titan discovered,
The royal Virgin shook off drowsy bed.
Now sullen night with slow sad pace descended
To ugly hell, when lo the blushing morrow
Lends light to all fair eyes that light will borrow.
Soon as the morrow saire with purple beams,
Dispersed the shadows of the misty night,
And Titan playing on the eastern streams,
Gan clear the dewy air with springing light.
The dewy Rose at morn had with her hairs,
In sundry sorts the Indian clime adorned,
And now her eyes appareled in tears,
The loss of lovely Memnon long had mornde.
The gaudy morn out of her golden sleep
Awaked, and little birds uncagde gan sing,
To welcome home the bridegroom of the sea.
The gray-eyed morn smiles on the frowning night,
Cheering the eastern clouds with streams of light,
And darkness flected like a drunkard reels,
From forth days pathway made by Titans wheels.
Now had the morn espied her lovers steeds,
Whereat she starts, puts on her purple weed,
And red for anger that he stayed so long,
All headlong throws her self the clouds among.
As soon as morning her shining hairs fro the mountains
Had shown forth and driven all star-light quite fro the heavens.
Fair Aurora betimes by the days break rose from her husband
Husband, old and cold, and drave back clouds fron Olympas
Making way to the sun, taking her way to the younker,
Brave yonker Cephalus whom fair Aurora desired.
Now was the time when as Aurora fair,
Began to shew the world her golden head,
And look abroad to take the cool fresh air,
Jealous Tithono lying still in bed.
The sable night dislodgd and now began,
Auroraes usher with a windy fan,
Sweetly to shake the woods on every side,
The whilst his mistress like a stately bride,
With flowers, with gems, and Indian gold doth spangle
Her lovely locks her lovers looks to tangle,
When passing through the air in mantle blue,
With silver fringe she drops the pearly dew,
With her goes Abram out.
The rosy fringed morn with gladsome ray,
Rose to her task from old Tithonas lap.
The night begins be angry when she sees
She can distil no sleep in lovers eyes,
Tossing her self among the clouds now hath
Sent the red morn as harauld of her wrath,
Whose lover Phebus rising from his bed,
With dewy mantle hath the world overspread,
Shaking his tresses our Neptunes ebb:
And giving tincture to the spiders web,
These fair nimphs rose, seeing the light did call.
Aurora bright her crystal gates unbatred,
And bridegroom like stepped forth the glorious sun
The dewy tressy morning newly wake,
With golden tinsel scarce had crowned her brow,
Riding in triumph on the Ocean lake,
Embellishing the hony-fringed bows.
The purple morning left her crimson bed,
And dond her robes of pure vermilion hue,
Her amber locks she crowned with roses red,
In Edens flowery gardens gathered new.
