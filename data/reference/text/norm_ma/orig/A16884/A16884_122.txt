O malcontent seducing ghuest,
Contriver of our greatest woes:
Which borne of wind and fed with shows,
Dost nurse thy self in thine unrest,
Judging ungotten things the best,
Or what thou in conceit designest.
Thou all things in the world dost deem,
Not as they are, but as they seem,
Thou soul of pleasure, hours only substance,
Great arbitrator, umpire of the earth,
Whom fleshly Epicures call virtues essence
Thou moving Orator, whose powerful breath
Sways all mens judgements, Great Opinion,
Opinion is as various as light change,
Now speaking courtlike friendly, straight as strange.
She's any humours perfect parasite,
Displeased with her, and pleased with her delight.
She is the Echo of inconstancy,
Soothing her no with nay, her I with yea.
This syren or Opinion, windborn lame,
Seeking to ease us, brings us to unrest:
For it adjudgeth nothing it doth see,
By what it is, but what it seems be.
We must in matters moral, quite reject
Vulgar Opinion, ever led amiss:
And let autenticke reason be our guide,
The wife of truth, and wisdoms governess.
