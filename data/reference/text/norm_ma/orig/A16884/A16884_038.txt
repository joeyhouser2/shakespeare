On the one side doubt, on the other sat Delay,
Behind the gate, that none her might espy;
Whose manner was, all passengers to stay,
And entertain with her occasions sly.
Through which, some lost great hope unheedilie,
Which never they recover might again:
And others quite excluded forth did lie.
Long languishing there, in unpitied pain,
And seeking often entrance afterward in vain.
—Danger grows by lingering till the last,
And physic hath no help when life is past.
—Oft things done, perhaps, do less annoy
Then may the doing, handled with delay.
Delay in close await
Caught hold on me, and thought my steps to stay,
Feigning still, many a fond excuse, to prate:
And time, to steal the treasure of mans day,
Whose smallest minute lost, no riches render may.
—Times delay new hope of help still breeds.
—Fearful tormenting
Is leaden servitor, to dull delay.
He that will stop the brook must then begin
When summers heat hath dried up the spring:
And when his pittering streams are low and thin▪
For let the winter aid unto them bring,
He grows to be of watery floods the king:
And though you dam him up with lofty ranks,
Yet will he quickly overflow his banks.
Ill news deferring, is a plague as great as an ill news.
Delay in love breeds doubts, but sharp denial death·
— Intermission suffers men dispute,
What dangers are, and cast with further care,
Cold doubt cavels with honour, scorneth fame,
And in the end fear weighs down faith with shame▪
Where hearts be knit, what helps if not in joy?
Delay breeds doubts, no cunning to be joy.
