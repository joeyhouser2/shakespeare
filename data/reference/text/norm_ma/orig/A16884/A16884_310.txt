I walked along a stream for pureness rare,
Brighter then sunshine, for it did acquaint
The dullest sight with all the glorious pray,
That in the pebble paved channel lay.
No molten Christall, but a Richer mine,
Even natures rarest alchemy ran there,
Diamonds resolved, and substance more divine,
Through whose bright gliding current might appear
A thousand naked Nymphs, whose yvorie shine,
Enameling the banks, made them more deare
Then ever was that glorious Pallas gate,
Where the day-shining sun in triumph sat.
Upon this brim the Eglantine and Rose,
The Tamoriscke, Olive, and the Almond tree,
As kind companions in one union grows,
Folding their twindring arms as oft we see,
Turtle taught lovers either other close,
Lending to dulness, feeling Sympathy.
And as a costly vallance ore a bed,
So did their garland tops the brook orespred:
Their leaves that differed both in shape and show,
(Though all were green) yet difference such in green
Like to the checkered bent of Iris bow,
Prided the running main as it had been.
—In that mead proud making grass,
A River like to liquid glass,
Did with such soundful murmur pass,
That with the same it wanton was.
Hard by this brook a Pine had seat,
With goodly furniture compleat:
To make the place in state more great,
And lesning the the inflaming heat,
Which was with leaves so beautified,
And spred his brest so thick and wide,
That all the Suns estranged pride,
Sustained repulse on every side.
The well of life, to life can dead restore,
And gilt of sinful crimes clean wash away:
Those that with sickness were infected sore,
It could recure, and ages long decay
Renew, as it were borne that very day.
Both Silo this and Jordan did excel,
And the English Bath, and eke the Germane Spanie,
Ne can Cephise nor Hebrus match this well.
Rich Oranochie though but known of late,
And that huge River which doth bear his name
Of warlike Amazons, which do possess the same.
—With the murmuring cadence of the wave,
Which made a pretty wrangling as it went:
Chiding the banks which no more limit gave,
There joined their wel-rund throats with such consent,
That even mad grief at sight thereof grew grave,
And as inchanted, staid from languishment.
Proving, then their delight was never greater,
And grief how much the more, so much the better.
—Laid at ease a cubit from the ground,
Upon a Jasper fringed with Ivy round.
Pu•fled with waves, thick thrumbd with mossy rushes,
He falls a sleep fast by a silent river,
Whose captive streams through crooked pipes still rushing,
Make sweeter music with their gentle gushing.
Then now at Tivoli, th'Hydrantike brawl,
Of rich Ferraras stately Cardinal,
Or C•esibes rare engines, framed there
Whereas they made of Ibis, Jupiter.
