Dumb Silence, sworn attendant on black night,
Thou that hast power to close up murmurs jawe:
To stop the barking of the watchful hound,
And charm the gaggling of those waking fowl,
That saved Joves Capitoll, mild Queen of rest.
Soft Silence, and submiss obedience,
Both linked together never do depart:
Both gifts of God, nor gotten but from thence,
Both girlands of his saints, against their foes offence.
—Silence wisdoms mother.
Silence doth seem the mask of base oppression.
