Her face was ugly, and her mouth distort,
Foaming with poison round about her gills,
In which her cursed tongue full sharp and short,
Appeared like Asps sting, that closely kills,
Or cruelly does wound, whom so she wills,
A distaff in her other hand she had,
Upon the which she little spins but spills,
And faine to weave false tales and leasings bad
To throw amongst the gods which others had dispread.
Her nature is, all goodness to abuse,
And causeless crimes continually to frame:
With which she guiltless persons may abuse,
And stole away the crown of her good name,
Ne ever knight so bold, ne ever dame
So chaste and loyal lived, but she would strive
With forged cause, them falsely to defame.
Ne ever thing was done so well alive,
But she with blame would blot, and of due praise deprive.
All like the stings of Asps, that kill with smart,
Her spiteful words do pierce and wound the inner part.
Foul canker of fair vertuous action,
Vile blaster of rhe fresh blooms here on earth,
Envies abhorred child detraction.
Happy is he that lives in such a sort,
That need not fear the tongues of false report.
The vulgar tongues are armed evermore
With slanderous brute, to blemish the renown
Of vertuous dames; which though at first it spring,
Of slender cause, yet doth it swell so fast,
As in short space it filleth every ear
With swift report of undeserved blame.
—It ever hath bene known,
They other virtues scorn that doubt their own.
No plaister heals a deadly poisoned sore,
No secret hid where slaunder keeps the door.
Against bad tongues goodness cannot defend her,
Those be most free from faults, they least will spare,
But prate of them whom they have scantly known,
Judging their humours to be like their own.
Slaunder once set on foot though false, is talked in every street.
No wound with warlike hand of enemy
Inflict with dint of sword so sore doth light,
As doth the poisonous sting which infamy
Infuseth in the name of noble wight.
It never can recured be again,
Ne all the skill which that immortal spright
Of Podalyrius did in it retain,
Can remedy such hurts: such hurts are hellish pain.
A sprightly wit disdains detraction.
