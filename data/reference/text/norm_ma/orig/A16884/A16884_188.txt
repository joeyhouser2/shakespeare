Night was far spent, and now in Ocean deep,
Orion flying fast from hissing snake,
His flaming head did hasten for to steep.
By this th'eternall lamps wherewith high Jove,
Doth light the lower world, were half yspent,
And the moist daughters of huge Atlas strove
Into th'ocean deep to drive their weary drove.
— The gentle humorous night,
Implies her middle course, and the sharp east,
Breathes on my spirit with his fiery steeds.
The silent night that long had sojourned,
Now gan to cast her sable mantle off,
And now the sleepy wainman softly drove
His slow-paced teem that long had travailed.
