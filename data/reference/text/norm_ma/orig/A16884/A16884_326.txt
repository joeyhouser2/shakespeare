—Ah that language sweet
Sure bond of Cities friendships mastic sweet,
Strong curb of anger yerst united, now
In thousand dry brooks strays, I wot not how:
That rare rich gold, that charm grief, fancy mover•
That calm-rage, hearts thief, quel-pride conjure-lover,
That purest coin then currant in each coast
Now mingled, hath sound, waight, and colour lost.
Tis counterfeit, and over every shore,
The confused fall of Babell yet doth roar.
— Then all spake the speech
Of God himself, th'old sacred Idiom rich,
Right perfect language, where's no point nor sign.
But hides some rare deep mystery behind.
