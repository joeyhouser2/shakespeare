She pittious nurse applied her painful thought
To serve and nourish them that her upbrought;
Like to the grateful Stork, that gathereth meat,
And brings it to her elders for to eat.
And on a Fir-tree high, with Boreas blown
Gives life to those of whom she had her own.
As the bright sun what time his fiery term
Toward the Western brim begins to draw,
Gins to abate the brightness of his beam,
And feruor of his flames somewhat adaw,
So did this mighty Lady when she saw
Those two strange Knights such homage to her make,
Bate somewhat of her majesty and awe
That whilom wont to do so many quake,
And with more mild aspect those two to entertake.
As when the Southern wind with lukewarm blast
Breathing on hills where winter long had dwelt
Dissolves the rocks of Ice that hung so fast,
And all the new made mounts of snow doth melt:
So with this gentle prayer, though spoke in hast,
The damsel such an inward motion felt
That suddenly her armed hart did soften,
As unto womenkind it chanceth often.
Like as the wind stopped by some wood or hill
Grows strong and fierce, tears bows and trees in twaine,
But with mild blasts more temperate gentle still
Against the rocks as sea-waves murmur shrill
But silent pass amid the open main:
Rinaldo so when none his force withstood,
Asswagde his fury, calmed his angry mood.
