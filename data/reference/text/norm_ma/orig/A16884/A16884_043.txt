Hells prince, sly parent of revolts, and lies.
O ruthless murderer of immortal souls,
A lass, to pull us from the happy poales,
And plunge us headlong in the yawning hell,
Thy ceaseless frauds and fetches who can tell?
Thou playest the Lion when thou doost ingage,
Bloodthirsty Neroes barbarous heart with rage,
While fleshed in murders, butcherlike he paints
The Saint-poore world, with the dear blood of Saints▪
Thou playest the dog, when by the mouth prophane,
Of some false Prophets thou doest belch thy bane.
Where from the Pulpit barkingly he rings,
Bold blasphemies against the King of Kings.
Thou playest the swine when plunged in pleasures vile,
Some Epicure doth sober minds defile,
Transforming lewdly by his lose impiety,
Sweet Lacedemon to a soft society.
Thou playest the Nightingale, or else the swan,
When any famous Rhetorician
With captious wit, and curious language draws,
Seduced hearers, and subverts the laws.
Thou playest the fox when thou doest faine aright.
The face and phrase of some deep Hypocrite.
True painted tomb, dead seeming cole, but quick,
A scorpion fell, whose hidden tail doth prick:
Yet this were little, if thy spight audacious,
Spared (at the least) the face of angels gracious,
And if thou didst not apely imitate
Th'almighties works, the wariest wits to mate.
The ghostly enemy doth not stay,
Till tempted persons do obay.
Yield to him, he a Lion is,
Gain stood a fly, his pray doth miss.
A subtle Pandar with more inticing rights,
Then sea hath fish, or heaven hath twinkling lights.
As a false Lover that thick snares hath laid,
To entrap th'honour of a fair yoong maid,
When she (though listening) little ear affords,
To his sweet courting deep affected words,
Fears some assuaging of his freezing flame,
And sooths himself with hope to gain his game,
And wrapt with joy upon this point persists,
That parleying city never long resists.
Even so the serpent that doth counterfet
A guileful call to allure us to his net:
Perceiving Eve his flattering gloze disgest,
He prosecutes, and jocund doth not rest,
Till he have tried foot, hand, head and all,
Upon the breach of this new battered wall.
