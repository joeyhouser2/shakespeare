Three kinds there are for natures skill:
The first they natural do name,
In which by herbs and stones they will
Work wondrous things, and worthy fame.
The next is Mathematical,
Where Magic works by nature so,
That brazen heads make speak it shall,
Of woods, birds, bodies, fly and go,
The third Venefical, by right
Is named, for by it they make
The shape of bodies changed in sight
And their forms on them to take.
—Oh who can tell
The hidden power of herbs, and might of magic skill?
