A sturdy villain stirring strife and bold,
As though the highest God defy he would:
In his right hand an iron club he held,
But he himself was all of golden mould,
Yet had both life and sence, and well could weilde
That cursed weapon, when his cruel foes he quelled,
D•sdaine he called was, and did disdain
So to be called, and who so him did call.
