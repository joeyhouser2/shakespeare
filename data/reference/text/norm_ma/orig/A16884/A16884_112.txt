The cause once gone, th'ffects thereof surcease,
And mischiefs being prevented whil'st they are yong
Cannot branch forth themselves to do that hurt,
That time, their natures, and bad men would work.
A Mischief seen may easily be prevented,
But being hapt, not helped, yet still lamented.
Fair goodness is foul ill, if mischiefs wit,
Be not represt from lewd corrupting it.
Mischief is oft made good by speeding well.
