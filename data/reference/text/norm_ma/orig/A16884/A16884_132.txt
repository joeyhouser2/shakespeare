— Such is th'ffect of two much store,
It makes them loathe that which they loved before.
The stately Eagle on his pitch doth stand
And from the main the fearful foul doth suit,
Yet scorns to touch them lying on the land,
When he hath felt the sweet of his delight,
But leaves the same a pray to every Kite,
With much we surfeit, Plenty makes us poor,
The wretched Indian scorns the golden Oar.
