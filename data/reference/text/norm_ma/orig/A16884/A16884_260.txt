A trump more shrill then Tritons on the Sea,
The said Renown precursor of the train,
Did sound (for who rings louder then Renown:)
He mounted was upon a flying horse,
And clothed in Falcons feathers to the ground,
By his Escutcheon justly might you gesse,
He was the Herald of Eternity,
And Pursevant at Arms to mighte Jove.
