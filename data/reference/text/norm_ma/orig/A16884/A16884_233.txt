As then no wind at all there blew,
No swelling cloud accloyed the air,
The sky like grass of watched hue
Reflected Phaebus golden hair:
The garnished trees no pendant stirred,
Nor voice was heard of any bird.
The King of windes calls home his posts again,
And Amphitrite smooth's her watery plain,
The air his clouds hath changed to christall clear,
And now the lamps of light from heaven appear.
