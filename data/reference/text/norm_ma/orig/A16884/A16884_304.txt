Like when some mastiff whelp disposed to play,
A whole confused heard of beests doth chase,
Which with one vile consent run all away,
If any hardy then the rest in place.
But turn the head that idle fear to stay,
Back strait the daunted chacer turns his face:
And all the rest with bold example led,
As fast run on him as before they fled.
So with this bold opposer rushes on
This many headed monster multitude.
As when the daughter of Thaumantes fair
Hath in a watery cloud displayed wide
Her goodly bow which paints the liquid air,
That all men wonder at her colours pride:
All suddenly ere one can look aside,
The glorious picture vanisheth away,
Ne any token doth thereof abide.
So did this Ladies goodly form decay,
And into nothing go, ere one could it bewray.
