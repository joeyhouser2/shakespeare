A silly man in simple weed forworn,
And soiled with dust of the long dried way,
His sandals were with toilsome travel torn,
And face all tand with scorching sunny ray,
As he had travailed many a summers day,
Through boiling sands of Affrica and Inde,
And in his hand a Jacobs staff to stay
His weary limbs upon, and eke behind,
His scrip did hang, in which his needmets he did bind
