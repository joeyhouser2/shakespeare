At last the golden Oriental gate
Of greatest heaven gan to open fair,
And Phoebus fresh as bridegroom to her mate,
Came dancing forth, shaking his dewy hair,
And hurls his glistering beams through gloomy air.
The fiery sun was mounted up on hight,
Up to the heavenly towers, and shot each where
Out of his golden chariot glistering light:
And fair Aurora with her rosy hair,
The hateful darkness now had put to flight.
The golden sun rose from the silver wave,
And with his beams enameled every green.
The snoring snout of restless Phlegon blew,
Hot on the Indes, which did the day renew
With scarlet sky.
