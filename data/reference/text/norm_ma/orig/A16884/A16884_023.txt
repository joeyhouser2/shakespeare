Sacred Counsel, true heart suppling balm,
Soul-curing plaister, true preserving bliss,
Water of life in every sudden qualm,
The heavens rich storehouse, where all treasure is.
True guide, by whom foul errors due we miss,
Night burning-beacon watch, against mishaps,
Foresight, avoiding many after claps,
— Every straw proves fuel to the fire,
When Counsel doth concur with our desire.
What eld hath tried and seen good counsel is.
— Counsel still is one.
When fathers, friends, and worldly goods are gone.
Counsel that comes when ill hath done his worst,
Blesseth our ill, but makes our good accurst.
Vain sounds of pleasure we delight to hear▪
But Counsel jars as discord in our ear.
A King that aims his neighbours crown to win,
Before the fruit of open wars begin,
Corrupts his Counsel, with rich recompenses,
For in good Counsel stands the strength of Princes.
A Kingdoms greatness hardly can he sway,
That wholesome Counsel did not first obey.
Even as by culling fruitful Vines encrease,
So faithful counsels work a Princes peace.
