Prayers heart and sides, and feet, are full of wings
(Like to th'Arcadian which Joves arrand brings)
Her body burning, from her lips doth come
The smoke of Incense, and of sweet Amome.
Heavens are propitious unto fearful prayers.
Fasting (though faint) her face with joy she cheers,
In weakness strong, and young in aged years.
Quick health preserver, curbing Cupids fits,
Watchful, purge humours, and refining wits.
