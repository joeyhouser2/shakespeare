Grief all in sables sorrowfully clad,
Down hanging his dull head with heavy cheare,
Yet inly beine more, then seeming sad,
A pair of pincers in his hand he had.
With which, he pinched people to the heart,
That from thenceforth, a wretched life they lad:
In wilful languor and consuming smart,
Dying each day with impaired wounds of dolors dart.
Grief only makes his wretched state to see,
(Even like a top, which nought but whipping moves)
This man, this talking beast, this walking tree,
Grief is the stone, which finest judgements proves,
For who grieves not, hath but a blockish brain,
Since cause of Grief we cause, from life removes.
— Griefs deadly sore,
Unkindness breeds, unkindness fostereth hate.
Grief to it self most dreadful doth appear,
And never yet was sorrow void of fear:
But yet in death, they both do hope the best.
Griefs be long lived, and sorrows seldom die.
Grief hath two tongues, and never woman yet
Could rule them both, without tenne womens wit.
He oft finds medicine, who his griefs imparts,
But double Grief afflicts concealing hearts,
As raging flames, who striveth to suppress.
Found never help, who never could his grief impart.
No greater ease of heart the griefs to tell,
It daunteth all the dolours of the mind:
Our careful hearts thereby great comfort find.
An Oven that is stopped, or River stayed,
Burneth more hotly, swelleth with more rage:
So of concealed Grief it may be said.
Free vent of words, loves fier doth asswage.
But when the hearts attorney once is mute,
The Client breaks, as desperate in his sute.
No one thing doth avail man more,
To cure a grief, and perfectly to heal it,
Then if he do unto some friends reveal it.
— Grief it is inough to vexed wight,
To feel his fault and not be farther vexed.
— Some grief shows much of love,
But much to grief shows still some want of wit.
— Great grief can not be told,
And can more easily be thought then found.
