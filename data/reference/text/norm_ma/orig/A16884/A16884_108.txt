The meanest fault is hie offence, urged of a mighty foe.
To shadow sin Might can the more pretend.
— Might is ever absolute alone,
When of two powers there's true conjunction.
Power constrained is but a glorious slave.
— Slight, force, are mighty things,
From which, much, if not most, earths glory springs:
If virtues self were clad in human shape,
Vertue without these, might go beg and scrape.
United powers, makes each the stronger prove.
— Honey words make foolishness,
And power the greatest wit with error blinds.
All as the highest trees do shield the shrubs,
From posting Phlegons warmth, and warming fier,
So mighty men obscure each others fame,
And make the best deserver fortunes game.
— Excellency never bears this mind,
By no inferior skill to be definde.
Where power decreed hath to find th'offence,
The cause is better still, then the defence.
