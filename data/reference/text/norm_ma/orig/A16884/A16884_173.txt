With victory revenge doth ever cease,
He liveth long that lives victorious.
The victor can no honour justly claim,
To lose the men who should advance the same.
—That fisher is not fine,
Who for a frog will lose a golden line:
The holy headband seems not to attire
The head of him, who in his furious ire,
Prefers the pain of those that have him teend,
Before the health and safety of one friend.
Vain is the vaunt and victory unjust,
That more to mighty hands then rightful cause doth trust.
Loss is no shame, nor to be less then foe,
But to be lesser then himself, doth mar
Both loosers lot, and victors praise also,
Vain others overthrows, who self doth overthrow.
