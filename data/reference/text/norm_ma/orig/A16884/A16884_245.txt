Thetis the Mother of the pleasant springs,
Grandome of all the Rivers in the world,
To whom earths veins a moistening tribute brings,
Now with a mad disturbed passion hurled
About her Cave (the worlds great treasure) flings,
And with wreathed arms, and long wet hair uncu•led,
Within herself laments a loss unlost,
And mones her wrongs, before her joys be crost.
