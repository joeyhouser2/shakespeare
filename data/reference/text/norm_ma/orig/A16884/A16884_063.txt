Tis wisdom to give much, a Gift prevails,
When deep persuading Oratory fails.
A giving hand though foul, shall have fair praise.
— The greatest Gifts whereof we boast,
Are those which do attempt and tire us most.
— Only wisdom grave, and judgements clear,
Gifts given from heaven, that are not common here.
Goods Gifts are often given to men past good.
Good Gifts abused, to mans confusion turn.
Testators and Executors so give and so receive,
As doubtful whethers joy or grief, is more to take or leave
For as doe hogs their troghs to hounds, so these give and get place
Death not the Dier gives bequests, and therefore but grave grace.
To loiter well deserved Gifts, is not to give but sell,
When to requite ingratitude, were to do evil well.
