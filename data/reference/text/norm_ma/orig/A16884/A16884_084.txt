Prides coach was drawn of sixe unequal beasts,
On which her sixe sage counsellors did ride:
Taught to obey her bestial behests,
With like conditions to their kinds applied.
Of which the first that all the rest did guide,
Was sluggish Idleness, the nurse of sin,
Upon a slothful Ass he chose to ride,
Arrayed in habit black and amiss thin,
Like to an holy Monk, the service to begin.
—Idleness pure innocence subverts,
Defiles our body, and our soul perverts:
Yea soberest men it makes delicious,
To vertue dull, to vice ingenious.
—Ill humours by excessive ease are bred,
And sloth corrupts and chokes the vital sprights,
It kills the memory, and hurts the sights.
—Drowsy sloth that counterfeiteth lame
With Snail like motion measu•ing the ground:
Having her arms in willing fetters bound.
Foul, sluggish drone, barren (but sin to breed)
Diseased, beggar, starved with sinful need.
If thou fly Idleness, Cupid hath no might,
His bow lyeth broken, his torch hath no light.
