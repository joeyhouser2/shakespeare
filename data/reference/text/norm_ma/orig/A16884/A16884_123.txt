Opportunity thy guilt is great,
Tis thou that executest the traitors treason,
Thou settest the wolf where he the lamb may get,
Who ever plots the sin, thou points the season.
Tis thou that spurnest at right, at lawyers reason:
And in thy shady Cell where none may spy him,
Sits sin, to fear each soul that wanders by him.
Fair Opportunity can win the coyest she that is,
Then he that rules her gamesome vain, and tempers toys with art,
Brings love that swimmeth in her eyes, to dive into her hart.
When love hath knit two parts in perfect unity,
They seldom fail to find th'opportunitie.
