Beauties great enemy, and to all the rest
That in the garden of Adonis springs,
Is wicked Time, who with his sith addrest,
Does mow the flowing herbs and goodly things,
And all their glory to the earth down flings,
Where they do wither, and are foully marred,
He flies about, and with his flaggy wings,
Beats down both leaves and buds without regard.
Ne ever pity may relent his malice hard.
Mishapen Time, coapsmate of ugly might,
Swift subtle post, carrier of grisly care,
Eater of youth, false slave to false delight,
Base watch of woes, sinnes packhorse, virtues snare,
Thou nursest all, and murderest all that are.
Stealing Time the subject to delay.
—Times golden thigh
Upholds the flowery body of the earth,
In sacred harmony and every birth
Of men, audacious makes legitimate,
Being used aright, the use of times is fate.
No mortal form that under moon remains,
Exempt from traitorous Time, continueth one.
Now mounts the flood, and straight his waves restrains
Now flows the tyde, and strait the source is gone,
Who toils by Sea, must choose the fairest gale,
For time abodes our good or bad avail▪
All those that live and think themselves but slime,
Must choose and thrive by favour of the time.
Swift speedy Time, feathered with flying hours,
Dissolves the beauty of the fairest brow.
Time doth consume fame, honour, wit, and strength,
Time roots out youth and beauties look at length.
Time wanting bonds, still wanteth certainty.
To Fames rich treasure Time unlocks the door,
Which angry sorrow had shut up before.
Time is a bondslave to eternity.
All that doth live is subject to his law,
All things decay in time, and to their end do draw.
What wrong hath not continuance outworn,
Years makes that right that never was so borne.
Good time is blest, bad time we hold accurst,
Time hurts them oft that he did help at first.
Times glory is to calm contending kings,
To unmask falsehood, and bring truth to light,
To stamp the seal of time in aged things,
To wake the morn, and sentinel the night,
To wrong the wronger till he render right:
To ruinate proud buildings with his hours,
And smeare with dust their glittering golden towers,
To fill with worm holes stately monuments,
To feed oblivion with decay of things,
To blot old Books, and alter their contents,
To pull the quills from ancient Ravens wings,
To dry the old oaks sap, and cherish springs
To spoil antiquities of hammered steel,
And turn the giddy round of fortunes wheel,
To shew the Beldame daughters of her daughters,
To make the child a man, the man a child,
To slay the tiger that doth lively slaughter,
To tame the unicorn and the lion wild,
To make the subtle in themselves be guild,
To cheer the plowman with increasefull crops,
And waste huge stones with little waterdrops.
