—The Inginers have the Trepan drest,
And reared up the Ram for battery best.
Here bends the Briccoll while the Cable cracks,
There Crossbows were uprent with yron Racks.
Here crooked Coruies fleeing Bridges tall,
Their scathfull Scorpions that ruins the wall,
On every side they raise with jointure meet,
The timber Towers for to commaund the street.
The painful Pioneers wrought against their will,
With fleaks and Faggots, ditches up to fill.
* —The happy Arabs those that builds
In thatched Wagons wandering through the fields.
The subtle Tirians they who first were Clerks,
That staid the wandering words in leaves and barks.
* At Babell first confused tongues of every language grew.

*Lord Dane the same was called then, to then a pleasing name,
Now odiously Lordan say we, when idle mates we blame.
* The Turtle that is true and chaste in love,
Shows by her mate something the spirit doth move.
The Arabian bird that never is but one,
Is only chaste because she is alone.
But had our mother Nature made them two,
They would have done as Doves and Sparrows do.
But therefore made a Martyr in desire,
And doth her penance lastly in the fire.
*I cast not with fools, suffer Saints, let mighty fools be mad,
Note, Seneca by news done for precepts, penance had.
