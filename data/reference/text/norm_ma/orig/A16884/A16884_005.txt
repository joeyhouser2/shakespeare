Affection is a coal that must be coolde,
Else suffered, it will set the heart on fire,
The fire hath bounds, but deep desire hath none.
Affection by the countenance is descri'de,
The light of hidden fier it self discovers,
And love that is concealed, betrays poor Lovers.
—Most wretched man,
That to affections doth the bridle lend,
In their beginning they are weak and wan,
But soon through sufferance grow to fearful end,
Whil'st they are weak, betime with them contend.
For when they once to perfect strength do grow,
Strong wars they make, and cruel battery bend,
Gainst fort of reason, it to overthrow.
