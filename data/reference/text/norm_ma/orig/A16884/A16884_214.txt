— Fair Albion glory of the North,
Neptunes best darling held betweene his arms,
Divided from the world, as better worth,
Kept from himself, defended from all harms.
This royal throne of Kings, this sceptred yle.
This earth of majesty, this seat of Mars,
This other Eden, this demi-paradise,
This fortress built by nature for her self,
Against intestion and the hand of war,
This happy breed of man, this little world,
This precious stone set in the silver sea,
Which serves it in the office of a wall,
Or as a Moat defensive to a house,
Against the envy of less happier lands,
This nurse, this teeming womb of royal Kings,
Feared by their breed, and famous by their birth,
Renowned in their deeds as far from home,
For charity, service, and true chivalry,
As is the Sepulchre in stubborn Jewrie.
