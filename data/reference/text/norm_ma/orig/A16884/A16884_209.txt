— Lo a knight unto his socour went
All armed in shining steel, and on his shield,
He bare a yoke in sundry pieces rent.
And flames of fire all in a yellow field:
So weaponed he was, as if he ment
To make all that incountred him to yield:
A sword and spear he had, and to the same
A mace, from whence he threw continual flame,
His mace was stored with everlasting fire,
That ever burned and did never waste,
No other wagon needed one desire
To make good way which way soere he past,
And sure Rinaldoes danger did require,
Quick remedy, wherefore the knight did haste,
And when he saw this monster and did vew her,
With his stiff spear forth with he overthrew her:
But this same fall did her no whit annoy,
Wherefore to use his spear he now misliketh,
Only he will his fiery face imploy,
And with thar same the monster foul he striketh,
Then she no longer could her force inioy.
