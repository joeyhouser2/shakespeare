Derive thy laws from wisest heads, to be upholden still,
Not adding or abstracting, as conceited tire brains will.
Encourage good men by thy love, reform the bad by law,
Reserve an ear for either plea, and borrow leave of awe.
In vain be counsels statutes, human laws,
When chief of Counsels pleads the justest cause.
So constantly the Judges conster laws,
That all agree still with the stronger cause.
Pansamias and Lisander, by their swords
And warlike virtues made Lacaena rich,
Fame followed them where they the tents did pitch,
But grave Licurgus by his laws and words,
Did merit more then these renowned Lords.
Licurgus for good laws, lost his own liberty,
And thought it better to prefer common commodity.
That Lawyer thogh he more by art then right doth overthrow
Consents to sin, deceives the Judge, wrong right is justice foe.
