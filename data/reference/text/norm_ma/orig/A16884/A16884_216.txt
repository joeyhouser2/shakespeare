Hierusalem is feared on two hills,
Of height unlike, and turned side to side,
The space betweene a gentle vallie fills,
From mount to mount exspansed fair and wide,
Three sides are sure imbarde with crags and hills,
The rest is easy •cant to rise espied,
But mighty bulwarks fence that plainer part,
So art helps nature, nature strengtheneth art.
The town is stored of troughs and cestornes made,
To keep fresh water, but the country seems
Devoid of grass, unfit for plowmens trade,
Not fertile, moist, with rivers, wells, and streams,
There grow few trees, to make the summers shade,
To shield the parched land from scorehing beams,
Save that a wood stands sixe miles from the town,
With aged Cedars, dark and shadows brown:
By east among the dusky valleys glide,
The silver streams of Jordanes siler flood,
By west the midland sea with bounders tyde,
O• sandy showers, where loppa whilom stood,
By North Samaria stands, and on that side,
The golden Calf was reard in Bethell wood,
Bethlem by South, where Christ incarnate was,
A pearl in steel, a diamond set in brass.
