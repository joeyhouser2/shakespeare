Sacred Apollo, God of Archery,
Of Arts, of pleasure, and of Poetry,
Joves fair haird son, whose yellow tresses shine,
Like curled flames; hurling a most divine
And dazzling splendour, in those lesser fires
Which from thy guilt beams (when thy Car retires,)
Kindle those Tapers that lend eyes to night,
O thou that art the Landlord of all light,
Birdegroome of morning, days eternal King,
To whom nine Muses (in a sacred ring)
In dances spherical trip hand in hand,
Whilst thy seaven-stringed Lute their feet commaund,
whose motion such proportioned measure bears,
That to the music dance nine heavenly spheres.
Great Delian Priest, we to adore thy name,
Have burnt fat thighs of Bulls in hallowed flame,
whose savour wrapt in smoke and clouds of fire
To thy starspangled Palace did aspire.
