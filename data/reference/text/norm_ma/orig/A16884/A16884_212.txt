Her eyes were sunk into her head,
Her cheeks were lean and lank,
Out stood her chin,
Into her mouth her bloodless lips they sank,
Her toothless chaps
Disgraste her tongue in telling of a tale,
And suck she might
A teat for teeth and spoonage too did fail,
Her hair since sixtie years
Not black, was now, nor white, or none,
The substance of her wrinkled face
Were only skin and bone,
Dim were her eyes,
Deaf were her ears, rank smelt, if she could sent,
A palsy made her feeling cease,
Down tastlesse food it went.
