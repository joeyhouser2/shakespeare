Seven of them came together in a knot,
With womens faces, wanne, with deadly cold,
So hunger-starved, as death it self might not
Be at first sight more hideous to behold:
Their wings were great, but foul black wings god wot,
Their talents sharp to gripe, and strong to hold,
A large foul panch, a filthy tail and long,
From whence there came a mighty odour strong.
