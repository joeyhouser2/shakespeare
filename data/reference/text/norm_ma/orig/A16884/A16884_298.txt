As when to purge excessive moist descending,
From Saturns sphere, or else superfluous heat,
Jove stirred up by Mars (common good entending)
Sends lightning flash to lay their angry threat.
So wiser heads that knew the scourge of war,
Sought sooth fast means to mitigate the jar.
As when a skilful mariner doth read
A storm approaching that doth peril threat,
He will not bide the danger of such dread,
Burr strikes his sails and vereth his manisheat,
And lends unto it leave the empty air to beat:
So did the fairy knight himself abear.
As Pilot well expert in perilous wave,
That to a steadfast star his course hath bent,
When foggy mists or cloudy tempests have,
The faithful light of that fair lampy blent,
And covered heaven with hideous dreariment,
Upon his card and compass forms his eye,
The masters of his long experiment.
And to them does the steddie helm apply,
Bidding his winged vessel fairly forward fly.
So Guion.
