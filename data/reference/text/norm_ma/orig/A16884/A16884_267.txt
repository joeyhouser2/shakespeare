Daughter of Time, sincere Posterity,
Always new borne, yet no man knows thy birth,
The arbitress of pure Sincerity,
Yet, changeable, (like Proteus) on the earth,
Sometime in plenty, sometime joined with dearth.
Always to come, yet always present here,
Whom all run after, none come after neere.
Unpartial Judge of all save present state,
Truth's Idiom of the things are past,
But still pursuing present things with hate,
And more injurious at the first then last,
Preserving others, while thine own doe wast:
True treasurer of all antiquity,
Whom all desire, yet never o•• could see.
