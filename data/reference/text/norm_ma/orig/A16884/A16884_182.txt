Woe all in black within her hands did bear,
The fatal torches of a funeral,
Her cheeks were wet, dispersed was her hair,
Her voice was shrill (yet lothsome therewithal)
Short time seems long in sorrows sharp sustaining,
Though woe be heavy, yet it seldom sleeps,
And they that watch see time how slow it creeps.
—Fellowship in woe, doth woe asswage,
As palmers that make short their pilgrimage.
Tis double death to drown in ken of shore,
He ten times pines, that pines beholding food:
To see the salve doth make the wound ake more,
Great griefs greeve most at that would do it good,
Dear woes rowle forward like a gentle flood:
Who being stopped, the bounden banks ore flows,
Grief dallied with, nor law nor limmit knows.
Distress likes dumps, when time is kept with tears.
For stronger woe we hardly long may wrest,
The depth of grief with words is sounded least.
—The Painter
Who thought his colours pale could not declare
The special woe King Agamemnon bare,
When sacrificed was his only rage,
With bend of black he bound the fathers face.
