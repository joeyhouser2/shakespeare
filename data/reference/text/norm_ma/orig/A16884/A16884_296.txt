As great a noise as when in Cymbrian plain,
An heard of bulls, whom kindly rage doth sting,
Do for the milky mothers want complain,
And fill the fields with troublous bellowing,
The neighbor woods around with hollow murmuring.
As when the shapeless huge Leviaethan
Hath thrust himself upon the sandy shore,
Where (monsterlike) affrighting every man,
He belloweth out a fearful deadly rore.
Even such a Clamour through the air doth thunder,
The doleful presage of some fearful wonder.
