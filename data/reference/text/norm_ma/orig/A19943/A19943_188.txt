The Poets fain that when the world began,
Both sexes in one body did remain;
Till Jove (offended with this double man)
Caused Vulcan to divide him into twaine.
In this devision, he the hart did sever,
But cunningly he did indent the hart,
That if there were a reuniting ever,
Each part might know which was his counterpa••
See then (deer love) th'Indenture of my hart,
And read the Covenants writ with holy fire:
See (if your hart be not the counterpart,
Of my true hearts indented chaste desire.)
And, if it be, so may it ever be,
Twoo hearts in one, twixt you my Love and me.
