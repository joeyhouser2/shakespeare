IF my harsh humble style, and Rhymes ill dressed,
Arrive not to your worth and beauty glorious
My Muses shoulders are with weight oppressed,
And heavenly beams are over my sight victoriou•
If these dim colours have your worth expressed
Laid by Loves hand, and not by Arte laborious
Your Sunlike rays have my wits harvest blesse•
Ennabling me to make your praise notorious.
But if alas! (alas the heavens defend it,)
My lines your eyes, my love your hart displeasing
Breed hate in you, and kill my hope of easing;
