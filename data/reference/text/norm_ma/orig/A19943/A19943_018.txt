THE fairest Eyes, (O Eyes in blackness fair!)
That ever shinde, and the most heavenly face,
The daintiest smiling, the most conquering grace
And sweetest breath that ever perfumed the air
The cherriest lips, whose kiss might well repair
A dead mans state; that speech which did displac•
All mean desires, and all affections base,
Clogging swift H••••, and winging dead Despair
That snow-white breast, and all those faultless feature
Which made her seem a personage divine,
And far excelling fairest humane creatures,
Hath Absence banished from my cursed Eine.
But in my Heart, as in a Mirror clear,
All these perfections to my thoughts appear.
