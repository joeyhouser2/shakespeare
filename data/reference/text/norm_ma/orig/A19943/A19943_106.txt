Wrongde by Desire I yielded to disdain,
Who called revenge to work my spite thereby.
Rash was Revenge and sware desire should die.
No price nor prayer his pardon might obtain.
Down to my Hart in rage he hastes amain,
And stops each passage lest Desire should fly:
Within my Ears disdainful words did lie,
Proud looks did keep mine Eyes with scornful train.
Desire that earst but flickered in my brest,
And wantonlike now pricked, now gave me rest,
For fear of death sunk deeper in my hart.
There reigns he now, and there will reign alone,
Desire is jealous, and gives part to none,
Nor he from me, nor I from him can start.
