So I be graeious in your heavenly Face,
I wey not Fates, nor Stars, nor Fortunes yre.
T'is not the influence of Heavens Fire,
Hath power to make me blessed in my Race,
Nor in my happiness hath Fortune place,
Nor yet can Fate my poor lifes date expire.
T'is your fair Eyes (my Stars) all bliss do give,
Tis your disdain (my Fate) hath power to kill,
T'is you (my Fortune) make me happy live,
Though Fortune, Fate, and Stars conspire mine ill.
Then (blessed Saint) into your favour take me,
