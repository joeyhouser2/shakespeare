Fairest and kindest of all womankind:
Since you did me the undeserved grace,
•n your fair Eye to shew me my bad face,
With loan Ile pay you in the self same kind;
Look in mine Eye, and I will shew to you,
The fairest face that Heavens Eye doth view.

But the small worthless Glass of my dim Eye,
Scarce shows the Picture of your heavenly face,
Which yet each slightest turn doth strait deface.
But could, O could you once my Heart espy,
Your form at large you there engraved should see,
Which, nor by Time, nor Death can razed be.
