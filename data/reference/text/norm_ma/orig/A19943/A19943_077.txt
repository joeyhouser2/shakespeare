Sweet, to my cursed life some favour show,
Or let me not (accurst) in life remain,
Let not my Senses sence of life retain,
Since sence doth only yield me sence of woe.
For now mine Eyes only your frowns do know;
Mine Eeares hear nothing else but your disdain,
My lips taste nought but tears: and smel is pain,
Banished your lips, where Indian Odours grow.
And my devoted Hart your Beauties slave,
Feels nought but scorn, oppression, and distress,
Made even of wretchedness the wretched Cave,
Nay, too too wretched for vild wretchedness.
For even sad sighs, as loathing there to rest,
Struggle for passage from my Greefe-swolne brest.
