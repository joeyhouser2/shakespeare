Some men, they say, are Poets borne by kind,
And suck that science from their mothers brest:
An easy Arte that comes with so great rest,
And happy men to so good hap assigned.
In some, desire of praise enflames the mind,
To clime with pain Parnassus double crest:
Some, hope of rich Rewards hath so possest,
That Gold, in Castall Sands they seek to find.
Me, neither Nature hath a Poet made,
Nor love of Glory mou'de to learn the trade,
Nor thirst of Gold persuaded for to write;
For Natures graces are too fine for me,
Praise like the Peacocks pride her self to see,
Desire of Gain the basest minds delight.
