SORROW seldom killeth any,
Sudden Joy hath murdered many.
Then (Sweet) if you would end me,
Tis a fond course with lingering grief to spend met.
For, quickly to dispatch me,
•our only way is, in your arms to catch me,
And give me a sweet Kiss:
•or such excessive and unlooked for bliss,
Would so much over-joy me,
As it would strait destroy me.
