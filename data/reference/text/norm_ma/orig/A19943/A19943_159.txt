O Why did Fame my Hart to Love betray,
By telling my Dears vertue and perfection?
Why did my Traitor Ears to it convey,
That Syren-song cause of my Hearts infection,
Had I bene deaf, or Fame her gifts concealed,
Then had my Hart been free from hopeless Love:
Or were my state likewise by it revealed,
Well might it Philomel to pity move.
Then shold she kno how love doth make me languish,
Distracting me twixt hope and dreadful fear:
Then shold she kno my care, my plants, and anguish
All which for her deer sake I meekly bear.
Yea I could quietly deaths pains abide,
So that she knew that for her sake I dide.
