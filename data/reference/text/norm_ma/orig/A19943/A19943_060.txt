Shut not (sweet Breast) to see me all of fire.
Fly not (deer Hart) to find me all of snow.
Thy snow inflames these flames of my desire.
And I desire, Desires sweet flames to know.
Thy Snow nill hurt me.
Nor thy Fire will harm me.
This cold will cool me.
And this heat will warm me.
Take this chaste fire to that pure virgin snow▪
Being now thus warmed, Ile never seek other fire▪
Thou givest more bliss than mortal hearts may know
More bliss I take than Angels can desire.
Let one grief harm us;
And let one joy fill us:
Let one love warm us;
And let one death kill us.
