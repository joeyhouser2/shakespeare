When as the Sun eclipsed is, some say,
It thunder, lightning, rain, and wind portende•
And not unlike but such things happen may,
Sith like effects my Sun eclipsed sendeth.
Witness my throat made hoars with thundering cry
And hart with loves hot flashing lightnings fire
Witness the showers which still fall from mine eye
And brest with sighs like stormy winds near rive•
Shine then once again, sweet Sun on me,
And with thy beams dissolve clouds of dispair
Whereof these raging Meteors framed be,
In my poor hart by absence of my fair,
So shalt thou prove thy Beams, thy heat, thy ligh•
To match the Sun in glory, grace, and might.
