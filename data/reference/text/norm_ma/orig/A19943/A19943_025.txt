LOVE, if a God thou art,
Then evermore thou must,
Be merciful and just.
I• thou be just; O wherefore doth thy Dart,
Wound mine alone, and not my Ladies Hart?

If merciful, then why
Am I to pain reserved,
Who have thee truely served:
While she that by thy pour sets not a sly.
Laughs thee to scorn, and lives in libettie?

Then, if a God thou would'st accounted be,
Heal me like her, or else wound her like me.
