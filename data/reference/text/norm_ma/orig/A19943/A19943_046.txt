Dare not in my masters bosom rest,
That flaming Etna would to Ashes burn me:
•or dare I harbour in his Mistress brest,
•he frosty Climate into yce would turn me:
So, both from her and him I do retire me,
Lest th'one should freeze me, and the other fire me.

Winged with true Love, I fly to this sweet Brest,
Whose Snow, I hope, will cool but t'yce not turn me:
Where fire and snow, I trust, so tempered rest,
•s gentle heat will warm, and yet not burn me:
But (O deer Brest) from thee Ile never retire me,
Whether thou cool, or warm, or freeze, or fire me.
