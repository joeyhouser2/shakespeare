Break heavy hart, and rid me of this pain,
This pain that still encreaseth day by day:
By day with sighs I spend my self in vain,
In vain by night with tears I waste away:
Away I waste with tears by night in vain,
Tears, sighs, by night, by day encrease this pain.

Mine Eyes no Eyes, but fountains of my tears,
My tears no tears, but floods to moist my hart:
My hart no hart, but harbour of my fears,
My fears no fears, but feelings of my smart:
My smart, my fears, my hart, my tears, mine eyes
Are blind, dryde, spent, past, wasted with my cries.

And yet mine Eyes, thogh blind, see cause of grief:
And yet my tears, thogh dride, run down amain:
And yet my hart, though spent, attends relief,
And yet my fears, though past, encrease my pain:
And yet I live, and living, feel more smart,
And smarting, cry in vain, break heavy hart.
