•Yme, poor Soul, whom bound in sinful chains
This wretched body keeps against my will!
Aye me poor Body, whom for all my pains,
This froward soul causeless condemneth stil.
Causeless? whenas thou strivest to sin each day?
Causeless: whenas I strive thee to obay.
Thou art the means, by which I fall to sin,
Thou art the cause that settest this means awork
No part of thee that hath not faulty bin:
I shew the poison that in thee doth lurk.
I shall be pure when so I part from thee:
So were I now, but that thou stainest me.
