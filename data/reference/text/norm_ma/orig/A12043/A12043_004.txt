Unthrifty loveliness why dost thou spend,
Upon thy self thy beauties legacy?
Natures bequest gives nothing but doth lend,
And being franck she lends to those are free:
Then beauteous niggard why doost thou abuse,
The bounteous largess given thee to give?
Profitles userer why doost thou use
So great a sum of sums yet can'st not live?
For having traffic with thy self alone,
Thou of thy self thy sweet self dost deceave,
Then how when nature calls thee to be gone,
What acceptable Audit can'st thou leave?
Thy unused beauty must be tombed with thee,
Which used lives th'executor to be.
