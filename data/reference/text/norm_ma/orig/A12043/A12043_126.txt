O Thou my lovely Boy who in thy power,
Doest hould times fickle glass▪ his sickle, hower:
Who hast by waining grown, and therein shou'st,
Thy lovers withering, as thy sweet self growest.
If Nature (sovereign misteres over wrack)
As thou goest onwards still will pluck thee back,
She keeps thee to this purpose, that her skill.
May time disgrace, and wretched mynuit kill.
Yet fear her O thou minion of her pleasure,
She may detain, but not still keep her tresurel
Her Audite (though delayed) answered must be,
And her Quietus is to render thee.
