Sweet love renew thy force, be it not said
Thy edge should blunter be then appetite,
Which but too day by feeding is alaied,
To morrow sharpened in his former might.
So love be thou, although too day thou fill
Thy hungry eyes, even till they winck with fullness,
Too morrow see again, and do not kill
The spirit of Love, with a perpetual dulness:
Let this sad Intrim like the Ocean be
Which parts the shore, where two contracted new,
Come daily to the banks, that when they see:
Return of love, more blest may be the view.
As call it Winter, which being full of care,
Makes Summers welcome, thrice more wished, more rare:
