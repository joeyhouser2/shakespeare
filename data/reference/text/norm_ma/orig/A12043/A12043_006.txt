Then let not winters wragged hand deface,
In thee thy summer ere thou be distilled:
Make sweet some vial; treasure thou some place,
With beautits treasure ere it be self killed:
That use is not forbidden usury,
Which happies those that pay the willing lone;
That's for thy self to breed an other thee,
Or ten times happier be it ten for one,
Ten times thy self were happier then thou art,
If ten of thine ten times refligured thee,
Then what could death do if thou should'st depart,
Leaving thee living in posterity?
Be not self-willed for thou art much too fair,
To be deaths conquest and make worms thine h•ire.
