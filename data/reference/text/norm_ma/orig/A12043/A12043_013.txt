O That you were your self, but love you are
No longer yours, then you your self here live,
Against this cumming end you should prepare,
And your sweet semblance to some other give.
So should that beauty which you hold in lease
Find no determination, then you were
You self again after your selves-decease,
When your swee••••ue your sweet form should bear.
Who lets so fair a house fall to decay,
Which husbandry in honour might uphold,
Against the stormy gusts of winters day
And barren rage of deaths eternal cold?
O none but unthrifts, deare my love you know,
You had a Father, let your Son say so.
