Mine eye and heart are at a mortal war,
How to devide the conquest of thy sight,
Mine eye, my heart their pictures sight would bar,
My heart, mine eye the freeedome of that right,
My heart doth plead that thou in him doost lie,
(A closet never pierced with christall eyes)
Bu• the defendant doth that plea deny,
And says in him their fair appearance lies.
To side this title is impaneled
A quest of thoughts, all tenants to the heart,
And by their verdict is determined
The clear eyes moiety, and the d•are hearts part.
As thus, mine eyes due is their outward part,
And my hearts right, their inward love of heart.
