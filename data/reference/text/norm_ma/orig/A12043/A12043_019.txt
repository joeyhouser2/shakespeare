Devouring time blunt thou the Lyons pawes,
And make the earth devour her own sweet brood,
Pluck the keen teeth from the fierce Tigers yaws,
And burn the long lived Phoenix in her blood,
Make glad and sorry seasons as thou fleetest,
And do what ere thou wilt swift-footed time
To the wide world and all her fading sweets:
But I forbid thee one most hainous crime,
O carve not with thy hours my loves fair brow,
Nor draw no lines there with thine antique pen,
Him in thy course untainted do allow,
For beauties pattern to succeeding men.
Yet do thy worst ould Time dispight thy wrong,
My love shall in my verse ever live young.
