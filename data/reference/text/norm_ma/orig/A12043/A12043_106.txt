When in the Chronicle of wasted time,
I see descriptions of the fairest wights,
And beauty making beautiful old rime,
In praise of Ladies dead, and lovely Knights,
Then in the blazon of sweet beauties best,
Of hand, of foot, of lip, of eye, of brow,
I see their antique Pen would h•ue exprest,
Even such a beauty as you maister now.
So all their praises are but prophe•jes
Of this our time, all you pre•iguring,
And for they looked but with devining eyes,
They had not still enough your worth to sing:
For we which now behold these present days,
Have eyes to wonder, but lack tongues to praise.
