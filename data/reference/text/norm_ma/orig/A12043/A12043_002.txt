When fortie Winters shall besiege thy brow,
And dig deep trenches in thy beauties field,
Thy youths proud livery so gazed on now,
Will be a tottered weed of small worth held:
Then being asked, where all thy beauty lies,
Where all the treasure of thy lusty days;
To say within thine own deep sunken eyes,
Were an all-eating shame, and thriftless praise.
How much more praise deserved thy beauties use,
If thou couldst answer this fair child of mine
Shall sum my count, and make my old excuse
Proving his beauty by succession thine.
This were to be new made when thou art ould,
And see thy blood warm when thou feelest it could,
