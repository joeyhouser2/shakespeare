NO! Time, thou shalt not bost that I do change,
Thy pyramyds built up with newer might
To me are nothing novel, nothing strange,
They are but dressings of a former sight:
Our dates are breefe, and therefore we admire,
What thou dost foist upon us that is ould,
And rather make them borne to our desire,
Then think that we before have heard them tould:
Thy registers and thee I both defy,
Not wondering at the present, nor the past,
For thy records, and what we see doth lie,
Made more or less by thy continual haft:
This I do vow and this shall ever be,
I will be true dispight thy syeth and thee.
