OH truant Muse what shall thy amends,
For thy neglect of truth in beauty died?
Both truth and beauty on my love depends▪
So dost thou too, and therein dignified:
Make answer Muse, wilt thou not haply sai•,
Truth needs no colour with his colour fixed,
Beauty no pensell, beauties truth to lay:
But best is best, if never intermixt.
Because he needs no praise, wilt thou be dumb?
Excuse not silence so, fored lies in thee,
To make him much outlive a gilded tomb:
And to be praised of ages yet to be.
Then do thy office Muse, I teach thee how,
To make him seem long hence, as he shows now.
