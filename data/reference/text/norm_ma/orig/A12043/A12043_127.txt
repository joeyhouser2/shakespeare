IN the ould age black was not counted fair,
Or if it wear it bore not beauties name:
But now is black beauties success•ue heir,
And Beauty slanderd with a bastard shame,
For since each hand hath put on Natures power,
Fairing the foul with Arts false borrowed face,
Sweet beauty hath no name no holy boure,
But is profaned, if not lives in disgrace.
Therefore my Mistersse eyes are Raven black,
Her eyes so suited, and they mourners seem,
At such who not borne fair no beauty lack,
Slandering Creation with a false esteem,
Yet so they mourn becoming of their woe,
That every toung says beauty should look so.
