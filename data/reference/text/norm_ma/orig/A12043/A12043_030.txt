I sommon up remembrance of things past,
I sigh the lack of many a thing I sought,
And with old woes new wail my deare times waste:
Then can I drown an eye (un-us•d to flow)
For precious friends hid in deaths dateless night,
And weep a fresh loves long since canceled woe,
And mone th'expence of many a vannisht sight.
Then can I greeve at grievances foregone,
And heavily from woe to woe tell ore
The sad account of fore-bemoaned mone,
Which I new pay as if not paid before.
But if the while I think on thee (deare friend)
All losses are restored, and sorrows end.
