Forcause I still preferde the truth before
Shameless untruth, and lothsome leesings lore,
I find my self yll recompensed therefore
Off thee my Tongue.
For good desert and guiding thee aright,
That thou for aye mightest live devoid of spight,
I reap but shame, and lack my chief delight
For silence kept.
When happy hap by hap advanced my case,
And brought me to my Lady face to face,
Where I her Corpse in safety might imbrace,
Thou heldst thy peace.
Thou madste my voice to cleave amids my throte,
And sute to cease unluckily (God wot)
Thou wouldst not speak, tho thou hadst quite forgot
My hearts behest.
My hart by thee suspected was of guile,
For cause thou ceased to use a loving style,
And words to forge and frame with finest file
As Lovers wont.
Thou madste my blood fro paled face to start,
And fly to seek some succor of the hart,
That wounded was long earst with dreadful dart
Off Cupids Bow.
And thou as cold as any Marble stone
When from my face the chilly blood was gone,
Couldst not devise the way to make my mone
By words appear.
And (ye my tears) that wonted were to flow
And stream adown as fast as thawed Snow,
Were stopped, as then ye had no pour to show
A Lovers sute.
My sighs that earst were wont to dim the Sky,
And cause a fume by force of flame to fly,
Were tho as slack, as Wells of weeping dry
Too show my Love.
The hart that lay incombred all within
Had fainted quite had not by looks ybin:
For they declared the case my hart was in
By tongues untroth.
