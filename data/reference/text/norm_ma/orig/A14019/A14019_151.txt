Long did I love, and liked her passing well
Whose beauty bred the thraldom of my thought,
Long did I sue to her for to expel
The foul disdain that beauties beams had wrought:
Long did I serve, and Long I would have done,
My mind was bent a thorow race to roon.
Long when I had looude, sude, and served so.
As mought have liked as brave a Dame as she,
Her Friend she forced not but let him go,
She looude at least besides him two or three:
Her common cheare to erie one that sude,
Bred me to deem she did her Friend delude.
Great was my grief at first to be refused
That Long had looude with true unfeigned hart,
But when I saw I had been long abused
I forced the less from such a Friend to part:
Yet ere I gave her up I gainde a thing
That grief to her, and ease to me did bring.
