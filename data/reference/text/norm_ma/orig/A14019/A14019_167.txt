The countenance of this Noble Cowntesse marck
When she thy Verse with eye that Saphire like
Doth shine surveys, let be thy only carck
To note her Looks: and if she ought mislike
Say that thou shouldst have hid it from her sight,
Thy Author made the best for her delight,
The worst he willde in covert scroll to lurk
Until the Bear were overlickt afresh,
For why in deep this hasty hatched work
Resembleth much the shapeless lump of flesh
That Bears bring forth: So when I lick thee over
Thou shalt (I trust) thy perfect shape recover.
