Discharge thy dole,
Thou subtile soul,
It stands in little steed
To curse the kiss
That causer is
Thy chirrie lip doth bleed.
Thy blood ascends
To make amends
For domage thou hast done:
For by the same
I felt a flame
More scorching than the Sun.
Thou reftst my harte
By secret Arte,
My sprites were quite subdued:
My Senses fled
And I was ded,
Thy lips were scarce imbrued.
The kiss was thine,
The hurt was mine,
My hart felt all the pain:
Twas it that bled
And looked so red,
I tell thee once again.
But if you long
To wreak your wrong
Upon your friendly foe:
Come kiss again
And put to pain
The man that hurt you so.
