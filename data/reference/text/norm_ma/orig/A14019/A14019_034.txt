BE far from me you woeful wonted cries,
Adue Despair, that madste my hart agries:
Ye sobbing sighs farewell and pensive plaint,
Resign your rooms to joy, the long restraint
Without desart endure.
Reject those ruthful Rhymes thou (quaking Quill)
Which both declared my woe and want of skill:
(Mine eyes) that long have had my Love in chase,
With tears no more imbrue your Mistress face
But to your Springs retire.
And thou (my Hart) that long for lark of Grace
Forepinde hast bene and in a doleful case,
Lament no more, let all such gripings go
As bred thy bale, and nursed thy cankered woe
With milk of mournful Dug.
To Venus do your due (you Senses all)
And to her Son to whom you are in thrall:
To Cupid bend thy knee and thanks repay
That after lingered sute, and long delay
Hath brought thy ship to shore.
Let crabbed Fortune now express her might,
And do thy worst to me thou stinging spite:
My hart is well defenst against your force,
For she hath vowed on me to have remorce
Whom I have looude so long.
Henceforth exchange thy cheer and woeful voice
That hast yfounde such matter to rejoice:
With mirrie quill and pen of pleasant plight
Thy blissful haps and fortune to endight
Enforce thy barren Skull.
