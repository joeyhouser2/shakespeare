Friend Googe, give me the faithful friend to trust,
And take the fickle Coin for me that lust.
For Friends in time of trouble and distress
With help and sound advise will soon redress
Each growing grief that gripes the pensive brest,
When Monie lies locked up in covert Chest.
Thy Coin will cause a thousand cares to grow,
Which if thou hadst no Coin thou couldst not know,
Thy Friend no care but comfort will procure,
Of him thou mayest at need thy self assure.
Thy Monie makes the Thief in wait to lie,
Whose fraud thy Friend and falsehood will descry.
Thou canst not keep unlocked thy careful Coin,
But some from thee thy Monie will purloin:
Thy faithful Friend will never start aside,
But take his share of all that shall beride.
When thou art dead thy Monie is bereft
But after life thy trusty Friend is left:
Thy Monie serves another Maister than,
Thy faithful Friend links with none other man.
So that (Friend Googe) I deem it better I,
To choose the Friend and let the Monie lie.
