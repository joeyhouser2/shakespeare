MARCKE felt himself diseased,
the Soothsayer said: There be
Sixe yet remainder days of life,
no mo (Friend Mark) to thee
Then skilful Alcon came,
he felt the pulses beat:
And out of hand this Marcus dide,
there Phisick wrought his feat.
This shows Physician doth
the Soothsayer far exceed:
For thone can make a short dispatch,
when thother makes no speed.
