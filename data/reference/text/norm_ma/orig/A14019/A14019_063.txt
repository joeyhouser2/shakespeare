MY Friend, where as thou seest thy self
to be a man in deed,
Eat, quaff, and play, with present joys
thy greedy fancy feed.
For I (thou seest) am dust become
that earst so wealthy was:
I have that I alive did eat,
the reast away did pass.
What so I poorde in pampered paunch
and to my guts conveyed,
To gaping ground with me I bore,
the reast behind is stayed.
My haughty buildings huge to see,
my Turrets and my train,
My Horse, my Hounds, my cofred Coin
for others do remain.
Wherefore a Mirror make of me
and drown thee in delight:
For Death will sweep away thy wealth
and reave thy pleasures quite.
