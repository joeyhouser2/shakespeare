J Dido and the Queen of Carthage ground,
Whose limbs thou seest so lively set to sight:
Such one I was, but never to be found
So far in love as Vergill seems to wright,
I lived not so in lust and fowl delight.
For neither he that wandering Duke of Troie
Knew me, nor yet at Lybie land ariude:
But to escape Jarbos that did noie
Me sore, of life my Carcase I depriude,
To keep my Hest that he would tho have riude.
No storm of love, or dolour made me die,
I slue my self to save my Sheet of shame
Wherein good Sycheus wrapped me perdy:
Then Vergill then the greater be thy blame,
That so by love dost breed my fowl defame.
