O Proclus, tis in vain
that thou about dost stand,
For well I see thou mindste to wipe
thy Nares with thy hand.
Truth is that though thou be
fowl fisted out of frame:
Yet doth this tossing Nose of thine
in bigness pass the same.
When neezing thou on Jove
for succour seemste to cry
Thou canst not hear, thy Nose debars
the noise to Ear to fly.
It beateth back the sound,
it stands in middle place
Twixt Ear and Mouth, but sure it casts
a shade to all the face.
