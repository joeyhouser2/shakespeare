Come Caron come with speed:
What haste? who calleth me?
A woful wight drownd in despair,
which now hath need of thee.
Who craves my help wants hap:
But what afflicts the so?

My hope is turned to despair,
My friend become my foe.

Who vowed her self to me,
But perjured of her faith,
Performeth not she promised,
As careless what she saith,

Ah tyrant that she is:
But what doost thou intend• ▪
That with one death ten thousand deaths
might have their final end.
Oh man for ferry boat,
Go do what is assigned,
Despairing souls of Lovers feigned
May here no passage find.

Oh Caron cruel wretch,
That thus hast mocked me:
These hands of mine shall make a boat
To pass in spight of thee.

These eyes that stand with ••ares
Shall make a flood to flow▪
This heart shall stuff my sails with sighs,
And force my boat to go.
