Still in the trace of my tormented thought,
My ceaseless cars must mattch on to my death:
Thy least regard too dearly have I bought,
Who to my comfort never deignest a breath.
Why should'st thou stop thine cares now to my cries,
Whose eyes were open, ready to oppress me?
Why shuttest thou not the cause whence all did rise,
Or hear me now, and seek how to redress me?
Injurious DELIA, yet He love thee still,
Whilst that I breathe in sorrow of my smart:
Ile tell the world that I deserved but ill,
And blame my self for to excuse thy hart.
Then judge who sinnes the greater of us twaine,
I in my love, or thou in thy disdain.
