DELIA, these eyes that so admireth thine,
Have seen those walls the which ambition reared,
To check the world, how they intombd have lyen.
Within themselves; and on them ploughs have eared.
Yet found I that no barbarous hand attained.
The spoil of same deserved by vertuous men:
Whose glorious actions luckily had gained,
Th'eternall Annals of a happy pen.
Why then though DELIA fade, let that not move her,
Though time do spoil her of the fairest vaile
That ever yet mortality did cover;
Which must instarre the needle and the Rail.
That grace, that vertue, all that served t'in-woman,
Doth thee unto eternity assommon.
