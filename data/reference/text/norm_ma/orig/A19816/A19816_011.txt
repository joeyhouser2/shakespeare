IF this be love, to draw a weary breath,
Paint on floods, till the shore cry to th'ayre:
With downward looks, still reading on the earth;
The sad memorials of my loves despair.
If this be love, to war against my soul,
Lie down to wail, rise up to sigh and grieve,
The never-resting stone of care to roll,
Still to complain my griefs, whilst none relieve.
If this be love, to cloth me with dark thoughts,
Haunting untrodden paths to wail apart;
My pleasures horror, Musique tragic notes,
Tears in mine eyes, and sorrow at my hart.
If this be love, to live a living death,
O then love I, and draw this weary breath.
