THE star of my mishap imposed this pain,
To spend the Aprill of my years in wailing,
That ever found my fortune in the wain,
With still fresh cares my present woes assailing.
Yet her I blame not, though for her tis done,
But my desires wings so high aspiring,
Which now are melted by that glorious Sun,
That makes in fall from off my hie desiring.
And in my fall, I cry for help with speed.
No pitying eye looks back upon my mourning,
No succour find I now when most I need,
Th' Ocean of my tears must drown me burning.
Whilst my distress shall christen her a new,
And give the Cruel Fair this title due.
