Mark when she smiles with amiable cheare,
And tell me whereto can ye liken it:
when on each eyelid sweetly do appear,
an hundred Graces as in shade to sit.
Likest it seemeth in my simple wit
unto the fair sunshine in somers day:
that when a dreadful storm away is flit,
through the broad world doth spred his goodly ray
At sight whereof each bird that sits on spray,
and every beast that to his den was fled:
comes forth afresh out of their late dismay,
and to the light lift up their drooping hed.
So my storm beaten hart likewise is cheered,
with that sunshine when cloudy looks are cleared.
