What guile is this, that those her golden tresses,
She doth attire under a net of gold:
and with sl• skill so cunningly them dresses,
that which is gold or hear, may scarce be told?
Is it that mens frail eyes, which gaze too bold,
she may entangle in that golden snare:
and being caught may craftily enfold,
their weaker hearts, which are not well aware?
Take heed therefore, mine eyes, how ye do •tare
hence forth too rashly on that guileful net,
in which if ever ye entrapped are,
out of her bans ye by no means shall get.
Fondness it were for any being free,
to covet fetters, though they golden be.
