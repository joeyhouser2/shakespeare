Leave lady in your glass of christall clene,
Your goodly self for evermore to vew:
and in my self, my inward self I mean,
most lively like behold your semblant trew.
Within my hart, though hardly it can shew,
thing so divine to vew of earthly eye:
the fair Idea of your celestial hew,
and every part remains immortally:
And were it not that through your cruelty,
with sorrow dimmed and deformed it were:
the goodly ymage of your visnomy,
clearer then christall would therein appear.
But if your self in me ye plain will see,
remove the cause by which your fair beams darkened be.
