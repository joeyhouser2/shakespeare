One day as I unwarily did gaze
on those fair eyes my loves immortal light:
the whiles my stonisht hart stood in amaze,
through sweet illusion of her looks delight.
I mote perceive how in her glancing sight,
legions of loves with little wings did fly:
darting their deadly arrows fyry bright,
at every rash beholder passing by.
One of those archers closely I did spy,
aiming his arrow at my very hart:
when suddenly with twincle of her eye,
the Damosel broke his misintended dart.
Had she not so done, sure I had bene slain,
yet as it was, I hardly scaped with pain.
