The weary year his race now having run,
The new begins his compast course anew:
with shew of morning mild he hath begun,
be•okening peace and plenty to ensew,
So let us, which this change of weather vew,
change •eke our minds and former lives amend
the old years sinnes forepast let us eschew,
and fly the faults with which we did offend.
Then shall the new years joy forth freshly send,
into the glooming world his gladsome ray:
and all these storms which now his beauty blend,
shall turn to caulmes and timely clear away.
So likewise love cheare you your heavy spright,
