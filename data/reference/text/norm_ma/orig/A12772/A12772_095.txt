She took him streight full piteous lamenting,
and wrapt him in her smock:
She wrapt him softly, all the while repenting,
that he the fly did mock.
She drest his wound and it embalmed well
with s•lue of sovereign might:
And then she bathed him in a dainty well
the well of deare delight.
Who would not oft be stung as this,
to be so bathed in Venus bliss.
