The laurel leaf, which you this day do wear,
guies me great hope of your relenting mind:
for since it is the badge which I do bear,
ye bearing it do seem to me inclined:
The pour thereof, which o•te in me I find,
let it likewise your gentle brest inspire
with sweet infusion, and put you in mind
of that proud maid, whom now those leaves attire
Proud Daphne scorning Phaebus lovely fire,
on the Thessalian shore from him did fly:
for which the gods in their revengeful yre
did her transform into a laurel tree.
Then fly no more fair love from Phebus chace,
but in your brest his leaf and love embrace.
