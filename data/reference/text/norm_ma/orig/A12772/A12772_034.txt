The painful smith with force of fervent heat,
the hardest yron soon doth mollify:
that with his heavy sledge he can it beat,
and fashion to what he it list apply.
Yet cannot all these flames in which I fry,
her hart more harden then yron soft awhit:
ne all the plaints and prayers with which I
do beat on th'anduyle of her stubborn wit:
But still the more she fervent sees my sit:
the more she frieseth in her wilful pride:
and harder grows the harder she is smit,
with all the pla•nts which to her be applied.
What then remains but I to ashes burn,
and she to stones at length all frozen turn?
