Not stayed state, but feeble stay, not costly robes, but bare array,
Not passed wealth, but present want, not heaped store, but slender skant
Not plenties purse, but poor estate, not happy hap, but froward fate:
Not wish at will, but want of joy, not hearts good health, but hearts annoy.
Not freedoms use, but prisoners thrall, not costly seat, but lowest fall:
Not weal I mean but wretched woe, doth truely try the friend from foe:
And naught but froward fortune proves, who fawning feigns, or simply loves.
