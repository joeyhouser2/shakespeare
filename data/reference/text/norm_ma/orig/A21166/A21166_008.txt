What grieves my bones, and makes my body faint?
What pricks my flesh, and tears my head in twaine?
Why do I wake, when rest should me attaint?
When others laugh, why do I live in pain?
I toss, I turn, I change from side to side,
And stretch me oft, in sorrows links betide.

I toss, as one betost in waves of care,
I turn, to fly the woes of loathsome life?
I change, to spy if death this corpes might spare,
I stretch to heaven, to rid me of this strife.
Thus do I stretch, and change, and toss, and turn,
While I in hope of heaven, my life do burn.

Then hold thee still, let be thy heaviness,
Abolish care, forget thy pining woe:
For by this means, soon shalt thou find redress,
When oft betost, hence thou to heaven must go.
Then toss and tourne, and tumble frank and free,
O happy thrice, when thou in heaven shalt be.
