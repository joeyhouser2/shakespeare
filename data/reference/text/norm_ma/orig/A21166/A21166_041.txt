A Trusty frend is rare to find, a fawning foe may soon be got,
A faithful frend bear still in mind, but fawning so regard thou not.
A faithful frend no cloke doth crave, to coler knavery withal,
But Sicophant a gun must have, to bear a port what ere be fall.
A nose to smell out every feast, a brasen face to set it out,
A shameless child or homely gest, whose life doth like to range about:
A fawning foe while wealth doth last, a thief to rob and spoil his frend,
As strong as oke while wealth doth last, but rotten stick doth prove in the end.

Looke first, then leap, beware the mire,
Burnt child is warned to dread the fire▪
Take heed my frend, remember this,
Short horse (they say) soon curried is.
