Beware of had I wist, whose fine brings care and smart,
Esteem of all as they deserve, and deem as deemed thou art:
So shall thy perfect frend, enjoy his hoped hire,
And faithless fawning foe shall miss, th'effect of his desire:
Good will shall have his gain, and hate shall heap despight,
A faithless frend shall find distrust, and love shall reap delight:
Thy self shall rest in peace, thy frend shall joy thy fate,
Thy foe shall fret at thy good happen, and I shall joy thy state:
But this my fond advise, may seem perchance but vain,
As rather teaching how to lose, then how a frend to gain:
But this not my intent, to teach to find a friend,
But safely how to love and live, is all that I intend:
And if you prove in part, and find my counsel true,
Then wish me well for my good will, tis all I crave adue,
