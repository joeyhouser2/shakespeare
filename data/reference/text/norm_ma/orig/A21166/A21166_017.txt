The day delayed, of that I most do wish,
Wherewith I feed, and starve in one degree:
With wish and want, still served in one dish,
A live as dead, by proof as you may see:
To whom of old, this Proverb well it serves,
While grass doth grow, the silly horse he starves:
Tween these extremes, thus do I room the race,
Of my poor life, this certainly I know:
Tween would and want, unwarely that do pass,
More swift then shot, out of the Archers vow:
As Spider draws her line all day,
I watch the net, and others have the pray.

And as by proof, the greedy Dog doth gnaw,
The bared bone, all only for the taste:
So to and fro, this lothsome life I draw,
With fancies forced, and fed with vain repast:
Narsissus brought, unto the water drink,
So aye thirst I, the more that I do drink.

Loe thus I die, and yet I seem not sick,
With smart unseen my self, my self I wear:
With prone desire, and power that is not quick,
With hope a loft, now drenched in disprayre:
Trained in trust, for no reward assigned,
The more I hast, the more I come behind.

With hurt to heal, in frozen Ise to fry,
With loss to laugh, this is a wonderous case:
Fast fetred here, is forced away to fly,
As hunted Hare, that Hound hath in the chase:
With wings and spurs, for all the hast I make,
As like to lose, as for to draw the stake.

The days be long, that hang upon desart,
The life is irk of joys that be delayed:
The time is short, for to requite the smart,
That doth proceed, of promise long unpayed:
That to the last, of this my fainting breath,
I wish exchange of life, for happy death.
