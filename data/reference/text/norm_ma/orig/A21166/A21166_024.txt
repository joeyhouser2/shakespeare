The deep turmoiled wight, that lives devoid of ease,
Whose wayward wits are often found, more wavering then the seas:
Seeks sweet repose abroad, and takes delight to room,
Where reason leaves the Snail for rule, to keep a quiet home.

Leap not before thou look, lest harm thy hope assail,
Hast havoc makes in hurtful wise, wherefore be slow as Sail:
Refrain from rash attempt, let take heed be thy skill,
Let wisdom bridle brainsick wit, and leisure work thy will.

Dame reason bids I say, in things of doubt be slack,
Lest rashness purchase us the wrong, that wisdom wills us lack:
By rashness divers have bene deadly overcome,
By kindly creeping on like Snail, duke Fabe his fame hath won.

Though some as swift as hawks, can stoop to every stale,
Yet I refuse such sodayne flight, and will seem slow as Snail:
Wherefore my pretty Snail, be still and lap thee warm.
Save envies frets mauger their fumes, there• few shall do thee harm.

Because in some respect, thou holds me to be wise,
I place thee for a Presedent, and sign before mine eyes:
Was never any yet, that harm in thee could find,
Or dare avow that ever Snail, wrought hurt to human kind.

I know dame Physic doth, thy friendly help implore,
And crau's the salve from thee ensues, to cure the crazed sore:
Sith Physic then allows, the virtues in degree,
In spight of spight I wear thee still, that well contenteth me.
