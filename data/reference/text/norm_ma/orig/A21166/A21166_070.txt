Like as the doleful Dove, delights alone to be,
And doth refuse the bloumed branch, choosing the leafless tree:
whereon wailing his chance, with bitter tears besprent,
Doth with his bill, his tender breast, oft pierce and all to rent.
Whose grievous groanings tho whose gripes of pining pain,
whose gastly looks, whose bloody streams out flowing from each vain:
Whose falling from the tree, whose panting on the ground,
Examples be of mine estate, tho there appear no wound.
