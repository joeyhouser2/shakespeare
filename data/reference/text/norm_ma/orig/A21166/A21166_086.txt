IF frendlesse faith? if guiltless thought may shield?
If simple truth, that never meant to swerve?
If deare desire, accepted fruit do yield,
If greedy lust, in loyal life do searue.
then may my plaint, bewail my heavy harm,
That seeking calm, have stumbled on the storm.

My wonted cheare, Eclipsed by the cloud,
Of deep disdain, through error of report,
If weary woe, enwrapped in the shroud,
Lies slain by tongue, of the unfriendly sort.
Yet heaven and earth, and all that nature wrought,
I call to vow of my unspotted thought.

No shade I seek, in part to shield my tainte,
But simple truth, I hunt no other sute:
On that I gape, the issue of my plaint,
If that I quail, let justice me confute.
If that my place, emongest the guiltless sort,
Repay by doom, my name and good report.

Go heavy verse, pursue desired grace,
Where pity shrinde, in cell of secret brest,
Awaits my hast, the rightful lot to place,
And loathes to see, the guiltless man opprest.
Whose virtues great, hath crowned her more with fame,
then kingly state, though largely shine the same.
