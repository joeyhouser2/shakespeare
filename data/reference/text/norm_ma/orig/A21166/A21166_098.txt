O Heavenly God, O Father deare, cast down thy tender eye,
Upon a wretch, that prostrate here, before thy face doth lie:
O pour thy precious oil of grace, into my wounded hart,
O let the drops of mercy swage, the rigour of my smart.

My fainting soul suppressed sore, with careful clog of sin,
In humble sort submittes it self, thy mercy for to win:
Graunt mercy then, O Saviour sweet, to me most woeful thrall,
Whose mournful cry, to thee O Lord, doth still for mercy call.

Thy blessed will I have despised, upon a stubborn mind,
And to the sway of worldly things, my self I have inclined:
Forgetting heaven, and heavenly powers, where God and Saints do dwell,
My life had like to tread the path, that leads the way to hell.

But now my Lord, my Lodestar bright, I will no more do so,
To think upon my former life, my hart doth melt for woe:
Alas I sigh, alas I sob, alas I do repent,
That ever my licentious will, so wickedly was bent.

Sith thus therefore, with careful plaint, I do thy mercy crave,
O Lord for thy great mercies sake, let me thy mercy have:
Restore to life the wretched soul, that else is like to die,
So shall my voice unto thy name, sing praise eternally.

Now blessed be the Father first, and blessed be the Son,
And blessed be the holy Ghost, by whom all things are done:
Bless me O blessed Trinitie, with thy eternal grace,
That after death my soul may have, in heaven a dwelling place.
