I Am not as I seem to be, nor when I smile, I am not glad,
A thrall although you count me free, I most in mirth, most pensive sad:
I smile to shade my bitter spight, as Haniball that saw in sight,
His country soil with Carthage town, by Roman force defaced down.

And Caesar that preserved was, with noble Pompeis princely hed,
As twere some Judge to rule the case, a flood of tears he seemed to shed:
Although in deed it sprong of joy, yet other thought it was annoy,
Thus contraries be used I find, of wise to cloke the covert mind.

I Haniball that smiles for grief, and let you Caesars tears suffice,
The one that laughs at his mischief, the other all for joy that cries:
I smile to see me scorned so, you weep for joy to see me woe,
And I in heart by Love slain dead, presents a place of Pompeis head.

O cruel hap, and hard estate, that forceth me to love my foe,
Accursed be so foul a fate, my choise for to prefix it so:
So long to fight with secret sore, and find no secret salve therefore,
Some purge their pain by plaint I find, but I in vain do breath my wind.
