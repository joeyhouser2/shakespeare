IN quest of my relief, I find distress,
In recompence of Love, most deep disdain:
My languor such as words may not express,
A shower of tears, my watery eye doth rain.
I dream of this, and do define of woe,
I wander in the thoughts of my sweet foe.

I would no peace, the cause of war I fly,
I hope, I fear, I burn, I chill in Frost:
I lie a low, yet mounts my mind on hye,
thus doubtful storms, my troubled thoughts have tost.
And for my pain, this pleasure do I prove,
I hate my self, and pine in others Love.

The world I grasp, yet hold I nought at all,
At liberty I seem, in prison pent:
I taste the sweet, more sower then bitter gall,
My ship seems sound, and yet her ribs be rent.
And out alas, on Fortune false I cry,
Look what I crave, that still she doth deny.

Both life and death, be equal unto me,
I do desire to die, yet crave I life:
My wits with sundry thoughts do disagree,
My self am with my self at mortal strife.
As warmeth of Sun, doth melt the silver snow,
The heat of Love, behold consumes me so.
