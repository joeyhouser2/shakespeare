O Sovereign salve of sin, who doest my soul behold,
That seeks her self from tangling faults, by striving to unfold,
What plea shall I put in, when thou doest Summons send:
To judge the people of the yearth, and give the world and end,
When every deed and word, yea every secret thought,
In open vewe of all the world, shall unto light be brought.

So many Judges shall against me sentence give,
As by example of good works, hath taught how I should live:
So many pleaders shall confound my careful case,
As have in one by sound advise, sought to engraft by grace.
So many shall that time, against me witness bear,
As have beheld my fruitless faith, and saw my sinnes appear.

Whereon whils I do muse, in my amazed mind,
Froward thoughts, familiar foes, most fiers assaults I find:
My conscience to my face, doth flatly me accuse,
My secret thoughts within my ears, do whisper still these news.
Mine avarice and bribery▪ my pride doth brag me down,
Mine envy frets me like a file, at other folks renown.

Concupiscence inflames, and lustest my limbs infect,
My meat doth burthen, and my drink my weakness doth detect:
My slanders rend my fame, ambition doth supplant,
My greediness is not content, but makes me wail for want.
My mirth but flattery is, my sorrows are unkind,
Sith pleasures run me out of breath, and griefs suppress my mind.

Behold my God, whose might, may me a freeman make,
These were my friends, whose counsels curst, I was content to take:
These were the lawless Lords, whom I did serve alway,
These were the masters whose mad hests, I did too much obaie
Behold my faults most foul, which folly first did frame,
In loving them I should have loathed, whens breedeth all my bane.

Now I do look aloft, with bashful blushing face,
On glory thine, that so I may discern my own disgrace
My many spots and great, must needs encrease my gilt,
Unless thou wash them in the blood, that for my sake was spilt.
Forgive the faults O Lord, which I from hart repent,
And graunt my days to come, may be in thy sweet service spent.
