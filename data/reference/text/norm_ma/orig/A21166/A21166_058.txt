Where gripping grief the hart would wound, and doleful dumps the mind oppress,
There Music with her silver sound, is wont with speed to geve redress
Of troubled minds for every sore, sweet Music hath a salve in store,
In joy it makes our mirth abound, in grief it cheers our heavy sprites,
The careful head relief hath found, by Musics pleasant sweet delights,
Our senses, what should I say more, are subject unto Musics lore.
The Gods by Music hath their pray, the foul therein doth joy,
For as the Roman Poets say, in seas whom Pirates would destroy:
A Dolphin saude from death most sharp, Arion playing on his Harp,
Of heavenly gift, that turns the mind like as starne doth rule the ship:
Oh Music whom the Gods assigned to comfort man, whon cares would nip,
Sith thou both man and beast doest move, what wisman then will thee reprove.
