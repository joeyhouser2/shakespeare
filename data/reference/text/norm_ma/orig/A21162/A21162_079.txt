IF care or skill, could conquer vain desire,
Or reasons reigns, my strong affection stay
Then should my sighs to quiet brest retire,
And shun such signs, as secret thoughts bewray.
Uncomely Love, which now lurks in my breast,
Should cease my grief, through wisdoms power opprest.

But who can leave, to look on Venus face?
Or yieldeth not, to Junos high estate:
What wit so wise, as gives not Pallas place,
These virtues rare, each Gods did yield amate.
Save her alone, who yet on yearth doeth reign,
Whose beauties string, no God can well destraine.

What worldly wight, can hope for heavenly hire,
When only sights, must make his secret move:
A silent sute, doeth selde to grace aspire,
My hapless hap doeth roll the restless stone,
Yet Phebe fair, disdainde the heavens above,
To joy on yearth, her poor Edimions love.

Rare is reward, where none can justly crave,
For chance is choise, where reason makes no claim:
Yet luck sometimes, despairing souls doeth save,
A happy Star made Giges joy attain.
A slavish Smithe, of rude and rascal race,
Found means in time, to gain a Goddes grace.

Then lofty Love, thy sacred sails advance,
My sithyng seas shall flow with streams of tears:
Amids disdain, drive forth my doleful chance.
A valiant mind, no deadly danger fears.
Who loves aloft, and sets his harte on hie,
Deserves no pain, though he do pine and die.
