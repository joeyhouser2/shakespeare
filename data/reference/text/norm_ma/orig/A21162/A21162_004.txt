Not stayed state, but feeble stay, not costly robes, but bare array:
Not passed wealth, but present want, not heaped store but sclender skant
Not plenties purse, but poor estate, not happy hap, but troward fate:
Not wish at will, but want of joy, not hearts good health but hearts annoy:
No freedoms use, but prisoners thrall, not costly seat, but lowest fall:
Not weal I mean, but wretched woe, doth truely try, the fiend fron foe:
And nowght but forward Fortune proves, who fawning feigns, or simply loves.
