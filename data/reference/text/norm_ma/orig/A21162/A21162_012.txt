All mortal men this day rejoice, in Christe that you redeemed hath,
By death with death sing we with voice, to him that hath appesd gods wrath
Due unto man for sinful path, wherein before he went astray,
Give thanks to him with perfect faith that for man kind hath made this glorious day.
This day he rose fron tomb again, wherein his precious corse was laid,
Whom cruelly the Jewes had slain, with bloody wounds full ill arrayed:
O man be now no more dismayed, if thou henceforth from sin do stay,
Of death thou needest not be afraid, Christe conquered death for this his glorious day.
His death prevailed had not whit, As Paule the apostle well doth write:
Except he had uprisen yet, from death to life by Godlike might:
With most triumphant glittering light,
This day his glory shined I say, and made us bright as sun this glorious day
O man arise with Christ therefore, since he from sin hath made thee fre,
Beware thou fall in sin no more, but rise as Christ did rise for thee:
So maist thou him in glory see, when he at day of doom shall say,
Come thou my child and dwell with me, God graunt us all to see that glorious day.
