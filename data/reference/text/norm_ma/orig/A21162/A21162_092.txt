IN terrors trap with thraldom thrust,
Their thorny thoughts to taste and try:
In conscience clear from cause unjust,
With carping tears did call and cry.
And said O God, yet thou art he,
That can and will deliver me. Bis.

Thus trembling there with tears I trod,
To totter tide in truths defence:
With sighs and sobs, I said O God,
Let right not have this recompence.
Lest that my foes might laugh to se,
That thou wouldest not deliver me. Bis.

My soul then to repentance ran,
My ragged clothes all rent and torn:
And did bewail the loss it wanne,
With lothsome life so long forlorn.
And said O God, yet thou art he,
That can and will deliver me. Bis.

Then comfort came with clothes of joy,
Whose seems were faithful steadfastness:
And did bedeck the naked boy,
That erst was full of wretchedness.
And said be glad for God is he,
That shortly will deliver thee. Bis.
