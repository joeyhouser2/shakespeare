Jf Fortune be thy stay, thy state is very tickle,
She bears a double face, disguised, false, and fickle:
This day she seems to smile, to morrow will she frown,
What now she sets aloft, anon she throweth down:
Fly Fortunes sly deceits, let Vertue be thy guide,
If that you do intend in happy state to abide.

Upon the settled rock thy building surest stands,
Away it quickly wears, that resteth on the sands:
Dame Vertue is the rock, that yields assured stay,
Dame Fortune is the sand, tha skoureth soon away:
Choose that is certain, let thing uncertain pass,
Prefer the precious gold, before the brittle glass.

Sly Fortune hath her slights, she plays upon the pack,
Look whom she favours most, at length she turns to wrack:
But Vertue simply deals, she shuns deceitful train,
Who is by Vertue raised up, shall never fall again:
Stick fast to Vertue then, that gives assured trust,
And fly from Fortunes frekes, that ever prove unjust
