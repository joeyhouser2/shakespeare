I Would it were not as I think I would it were not so,
I am not blind although I wink, I feel what winds do blow:
I know where craft, with smiling cheare, creps into boldened breast.
I hear how feigned speech speaks fair, where hatred is possess.
I see the Serpent lie and lurcke, under the green allow,
I see him watch a time to work, his poison to bestow.

In friendly looks such fraud is found, as faith for fear is fled,
And friendship hath receiude such wound, as he is almost ded.
And hateful harte with malice great, so boils in cankered mind,
That flattery flearyng in the face, had almost made me blind.
But now I see all is not gold, that glittereth in the eye,
Nor yet such friends as they profess, as now by proof I try.

Though secret spight by craft, hath made a coat of Panter skin,
And thinks to find me in the shade, by sleight to wrapp me in,
Yet God be praised my eye is clear, and can behold the Sun:
When falsehood dare not once appear, to end that he begun,
Thus time shall try the thing amiss, which God save shortly send,
And tourne the harte that feigned is, to be a faithful friend.
