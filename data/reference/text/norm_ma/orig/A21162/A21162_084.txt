IN wealth we se some wealthy men, abound in wealth most welthely
In wealth we se those men again, in wealth do live most wretchedly.
And yet of wealth having more store,
Then erst of wealth they had before.
These wealthy men do seem to want, they seem to want the most they have
The more posts, the more they crave, the more they crave the greater store
That most they have, they think but skant.
Yet not content, woe be therefore,
The simple men that less wealth have with lesser wealth we se content,
Content are they twixt wealth and scathe, a life to lead indifferent.
And thus of wealth these men have more,
Then those of which we spoke before.
