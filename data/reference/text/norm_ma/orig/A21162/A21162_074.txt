Shall I no way win you, to graunt my desire?
What woman will graunt you, the thing you require:
You only to Love me, is all that I crave,
You only to leave me, is all I would have.
My deare alas, now say not so,
To Love you best I must say no:
Yet will I not flit,
then play on the bit.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not.
Make me your man,
beshrew me than.
The swifter I follow, then you fly away,
Swift hauks in their flying, oft times miss their pray,
Yet some killeth deadly, that fly to the mark:
You shall touch no feather, thereof take no cark.
Yet hope shall further my desire,
You blow the coals, and raise no fire,
Yet will I not flit,
then play on the bit,
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,

beshrew me than.
To Love is no danger, where true Love is ment,
I will Love no ranger, least that I repent:
My Love is no raunger, I make God avow,
To trust your smooth saiyngs, I sure know not how.
Most truth I mean, as time shall well try,
No truth in men, I ofte espy,
Yet will I not flit,
then play on the bit.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrew me than.
Some women may say nay, and mean Love most true,
Some women can make fools, of as wise men as you:
In time I shall catch you, I know when and where:
I will soon dispatch you, you shall not come there.
Some speds at length, that ofte have mist,
I am well armed, come when you list.
Yet will I not flit,
then play on the bit.
I will,
do still,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
beshrew me than.
Yet work your kind kindly, graunt me Love for Love,
I will use you friendly, as I shall you prove:
Most true you shall find me, I this do protest,
Then sure you shall bind me, to grant your request.
O happy thread, now have I spun,
You sing before the conquest won.
Why then, will you swerve,
even as you deserve:
Love still,
I will,
yet kill not,
I will not,
Make me your man,
come to me than.
