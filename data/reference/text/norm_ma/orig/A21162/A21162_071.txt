A Lack when I look back, upon my youth thatz past,
And deeply pouder youths offence, and youths reward at laste.
With sighs and tears I say, O God I not deny,
My youth with folly hath deserved, with folly for to die.
But yet if ever sinful man, might mercy move to ruth,
Good Lord with mercy do forgive, the follies of my youth.

In youth I ranged the fields, where vices all did grow,
In youth alas I wanted grace, such vise to overthrow,
In youth what I thought sweet, most bitter now do find,
Thus hath the follies of my youth, with folly kept me blind
Yet as the Egle casts her bill, whereby her age reneweth,
So Lord with mercy do forgive, the follies of my youth.
