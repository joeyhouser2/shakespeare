The fire shall freze, the frost shall fry the frozen mountains hie
What strange things hath dame natures force, to turn her course awry
My Love hath me left, and taken a new man:
This is not strange, it haps ofte times, the truth to scan,
The more is my pain,
her Love then refrain.
Who thought she would flit,
each one that hath wit,
Is this not strange,
light Love will change.

Why then there is no choise, but all women will change,
As men do use, so some women do Love to range.

Sith slipper gain falls to my lot, farewell that gliding pray,
Sith that the dice doeth run awry, betimes leave of thy play.
I will no more lament, the thing I may not have,
Then by exchange the loss to come, all shalt thou save.
Love will I refrain,
thereby thou shalt gain,
With loss I will leave,
she will thee deceive:
That is not strange,
then let her range.
