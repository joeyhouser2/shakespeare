A Trusty frend is rare to find, a fawning foe may soon be got:
A faithful frend bere still in mind, but fawning foe regard thou not
A faithful freend no cloke doth crave, to colour knavery withal:
But Sicophant a Gone must have, to bear a port what ere befall.
A nose to smel out every feast, a brasen face to set it out:
A shameless child or homly geast, whose life doth like to range about.
A fawning foe while wealth doth last, a thief to rob and spoil his freend:
As strong as oke till wealth doth last, but rotten stick doth prove in the end

Look first, then leap, beware the mire:
Burnt Child is warned to dread the fire.
Take heed my freend, remember this.
Short horse (they say) soon curried is.
