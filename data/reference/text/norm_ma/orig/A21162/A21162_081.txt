I Am not as seem to be, nor when I smile, I am not glad:
A thrall although you count me free, I most in mirth, most pensive sad:
I smile to shade my bitter spight, as Haniball that saw in sight:
His country soil with Carthage town: by Romain force defaced doun.

And Cesar that presented was, with noble Pompeis princely head,
As twere some judge to rule the cace a flould of tears he semde to shed
Although in deed it sprong of joy, yet other thought it was annoy,
Thus contraries be used I find, of wise to cloke the covert mind.

I Haniball that smiles for grief, and let you Cesars grief suffice,
The one that laughs at his mischief, the other all for joy that cries:
I smile to see me scorned so, you weep for joy to see me woe,
And I a harte by Love slain dead, presents in place of Pompeis head.

O cruel hap, and harden estate, that forceth me to love my foe,
Accursed be so foul a fate, my choice for to prefix it so:
So long to fight with secret sore, and find no secret salve therefore,
Some urge their pain by plaint I find, but I in vain do breath my wind.
