The sailing ships with joy at length, do touch their long desired port,
The hewing axe the Oke doth wast, the battering Canon breaks the fort.
Hard haggard hawks stoop to the lure, wild colts in time the bridle tames:
There is nothing so out of ure, but to his kind long time it frames.
Yet this I find in time, no time can win my sute,
Though oft the tree I climb, I can not catch the fruit.

And yet the pleasant branches oft, in yielding wise to me do bow,
When I would touch they spring aloft, soon are they gone I wot not how
Thus I present the fleeting flood, like Tantalus in hell below,
Would God my case she understood, whirh can full soon releeve my woe
Which if to her were known, the fruit were surely mine,
She would not let me grone, and brouse upon the rine.

But if my ship with tackle torn, with rented sails must needs retire.
And stream and wind hath plainly sworn, by force to hinder my desire
Like one that stricks upon the rocks, my weary wrack I should bewail
And learn to know false fortunes mocks, who smiles on me to small avail.
Yet sith she only can, my rented ship restore,
To help her wracked man, but once I seek no more.
