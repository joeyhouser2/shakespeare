Where gripping grief the hart would wound, and doleful dumps the mind oppress,
There Music with her silver sound, is wont with speed to give redress:
Of troubled minds for every sore, sweet Music hath a salve in store.
In joy it makes our mirth abound, in grief it chers our heavy spirits,
The careful head relief hath found, by Musics pleasant sweet delits:
Our senses, what should I say more, are subject unto Musics lore.

The Gods by Music hath their pray, the foul therein doeth joy,
For as the Roman Poets say, in seas whom Pirates would destroy,
A Dolphin saude from death most sharp, Arion playing on his harp
Oh heavenly gift, that turns the mind, like as the stern doeth rule the ship,
Of music whon the gods assigned to confort man, whom cares would nip
Sith thou both man and beast doest move, what wiseman then will thee reprove.
