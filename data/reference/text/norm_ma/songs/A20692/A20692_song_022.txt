1 O, I am as heavy as earth,
Say then who is Humor now.
1 Why then tis I am drowned in Woe,
But never Humor, etc.
1 Mirth then is drowned in sorrows brim,
Oh, in sorrow all things sleep.
1 In her presence all things smile,
Humor frolic then a while.
But never Humor, etc.

2 I am now inclined to mirth, humor I as well as thou.
2 No no wit is cherished so, but never humor: etc.
2 No no fool the light's things swim▪ heavy things sinck to the deep: but never humor: etc.
